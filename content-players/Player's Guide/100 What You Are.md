# 100 · What You Are

Nine Progenitors were born out of the Planar Collision. Each one wanted company and each one made a people.

That is where mortals come from. Every one of the nine is playable and none of them is the default.

*[[110 Humans|Humans]], made by Minem.* In every kingdom and in charge of none of them.

*[[120 Giantkin|Giantkin]], made by Kleipur,* who was made first and out of the most material.

*[[130 Elves|Elves]], made by Sylvana Lorien,* whose name is gone.

*[[140 Orcs|Orcs]], made by Shram.*

*[[150 Omens|Omens]], made by Thean,* out of the leftover pieces of the split Judgements.

*[[160 Beastfolk|Beastfolk]], made by Numen* with help from the dragons.

*[[170 Elementals|Elementals]], the genasi, made by Genasi* with help from the dragons.

*[[180 Earthborn|Earthborn]], made by Talav.*

*[[190 Feyborn|Feyborn]], made by Sithi,* who paid the Archfey with their own name.

Half-species are all available. They were not made by anyone in particular, which is its own kind of interesting.

If you want to play something not on this list, ask me. The answer is usually yes and I will tell you which category it lands in, or whether it is something stranger.

# 330 · Sorcerer

Sorcery is inherited. In a world where the powerful are specific, reachable beings with family histories, that means your power came from somewhere traceable.

**The usual sources.**

*Dragons.* Ten of them, five ruling the elemental realms and five made by those five. Dragon blood in a mortal line is not unusual and the Zygaris Society takes an interest in people who have it.

*The fey.* The archfey have been involved with mortals for as long as mortals have existed, and feyborn are an entire category of people. Fey ancestry is common and rarely documented.

*The archdevils and archangels.* It happens, and there is a subclass written for it: [[Sorcerer Devil|Sorcerer, the Devil bloodline]], where the devil in your line decides what your magic is made of. The Archdevil of Pride had a child with the dragon who rules the elemental realm of fire, and that child became a king of Geno, so the bar for what is possible here is quite high.

*The elements.* The genasi were made from the elemental realms by a Progenitor who used them as the model. An elemental line is a different thing from a fey or draconic one.

*An ascendant god.* The newest option. They were mortals, they had families, and most of those families are gone, which raises the question of what happened to the ones that are not.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character knows where it came from. Plenty of sorcerers do not, and finding out is a whole campaign for some people.

# 310 · Cleric

Your god exists, has a location, and can be spoken to. People do it regularly. That changes what being a cleric means here.

**They know you.** Not in a general sense. A cleric is a known quantity to the thing they serve, and the relationship runs both ways.

**Worship is organised.** Temples have hierarchies, ranks and politics, and most clerics came up through one. You probably have a mentor, an order, someone you report to and someone who reports to you. Mathair restricts no worship at all and has a temple district to prove it. Krotaphos worships the archangels and archdevils both and has rules about how. Geno worships the dragons more than anywhere else does.

**Being sent somewhere is normal.** Temples give their clerics errands, and a cleric a long way from home is usually a cleric on an errand.

Your domain is between you and the Player's Handbook. Who you serve is the part that matters here, and you can serve anything in the world large enough to notice you.

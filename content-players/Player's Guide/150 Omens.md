# 150 · Omens

Thean made the Omens, and Thean had the strangest material to work with. When the King of Night split the six Judgements in two, there were pieces left over. The Omens were made out of those pieces mixed with everything else.

That is why Omens come in two leanings. **Ill**, which plays as a tiefling, and **Good**, which plays as an aasimar. Neither leaning says anything about the person. The archdevils are not evil by nature and the archangels are not good by nature, and the same goes for the people made from their leftovers.

**They built Krotaphos**, the kingdom at Panthea, and ran it as a religious power for centuries. Then they lost a war to Sylfaen in 427 AM and have had very little influence since. Iudimian, their language, is restricted to religious use.

Being an Omen in the wrong room still means something. The archangels and archdevils both have opinions about your people, and not everyone waits to find out which leaning you are.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character practises, and what they think of what Krotaphos has become.

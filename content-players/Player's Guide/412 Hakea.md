# 412 · Hakea

Hakea rules Lush, where everything grows constantly and nothing dies back. She looks like a woman in her middle fifties with a staff and a mossy cloak, and she behaves like one.

**She sees people in order of arrival.** No precedence, no favourites, no skipping the queue because you are important. She gives them tea and tells them things they did not want to know.

She is the most straightforward of the seven to deal with and the most uncomfortable, because she will answer the question you actually asked.

**What she tends to want.** Things kept growing, things kept cut back, and a long-running private matter in the Seasons domain that she has never once confirmed out loud.

**What people assume about you.** That you are harmless. This is useful and it is wrong.

**Decide one thing.** What she told you that you did not want to hear.

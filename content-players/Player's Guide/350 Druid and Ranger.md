# 350 · Druid and Ranger

Druids and rangers draw on the land rather than on a person. In Agra the land is not neutral.

**Every wild place belongs to something.** The five elemental realms each have a dragon over them. The seven fey domains each have an archfey. Even the ground in the mortal realm was assembled out of pieces of the other planes by the Progenitors, who are still alive and still have views.

So a druid drawing deeply on a place is drawing on something that is ruled. Whether the ruler notices or cares is a separate question, and the answer varies enormously. The Archfey of Lush will notice. The dragon who counts the entire earth realm as already-counted hoard will certainly notice.

**Rangers have the same arrangement with a smaller footprint**, which is generally safer.

**Worth deciding.** Which place your character is tied to, and whether they have ever been there. A druid of the deep forest who has never left the kingdom they were born in is a different character from one who has walked in Iris.

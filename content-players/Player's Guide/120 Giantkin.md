# 120 · Giantkin

Kleipur was the first Progenitor made, out of more material than any of the others, and Kleipur is a giant. The giantkin came from that.

**They are older in the world than most.** Kleipur handed power to the Titans in the first year of the Age of the Many, before most of the kingdoms existed, and giantkin memory of that period is long and specific.

Most giantkin live in Oinarria, which they share with the orcs. The arrangement is old, it mostly works, and it surprises outsiders who expect it not to.

Goliaths are the usual build. If you want something else under the giantkin heading, ask me.

**Worth deciding.** What your character's family remembers that other people have forgotten.

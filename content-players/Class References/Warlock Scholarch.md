Your patron is one of the three Scholarchs of Temporalis, who are also the three sphinxes: It That Was, It That Is, and It That Will Be. Each holds one of the realm's three areas and the three of them together keep the balance.

Not many mortals make the trip. Those who do are usually after knowledge, and a few of them leave with a pact instead.

What you are given is not power over time. It is a piece of what your patron already knows, handed over in advance.

## Expanded Spell List

The Scholarch lets you choose from an expanded list of spells when you learn a warlock spell.

| Spell Level | Spells |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | comprehend languages, identify |
| 2nd | augury, see invisibility |
| 3rd | clairvoyance, nondetection |
| 4th | arcane eye, divination |
| 5th | legend lore, scrying |

## Level 1 Foreknowledge

Choose which of the three took you on. Your choice determines how your foreknowledge works, as shown in the Scholarch table. You can change this choice only if your patron changes.

When you finish a short or long rest, roll one d20 and record the number rolled. You can replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by you, or by a creature you can see within 60 feet of you, with this number. Once you do, you lose that recorded die until you finish a rest.

Unless the table below says otherwise, you must choose to use a recorded die before the roll is made.

| Patron | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| It That Was | You may use a recorded die after the roll is made and its result is known, replacing what happened |
| It That Is | You may use a recorded die while incapacitated or surprised, and on a creature within 60 feet that you cannot see |
| It That Will Be | You record one additional die at each rest. Any die you have not used by the end of your next rest is lost |

## Level 6 Consult the Record

Starting at 6th level, you can put a question to your patron. It answers truthfully, in the tense it keeps. It That Was answers about what has happened, It That Is about what is happening now, and It That Will Be about what is likely to happen within the next seven days.

Your patron answers only what it chooses to answer, and its answers are rarely longer than they need to be.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Level 10 Nothing Sudden

At 10th level, you record one additional die when you finish a rest.

You also cannot be surprised while you are conscious.

## Level 14 The Other Two

At 14th level, your patron allows you the methods of its colleagues.

You record one additional die when you finish a rest, and whenever you finish a long rest you can choose which of the three entries in the Scholarch table applies to your recorded dice until your next long rest.

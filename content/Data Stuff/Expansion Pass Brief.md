Read this first, then [[Sourcebook Conventions]], then [[Flavor Box Brief]]. Those two hold the house style. This page holds everything a new session needs to know about the world and about working with Noah, so that nothing has to be rediscovered.

The vault is the source of truth. It lives at `/Users/Noah/Vaults/Project Planar` on Noah's Mac and is a personal app similar to Obsidian, with `[[ ]]` links and YAML frontmatter. It is not Obsidian. When Noah edits a note himself the app sets `updatedAt` in the frontmatter, which is how to tell his edits from ours.

## The Next Task

Noah wants more text basically everywhere. Each sourcebook page should be the equivalent of a printed page in a real sourcebook, which is at least 350 to 400 words of body text, not counting stat blocks, design notes, or Open sections. Many pages are currently 150 to 250.

How to work:

- Go group by group, in the same order as the earlier passes, and show Noah each group before moving on.
- Expand from what is already canon. Read the related pages before writing, because almost every page is cross-referenced by several others and contradictions are easy to make.
- Ask Noah before inventing anything major: a new character, a new event, a motive, a relationship, a ruler. He has said he trusts us with filler, meaning texture, minor places, minor people, daily life, customs. Anything that changes the shape of the world goes to him first, and anything genuinely unsettled goes in the Open section instead of the body.
- Add depth through the specific. What a place smells like, what a domain does to a person over a year, what the bureaucracy looks like, what visitors get wrong. Avoid padding.
- Keep the two layers apart. In-world text in the body, reasoning in Design Notes, gaps in Open.

### Noah's direction on the Hell of Indulgence

Recorded here in his terms so it is not lost. Indulgence ([[Belphegor]]'s hell) is a constant, suffocating, nauseating party. Every sense is flooded with sound, sight, smell, and taste. People arriving think at first that they are having a good time, and slowly or quickly realise it is a hell in its own right. There is nowhere to go where you cannot see, hear and smell people vomiting, drinking and having sex, all of it at once, all the time. The current page reads as too gentle beside this and should be rewritten toward it. This also sets the bar for the other eleven afterlives: each should be concrete enough that a reader can feel what a year there would do to them.

## Working With Noah

**Writing tells to avoid, always.** These are his stated preferences and he notices every one.

- No em dashes.
- No "not X, but Y" or "rather than" pivots used as rhetoric.
- No three-item rhythms. Use two items or four. This includes three adjectives, three clauses, and three parallel sentences.
- No portentous one-line closers at the end of a paragraph or section.
- No editorialising about how clever, elegant or interesting something is, and no telling the reader how to feel.
- No repeated sentence shapes or boilerplate across sibling pages. Shared guidance lives on one parent page and is linked.
- Gender-neutral language where a gender is not set ("ancestors", not "forefathers").
- Rules text is flat and declarative, modelled on the 5e PHB and DMG. Flavor lives in italic quote boxes.

**Process preferences.**

- He wants to collaborate. Ask for his input on major content, offer recommendations, and let him choose.
- Show work group by group, with a short summary of each box or page and a list of what was invented.
- Touch only what the task covers. If something else looks wrong, flag it instead of fixing it silently, unless it is a plain error such as a broken link.
- [[Genoan]] is finalised. Do not revise it.
- Keep the template instruction lines in Class References and in the language notes.
- The Player's Guide is Noah's own voice: casual, direct, funny, second person. Only fix egregious tells in his text. New Player's Guide pages are drafted to match his voice, and he finalises the wording.

**Things that were got wrong before and should not be repeated.**

- Mortals did not exist before the Syzygy. Arenis was made in 10 AF.
- A flavor box must never fix the narrator's own date. No "this year", "last spring", or "before the arrests". The historian is undated.
- Player's Guide pages must never contain DM secrets. Hint that something is there instead.
- Do not present a choice to players as a list of answers. A Player's Guide page gives the player questions to decide, never the answers.
- Check who is who before writing. Sylvana Lorien kept her name; Sithi is the Progenitor who gave hers away. Hekate is not a Titan-campaign PC.

## The Narrator

An unnamed, ungendered, first-person travelling historian who meets people as well as reading records, and finds the two do not always agree. They were made to learn the languages and resent it visibly. Thorough, truthful, dry, with jokes in it. They do not explain how clever anything is.

**Reach.** They have met the archfey, the dragons, people in all five elemental realms, some archangels and archdevils, the winds, the Scholarchs, and many of the ascendant gods. They have never met the Kings, since Nullis is the least reachable realm and nobody can get a letter into its dark half. They have not met Thean, who is in hiding, or Sithi, who has not been seen since the Council of Creation. The reachability ladder on [[Arenis]] decides what anyone plausibly knows. They cannot have witnessed anything before their own lifetime, and they have no fixed date.

**Box style.** A box is a small scene the narrator was present for, grounded in the specific rule of its subject, usually with the narrator slightly worse off at the end. Good models: Tarkis (the woman with the slate, disappointed in the narrator's short list), Lux (the narrator praises him in public and he writes it on the wall), Lilien (the upsold map), Moto (reading the gods' list aloud and setting the rafters alight).

## The Cosmology

The planes are concentric rings around a hole. Each realm has one more portion than the one inside it.

| Ring | Realm | Portions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | [[Nullis]] | Night and Day, the realm of the Kings |
| 3 | [[Temporalis]] | It That Was (a lake ringed by unreachable waterfalls), It That Is (a busy city), It That Will Be (an endless starlit field with unreachable trees) |
| 4 | [[Ventis]] | Four wind quarters that bleed together, floating glass islands, a storm at the centre |
| 5 | [[Tarkis]] | Five elemental realms, Fire, Water, Earth, Noxia, and Air, each touching the other four, with ten inhabited seams between them |
| 6 | [[Iudis]] | Six heavens and six hells branching from [[Liminia]] |
| 7 | [[Iris]] | Seven fey domains: Seasons, Lush, Time, Hunt, Rot, Trickery, Bone |
| 8 | [[Arcanis]] | Eight schools of magic, once thousands until the Coalescence |
| 9 | [[Arenis]] | The mortal world, made from pieces of every other realm |

The ten Tarkis seams are steam, ash, magma, smoke, ice, marsh, acid, dust, plague, and metal. The genasi are modelled on them.

## Calendar and Timeline

The Age of the Few (AF) counts down to the Age of the Many (AM), which counts up. There is no year zero. A year is four months of a hundred days: Aucaela, Auctera, Morcaela, Mortera. The week is nine days, named for the Progenitors. The campaign begins on 85 Morcaela, 702 AM.

Key anchors (the full list is [[Timeline]]):

- c. 10,000 AF. The Judgements, the Kings and the five dragon lords come into being.
- 3,097 AF. The Division. Night splits each Judgement into an archangel and an archdevil.
- 3,096 AF. The Syzygy, the Kings' divorce. Tiyanak kills the original Indri and takes his place.
- 2,987 to 50 AF. The Divine War.
- 50 AF. The war ends, the Nekerukes are made, the Great Separation, the Planar Collision.
- 39 to 10 AF. The Coalescence. 10 AF, Arenis is made and the Progenitors are born.
- 1 AM. The Council of Creation. Ius Primum signed 38 Aucaela, 1 AM, about seven hundred years before the campaign.
- 202 AM. Alistar Stormjaw opens the breach to Tarkis in Geno and founds Tama.
- 397 to 427 AM. Krotaphos loses its war with Sylfaen.
- 405 AM. Iras kills Genasi VII and takes Geno. He abdicates in 419.
- 428 AM onward. La Grande Mascarade, every other year, rotating host.
- 652 AM. Afthor kills Zaros and Vari.
- 700 AM. Indri escapes the underworld as the god of vengeance.
- 702 AM. At the Mascarade in Omphalos, Theophilius Fanatis, enchanted, attacks the king of Sylfaen. Sylfaen orders every citizen of Krotaphos arrested.

**The ascendant gods.** Relic and source for each are on their pages. ## DM Secrets

None of these may appear in the Player's Guide.

- Tiyanak killed the original Indri in 3,096 AF and has worn his face since. The real Indri escaped the underworld in 700 AM as the god of vengeance. Tiyanak knows, has sealed the cage, and is sending warlocks to hunt Indri under various lies: that Indri is causing chaos and must be stopped, that he is an imposter.
- Tiyanak wants [[Ius Primum]] broken. Atris wants out of it.
- Grixt is the author of Orcus's plan against the King of Night. Orcus thinks it is his own. He is recruiting Charis, Boreas and Belphegor by their grievances.
- Belphegor wants the Judgements remade by each archdevil eating its archangel.
- Archon is working his way into every ruler in Arenis. Krotaphos is furthest gone.
- Okzu runs the dragons' effort to resurrect Zaros and Vari. Warlocks of the dead pair draw on what remains of the domain plus their own belief.
- Genasi means to kill Iras and is courting Moto for it. Lux will try to stop him.
- Eurus has not told Esta about the winds' plan. Zephyros has never actually agreed to it.
- Neema has been moving ill-leaning Omens out of reach since c. 430 AM. The archangels' purge of the Omens is to be worked out in play and stays out of the Player's Guide.
- Verita loves Hieronymous.
- Nausicaä played Themis and Archon against each other for her source. Chronos tricked Themis into making XLZL.
- Cleome left Ignatius's notes where Nausicaä would find them.

## Tooling

Work happens on Noah's Mac through the device shell, where the vault is at `$HOME/mnt/Project Planar`. Deleting is off by default; `mv` works. Previous sessions kept scripts in `~/fbwork/` on the device: `check.py` for broken links, stray notes, doubled parentheses, and shared respellings, and `tell.py` to flag em dashes, "not X but Y", and triads. They may need recreating. Make edits by exact-string replacement that asserts a single match, keep backups before bulk changes, and run the link check after every group.

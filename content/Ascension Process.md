Mortals have ascended to godhood, but how it is done is not widely known. Most of those who managed it got there by different means, and few of them left anything behind. A handful kept notes, and someone who can find enough of those notes and piece them together can work out a method that has been made to work. It is attempted far more often than it succeeds.

## The Three Steps

This is the method as it can be reconstructed from the notes that survive. It is one route among several, and most of the recorded ascensions below went some other way.

**1. Find a source for your power.** Another being, a realm, a powerful ritual or spell, or an item.

**2. Obtain a relic.** A magical item, created, found or otherwise acquired, which becomes the channel.

**3. Channel the magic from the source, through the relic, into yourself.** All of the following apply:

- Recite the ascension ritual.
- Be touching the relic.
- Consume some part of the source. From a being, a part of their body. From a realm, a piece of that realm, and the larger the piece the greater the power. From an item, eat it or allow it to consume part of you. From a ritual or spell, several methods work.
- Arrange protection of some kind. Runes, further magic items, a spell, or anything else that will hold.

## Failures

Attempts on [[Arcanis]] are the most common and the most fatal. Many have tried to repeat what Hekate did and have died horrifically doing it. She has never explained her method to anyone and has never written it down.

> *Nobody had written this down in one place before. I put it together from the notes of people who did it, which disagree about almost everything except the eating. I have left the protection vague on purpose, because that is where they differ most and where most of the people who tried it died.*

## Design Notes

The method is hard to find rather than secret. It exists in pieces, in the notes of the few who left any, so a party that wants it has a research problem before it has an execution problem.

It is also only one route. Most of the recorded ascensions went some other way, and a DM is free to invent more.

The fourth requirement, protection, is where attempts fail, and it is deliberately vague about what counts.

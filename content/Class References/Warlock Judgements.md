Your patron is one of the twelve: an archdevil or an archangel. Each of them was half of a [[The Judgements|Judgement]] until [[The King of Night]] cut the six of them apart in [[The Division]], and each kept half of what that Judgement was.

A Judgement was an act together with the measure of that act, held in one being. Your patron kept one of those and its counterpart kept the other. None of the twelve can finish its own act now.

You are the part your patron is missing. The work is to finish the act on its behalf.

Shared guidance for all six pacts sits here. Each Judgement's own page carries only what is particular to it.

## Choosing Your Half

At 1st level you choose which of the two took you. That choice sets which row of your Judgement's table applies to you. You can change it only if your patron changes.

Every feature on a Judgement's page that names a half uses the row you chose.

## Expanded Spell List

Each Judgement grants an expanded spell list on its own page. The spells on it come in pairs, one for each half of the act. You may choose freely from the whole list whichever half took you.

## Level 14 The Offer

[[Belphegor]] intends for each archdevil to eat its archangel. A half that consumes its counterpart properly takes back the whole of what the Judgement had, the strength along with the domain, and keeps its own mind inside it. He has put this to the other five archdevils. None of them has managed it.

Your patron can reach for it through you.

As an action, you take on the half your patron lacks alongside the half it holds. For 1 minute you gain the benefits of both rows of your Judgement's table. Whenever a feature of this subclass offers a choice between the two halves, you gain both effects instead.

When the minute ends, you cannot use any feature of this subclass until you finish a long rest.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

> *I asked a Krotaphian canon lawyer what the twelve would be if one of them ever finished. She said the word for it had been worn down into a prefix of denial about nine thousand years ago, and that this was the only joke her discipline had.*

## Design Notes

Six pages rather than one subclass for the archdevils and one for the archangels. The twelve are six acts cut in half, so the act is the design unit and the half is a choice inside it. Both 420 and 430 route into the same six pages.

A devil half and an angel half of one Judgement are the same subclass played from opposite ends. That is what their patrons are.

The Offer sits here because Belphegor's plan is the same in all six cases. Putting it on each page would repeat it six times.

The Offer costs more than it returns. A minute of the whole act spends the subclass for the rest of the day.

## Open

- Whether an archangel patron will permit The Offer at all. Only [[Neema]] is on record against killing the archdevils.
- Whether a warlock could be made the instrument of an actual consumption rather than a borrowed minute of one.

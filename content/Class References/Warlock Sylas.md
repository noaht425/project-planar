Your patron is [[Tiyanak]], the Archdevil of Deceit, or [[Verita]], the Archangel of Truth. They were [[Sylas]], The Poet, whose act was to compose verse.

A verse of Sylas's was an account of something and a piece of work at the same time. No version of it existed that was only the account. Every judgement the six passed is a verse in [[Old Iudimian]], in one hand, and the verse is the ruling. No plain copy was filed anywhere.

The form had a say in the finding. A ruling the six had already agreed on still had to be one Sylas could compose, and anything that would not go into verse came back to the panel to be settled again.

The Division split the making from the subject. Tiyanak kept the composing and took the name as well, so [[Verita]] carries a name that was never Sylas's. Verita kept the subject. That is why Verita cannot lie and can still be deceived. Verita holds the truth of a thing and has no feel at all for the shape it has been put into.

For what applies to all six pacts, including your half and The Offer at 14th level, see [[Warlock Judgements]].

## Level 1 Which Half

| Half | Damage Type | What your patron kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Tiyanak]], Deceit | Psychic | The making. An account of yours holds whether or not it is true |
| [[Verita]], Truth | Thunder | The subject. An account of yours is true, and you cannot knowingly speak a falsehood |

## Level 1 Compose

You give an account of a creature, and the account is the ruling.

As an action, choose a creature you can see within 60 feet and one statement from the following list. The creature makes a Charisma saving throw against your warlock spell save DC. On a failed save the statement holds for 1 minute, or until you use this feature again.

| Statement | What holds |
| --- | --- |
| It is slow | The creature's speed is halved |
| It is blind | The creature has the blinded condition |
| It is afraid | The creature is frightened of you |
| It is wounded | The creature has vulnerability to your half's damage type |

You gain the following benefit according to your half.

**Tiyanak.** You can compose about a creature you cannot see, so long as you know its name.

**Verita.** A creature cannot repeat the saving throw against your composition, and the composition cannot be ended by any effect short of its duration running out.

## Level 6 Back to the Panel

Starting at 6th level, an account that will not go into verse is settled again.

When a creature succeeds on a saving throw against your composition, you can use your reaction to send the finding back. The creature repeats the saving throw.

**Tiyanak.** The creature subtracts your Charisma modifier from the repeated saving throw.

**Verita.** If the creature fails the repeated saving throw, the statement holds for 10 minutes rather than 1.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Level 10 In One Hand

At 10th level, you can have two compositions running at once, each on a different creature, and you gain the following benefit according to your half.

**Tiyanak.** When you compose, you can name two statements instead of one. The creature makes a single saving throw against both.

**Verita.** When you compose, you can name a second creature within 30 feet of the first. It makes its own saving throw against the same statement.

## Design Notes

A named statement that becomes true is the only way to put a verdict in verse into a warlock's hands. The four statements are fixed so a DM is not adjudicating free text every round, and each of them maps to a condition the game already has.

Tiyanak gets reach and volume because the making was his. Verita gets permanence because the subject was Verita's, and a true account cannot be argued down. That is also why Verita's compositions ignore dispelling while Tiyanak's do not.

Verita's half carries a roleplay cost in the table rather than a mechanical one. A warlock who cannot knowingly lie is a real constraint at most tables. No other cost in the six is paid that way.

Sylas's name is not referenced in any feature. It is a thing a party can find and say, and putting it in a rule would turn a hook into a resource.

## Open

- Whether [[Tiyanak]] takes an interest in a warlock of Verita's who composes something false by accident is unwritten.
- Whether a composition counts as a spell for dispelling, counterspelling, or antimagic is unsettled, and Verita's half assumes it does not.
- The odd account in [[The University of Divine Judgement]] has no author. A warlock of this Judgement is the most likely person to work out who wrote it.

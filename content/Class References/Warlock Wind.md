Your patron is one of the four [[Wind Gods]] of [[Ventis]]: [[Boreas]] in the north, [[Eurus]] in the east, [[Notus]] in the south, or [[Zephyros]] in the west. Their quarters bleed into one another so heavily that they are barely separate realms, and the four of them are permanently in each other's business.

Which of them took you matters less than it would anywhere else. A pact with one wind is a pact with the weather of all four.

It also makes you part of something nobody will have mentioned. The four share a single plan to kill [[Afthor]] and take back the elemental realm of air, and they are not in the habit of explaining themselves to their warlocks.

## Expanded Spell List

The Wind lets you choose from an expanded list of spells when you learn a warlock spell.

| Spell Level | Spells |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | feather fall, thunderwave |
| 2nd | gust of wind, misty step |
| 3rd | fly, wind wall |
| 4th | freedom of movement, ice storm |
| 5th | cone of cold, control winds |

## Level 1 The Four Quarters

You are always in one of the four quarters, and you begin each day in whichever one you choose.

As a bonus action, you can change which quarter you are in. You can do so a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

| Quarter | Damage Type | While you are in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North, Boreas | Cold | You have resistance to cold damage, and difficult terrain does not slow your movement |
| East, Eurus | Radiant | You shed bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for 20 feet beyond, and no creature can be hidden from you within the bright light |
| South, Notus | Thunder | When you cast a spell that deals damage, you can move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks |
| West, Zephyros | Psychic | You can take the Hide action as a bonus action, and you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks |

## Level 1 Wind's Edge

When you cast a warlock spell that deals damage, you can change that damage to the type listed for the quarter you are currently in.

## Level 6 Crosswind

Starting at 6th level, when a creature you can see within 30 feet of you hits you or a creature friendly to you with an attack, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Strength saving throw against your warlock spell save DC. On a failed save, it is pushed up to 10 feet away and takes damage equal to your Charisma modifier of the type listed for your current quarter.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Level 10 Two at Once

At 10th level, you can occupy two quarters at the same time. When you change quarters, you can choose to hold two of them, gaining the benefits of both. Wind's Edge can use the damage type of either.

## Level 14 The Storm at the Centre

At 14th level, you can become what stands where all four winds meet.

As an action, you take on all four quarters at once for 1 minute. For the duration you gain the benefits of all four, you have a flying speed equal to your walking speed, and at the start of each of your turns each creature of your choice within 15 feet of you takes damage equal to your Charisma modifier of any of the four types, chosen for each creature.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

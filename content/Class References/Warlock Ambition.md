Your patron is [[Ignatius]], who was a mortal and is now the god of Ambition. He is the only one, so there is no other source for this pact.

He wants you to succeed. He wants it more than you do, and he does not much care what it costs you. He does not accept failure and he will not tolerate hesitation. What he gives you is the ability to keep going past the point where you should have stopped, and a bill that arrives afterward.

## Expanded Spell List

Ignatius lets you choose from an expanded list of spells when you learn a warlock spell. The following spells are added to the warlock spell list for you.

| Spell Level | Spells |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | hunter's mark, shield of faith |
| 2nd | enhance ability, warding bond |
| 3rd | counterspell, haste |
| 4th | death ward, fire shield |
| 5th | hold monster, telekinesis |

## Level 1 Resistance

You learn the *resistance* cantrip. It does not count against your number of cantrips known.

## Level 1 Unrelenting

When you would take damage, you can use your reaction to take no damage instead. At the start of your next turn you take half of that damage, rounded up. At the start of the turn after that, you take the other half.

If the creature that dealt the damage dies before either payment comes due, that payment never happens.

You can use this feature a number of times per day equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of once).

## Level 6 Press On

Ignatius does not let you stop at a failure.

When you miss with an attack roll or fail a saving throw or ability check, you can reroll it. You must use the new roll.

The first time you do this after a long rest costs you nothing. Every time after that, you take 1d8 psychic damage for each time you have already used this feature since your last long rest. This damage cannot be reduced or prevented in any way, including by Unrelenting.

## Level 10 Nothing Wasted

Your patron counts a kill as a debt settled, whoever it belonged to.

When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points, all damage you are currently carrying from Unrelenting is cancelled.

You also become immune to the frightened condition, and you cannot be forced to move against your will by any effect that would carry you away from a creature you have attacked since the end of your last turn.

## Level 14 At Any Cost

When you drop to 0 hit points, or when you fail a saving throw against an effect that would end your turn or take you out of the fight, you can refuse it. You stay standing at 1 hit point, or the effect does not apply to you, and you act normally.

Ignatius settles it afterward. When the encounter ends you gain two levels of exhaustion. Those levels cannot be removed by any means, including a long rest or *greater restoration*, until you have accomplished the thing you were trying to do when you used this feature. Your DM decides when that has happened.

You can use this feature once per long rest.

Your patron is [[Lux]], the Archdevil of Pride, or [[Pavlas]], the Archangel of Humility. They were [[Charios]], The Entertainer, whose act was to perform.

Performing takes a performer and somebody to perform to, and Charios was both, so a show in Charios's house needed nobody brought in from outside. Verdicts were announced from a stage to whichever of the dead chose to come, and attendance was heavy. A soul's worst act was recited aloud, in [[Sylas]]'s verse, to a full room.

A voice from those boards carried to the last row without being raised.

The Division split the performance from the audience. Lux kept the performing and plays to a bigger room than the one he is standing in. Pavlas kept the watching, holds a bow past the point where anyone is looking, and has nothing of its own to put on.

For what applies to all six pacts, including your half and The Offer at 14th level, see [[Warlock Judgements]].

## Level 1 The Delivery

You announce a creature's worst act to everyone in the room.

As a bonus action, choose a creature you can see within 60 feet. It makes a Charisma saving throw against your warlock spell save DC. On a failed save it is delivered for 1 minute, or until you use this feature again.

You can also be heard clearly by any creature you choose within 300 feet, whatever the noise between you, and you have advantage on Charisma checks made in front of three or more creatures.

While a creature is delivered, you gain the following benefit according to your half.

**Lux.** The first time on each of your turns that you deal damage to the delivered creature, that damage increases by your Charisma modifier.

**Pavlas.** The first time on each turn that a creature other than you deals damage to the delivered creature, that damage increases by your Charisma modifier.

## Level 6 The Front Row

Starting at 6th level, a delivery finishes in front of the house.

**Lux.** When the delivered creature drops to 0 hit points, each creature hostile to you that could see it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your warlock spell save DC or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

**Pavlas.** When a creature other than you reduces the delivered creature to 0 hit points, that creature gains temporary hit points equal to your warlock level and has advantage on its next attack roll.

## Level 10 A Bigger Room

At 10th level, you can have two creatures delivered at a time, and you gain the following benefit according to your half.

**Lux.** A delivered creature cannot gain advantage on attack rolls.

**Pavlas.** While you can see a delivered creature, creatures other than you have advantage on attack rolls against it.

## Design Notes

Lux takes the credit and Pavlas hands it out. That is the whole subclass. It is why the two halves want opposite parties. Lux wants to be the one who lands the hit. Pavlas wants to be standing next to the one who does.

Pavlas's half is the only support subclass in the six. It fits a player who wants a warlock that makes the rest of the table better, and that position was open.

Seating by seniority did not become a feature. It is a fact about the afterlives rather than something a warlock carries, so it stays on [[Charios]].

The 300-foot voice is on the 1st-level feature because the carrying voice belonged to the house rather than to either half, so both of them have it.

## Open

- Whether Lux will accept a warlock who lets somebody else land the blow is unwritten. The mechanics permit a Lux warlock to play the Pavlas way and Lux would notice.
- Whether a delivery can be performed on an unconscious or absent creature is unsettled. The original deliveries were given with the soul present and silent.

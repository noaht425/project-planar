Your patron is [[Lux]], the Archdevil of Pride, or [[Pavlas]], the Archangel of Humility. They were [[Charios]], The Entertainer, whose act was to perform.

Performing takes a performer and somebody to perform to, and Charios was both, so a show in Charios's house needed nobody brought in from outside. Verdicts were announced from a stage to whichever of the dead chose to come, and attendance was heavy. A soul's worst act was recited aloud, in [[Sylas]]'s verse, to a full room. A voice from those boards carried to the last row without being raised.

The Division split the performance from the audience. Lux kept the performing and plays to a bigger room than the one he is standing in. Pavlas kept the watching, holds a bow past the point where anyone is looking, and has nothing of its own to put on.

For what applies to all six pacts, including your half and The Offer at 14th level, see [[Warlock Judgements]].

## Level 1 A Full House

What you can do depends on how many people are watching.

At the start of each of your turns, count the creatures other than you that can see you. That number is your house for the turn, to a maximum of your Charisma modifier.

You also gain the following according to your half.

**Lux.** Once on each of your turns, when you deal damage to a creature, you can add radiant damage equal to your house.

**Pavlas.** Once on each of your turns, when a creature other than you deals damage to a creature you can see, you can add thunder damage equal to your house.

Whichever half took you, you can be heard clearly by any creature you choose within 300 feet, whatever the noise between you, and you have advantage on Charisma checks made in front of three or more creatures.

## Level 6 The Delivery

Starting at 6th level, you can recite what somebody has done.

As a bonus action, choose a creature you can see within 60 feet. Every creature that can hear you learns the worst thing that creature has done. Your DM gives you that in a sentence. The named creature then makes a Charisma saving throw against your warlock spell save DC.

**Lux.** On a failed save, the creature is frightened of you until the end of your next turn, and your house counts double until the end of your next turn.

**Pavlas.** On a failed save, the first attack roll each other creature makes against it before the end of your next turn has advantage.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Level 10 A Bigger Room

At 10th level, the maximum for your house becomes your Charisma modifier plus your proficiency bonus.

Creatures that cannot see you count toward your house as well, so long as they are within 60 feet of you. You play to a bigger room than the one you are standing in.

You also gain the following according to your half.

**Lux.** You count yourself toward your house.

**Pavlas.** The thunder damage you add is dealt to the target and to one other creature of your choice within 5 feet of it.

## Design Notes

A headcount rather than a mark. The act needed a performer and a room, so this subclass scales with how many creatures are present and marks nobody. It is the only one of the six that gets stronger in a crowd and weaker in a corridor.

Lux adds the damage himself and Pavlas adds it to somebody else's hit. That is the whole quarrel about credit. It is why the two halves want opposite parties. Lux wants to be the one who lands the blow. Pavlas wants to be standing next to the one who does.

Pavlas is the only support half in the six, and that position was open.

The 300-foot voice sits on the 1st-level feature for both halves, because the carrying voice belonged to the house rather than to either of them.

## Open

- Whether Lux will accept a warlock who lets somebody else land the blow is unwritten. The rules permit a Lux warlock to play the Pavlas way and Lux would notice.
- Whether a delivery can be performed on a creature that is unconscious or not present is unsettled. The original deliveries were given with the soul in the room and silent.

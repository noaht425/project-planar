## Grammar: Word Order

VSO — Verb-Subject-Object. The verb leads the sentence, followed by the subject, then the object.

## Grammar: Adjectives

Agree in all aspects with the noun they modify, taking the ending of the noun, come after nouns

Adjectives ending in as/os have their endings stripped, whereas adjectives ending in oth;r letters have the endings attached.

## Grammar: Numbers, Tenses, etc.

Numbers:
- Singular: one thing
- Plural: a large group of things
- Communal: a small group of (usually important) things, used for the formal singular

Tenses:
- Present: currently happening (I run, I am running, I do run)
- Future: will happen (I will run)
- Past: happened (I ran, I was running)

## Grammar: Prefix and Suffix Rules
- Attach to the word aftter the word is conjugated/declined
- When -de attaches to a pronoun, it attaches to the subject form if the pronoun that is doing the possessing is the subject, otherwise it attaches to the objet form
- When a prefix or suffix modifies an embedded infinitive inside a modal construction, it attaches to the infinitive verb

## Grammar: Irregular Nouns
Nouns ending in irregular endings in the nominative are neuter and will have the neuter endings attached except in a case where a word ends in an ending (such as a word ending in -is) where it will just be the same in that form.

## Grammar: Vowel Combinations
If certain combinations of vowels would be put next to each other due to the addition of a prefix, suffix, stem, etc. the following transformations are applied

a + a = äa

a + o = ω

e + e = e

e + o = u

e + u = ü

i + i = y

o + o = oö

u + a = ü

u + u = u

When followed by a consonant, y changes to u

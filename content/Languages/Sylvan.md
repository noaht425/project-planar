Language of the [[Elves]], spoken in the [[Kingdom of Thalanea]]. Descended from the [[High Fae]] taught to the elves at their creation in 1 AM. Elves and fae spoke it together in [[Kingdom of Thalamas|Thalamas]] until the civil war of 105 AM, after which the elves purged much of its everyday vocabulary.

> *After the war the elves threw out the everyday words, the ones that made them sound like Kumas. They kept every word touching names, oaths, debts, and bargains, because their old agreements are worded in them. The result is a language in which I can negotiate a binding contract and cannot reliably ask for bread.*

## Grammar: Sounds
- Sylvan has no accents and few double letters. Ch is said as in loch, th as in thin, and j as the y in yes.
- Stress falls on the first syllable.
- The words the elves made after 105 AM sound plainer and clearer than the inherited ones, which keep traces of High Fae spelling: compare ondra (water, new) with ceanach (moss, inherited).

## Grammar: Sound Changes
Sylvan descends from the [[High Fae]] of 1 AM through these changes.
- Long vowels lost their length: á became a, é became e, and so on.
- The extra vowels written in High Fae were dropped: ai became a, ei became e, ie and io became i, oi became o, ui became u, and ao became e. Ea stayed.
- I before another vowel at the start of a word became j: Iós became Jos.
- W became v.
- Bh and mh became w between vowels and v elsewhere.
- Dh and gh fell silent and then dropped out. Fh dropped, ph became f and sh became s.
- Double consonants became single.
- A few words came into Sylvan in their softened form, because the elves heard them most often after the article or a feminine noun: Tha (bright) from thái, and Chore (dance) from an chóire.

## Grammar: The Purge
- After the civil war of 105 AM, the elves of [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] replaced much of their everyday vocabulary so that ordinary Sylvan would no longer sound like the speech of [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]].
- They left every word touching the fae untouched: names, deals, debts, oaths, magic, the archfey, and the fey realms. The terms of [[Ius Primum]] and every bargain made by their ancestors are worded in these words, and changing them was seen as too dangerous.
- The new words were made by scholars in Lenetha and spread through the schools within a generation. Some everyday words escaped the purge, including silva, darawe, terpis, and cleama.
- Lene survived by changing meaning. When doma replaced it as the word for house, lene took over the meaning city from the old word lanis, which dropped out of use.
- [[Low Fae]] kept most of the old everyday words.

## Grammar: Gender
- Sylvan has no grammatical gender. The softening that marked feminine nouns in High Fae disappeared from everyday speech by the time of the purge.
- Its only traces are Tha and Chore, which entered the language in their softened forms.

## Grammar: Names
- The elves kept the fae system of names in full. Every elf has a true name and a use-name, and a use-name is softened when spoken.
- Softening survives in Sylvan only for names:

| Sound | Softened |
| --- | --- |
| p | f |
| t | th |
| c | ch |
| b | v |
| m | v |
| f | dropped; the name takes h- |
| a vowel | h- |
| d, g, s, l, n, r | no change |

- Names that begin with d, g, s, l, n or r cannot be softened, so they are never given as use-names.
- A is used to address someone and softens the name after it. Someone whose use-name is Tarin is called A Tharin.
- Fola is the pronoun for someone the speaker will not name.
- Conv tanav. (I hold your name.) Docluth Fola manav. (The one I will not name stole my name.)

## Grammar: Nouns
- The article is an in the singular and na in the plural. There is no word for "a".
- A noun that ends in a consonant adds -a in the plural. A noun that ends in a vowel adds -na: silva, silvana.
- Possession is shown by order alone. The possessor follows: Lene an tarvis (the ruler's city).
- After a number, a noun stays singular.

## Grammar: Adjectives
- Adjectives follow the noun and do not change: Silva calis (a beautiful forest), Silvana calis (beautiful forests).

## Grammar: Word Order
- Verb, then subject, then object: Dofid an felin an silvin (The fae saw the elf).
- No, Ce and Ad come before the verb.
- Sylvan has no verb for "have". A thing someone has is "at" them: Bi anav acum (I have a name).

## Grammar: Verbs
- Consonant verbs end in a consonant. Vowel verbs end in a vowel, which drops before an ending.
- The past adds Do to the front of every verb, or D' before a vowel. In High Fae only verbs beginning with a vowel or f took D', but once softening was lost the elves used it everywhere.
- The bound mood is Ad followed by the future. The elves still use it for oaths and deals, and are held to it as the fae are.
- No makes a verb negative: No fidim (I do not see). Ce asks a question: Ce fidir? (Do you see?)
- The command form is the bare stem for one person, and adds -iv for more than one: Gav! Gaviv!

- Os states what something is, for good: Os felin Es (They are a fae). It does not change for person, and its past is Ves.
- Bi states how something is now: Bi Es fuare (They are cold).

## Grammar: Numbers
- Sylvan counts in tens. The elves dropped counting in twenties along with the rest of the everyday words they purged. Fithe, the old word for twenty, survives as "a score" in songs.
- Tens are counted by multiplying dechna (10): Dath dechna (20), Trean dechna (30). The rest is added after: Dath dechna far (21), Neth dechna neth (99).
- Hecta is 100 and Myra 1000.
- Ordinals add -is: Dathis (second), Treanis (third). Far has the old ordinal Faris (first).

## Grammar: Place Names
- Thalamas: from tha (bright) and lamas (alliance), "the bright alliance". The kingdom the elves and fae shared from 3 AM to 105 AM.
- Thalanea: from tha (bright) and lanea (castle), "the bright castle". The elves kept the first part of the old kingdom's name.
- Lenetha: from lene (city) and tha (bright), "the bright city". The capital of Thalanea.
- Velanea: from velas (glory) and lanea (castle), "the castle of glory". Named before the split; now the capital of Kumas.
- Kumas: the [[Low Fae]] form of cumas (hollow, a sheltered place under a hill). The fae chose it for their kingdom after the split.
- Drow: from darawe (mountain).

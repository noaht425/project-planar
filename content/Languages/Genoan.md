Language of the [[Kingdom of Geno]]. It has no parent. [[Genasi]] made it.

Genasi was given Ancient Draconic at his creation and did not keep it. Within a few years of taking the throne in 2 AM he had begun building a language of his own, and he built it the way a maker builds: one ending for one job, no exceptions, every word sorted into its proper place. He took most of the element words, the numbers, the pronouns, and the communal number from Draconic, and threw out the rest. He then enforced the result for four hundred and three years. Nothing in Genoan is irregular that was in the language before 405 AM.

What Genasi sorted his language by was element. Draconic has three genders. Genoan has five classes, one for each of the realms inside [[Tarkis]], and a word does not take an ending to show its class. It shows it in its vowels. Every vowel in a word of the fire class is a or e, and every vowel in a word of the water class is u or o, and so on through the five. A speaker hears the class of a word before hearing the word.

> *You cannot say a word in Genoan without declaring what it is made of. The class is not an ending you can leave off, it is the vowels, and there is no spelling of the word that avoids them. Genasi sorted the whole vocabulary himself and his sorting is still the standard. So every time I say grief I am agreeing that grief is water. Every time I mention a lie I am calling it noxious. There is no neutral way to bring anything up.*

## Grammar: Sounds
- Ten vowels, in five pairs: a e, u o, i y, ä ö, ü ë. Each pair belongs to one class and the pairs never mix inside a word.
- The vowels are said as written. Ä is the a in cat, ö is the French eu, ü is the German ü, ë is a short uh, y is the German ü said short. Genasi took all five of the odd ones from Ancient Draconic, which already had ä, ö, ü, y and ω.
- Consonants: p, b, t, d, k, g, f, v, s, z, š (sh), h, m, n, l, r, j, and the pairs th and ch (as in loch).
- Stress falls on the first syllable, with no exceptions. Genasi left no room for one.
- A syllable may end in two consonants but may not begin with two. Loans that begin with two are broken up or take a vowel in front.

## Grammar: The Five Classes
- Every noun, verb, adjective and pronoun belongs to one of five classes, named for the five realms inside [[Tarkis]].
- A thing takes the class of what it is made of or most belongs to. A river is water, a bird is air, a wall is earth, a lamp is fire, a lie is noxious.
- Genasi sorted the whole vocabulary himself, abstractions included, and his sorting is still the standard. Anger is fire, grief is water, thought is air, patience is earth, fear is noxious.
- The class is not an ending. It is the vowels. There is no way to say a word without declaring its class.

| Class | Realm | Vowels | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ferra | Fire | a, e | Ancient Draconic Fyrrhu, fire |
| Vuthro | Water | u, o | Ancient Draconic Vethru, ice |
| Zyris | Air | i, y | Ancient Draconic Zaros, lightning |
| Thänö | Earth | ä, ö | Ancient Draconic Theinu, dirt |
| Ükzëlü | Noxious | ü, ë | Ancient Draconic Okzalu, venom |

- The five class names are themselves the only proper names in Genoan that harmonise. Every other name stands outside the system.

## Grammar: Harmony
- Harmony is one rule. Every vowel in a word comes from the same pair, and the pair is the class.
- Because of that rule an ending cannot carry a vowel of its own. Genoan endings are written as a consonant and an empty slot, and the word being inflected fills the slot. One ending has five shapes and a speaker counts it as one ending.
- Each class holds two vowels, a first and a second, and every ending specifies which of the two it takes. This is the only thing about an ending that has to be memorised.

| Class | First vowel | Second vowel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ferra | a | e |
| Vuthro | u | o |
| Zyris | i | y |
| Thänö | ä | ö |
| Ükzëlü | ü | ë |

- Genasi did not assign the two at random. The first vowel marks a thing at rest or at hand. The second marks a thing in motion or already gone.
- At rest, taking the first vowel: genitive, vocative, locative, plural, communal, infinitive, present, future.
- In motion, taking the second: dative, ablative, allative, instrumental, past, passive.
- So the locative takes a first vowel and the ablative and allative take second ones, because standing somewhere is not the same kind of act as coming from it or going to it. The past takes a second vowel and the present and future take first ones, on the same reasoning applied to time.
- Below are six endings drawn from three different jobs, to show that harmony is not confined to one part of the grammar. The rows are endings. The columns are classes.

| Ending | Job | Slot | Ferra | Vuthro | Zyris | Thänö | Ükzëlü |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Genitive | Case | First | -an | -un | -in | -än | -ün |
| Dative | Case | Second | -er | -or | -yr | -ör | -ër |
| Locative | Case | First | -as | -us | -is | -äs | -üs |
| Plural | Number | First | -lat | -lut | -lit | -lät | -lüt |
| Present | Tense | First | -ar | -ur | -ir | -är | -ür |
| Past | Tense | Second | -ez | -oz | -yz | -öz | -ëz |

- The dative and the allative are the one weak point in the set. Both are motion endings, so both take the second vowel, and only the consonant tells them apart. They fell together in the Unrest, and this is why.

## Grammar: Combined Classes
- Where two classes meet there is a sixth sort of word, and there are ten of them, one for each pair the five classes make.
- A combined word carries both vowel sets, the first class first. This is the only place a word may hold vowels from two pairs.
- The order is fixed: fire, water, air, earth, noxious. Ferravuthro is possible and Vuthroferra is not.
- The ten are also the names of the seam realms inside [[Tarkis]], and the names of the combined genasi who stand for them.

- Smiths, who have more use for these words than anyone, stopped observing that rule at some point in the Unrest. See below.

## Grammar: Nouns
- A noun takes its endings in one order: root, then number, then case. One ending for one job, and never two endings for one job.
- There is no article.
- Modifiers follow the noun and harmonise to it. The noun carries the endings.

| Number | Ferra | Vuthro | Zyris | Thänö | Ükzëlü |

| Singular | (none) | (none) | (none) | (none) | (none) |
| Plural | -lat | -lut | -lit | -lät | -lüt |

- The communal is Draconic. It marked a small group of important things and served as the formal singular. Genasi kept it for the court and regularised everything else.

- Every case ending but the accusative begins with a vowel, and no two share a consonant. The set is unambiguous by construction.
- Fully declined, earth-class Käröth (stone): Käröth, Kärötht, Käröthän, Käröthör, Käröthäh, Käröthäs, Käröthöl, Käröthök, Käröthöv.

## Grammar: Pronouns
- The pronouns are Draconic, harmonised. The plural is Genasi's own, built with the ordinary plural ending, replacing the three irregular Draconic plurals.
- The first person declares the speaker's element. The second person declares the listener's. There is no way to speak to someone in Genoan without saying what they are made of, and no polite way to guess wrong.

- Object pronouns are the same words with the accusative -t. Draconic's separate object pronouns are gone, except in the oath Hω nelimar, which no one can parse any more and everyone can recite.
- A classless second person, Te, came in during the Unrest. It is still refused at court and in any document Nara Veril signs.

## Grammar: Verbs
- A verb takes the class of its subject, then voice, then tense. Nothing marks person. The class does that work, and the pronoun does the rest.
- Two voices. The passive puts -2th between stem and tense.
- Three tenses, the same three Draconic has.

| Form | Ferra | Vuthro | Zyris | Thänö | Ükzëlü |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitive | -an | -un | -in | -än | -ün |
| Present | -ar | -ur | -ir | -är | -ür |
| Past | -ez | -oz | -yz | -öz | -ëz |
| Future | -ask | -usk | -isk | -äsk | -üsk |

- Negation is a prefix, n and a second vowel: fire Neburnar (does not burn), water Noburnur, air Nyburnir.
- Fire-class Barnar (burns): Barnan, Barnar, Bernez, Barnask. Passive: Barnether, Barnethez, Barnethask.
- A subject that has no class, meaning any name and any loan, puts its verb in the earth class. Earth is the class of the throne and of law, and it was the class the court used when it had nothing to go on. Iras and Gaius Flaminius were both spoken of in earth-class verbs for their whole reigns.

## Grammar: Word Order
- Verb, subject, object, as in Ancient Draconic.
- Sanar Ferra kärötht. Fire sees the stone. The verb is in fire class because the subject is.
- Sunur Vuthro kärötht is the same sentence with water doing the seeing. Only the verb's vowels have moved. The object does not change, because the stone is earth whoever is looking at it.
- Adjectives and other modifiers follow their noun. Genitives follow the noun they belong to.
- Questions take Mir or Kel in front and keep the order otherwise. There is no question ending.

## Grammar: What Stands Outside
- Harmony applies to nouns, verbs, adjectives and pronouns. Nothing else in the language has a class.
- Particles are classless: Lo, Vai, Dost, Neh, Esk, Veld, Tan, Sen, Ton, Kel, Mir, Ens, Ard, Esh, Nolt, Braht, Pel.
- The numbers are Draconic and unchanged. Counting is the one thing in Genoan that belongs to no element. That is why Geno's accounts and contracts can be read by outsiders when nothing else can.

- Proper names do not harmonise. No genasi's name has a class. Genasi, Iras, Nara Veril and Geno itself are all classless words.
- The four winds kept their Draconic names and their Draconic vowels: Boras, Euras, Notos, Zephyras. They are the only common nouns in the language with no class.

## Grammar: Classless Words and the Particle So
- A classless word cannot take a class ending, and most Genoan endings are class endings. The language needed a way to carry one anyway.
- The court built one in 405 AM, when Iras took the throne. His name would not harmonise and could not take the royal endings. The solution was a particle placed in front: So Iras, the classless one, Iras. The endings go on So.
- So has no class and so cannot borrow vowels. It declines with an invariant o: Son, Sor, Sot, Soh, Sos, Sol, Sok, Sov. It is the only word in Genoan with a fixed vowel.
- Känögär Son Iras: the throne of Iras. So carries the genitive and the name is left alone.
- The particle was made for one foreign king and sat almost unused for four hundred years. When the portal opened in Geno and modern Draconic goods and words began arriving, it was there waiting, and every loan now takes it.
- So mast (a mast), Son Iras (of Iras), Sos vekra (at a vekra). A Genoan speaker can tell a loan from a native word without knowing the word, because the loan is wearing So.

## Grammar: The Three Strata
- Draconic. The five class names, Varel, Minsys, Viry, Hadal, Amel, the four winds, the numbers, the pronouns and the communal number. Taken whole at Genasi's creation in 2 AM, which is why these are the words that break the patterns.
- Genasi's own. Everything else in the core vocabulary, coined between about 5 AM and 405 AM, all of it built on consonant roots filled out with class vowels. Regular by construction.
- Portal Draconic. Modern Draconic words arriving through the Geno portal, all of them classless and all of them wearing So. These are recent and a speaker can usually say which generation a loan arrived in.

## Grammar: The Maker's Standard, 2 to 405 AM
- Genasi ruled for four hundred and three years and taught the language himself for most of them. There is no attested irregularity from this period that he did not put there on purpose.
- The root-and-pattern system is from this period, and so is the whole case set, the five classes, the ten combined classes, and the rule that the verb agrees in class and not in person.
- He died in 405 AM fighting [[Lux]], and the language has been coming apart since. Everything irregular in Genoan can be dated after 405.

## Grammar: The Foreign Kings, 405 to 546 AM
- Iras reigned fourteen years and abdicated. Gaius Flaminius reigned a hundred and twenty-seven. Neither name harmonised.
- The particle So is from this period, and so is the earth-class default for classless subjects.
- Court Genoan of these years is more conservative than Genasi's own, not less. A court speaking for a king whose name the language could not hold had reason to be strict about everything else.

## Grammar: The Great Unrest, 546 to 657 AM
- A hundred and eleven years with no king, and no one enforcing anything. This is where a made language breaks, and it is where Genoan did.
- The dative and the allative fell together in speech. Both are -2r now outside court use, and the allative -2k is a written form that people learn and do not say.
- The communal came loose. It had marked a small group of important things. By 657 it was being used of any group of people the speaker wanted to flatter, and it is now an ordinary polite plural. Nara Veril's chancery has tried twice to pull it back.
- The classless second person Te appeared. Genasi's language had no room for it and a century without a court had constant need of it.
- The passive -2th wore down to nothing before a tense ending in fast speech, so that Barnethez and Barnez are both heard for was burned, and Barnez is also the past active. The collision has not been resolved and context carries it.
- Three combined-class words lost the canonical vowel order, all of them trade goods: metal, acid and steam. Smiths say Thänküzel for Thänökzëlü and have said it for three hundred years. The chancery spelling is unchanged.

## Grammar: The Restored Standard, 657 AM onward
- Nara Veril has reigned forty-five years. The standard she restored is Gaius Flaminius's court Genoan, not Genasi's, and the difference is not advertised.
- Written Genoan is close to the old standard. Spoken Genoan has nine cases on paper and seven in use, a communal that no longer means what it says, a passive that collides with the past, and a classless pronoun the court will not write.
- Both are called Genoan and no one in Geno finds this strange.

## Grammar: Reading Genoan
- Find the vowels first. They give the class before anything else does.
- A word with vowels from two pairs is a combined-class word and the first pair comes first.
- A word with vowels from two pairs in the wrong order is a mistake, a loan, or a smith.
- A word wearing So is not Genoan.
- The verb is the first word in the sentence and its vowels tell you what the subject is made of.

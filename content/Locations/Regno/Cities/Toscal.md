Toscal (TOS-cal) is a town of hills and vineyards in the [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]], and its wine is served at the [[Panarex]].

## The Hills

Toscal is terraced ground, worked in small holdings, most of them held by the same families for a long time. The holdings are too small to be farmed any other way and too valuable to be consolidated. The town's law makes selling to an outsider difficult enough that few try.

The wine is good. The town's own view is that about a third of it is very good and the rest is honest. That distinction matters more to Toscal than anyone outside it understands.

## The Panarex Contract

Toscal has supplied the [[Panarex]] for longer than anybody has questioned. So its wine is poured in the one room where every ruler in the world sits.

The town does not choose what goes. A buyer comes out from [[Caragna]], tastes across the holdings, and takes what the buyer takes. The vintners are not told whose wine was chosen, or which year was poured. The secrecy is deliberate. It is said to be the condition on which the arrangement was first made, so that no holding in Toscal could advertise itself as the Panarex vintner and ruin the rest.

> *The vintners of Toscal are never told which year's wine is poured at the Panarex. One of them told me she had once tasted it there by accident and had been insulted by the choice.*

## The Rest of the Trade

Most of Toscal's wine never leaves Regno. [[Marenos]] ships some, [[Campara]] sells a great deal, and the kingdom drinks the rest.

The town makes more money from its oil than from its wine and never mentions this to visitors.

## The Presses

The oil presses run in Mortera, after the vintage, and they are the reason Toscal has money in a bad wine year. Pickers come down from [[Campara]] for both seasons and are housed in the holdings that hire them. The arrangement is several generations old. Every few years there is a dispute about what housing means.

## Design Notes

Toscal is a comfortable, pretty, slightly vain place in a setting with a great deal going wrong in it, and a party will want to stay.

The contract is the hook. Anyone who wanted something into the Panarex, in a cup, has one town to work on.

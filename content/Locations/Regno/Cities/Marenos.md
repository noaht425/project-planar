Marenos (ma-RE-nos) is the port of the [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]], on a coast that is fogbound for a good part of the year.

> *The harbour of Marenos is marked by bells instead of lights, and every pilot there can bring a ship in by ear.*

## Bells

Marenos is marked by sound rather than by light. The approaches carry bells on frames and on buoys, each with its own note and its own interval. A pilot brings a ship in by hearing which bell is where.

The reason is the fog. A light is useless in it and a bell is not, so the harbour invested in sound several centuries ago and has gone on maintaining it. The bells are checked daily, tuned on a schedule, and replaced by a foundry that does nothing else.

Several of the best pilots in Marenos are blind. The harbour treats this as unremarkable, and the pilots themselves treat the question as tiresome. The work is listening, and they are the people doing it best.

## The Harbour

Regno's seaborne trade all comes through here. Wool and oil out, timber and metal in, with a steady traffic to [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]] and [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]] that the [[Trois Re]] keeps cheap for all three.

The port is not large by the standards of [[Plowo]] or the Sylfaen coast. It is reliable, which in fog is worth more than size.

## The Pilots' Hall

Pilotage is compulsory. No captain takes a ship in or out of Marenos without one of the hall's pilots aboard. The fees are posted. So the hall is one of the few bodies in Regno that can refuse a kingdom's officer to their face and make it stick.

A pilot who loses a ship is heard by the hall rather than by a court. The hall's judgements are harsher than a court's would be and nobody has ever successfully appealed one.

## The Fog

The worst of it is Morcaela and Mortera, when a ship may wait days offshore for sound it can follow. Captains who cannot afford the wait sometimes try it anyway, and the pilots' hall keeps a list of the ones who did.

## Design Notes

Bells instead of lights gives a DM a port where sound is the whole of navigation, which is a problem a party can be given directly. Silence the bells and nothing gets in.

The pilots' hall is a small institution with real power, and it is the fastest way to stop a ship leaving Regno.

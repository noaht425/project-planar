Caragna (ca-RAN-ya) is the capital of [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]], a northern city of arcades and old stone, and the seat of [[XLZL]].

> *XLZL keeps office hours in Caragna, and the queue for them forms the night before. I joined it at dusk and was seen a little after midnight, and XLZL apologised for the wait, which had been seven minutes shorter than the posted estimate.*

Lontaci (lon-TA-chee) is the mountain town of the far north of the [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]], and it is snowed in for most of Mortera.

## The Year

Lontaci's year has two halves. For three months it is a working mountain town, cutting timber, quarrying, and taking the tolls off the northern pass. For the fourth it is shut.

Morcaela is the month that matters. The whole town spends it laying in: food, fuel, fodder, and the repairs that cannot be done under snow. Anyone in Lontaci during Morcaela is expected to help, guests included, and the expectation is not negotiable.

> *Lontaci spends Morcaela laying in everything it will need for the winter. I arrived late in Morcaela and was put to work before I had unpacked.*

## The Shut Month

Nothing comes up the pass in Mortera. The town does not consider this a hardship. It does consider it a test of the preparation, and the preparation has not failed in living memory.

What the town does in the shut month is talk. Lontaci's reputation across the kingdom is for argument, for long memories, and for producing people who leave with something to prove.

## The Brothers

[[Orcus]] and [[Loime]] were born here. Two brothers out of a town that spends a month a year getting ready for a month a year of nothing, who both decided the world owed them more than it had given them, and who both made themselves gods.

Whether Lontaci should say so is the longest running argument in the town, which is saying something. One side holds that two ascensions out of one mountain town is the most remarkable thing that has ever happened in Regno. The other points out that one of them rules the dead and the other is the god of plagues, and asks what exactly is being celebrated.

Nothing has been built. The house still stands, it is lived in by somebody else, and the people in it are tired of being asked.

## The Pass

The toll on the northern pass is the town's other income. Traffic through it is light, seasonal, and entirely dependent on Lontaci's willingness to keep the road clear. That gives a small mountain town a say in anything that has to go north.

## Design Notes

A town that is cut off for a quarter of every year explains a great deal about the brothers, and it is where to start a party that wants to understand either of them.

The argument about the monument is the live scene. Both sides are reasonable and neither will win.

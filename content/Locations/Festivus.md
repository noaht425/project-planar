A theme park inside [[Pratta]] in the domain of [[Time]], built and still operated by [[Chronos]], and staffed entirely by automata.

## What It Is

It runs perfectly. The rides go when they are posted to go, the performances begin on the minute, the food is good, and the queues move. Nothing in the park has ever broken down in front of a guest.

The staff are the [[Fae|automata]] of Time, trained for it at Cóirne. They are warm, they are attentive, and they remember a guest's name across a whole visit. A family that comes back after a decade is greeted by the same attendant, who asks after the child by name.

Visitors enjoy themselves. Almost everyone who has written about Festivus reports leaving slightly unsettled and being unable to say what did it.

## What It Is For

Three things at once, which is why nobody can get a straight answer about any one of them.

It is the domain reduced to its purest form. A park is a schedule with people inside it, and this is what [[Chronos]] does, built small enough to walk round in an afternoon.

It is the only life the automata get. They have no afterlife, because the twelve have never accepted that a made thing has anything to send on, so Chronos built his automata somewhere they are looked forward to, thanked, and remembered. See [[Chronos]].

It is also evidence. Every guest spends a day being served, entertained, and looked after by made things, and treats them as people for the length of it. Cuimhne keeps the record of every appointment ever made in this domain, and the park's attendance is part of that record. The case Chronos is assembling will be able to call on several centuries of mortals who behaved as though the staff were persons.

> *The attendant who walked me out remembered a thing I had said six hours earlier, at the far end of the park, and asked whether I had enjoyed the rest of my day in the light of it. I said that I had. Then I asked, because it was the only question I had left, whether it enjoyed the work. It told me that it did, then asked whether I would be coming back. I said I would. I have not. I think about the question it asked me considerably more than the one I asked it.*

## Design Notes

The park is pleasant, the staff are kind, and nothing in it is a trap. The discomfort comes from understanding what the visit is for afterwards, which is the shape of everything [[Chronos]] does.

A party that has been to Festivus is in the record. That becomes relevant whenever the case is finally heard.

A valley near [[Oinsyl]] holding six colossal statues of the titans, and the road between [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]] and the [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Kingdom of Sylfaen]].

Construction began on 73 Auctera, 225 AM and finished on 3 Aucaela, 336 AM.

## The Statues

Six, one for each of the titans: [[Afthor]], [[Gallum]], [[Kodren]], [[Moto]], [[Sarma]], and [[Vato]]. Each is cut into the valley wall at a scale that makes the road beneath it look like a footpath.

All six titans hold that they have always been gods. [[Kleipur]] gave them their power on 98 Aucaela, 1 AM, which none of them acknowledges. A monument showing the six of them at the size of hills, with no progenitor anywhere in it, suits all six. None has ever objected to it.

The work took a hundred and eleven years and the craft changed over that time. A traveller going from the Oinarrian end to the Sylfaen end passes through several generations of masonry, and the joins are not disguised.

## Who Built It

Oinarria and Sylfaen built it together. It is the only thing the two kingdoms have ever made jointly.

Neither kingdom has produced a tidy account of whose idea it was. What is on the record is thinner. Both wanted the pass held. Neither wanted the other holding it. A monument is a claim that needs no garrison, and six titans were probably easier to agree on than a border.

The arrangement has outlasted a great deal. The two kingdoms were on the same side of the war with [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] by 424 AM. The valley is sometimes credited with that, and probably should not be.

## The Road

It is a working pass and the traffic through it is ordinary. Carts, herds, pilgrims, and both kingdoms' customs posts, one at each end, neither of which has authority in the middle.

Maintenance is shared and is the one standing joint obligation between the two crowns. Stone falls. Somebody has to go up.

> *The masons who keep the valley are Oinarrian and Sylfaen both, in one crew, as they have been for as long as the crew has existed. The foreman told me that the crown offices write to them separately. The letters disagree about a great many things. The crew has found it simplest not to pass them on to each other.*

## Design Notes

The valley is proof that the two kingdoms can cooperate, which matters in a setting this full of cold wars. It is also why they do not need to.

[[Afthor]]'s statue has become awkward since 652 AM, when he killed [[Zaros]] and [[Vari]] and took the realm of air. Nobody has proposed doing anything about it.

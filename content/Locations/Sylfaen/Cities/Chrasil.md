Name: from [[Dzoa]] chrasa (pine) and ilu (crown), "pine crown".

Chrasil is built in the crowns of the pines, for the Beaked peoples who founded it, and the stairs between its levels were added later for everyone else.

> *The Office of the Seal keeps its offices in Chrasil at the foot of the trees, so that every Beaked citizen with business there has to come down to it.*

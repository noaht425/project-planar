Capital of the [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]], on the border with [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]].

Name: from the [[Dzoa]] root d-w-r (guard) and ra (high), "the high watch".

Dawara is cut into a cliff above the border with [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]], and it is built to watch.

## The Cliff

The city is cut back into the rock rather than built out from it. Streets run along the face in tiers, joined by stairs cut from the same stone. The whole of it looks south.

Every window faces Krotaphos. The rooms on the north side have none at all, and they are cheaper for it. A visitor is usually put in one, and usually does not work out why the rent is low until the second week.

Water is the standing problem. It comes up from the river at the foot of the cliff, carried mostly by beastfolk the law files as Long-jawed, who hold the haulage almost entirely. A city built to outlast a siege has never been able to outlast a dry season.

## The Watch

The watch was built for the war with Krotaphos, which ran from 397 to 427 AM. Sylfaen won it. The watch was never stood down.

For most of three centuries it looked like paranoia, and in Dawara nobody said so out loud. The rotas were kept. The towers were manned. Clerks went on filing reports about a kingdom that had been cut down to a circle and could not have come at anybody.

## 702 AM

Then [[Theophilius Fanatis]] attacked King Dawir at [[La Grande Mascarade]], and the king ordered every citizen of Krotaphos arrested or killed.

Dawara runs that order. The watch already had the lists, and the desks, and three hundred years of practice at writing down who crosses. What it had never had was any use for the information. It has one now.

The old towers hold prisoners. Clerks who spent their careers filing reports nobody read now file warrants that are acted on the same week. People in the city mostly say that the watch was right all along. That is also what they say instead of talking about the rest of it.

> *Every window in Dawara faces south, toward Krotaphos. The rooms on the north side of the city have none, and the people who live in them told me they had stopped minding.*

## Design Notes

Dawara is the kingdom's instrument, and a party crossing from Krotaphos meets it at a desk before it meets anything else. The city was built for a threat that stopped existing in 427, and in 702 it was handed a purpose again. People here are relieved about that, and the relief is the uncomfortable part.

The north rooms are worth remembering. Anyone who wants to be in Dawara without being visible from a window lives on the side that has none.

Capital of the [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]], on the border with [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]].

Name: from the [[Dzoa]] root d-w-r (guard) and ra (high), "the high watch".

Dawara is cut into a cliff above the border with [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]], and it is built to watch.

> *Every window in Dawara faces south, toward Krotaphos. The rooms on the north side of the city have none, and the people who live in them told me they had stopped minding.*

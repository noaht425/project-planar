Port on the eastern coast of the [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]].

Name: from [[Dzoa]] plo (salt) and wo (mouth), "salt mouth".

Most of the dockworkers in Plowo are tortles, locathah and grung, the peoples Sylfaen records as unclean, and the port could not run a day without them.

## The Harbour

Plowo is the kingdom's eastern port, and most of what Sylfaen sends or receives by sea crosses its wharves. The water work is the reason. Locathah go under without gear, tortles work wet for a whole shift, and grung handle the loads nobody else will touch. No crew of Lipped or Snouted labour has ever matched the turnaround, and the harbour office has mostly stopped pretending to try.

## The Paperwork

An unclean filing can be amended. The harbour office will enter a worker as Lipped, with reservations, for a fee, and the fee is not small. The filing has to be renewed. A worker who falls behind on it goes back to being what they were.

So the way out of the category is personal, and it is bought. That is the important part. There is no petition and no exemption for the trade as a whole, only a counter where one person at a time pays to be something else on paper. Nobody has organised the Plowo docks, and this is most of the reason. A man saving for his own filing is not saving for anybody else's.

The office has every reason to keep it that way, and does.

## What It Buys

Not a great deal. A worker who has been refiled is no longer unclean in the records and is still unclean to everyone who knew them before. Children are filed separately and have to be paid for separately.

What it does buy is standing. A filed worker can sign for a cargo, hold a tenancy, and be heard if they are robbed. Those are worth real money, which is the whole cruelty of the arrangement.

> *The harbour office in Plowo keeps its records in a room that most of the people who work the harbour are not permitted to enter.*

## Design Notes

The combination is the point. An escape that can be bought one person at a time is what prevents the docks from bargaining together, so the kingdom gets a port it depends on and a workforce that cannot press it. Nobody designed that. It came out of a fee schedule.

A party will find the harbour office entirely cooperative and entirely unmoved.

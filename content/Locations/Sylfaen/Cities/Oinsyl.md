Named for its place on the border of [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]] and [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]]. The name is not Dzoa, and the Office of the Seal has never accepted it.

Oinsyl is a mixed city of beastfolk, orcs and giantkin on the steppe border with [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]], near the [[Valley of the Colossi]].

> *Every street sign in Oinsyl gives the name in Dzoa and in Gias. The Office of the Seal sends someone each year to paint out the Gias, and by the following spring it is always back.*

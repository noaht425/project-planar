Named for its place on the border of [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]] and [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]]. The name is not Dzoa, and the Office of the Seal has never accepted it.

Oinsyl is a mixed city of beastfolk, orcs and giantkin on the steppe border with [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]], near the [[Valley of the Colossi]].

## Two Administrations

Both kingdoms keep offices in Oinsyl, and both of them work. Sylfaen has a seal office, a court, and a customs post. Oinarria has a registry, a court of its own, and the weights. Neither recognises the other's authority here. Both of them answer their doors.

Residents use whichever is more convenient. A debt is registered where it will be enforced and denied where it will not. A marriage is recorded in the office that asks fewer questions about mouth type. A herd crossing the line is weighed on whichever set of scales the owner prefers, and nobody at either post is surprised by any of it.

Both offices know exactly what is going on. Neither will be the one that stops, because stopping would mean conceding that the other one governs here.

## The Name

Oinsyl is not a Dzoa word and cannot be made into one. The Office of the Seal has never entered it, and official Sylfaen correspondence calls the place the eastern steppe station, which nobody says aloud.

Street signs give the name in Dzoa and in Gias. The Seal sends somebody each year to paint out the Gias. By the following spring it is back, and the painters are usually local.

## The Road

The road from the [[Valley of the Colossi]] runs through Oinsyl, so a good deal of what moves between the two kingdoms moves through its market. The masons who keep the valley are hired out of here, in one crew, Oinarrian and Sylfaen together.

That crew is the nearest thing the city has to a government both sides accept. Nobody has suggested formalising it.

> *Every street sign in Oinsyl gives the name in Dzoa and in Gias. The Office of the Seal sends someone each year to paint out the Gias, and by the following spring it is always back.*

## Design Notes

Oinsyl is where the mouth law runs out. A party in trouble with one kingdom can walk to the other kingdom's office without leaving the city, and that is the most useful thing about the place.

The annual painting is the whole relationship in miniature. Neither side will escalate, and neither will give up.

## Open

- Whether Oinarria claims Oinsyl formally, or merely administers it, is unwritten.

The capital of the [[Kingdom of Thalamas]], which no longer exists. The city has stood empty since the civil war of 105 AM.

## The City

Aserlanis was built by elves and fae together, and that is legible in every street of it. The stonework and the growing work are set into each other rather than placed side by side, with both peoples' hands on the same buildings. It was the capital of the bright alliance for a hundred and two years.

It is subarctic. What was left when the city emptied has been held by the cold rather than rotted by the weather, so Aserlanis has not fallen so much as stopped. Doors are still on their hinges. There is furniture.

## Claimed by Both

[[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] and [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]] both hold title to Aserlanis, and have since 105 AM. Both claims are on paper, both are old, and neither kingdom has ever been willing to test the other's.

Neither will garrison it. A garrison is a claim that the other would have to answer, and the two have kept a cold war going for six centuries without either of them wanting the war warm. So the city stands as it fell, with two kingdoms holding the deeds and nobody holding the ground.

Travellers go in. There is no authority there to stop anyone, which means Aserlanis is the one place in either kingdom where a person can be reached by neither.

## What Is In It

Records, among other things. The war began over elves going missing, and over a rumour that a bargain had been made with the [[Archfey|archfey]] when the elves were created. Nothing was ever proved. Whatever Thalamas held on that subject was in its capital when the capital emptied.

Both kingdoms know this. Neither has sent anyone official to look.

> *Nobody has swept Aserlanis and nobody has looted it either, which took me a while to understand. There is nothing in that city that either kingdom wants found by the other.*

## Design Notes

Aserlanis is a dungeon with a political lock on it. The reason it has not been cleared is that clearing it would settle a six hundred year old argument, and neither party is sure the answer favours them.

[[Ius Primum]] is real and neither half of Thalamas has ever seen it. What the capital holds about the disappearances is a DM's to decide, and a party that brings something out of there is handing one kingdom a weapon.

Kingdom of the [[Fae]] of [[Arenis]]
Capital: Velanea
Official language: [[Low Fae]]

Name: the Low Fae form of cumas (hollow, a sheltered place under a hill).

In a cold war with [[Kingdom of Thalanea]]. Wants [[Kingdom of Tama|Tama]] brought into the Panarex because of their shared extraplanar origins.

Ruler: Kelvari

## The Kingdom

Kumas was formed on 72 Morcaela, 105 AM, out of the same split that made [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]]. The fae of Arenis kept this half. Its name is the Low Fae word for a hollow, a sheltered place under a hill.

After the split the fae of Kumas turned back toward [[Iris]] and took up the court speech of the archfey. The kingdom has the manners of a fey court in miniature, and its borrowed words are mostly about fashion and pleasure.

It is in a cold war with Thalanea. The fae of Kumas mock the words the elves invented to stop sounding like them, and several of those Sylvan words have come into Low Fae as insults.

Kumas is campaigning to have [[Kingdom of Tama|Tama]] brought into the [[Panarex]], on the grounds that both kingdoms came from somewhere else and should recognise each other.

Some fae left for other kingdoms when Thalamas broke, and their descendants are scattered through the rest of the world.

## Government

Kumas is ruled by Kelvari, addressed in Low Fae as A Chelvari.

## Language

[[Low Fae]], which continues the speech of Thalamas with many words borrowed from the court of Iris. It has no word for thanks. The nearest phrase means "I owe you", and it is meant literally.

## Cities

**[[Velanea]]** is the capital. The other cities are **[[Fylwa]]** and **[[Tithra]]**.

> *I thanked a waiter in Velanea for my dinner, in the best Low Fae I had. He went pale and fetched the owner, and the two of them spent some time working out what I now owed and how I could most quickly be rid of it. We settled on my helping with the washing up.*

## History
- 72 Morcaela, 105 AM: Formed when [[Kingdom of Thalamas]] split in civil war. Some fae left for other kingdoms.

## Design Notes

Kumas is the other side of Thalanea's grievance, and it should be likeable. The fae of Kumas did not sign Ius Primum and do not know its terms either, and they have spent six centuries being blamed for it by their former neighbours.

The missing word for thanks is the most useful thing on the page at a table. Any party that is polite in Kumas is running up debts.

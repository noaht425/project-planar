Kingdom of the [[Beastfolk]]
Capital: [[Dawara]]
Official language: [[Dzoa]]

Sylfaen is imperial and works to force its many peoples into one system. Its law recognizes four beastfolk mouth types and treats the peoples who fit none of them as outside the system. The Office of the Seal polices the purity of Dzoa and opposes the Iudimian words that have entered everyday speech.

Sylfaen discourages [[Iudimian]]. Since defeating [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]], it has allowed Iudimian in public only in religious settings.

## The Kingdom

Sylfaen was founded on 87 Mortera, 2 AM, and it is the kingdom of the beastfolk. Most beastfolk in the world live here. Since it defeated [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] in 427 AM it has been the strongest kingdom in Arenis.

It is openly imperial. The beastfolk are a great many peoples, and Sylfaen is working to bring all of them into one system that was not designed with all of them in mind. The law sorts beastfolk by mouth into four types, Lipped, Snouted, Beaked and Long-jawed, and records the peoples who fit none of them as unclean. The category a person is filed under decides a great deal about their life.

Sylfaen took in some of the dragonborn who were banished from Geno after the breach in 202 AM.

## Government

Sylfaen is ruled by a king.

## Language

[[Dzoa]]. The Office of the Seal polices its purity and treats the Iudimian loanwords in street Dzoa as a problem to be solved. Every borrowed word is filed into an outsider class, so a foreign word announces itself to everyone in earshot.

## Cities

**[[Dawara]]**, the high watch, is the capital, and it sits on the border with Krotaphos.

**[[Chrasil]]** is the pine crown. **[[Plowo]]**, the salt mouth, is the port on the eastern coast.

**[[Oinsyl]]** stands on the border with [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]]. Its name is not Dzoa, and the Office of the Seal has never accepted it.

The [[Valley of the Colossi]], near Oinsyl, joins Sylfaen to Oinarria. It holds statues of the six titans, and it took from 225 to 336 AM to build.

> *At the border a clerk of the Office of the Seal looked at my mouth for some time, consulted a chart, and wrote me down as Lipped. Then she asked me to say the name of the town I was going to, listened carefully, and amended the entry to Lipped, with reservations.*

## 702 AM

After [[Theophilius Fanatis]] attacked him at [[La Grande Mascarade]] in 702 AM, the king of Sylfaen ordered every citizen of Krotaphos arrested or killed.

## History
- 87 Mortera, 2 AM: founded
- 86 Auctera, 397 AM: War begins with [[Kingdom of Krotaphos]]
- 99 Morcaela, 427 AM: Defeats Krotaphos

## Design Notes

Sylfaen is the empire, and the mouth law is the detail that makes it one. It is administrative rather than violent on its face, and it reaches into every part of a person's life, which is how a party should meet it: at a desk.

The Office of the Seal is where Sylfaen's imperial project and its language meet. Dzoa marking every foreign word as foreign is the same policy as the mouth law, applied to speech.

Kingdom of the [[Beastfolk]]
Capital: [[Dawara]]
Official language: [[Dzoa]]

Sylfaen is imperial and works to force its many peoples into one system. Its law recognizes four beastfolk mouth types and treats the peoples who fit none of them as outside the system. The Office of the Seal polices the purity of Dzoa and opposes the Iudimian words that have entered everyday speech.

Sylfaen discourages [[Iudimian]]. Since defeating [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]], it has allowed Iudimian in public only in religious settings.

## History
- 87 Mortera, 2 AM: founded
- 86 Auctera, 397 AM: War begins with [[Kingdom of Krotaphos]]
- 99 Morcaela, 427 AM: Defeats Krotaphos

Kingdom of the [[Elementals]]

Capital: [[Geno (City)|Geno]]

Official language: [[Genoan]]

Located in: [[Arenis]]

## The Kingdom

Geno was the first kingdom in Arenis. [[Genasi]], the Progenitor who made the elementals, founded it on 56 Morcaela, 2 AM and was its first king. He made his people with the help of the [[Dragons]], and worship of the dragons runs higher in Geno than anywhere else in the mortal realm.

The kingdom has one problem that comes before all the others. In 202 AM [[Alistar Stormjaw]] tried to open a portal to [[Tarkis]] in his laboratory in the capital, and Tarkis has been bleeding into the city ever since. A wall shaped like a bubble holds the breach back. Keeping it standing is constant magical work and it is the first business of the crown. See [[Tarkis]] for the breach itself.

Dragonborn have been banned from the capital since the breach.

## Government

Geno is a monarchy, and the throne has had a difficult history. Seven kings named Genasi ruled in succession, the first of them the Progenitor himself. [[Iras]] killed [[Genasi VII]] in 405 AM, took the throne on the principle that Geno should be ruled by a descendant of dragons, and abdicated in 419 AM. [[Gaius Flaminius]] ruled after him for a hundred and twenty-seven years.

When Gaius Flaminius died in 546 AM no successor was chosen, and the kingdom went without a ruler for a hundred and eleven years. That period is [[The Great Unrest]]. It ended in 657 AM when [[Nara Veril]] became queen, and she has held the throne since.

## Language

[[Genoan]] descends from Draconic and sorts every word by what it is made of. Names from outside the system cannot take its royal endings, which caused the court real difficulty when Iras took the throne.

## Cities

**[[Geno (City)|Geno]]** is the capital and shares the kingdom's name. The colossal twin statues of Genasi at its main gate were defaced by Iras, and one of them was later altered, without much enthusiasm, into a statue of Iras.

The other cities are **[[Mensosde Talthu]]**, **[[Thraside Vethryfu]]** and **[[Varastra]]**.

Every so often she stopped talking, put her hand flat on the table until whatever it was had passed, and then picked her sentence up exactly where she had left it.*

## 702 AM

Geno has declared neutrality in the present trouble between [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] and [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]], much faster than it did in the first war, where neutrality came twenty-six years into a thirty-year conflict.

## Design Notes

Geno is a kingdom built around a single problem. Everything it does politically is downstream of the wall, which gives a party an easy way to matter there: anything that threatens the wall, or helps it, has the crown's attention at once.

The century without a ruler is the richest material on the page. Every family in Geno did something during the Great Unrest, and very few of them want it discussed.

## Open

The Player's Guide calls Gaius Flaminius an elected king, and his own page says [[Lux]] named him to replace Iras. Both can be true if Lux named him and the court ratified it, but it should be settled.

What the three cities outside the capital are like is unwritten.

## History
- 56 Morcaela, 2 AM: Founded
- 56 Morcaela, 2 AM: [[Genasi]] becomes king
- 98 Aucaela, 6 AM: [[Genasi II]] born
- 56 Morcaela, 62 AM: Genasi II becomes king
- 2 Mortera, 62 AM: [[Genasi III]] born
- 98 Morcaela, 140 AM: [[Genasi IV]] born
- 32 Auctera, 160 AM: Genasi II dies and Genasi III becomes king
- 46 Aucaela, 200 AM: [[Genasi V]] born
- 27 Morcaela, 221 AM: Genasi III dies and Genasi IV becomes king
- 12 Auctera, 283 AM: Genasi IV dies and Genasi V becomes king
- 1 Morcaela, 299 AM: [[Genasi VI]] born
- 5 Auctera, 325 AM: Genasi V dies and Genasi VI becomes king
- 1 Morcaela, 363 AM: [[Genasi VII]] born
- 100 Aucaela, 397 AM: Genasi VI dies and Genasi VII becomes king
- 33 Aucaela, 405 AM: Genasi VII dies and [[Iras]] becomes king
- 65 Mortera, 419 AM: Iras abdicated the throne and [[Gaius Flaminius]] becomes king
- 97 Morcaela, 423 AM: Kingdom of Geno declares neutrality in the war between [[Kingdom of Krotaphos]] and [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]]
- 32 Morcaela, 546 AM: Gaius Flaminius dies and no new king is elected
- 36 Morcaela, 546 AM: [[The Great Unrest]] begins in Geno
- 16 Auctera, 657 AM: The Great Unrest ends and [[Nara Veril]] becomes queen

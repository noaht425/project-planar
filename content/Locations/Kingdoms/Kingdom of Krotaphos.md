Capital: [[Panthea]]
Official language: [[Iudimian]], allowed in public only in religious settings since the defeat by Sylfaen
Worships the [[Archangels]] and [[Archdevils]]
Located In: [[Arenis]]

Has two kings at all times, a good Omen and a bad Omen chosen by

- 61 Morcaela, 2 AM: founded

- 99 Morcaela, 427: Loses to Sylfaen
- 3 Mortera, 427: Reshaped to be circular (excess territory given to [[Kingdom of Geno]] and [[Kingdom of Oinarria]])

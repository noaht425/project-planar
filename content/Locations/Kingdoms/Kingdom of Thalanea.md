Kingdom of the [[Elves]]
Capital: Lenetha
Official language: [[Sylvan]]

Name: from Sylvan tha (bright) and lanea (castle), "the bright castle".

In a cold war with [[Kingdom of Kumas]].

Ruler: Caleth

## The Kingdom

Thalanea was formed on 72 Morcaela, 105 AM, when [[Kingdom of Thalamas|Thalamas]] broke apart in civil war. The elves kept this half and the fae took [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]]. The two have been in a cold war since.

The split was about the rumours. Elves had been going missing, and there were stories of a bargain made with the Archfey when the elves were created. The elves of Thalanea do not know the terms of [[Ius Primum]]. But they know that elves disappear, and they hate the fey for it without being able to say exactly why. Dislike of the fey is the standing position of the whole people. An elf who feels otherwise is making a choice their neighbours will notice.

Thalanean children are told about Yupri, a faerie who carries off badly behaved elf children and eats them. Most adults remember it as a story. It is probably the last worn-down trace of the name Ius Primum.

## Government

Thalanea is ruled by Caleth, addressed in Sylvan as A Chaleth.

The ruler is elected for life by the oath-holders, the families and institutions that hold the kingdom's oldest contracts. Sylvan kept every word that touches names, oaths, debts, and bargains, so those contracts remain readable and enforceable. Holding one is what qualifies an elector. The electors meet in [[Lenetha]].

## Language

[[Sylvan]]. After the war the elves threw out the everyday words, the ones that made their speech sound like the fae language. They kept every word that touched names, oaths, debts, and bargains, because their old agreements are written in them. The result is a language that can negotiate a binding contract and struggles to ask for bread.

## Cities

**[[Lenetha]]** is the capital. The scholars who made the new Sylvan words after the war worked there.

The other cities are **[[Darasil]]** and **[[Terpisthla]]**.

> *In Lenetha I learned enough Sylvan to draw up a binding agreement for the rent of my room, with clauses, in less time than it took me to learn how to ask for bread. I still cannot ask for bread. My landlady says that nobody can, and that most people point.*

## History
- 72 Morcaela, 105 AM: Formed when [[Kingdom of Thalamas]] split in civil war, caused by rumors about [[Ius Primum]] and elves disappearing

## Design Notes

Thalanea is a people who are right to be afraid and wrong about why. They blame the fey in general for what two archfey and one contract did. And they have built their language around not sounding like the people they blame.

Every elf a party meets has an opinion about them, and the truth is written down in a contract the elves have never seen.

## Open

Caleth's title in Sylvan is unwritten. The language has dod for oath and conv for hold, and the morphology is yours.

How many oath-holders there are, and whether a contract can be bought to join them, is unwritten.

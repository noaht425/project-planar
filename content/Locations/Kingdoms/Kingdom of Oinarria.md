Kingdom of the orcs and the [[Giantkin]]

Capital: [[Goro]]

Official language: Gias

Located in: [[Arenis]]

## The Kingdom

Oinarria was founded in 3 AM and is one of the older kingdoms. Orcs and giantkin live in it together, and the sharing is the point of the place rather than a complication in it.

The giantkin have long memories. [[Kleipur]], the Progenitor who made them, gave the six titans their power in 1 AM, and giantkin in Oinarria still speak of it as recent. The orcs arrived to a kingdom that had already decided it was not going to be a small one.

The [[Valley of the Colossi]], with its statues of the titans, joins Oinarria to [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] near the border city of [[Oinsyl]].

## The War

In 424 AM Oinarria agreed to help Sylfaen against [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]]. Krotaphos lost three years later, and part of its land outside the new circular border went to Oinarria. Anyone in Oinarria over about eighty remembers the war, and the rest grew up among people who do.

## Government

The crown of Oinarria alternates between the two peoples. An orc succeeds a giantkin, and a giantkin succeeds an orc, and neither people has held it twice running since the kingdom was founded.

## Language

Gias. Most Oinarrians also speak Common, and plenty of them would rather not.

## Cities

**[[Goro]]** is the capital. The other cities a traveller is likely to hear of are **[[Tagra]]** and **[[Bacta]]**.

> *Every public door in Goro is two doors, a giant's door with an ordinary one cut into it. On my first morning I went to use the small one, and the orc who was showing me round stopped me and opened the large one, on the grounds that a guest should not be made to feel small. It took both of us to close it again.*

## History

- 3 AM: Founded
- 2 Mortera, 424 AM: Agrees to help [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]] against [[Kingdom of Krotaphos]]
- 3 Mortera, 427 AM: Receives part of the territory taken from Krotaphos

## Design Notes

Oinarria is the kingdom of two peoples who chose each other, and it should feel like it. The doors are the image: everything is built at two scales and nobody treats the second scale as an afterthought.

Its part in the war is the useful history. Oinarria tipped a thirty-year war in its last three years and was paid in land, and Krotaphos has not forgotten which neighbour came in late to finish it.

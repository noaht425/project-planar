Located In: [[Arenis]]

Joint Fae, Elf kingdom

Name: from [[Sylvan]] tha (bright) and lamas (alliance), "the bright alliance".

Elves and fae shared one language here, which became [[Sylvan]] in Thalanea and [[Low Fae]] in Kumas.

## The Kingdom

Thalamas was the joint kingdom of the elves and the fae, founded on 1 Aucaela, 3 AM. Its name is "the bright alliance". It no longer exists.

The elves and fae of Thalamas spoke one language, descended from the [[High Fae]] taught to the elves at their creation. Its words for the sun and the moon still carried the hidden names of [[The King of Day]] and [[The King of Night]].

It broke apart in a civil war in 105 AM. Elves had been going missing, and the rumour was that a bargain had been made with the Archfey when the elves were created. Nothing was proved. The elves formed [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]], the fae formed [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]], and the shared language split into [[Sylvan]] and [[Low Fae]].

> *The last coins struck in Thalamas show an elf on one face and a fae on the other, and both kingdoms still dig them up. In Lenetha they are handed in and melted down. In Velanea they are worn on a cord, with the elf turned inward.*

## History:
- 1 Aucaela, 3 AM: founded
- 72 Morcaela, 105 AM: Splits into [[Kingdom of Kumas]] and [[Kingdom of Thalanea]]

## Design Notes

Thalamas is history, and it is here so that Thalanea and Kumas have something to have broken. Every quarrel between them is about which of them betrayed the other in 105 AM.

The rumour that split the kingdom was true. [[Ius Primum]] exists, and neither half has ever seen it.

Kingdom of exiles

Capital: [[Liber]]

Located in: [[Arenis]]

## The Kingdom

Tama is the youngest kingdom by two hundred years. [[Alistar Stormjaw]], the dragonborn scientist who tore the hole in [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]] and was banished for it, founded it on 96 Morcaela, 202 AM, with exiles from every other kingdom.

Everyone in Tama came from somewhere else or is descended from someone who did. There is no native people of Tama, and in five hundred years that has not stopped being true.

It is not in the [[Panarex]]. The rulers of the other nine kingdoms meet there and Tama has never been invited. Being invited, and being recognised as a kingdom rather than a settlement of outcasts, is what Tama wants more than anything. [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]] supports the bid. Nobody else has said much.

## Government

Stormjaw was the first king. Since then Tama has chosen its rulers. Exiles and the descendants of exiles are always popular candidates, and voters tend to favour anyone with a rough background. A candidate with nothing to live down starts at a disadvantage.

## Cities

**[[Liber]]** is the capital.

> *The candidates' notices posted around Liber during my stay gave, under each name, the kingdom the candidate had been thrown out of and what for. The one who won had the longest list.*

## History

- 96 Morcaela, 202 AM: Founded by [[Alistar Stormjaw]], who becomes its first king

## Design Notes

Tama is the kingdom where a past is an asset. Any character with something to hide elsewhere has a reason to have ended up here, and a reason to be trusted once they arrive.

The Panarex invitation is Tama's whole foreign policy. A party that can move one more kingdom to support it has done Tama a service it will not forget.

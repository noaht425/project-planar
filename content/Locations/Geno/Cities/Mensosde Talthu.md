Mensosde Talthu (men-SOS-deh TAL-thoo), the hoard of intellect, was founded by followers of [[Mens]], and it is the kingdom's city of scholars.

It is cold and far north, and its libraries are its industry. The collection is run on Mens's terms: a reader adds to it, and is given time with it in return.

> *The libraries of Mensosde Talthu lend nothing. A reader brings something to add to the collection and is given time with the shelves in proportion to what was brought. I brought a chapter of this book and was given an afternoon.*

## Open

Whether the city has any tie to the Mens branch of the [[Zygaris Society]] is unwritten.

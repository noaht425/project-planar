Thraside Vethryfu (thrah-SEE-deh veth-RY-foo), the road of water, is where the river of water out of the breach comes down out of the sky. It runs from there to the sea.

## The Fall

The channel comes down over the northern edge of the city in a column that has never moved and has never stopped. Everything below it is wet. The buildings on that side are built to be wet, out of stone that does not mind. They are the oldest part of the city, because nobody has ever had to rebuild them for any other reason.

## The Canals

Half the city is canals, and they rise and fall with more than the tide. The sea comes in twice a day and is easy to plan around. The river from the sky does what it does.

A surge is not weather and cannot be read off the sky, so the city reads the column instead. Watchers are posted where they can see it, and the bells they ring are the reason a ferryman will stop mid-crossing and tie up without explaining himself.

## The Trade

What comes down the column is elemental water out of [[Tarkis]], and it is the one good thing the breach has produced. It is drawn off above the fall, barrelled, and sold in all four of the other realms and across the mortal kingdoms.

So this is the one city in Geno that is richer for the breach than it would have been without it. People here are careful about saying so, because most of the kingdom lost something in 202 AM and this city gained an industry.

Most of the drawing crews are water genasi. The work is not especially dangerous by the standards of the kingdom, which is faint praise.

> *A ferryman in Thraside Vethryfu told me the tide comes in twice a day and the river from the sky comes in whenever it likes.*

## Design Notes

A city with two tides on two different schedules, one of which cannot be predicted, is a useful place to need to be somewhere by a particular hour.

The wealth is the political point. If the wall at the capital were ever closed, Thraside Vethryfu would be ruined by it, and that is a motive nobody in the kingdom likes to look at directly.

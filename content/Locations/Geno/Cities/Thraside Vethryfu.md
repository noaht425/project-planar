Thraside Vethryfu (thrah-SEE-deh veth-RY-foo), the road of water, is the city where the river of water out of the breach comes down out of the sky and runs to the sea.

Half of it is canals, and the canals rise and fall with more than the tide.

> *A ferryman in Thraside Vethryfu told me the tide comes in twice a day and the river from the sky comes in whenever it likes.*

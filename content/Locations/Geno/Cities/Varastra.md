Varastra (va-RAS-tra) is the city in the sky. It floats on the river of air that runs out of the breach, high over the mountains, and it is tied down to them so that it does not drift away.

## The Lines

The lines run from the underside of the city to anchors cut into the peaks below. There are a great many of them and they are inspected daily.

The anchors are the politics. They are cut into mountains, and the mountains belong to the families that held them before there was a city overhead. So Varastra pays ground rent to landowners it looks down on, and has done since it was founded.

The holders have never raised the rent sharply and have never needed to. Everyone involved understands the shape of the arrangement. A city that can be untied does not argue about terms.

## The Weather

The air over Varastra is its own. It is calm through Aucaela and Auctera. In Morcaela it turns, with crosswinds and elemental gusts coming off the channel, and the city's people know which months not to travel.

The storm that settled over the [[Elemental Plane of Air]] after [[Zaros]] died has made the rough season rougher. Nobody here is sure whether that is the same weather reaching this far or a coincidence, and the line keepers have stopped waiting to find out. They inspect more often than they used to.

## Living Up There

Nothing comes up that somebody did not carry, so everything in Varastra costs more than it does below. Water is the worst of it. There are cisterns and there is rain, and in a dry Auctera the city buys lifts from the valley at prices that are discussed for years afterward.

Nobody is born in Varastra by accident. Families who live there have usually lived there for generations, and they talk about the ground the way a coastal people talk about inland.

> *A harbourmaster in Varastra checks the lines that hold the city down every morning, and told me that in thirty years she had never once found one slack. She checks them anyway.*

## Design Notes

The anchors are the leverage. A party that wants something from Varastra does not need to go up at all, and anybody who wants to threaten the city has a target on the ground, held by families with their own interests.

A sky city that pays rent to the valley is a better situation than a sky city that is simply remote.

## Open

- Which families hold the anchor peaks, and whether any of them have ever been bought out, is unwritten.
- What was agreed at the founding about raising the rent, if anything was, is unwritten.

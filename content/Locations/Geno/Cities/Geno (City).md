Capital of the [[Kingdom of Geno]]

History:
- 56 Morcaela, 2 AM: City founded
- 57 Morcaela, 2 AM: City declared as capital of the Kingdom of Geno
- 56 Morcaela, 202 AM (bicentennial): [[Alistar Stormjaw]] (scientist) tried to open a portal to [[Tarkis]] and ever since, Tarkis has been bleeding into the city
	- Dragonborn kicked out of the city

Geography:
- Colossal twin statues of [[Genasi]] flank the main gate
	- Defaced by [[Iras]]
	- One statue repaired and other is changed (somewhat halfheartedly) into a statue of Iras by [[Gaius Flaminius]]

## The City

Geno (JEH-no) is the capital of the kingdom and shares its name. The breach that [[Alistar Stormjaw]] opened in 202 AM is in the middle of it, under the wall that holds it back, and [[Tarkis]] has been bleeding through ever since.

The wall is shaped like a bubble and it is not a building. Keeping it standing is constant magical work, and that work is the first business of the crown. Everything else the capital does is arranged around it.

## The Sky Rivers

Rivers of the elements run through the sky over Geno. Fire, water, air, earth, and the noxious each come out of the breach in a channel of its own and pass over the rooftops. What falls on a street depends on which channel is overhead that morning.

Two of the five carry a long way. Water runs north and comes down at [[Thraside Vethryfu]]. Air holds [[Varastra]] up over the mountains.

The other three do not go anywhere good. Fire, earth, and the noxious each come down inside the kingdom, and what grew up at the foot of each is a working site rather than a city. One burns, one buries, and one poisons. People go to all three for wages and most of them do not stay long.

## The Statues

Colossal twin statues of [[Genasi]] flank the main gate. [[Iras]] had them defaced when he took the throne in 405 AM.

[[Gaius Flaminius]] had one repaired. The other he had altered into a statue of Iras, and he did not have much altered. The face was recut and very little else, so the figure still stands in Genasi's posture, holding what Genasi held.

## The Ban

Dragonborn have been banned from the capital since the breach. The ban is five hundred years old and it is still argued about every year.

Both sides use the breach. One says that a dragonborn opened it and that the city is owed the ban. The other says the wall has held for five centuries and that the city has had its due. The temples are the loudest voice for keeping it, which a visitor finds strange in a city that worships the dragons more than anywhere else in the mortal realm.

At the gate it is softer than the law reads. Most guards do not care and will wave a dragonborn through without looking up. Some care a great deal. A dragonborn arriving in Geno is gambling on which one is on the gate that day.

> *In Geno the rain is not always water. A shopkeeper on the main street keeps five awnings rolled up above her door and reads the sky every morning before she chooses one.*

## Design Notes

The sky rivers make every street a little dangerous and very easy to describe, and they are the visible proof that the breach is still open.

The ban is the hook. It is unenforced enough to be worth risking and enforced enough to go wrong, which puts the decision on the player rather than on the DM.

## Open

- The three places at the feet of the fire, earth, and noxious rivers are unnamed. Genoan names for them are yours to coin, since the vowel class has to agree with the element.
- Who leads the movement to repeal the ban, and whether the crown has a position, is unwritten.

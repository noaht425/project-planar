- 56 Morcaela, 202 AM (bicentennial): [[Alistar Stormjaw]] (scientist) tried to open a portal to [[Tarkis]] and ever since, Tarkis has been bleeding into the city

	- Defaced by [[Iras]]
	- One statue repaired and other is changed (somewhat halfheartedly) into a statue of Iras by [[Gaius Flaminius]]

## The City

Geno (JEH-no) is the capital of the kingdom and shares its name. The breach that [[Alistar Stormjaw]] opened in 202 AM is in the middle of it, under the wall that holds it back, and Tarkis has been bleeding through ever since.

Rivers of the elements run through the sky over Geno. Fire, water, air, earth and the noxious each come out of the breach in a channel of their own and pass over the rooftops, and what falls from them depends on which is overhead.

> *In Geno the rain is not always water. A shopkeeper on the main street keeps five awnings rolled up above her door and reads the sky every morning before she chooses one.*

## Design Notes

The sky rivers make every street in the capital a little dangerous and very easy to describe, and they are the visible proof that the breach is still open.

The planes are arranged as rings, one inside the next, with [[Nullis]] innermost and [[Arenis]] outermost. There is no innermost ring. At the centre is a hole, and the whole structure sits around it like a doughnut.

Each realm is divided into portions, and each holds one portion more than the realm within it. Nullis holds two, [[Temporalis]] three, and so on outward.

| Ring | Realm | Portions | What they are |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | [[Nullis]] | 2 | Night, Day |
| 3 | [[Temporalis]] | 3 | It That Was, It That Is, It That Will Be |
| 4 | [[Ventis]] | 4 | North, East, South, West |
| 5 | [[Tarkis]] | 5 | Fire, Water, Earth, Noxia, and Air |
| 6 | [[Iudis]] | 6 | The paired heavens and hells |
| 7 | [[Iris]] | 7 | Seasons, Lush, Time, Hunt, Rot, Trickery, Bone |
| 8 | [[Arcanis]] | 8 | The eight schools of magic |
| 9 | [[Arenis]] | 9 | The nine mortal species |

[[Arcanis]] did not keep the rule for most of its history. Its portions were thousands of minor schools that could not be counted or named. They healed into one another during [[The Coalescence]] and came out as eight, which put the ring in line with the rest.

## What Faces What

Within a ring, portions sit across from one another, and what faces what carries meaning.

On the [[Iudis]] ring each portion holds one heaven and the hell most like it. Temperance sits with Tyranny, Kindness with Desire, Truth with Pride, Justice with Indulgence, Sacrifice with Cruelty, and Humility with Deceit. This is not how the Judgements split. [[The Judgements|Each Judgement]] divided into one heaven and one hell, and those two halves land diametrically opposite each other on the ring. [[Baator]] became Temperance and Indulgence, and they sit facing each other across the realm. The same is true of all six. In physical space the two halves of each pair touch along the seam where they were joined. The ring shows their metaphysical distance and the seam shows their physical one.

On the [[Iris]] ring, Lush faces Rot, Bone faces Hunt, and Trickery faces Time. Seasons sits at the top with nothing facing it.

## The Map and the Reality

The rings are the clearest way to draw the structure on a page, and they are a flattening of it. Within a realm the portions are not laid out side by side in a circle. They occupy a space of more dimensions than a drawing has, and each portion is able to touch every other one.

This is how the seams of [[Tarkis]] work. Its five elements each touch all four of the others, which gives ten contacts, and those ten are the in-between realms the genasi types are named for.

## Design Notes

The nesting rule holds the cosmology together and decides the order of the rings. A realm's place is fixed by how many portions it has.

Arcanis breaking the rule and then falling into line is the one place the structure corrects itself. It is also a dating tool, since anything describing Arcanis as countable is after the Coalescence.

The Iudis arrangement does double duty. It reads as similarity up close, and as the wreckage of the Division from a distance.

[[Records of the Void]] records the whole structure without ever writing a number, since [[Theis]] cannot count past one, a pair and all. A reader who counts the lists finds the sequence anyway.

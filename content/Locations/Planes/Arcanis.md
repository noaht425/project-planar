Arcanis is the realm of magic and holds eight portions, one for each school. See [[The Cosmic Rings]].

It is an ethereal place. There is no ground to speak of and a visitor drifts through it rather than walking, taking in the magic around them as they go.

The eight regions are separated from one another and each is built around its own school. Every one of them is working on whoever is inside it. The sensation is of being studied, taken apart and operated on by the region itself, and it does not stop while a visitor remains.

> *I spent some time in Divination being read. It is a peculiar thing to be understood that thoroughly by somewhere rather than someone. It got me entirely right, which I resented, and it got me right before I had finished deciding, which I resented more.*

## The Eight Regions

**Illusion** is fractals without end, folding down into themselves at every scale, and a visitor goes down through the layers without ever reaching a bottom.

**Necromancy** is hard to be inside. A visitor's own energy is pulled out, pushed back, and rearranged, continually and without consultation.

**Abjuration** refuses. It wards, blocks and turns aside everything that moves through it, including the visitor, and it is as thorough about keeping a person from leaving as it is about keeping anything from reaching them.

**Conjuration** will not leave anything where it was. Objects and creatures arrive beside a visitor unasked, and the visitor is moved without having travelled.

**Divination** reads whoever is in it. It works out what a person is going to do, and then shows them, which is a less pleasant experience than it sounds.

**Enchantment** works on what a visitor wants. Preferences shift while they are there, and every shift arrives feeling like the visitor's own idea.

**Evocation** discharges constantly. Fire, ice and lightning come off it in raw form with nothing shaped or aimed about any of it.

**Transmutation** holds nothing in one shape, a visitor's own body included.

## What Lives There

Enormous magical creatures drift through the realm, the way whales move through water. They are closer to vast tardigrades than to anything else, and they go where they please.

## Before the Coalescence

Arcanis once held thousands of minor schools instead of eight. Each was a single piece of what is now a school.

Necromancy alone was blood, bone, death by one means, death by another, reanimation by one technique, reanimation by another, and the separate pieces of life and of the soul, each its own portion. Evocation was fire, and ice, and lightning, each apart from the others.

[[The Coalescence]] ran from 39 AF to 10 AF and brought all of them together into the eight.

No one saw it happen. What took place was the magic running through the fabric of the world rearranging itself, and that is not a thing a mortal can watch.

## Hekate's Hole

[[Hekate]] ascended by siphoning power out of Arcanis, which is harder than siphoning from a being. A being can be taken hold of. Arcanis is a smooth cliff with nothing to put a foot on, and she was strong enough to force a foothold into it.

The realm has never defended itself and has no way to. Nothing about it was built to be attacked, because nothing was ever expected to manage it.

What she left is a hole. It has been closing slowly ever since and has probably not closed yet.

## Getting There

Hekate reached Arcanis and came back. A great many have tried since and died in the attempt. A few have succeeded in arriving, and none of those have returned.

Hekate has never told anyone how she did either half of it, and she has never written it down.

## Who Rules

No one. Arcanis is the one plane with no ruler.

## Arcanis and Mortal Magic

The realm is studied from [[Arenis]], and the [[Aravis Academy]] works on it directly. Wizards there use what can be learned about the regions to get at the schools themselves, and new insight into a school is the usual reason anyone looks.

## Design Notes

Arcanis inverts how the other planes treat a visitor. Elsewhere a party arrives somewhere and acts on it. Here the place acts on them, region by region, and each region's method is the school it holds.

The thousands of minor schools are useful for anything set before 10 AF, since a spellcaster of that era would have trained in one narrow portion rather than in a school.

Hekate's route is deliberately a dead end. She is the only one who has gone and returned, she has never explained it, and the hole she left is the only evidence of the method. A party that wants in has to solve it from scratch.

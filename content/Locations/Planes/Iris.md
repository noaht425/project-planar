Iris is the fey realm and holds seven portions. See [[The Cosmic Rings]].

The whole plane is the feywilds. Areas of it have been claimed by individual [[Archfey]] and some of it has been claimed by nobody at all. Every domain is strange, and each is strange in its own particular way.

A domain is large. Any one of them holds many places inside it and runs to roughly the size of a kingdom.

There is no capital and no central court. Each archfey holds their own ground and answers to no one.

## The Domains

**Seasons**, held by [[King Thera]], is four territories with a fifth at the centre where Thera holds court. Each of the four is ruled by one of his children and is permanently and perfectly its own season. [[Prince Ivan]]'s spring is endlessly crisp and a little wet. [[Princess Esta]]'s summer is hot. [[Princess Verna]]'s winter is cold and snowing without pause. [[Princeps Autuun]]'s autumn is forever in the middle of turning.

**Time**, held by [[Chronos]], is mechanical throughout. Everything in it runs exactly as it was designed to run, exactly when it is meant to, and the precision of the place is the unsettling part. [[Pratta]] is here, and inside Pratta is [[Festivus]], a theme park staffed by automata.

**Lush**, held by [[Hakea]], grows without stopping. Everything in it is putting out new growth at every moment, fast enough to watch. A path walked in the morning is gone by the afternoon, and a traveller who sits down for long enough will be grown over where they sit. Nothing in Lush dies back, and that is its quarrel with Rot.

**Hunt**, held by [[Atris]], assigns every living thing in it a role the moment it arrives. A visitor is hunting or being hunted and will know which within the hour. The quarry is always given a real chance and the chase is always resumed, so the domain never runs out of either part.

**Trickery**, held by [[Indri]] or by whatever is wearing him, runs on rules that are genuine and can be worked out. They are kept to the letter every time. What they are for is the part that is never what it appears, and a visitor who wins on the terms offered has usually agreed to something else without noticing.

**Bone**, held by [[Alsa]], keeps everything that has ever died in it. Nothing breaks down here. The ground is bone, the buildings are bone, and what a creature was is still legible in what is left of it long after the creature is gone.

**Rot**, held by [[Grixt]], breaks everything down and puts it back to use. Nothing is wasted and nothing is kept. Much of what lives there is several things at once wearing a single shape, which is also true of Grixt.

Alsa and Grixt are kept at arm's length by the other archfey on the grounds of not being pleasant enough to look at. Their domains sit out at the edges of the feywilds. They are still their domains, and the two of them still hold them outright.

Lush faces Rot across the realm, Bone faces Hunt, and Trickery faces Time. Seasons sits at the top with nothing facing it.

## What the Collision Left

[[The Planar Collision]] put massive earthquakes through Iris. Most of the damage has long since been tidied away.

What remains is small and scattered. A hole in a tree that comes out somewhere it should not. A patch of grass that, walked back and forth over enough times, leaves a traveller somewhere other than where they started.

## The Thing in Trickery

[[Tiyanak]] killed the original [[Indri]] in 3,096 AF, took his name and most of his memories, and has held the Trickery domain ever since.

A couple of fey may suspect. Proving it is another matter. The domain of Trickery and the hell of [[Deceit]] both run on lying, so the other archfey have always known that whoever holds Trickery lies to them constantly. Knowing that gives them no way to sort one kind of lie from another.

Those who have got close to it have not done well. Ezra Linvale worked out that Indri had been replaced and was killed for it, which is the sort of thing that keeps the rest from looking too hard.

## Mortals in Iris

Mortals do end up in Iris, and most of them arrive by accident through a fairy circle or something like it.

For the unprepared it is extremely dangerous. Every fae a mortal meets wants something from them. Some will offer help, and a fae who asks for nothing in return is the one to worry about.

The rules around names are the practical danger. See [[High Fae]] for how true names and use-names work, and what a fae has done to you by the time you notice.

## The Feyborn

Every style of feyborn had a hand from each of the archfey, or at the least took something from each of them. The fairies, eladrin, changelings, firbolgs, nymphs, shifters, myconids and automatons line up with the seven domains, and none of them came from one domain alone.

> *I went to Iris with letters of introduction and nowhere to present them. There is no court to arrive at, nobody whose business it is to say yes. I spent the first fortnight working out that the only lawful way to be anywhere in that plane is to be somebody's guest, the second fortnight finding out what being a guest costs, which is not money. The letters came home with me unopened. I keep them, and take them out occasionally to remind myself what I had thought the place would be like.*

## Design Notes

Iris is the only plane carved into private holdings. There is no throne above the archfey and no body they all answer to, which means a party dealing with one of them is dealing with a sovereign.

The domains are each written as one idea taken to its limit. A season that never turns, a clockwork that never slips, a forest that never stops growing. The weirdness comes from things working perfectly rather than from things being broken.

The facing pairs are written as arguments. Lush grows and never sheds while Rot sheds and never keeps. Bone holds on to every death while Hunt keeps producing them. Time runs exactly as designed while Trickery keeps its rules exactly and means something else by them.

The Tiyanak substitution is protected by the domain it happened in. Trickery is the one seat where constant lying raises no alarm, which is why a fraud has held it since 3,096 AF.

Alsa and Grixt being pushed to the edges is a social fact rather than a political one. They lost no territory over it.

Tarkis is the realm of the dragons and holds five portions, which are the five elemental realms. See [[The Cosmic Rings]].

It is enormous, and it is physically strange. From any one of the five realms a traveller can reach all four of the others, so every one of the ten in-between places can be reached from anywhere.

Before [[The Planar Collision]] the five realms sat inside Tarkis under more control than they have now. The shattering loosened everything.

## The Five Realms

**[[Elemental Plane of Earth|Earth]]** is mountains, cliffs and cave systems. Ruled by [[Tenu]].

**[[Elemental Plane of Fire|Fire]]** is a hellscape of the usual sort. Ruled by [[Pyrrha]].

**[[Elemental Plane of Water|Water]]** is entirely underwater, with cities to match. Ruled by [[Vetur]].

**[[Elemental Plane of Air|Air]]** was once much like [[Ventis]], with more building on it and less open sky. It is covered in the storm now. See below.

**[[Elemental Plane of Noxia|Noxia]]** is industry and chemistry stacked on top of itself, smoke-choked and poisonous. Ruled by [[Okzu]].

## The Seams

Each of the five realms touches all four others, and the ten places where they meet are their own realms. All ten are habitable, and all ten are inhabited.

Each seam lies between the two realms that make it.

| Seam | Between |
| --- | --- |
| [[Steam]] | Fire and Water |
| [[Magma]] | Fire and Earth |
| [[Smoke]] | Fire and Air |
| [[Ash]] | Fire and Noxia |
| [[Ice]] | Water and Air |
| [[Marsh]] | Water and Earth |
| [[Acid]] | Water and Noxia |
| [[Dust]] | Earth and Air |
| [[Metal]] | Earth and Noxia |
| [[Plague]] | Air and Noxia |

No dragon has claimed a seam. A hoard stops at the edge of its realm, and the five lords send people into the seams to count what is there rather than to govern it. The counts do not agree with one another. Each seam settles its own disputes and has done so for a long time.

Pieces of [[Ventis]] fell into Tarkis during [[The Planar Collision]] and came to rest in the seams. Several of them are large enough to live on and are lived on.

The ten genasi types are named for the seams but did not come out of them. Every genasi was made by [[Genasi]] the progenitor, who took the seams as the model.

## The Dragons

Each elemental realm has a dragon over it, and each of those dragons made a second dragon beneath them.

| Realm | Lord | Second | Second's element |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | [[Pyrrha]] | [[Brilis]] | Radiant |
| Water | [[Vetur]] | [[Charis]] | Necrotic |
| Earth | [[Tenu]] | [[Mens]] | Psychic |
| Noxia | [[Okzu]] | [[Amol]] | Acid |
| Air | [[Zaros]] | [[Vari]] | Thunder |

Each lord rules from a seat shaped like a hoard, suited to the realm and to what that dragon thinks is worth piling up. The seat is the hoard, and the hoard is the claim.

**[[Pyrrha]]** rules Fire out of an enormous volcano, and what she keeps inside it is everything in the realm that fire cannot destroy. In a place where almost nothing lasts, the surviving remainder is worth piling up, and she has been piling it up since the realm began.

**[[Okzu]]** rules Noxia the way a crime baron does, out of a factory that has never stopped running. What he collects is what he is owed. Nearly everyone in the realm owes him something, and the debts are inherited and called in. That is how a dragon holds a realm without ever sitting on a throne in it.

**[[Tenu]]** rules Earth from inside a mountain that has been hollowed out chamber by chamber until the whole of it is one vault. The stone is the hoard. Anyone living in the earth realm is living in something Tenu considers already counted.

**[[Vetur]]** rules Water from a trench floor, over everything that has ever sunk into it. Ships, cargo and the drowned are laid out across the bed in order, and the arrangement is maintained.

**[[Zaros]]** ruled Air from a seat above its cities, and what he collected was the cities themselves. The realm was built up and busy under him for exactly that reason. [[Afthor]] holds the realm now and collects nothing, and that is most of what has gone wrong with it.

The second dragons keep to their own realms loosely and travel far more than the lords do, both between the elemental realms and out into the other planes.

> *Nobody checked my papers anywhere in Tarkis. On my way out, at the edge of the steam seam, a woman with a slate stopped me and read me a list of everything I had done in the realm that was large enough to be noticed, and then let me go. It was a short list, and she seemed disappointed in it.*

## The Storm

[[Zaros]] and [[Vari]] held the realm of air until [[Afthor]] killed them both and took it. He does not keep it.

The realm is under permanent storm, with lightning and thunder that never let up. Buildings have been destroyed and not rebuilt and the people there are poor. Life in it swings between revelry and panic and never settles into either.

## The Breach at Geno

On 56 Morcaela, 202 AM, a dragonborn scientist named [[Alistar Stormjaw]] tried to open a portal to Tarkis in his laboratory in [[Geno (City)]]. Tarkis has been bleeding into the city ever since.

Holding it back takes constant magical work, and what stands between the city and the breach is a magical wall shaped like a bubble over the split. Keeping it standing is the first business of the [[Kingdom of Geno]].

The fallout reached further than the realm did. Dragonborn were banned from the city, some were taken in by the [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]], and Stormjaw was banished by [[Genasi III]]. He founded the [[Kingdom of Tama]] with exiles from other kingdoms later the same year.

## Getting There

Mortals can and do reach Tarkis. The realm is far too large for anyone to police the way in, so arrivals are generally tolerated. The working rule is that a visitor who causes no trouble large enough to be noticed will not be removed.

## DM Notes

Zaros and Vari are dead and they are not gone for good. The other dragons are working to bring them back, and both can return if a campaign wants them to.

## Design Notes

Tarkis runs on familiar elemental-plane ground, with the five realms played close to what a player will expect, and the character comes from how each one is ruled. Noxia is the furthest from type, built as an industrial undercity.

The structure of the ring does real work here. Because every realm touches every other, the ten seams exist as places rather than as a naming scheme, and a party can walk to any of them from wherever they happen to be.

The storm over the realm of air is the one piece of Tarkis that is visibly broken, and it is held that way by an outsider who took the realm and has no interest in running it.

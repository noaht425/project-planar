Nullis, called the Eternal Void, is the innermost of the eight planes and holds two portions, light and darkness. The name is literal. Light and darkness make up almost the whole of it, and there is very little physical matter anywhere inside it.

At the centre of Nullis is a hole, and every other plane rings around it. See [[The Cosmic Rings]].

## Light and Darkness

Before the [[Syzygy]] the light and the darkness ran mixed together throughout the plane. Since the divorce of the Kings in 3,096 AF the two have been pulling apart into halves. The separation is not finished. The halves still bleed into one another along the boundary between them.

The wounds the Kings took in that fight can be read in the plane itself. [[The King of Night]] was blinded and [[The King of Day]] was left with a necrotic scar down the right side of his face. A visitor who looks closely, and who can see by something other than physical sight, will find both marks in the realm around them.

## The Thrones

Each King holds a throne in their own half. Neither throne is a piece of furniture. It is the standing of that King over that half of the plane, held rather than sat in.

## Inhabitants

The [[Beings of the Eternal Void]] are the two Kings and their two children, and no one else lives here. The Kings were born around 10,000 AF, married around 9,000 AF, and divorced in 3,096 AF.

[[Cado]] (Dusk) and [[Eos]] (Dawn) were born around 8,900 AF. Where they are now is not known.

The Beings speak [[Theis]]. The language has no name for either King. It calls them *dagur* and *takur*, the crowned one of the light and the crowned one of the dark.

[[The King of Night]] held the domain of death until [[Orcus]], a mortal, took it from him and built the throne of death to rule the dead from. Orcus is the only mortal who has ever stolen from either King.

## Getting There

Reaching the Eternal Void is possible in theory, and no one has ever done it. Anyone who arrives without the express permission of one of the Beings should not expect the visit to go well for them.

## Running a Campaign Here

This is the hardest plane in which to set a scene. It is close to open space, with no ground, no structures and almost nothing physical to interact with. Anything run here needs a reason for the party to survive the environment at all, which in practice means the invitation or protection of one of the four Beings.

## Design Notes

The plane is exactly what its name says. It is the one plane where the setting offers a party nothing to stand on.

The split halves are the visible record of the Syzygy. The halves still bleed into each other, since a clean division would suggest the fight is settled.

Cado and Eos are left unplaced. The author's own reading is that they are somewhere out in the other planes, which keeps them available to appear wherever a campaign needs them.

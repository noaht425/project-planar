Arenis is the mortal realm and holds nine portions, which are the nine mortal species. See [[The Cosmic Rings]].

The nine are a division of people rather than of land. The [[Progenitors]] made nine peoples at the [[Council of Creation]], and no part of the map belongs to a portion.

## The World

Arenis was made in 10 AF out of pieces of every other realm. It does not look like it. The progenitors spent time smoothing the place out before any mortal was put on it, and there is nowhere a traveller can point to and say that piece came from [[Tarkis]].

The [[Map of Arenis]] is the whole plane. Three continents, with [[The Nexus]] made by hand in the middle of them and the [[Panarex]] standing on it. Climate and local detail sit in the individual location notes.

> *The mortal realm is the least alarming of the nine and I say that with affection. Nothing here is trying to read your mind, rearrange your energy or grow over you while you sit still. People get up, go to work, complain about the weather and go to bed. After a year abroad I found this close to miraculous.*

## Magic and Making Things

Technology sits at a medieval level and draws on the whole of the world rather than one corner of it.

Magic is common everywhere. Most people do not practise it, and nothing stops them from learning. As a rough measure, a year of dedicated study takes a person from no training at all to a working first-level wizard. Someone reading in their own time, turning up spells where they can, gets there in a few years.

## Reaching the Other Planes

A great many powerful beings move between the realms freely, and stories about them are told everywhere.

Mortals reach those beings through warlock pacts, paladin oaths and cleric devotions. A mortal can speak to them, and they sometimes speak back.

What an ordinary person knows about the cosmos depends on who they are. A general description of every plane is available to anyone who goes looking. Detail drops off sharply the harder a plane is to get to.

## 702 AM

The current year. People follow current events in the ordinary way and know roughly what is happening in the world.

[[Indri]] got out of the underworld in 700 AM and has been killing people in the mortal realm since. What the public has is that there is some kind of wild man loose, leaving chaos and destruction behind him. The rest of it is not known.

## Design Notes

Arenis is written to be the most ordinary of the nine, and that is the point of it. The other eight are strange in ways a party cannot settle into. This is the realm of people with houses and jobs, and a campaign needs somewhere like that to push off from.

The smoothing done by the progenitors is what keeps the seams out of the landscape. The world was assembled from the other realms and reads as a world rather than as wreckage.

The reachability ladder decides what a character plausibly knows. A player with a fey patron can be expected to know a great deal about Iris. Nobody has much on Nullis, whoever they are.

Ventis is the realm of wind and holds four portions, one for each of the [[Wind Gods]]. See [[The Cosmic Rings]].

The four bleed into one another so heavily that they are barely separate realms at all. A traveller crossing from one quarter into the next will usually not be able to say where the boundary was.

At the very centre of Ventis, where all four winds meet and combine, stands a storm beyond imagining.

## The Land

Most of Ventis is open air. What ground there is comes in floating pieces, glass-like, drifting on the wind.

> *I asked a local how the islands stay up. They said the wind holds them. I asked what happens when the wind stops. They looked at me the way you look at a child who has asked what happens when the sun forgets to rise, and then they went back to their work, and I have been thinking about that look ever since.*

## The Courts

Each of the four Wind Gods keeps a court, built around that wind's own domain. They are populated places, with air genasi, pegasi and wind elementals among their people.

| Wind | Quarter | Temperament |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Boreas]] | North | Cold, durable, harsh |
| [[Eurus]] | East | Warm, bright |
| [[Notus]] | South | Flippant, changeable |
| [[Zephyros]] | West | Shy, mysterious |

Boreas is betrothed to [[Princess Verna]] of the [[Archfey]], who wants out of the arrangement. Eurus is seeing [[Princess Esta]].

The language of Ventis is Venten.

## The Collision

Pieces of Ventis broke away during [[The Planar Collision]] and fell into [[Tarkis]], where they went to the seams. The place they tore out of was visible for a short while. The winds here never stop moving, and they wore the wound smooth.

## Afthor

[[Afthor]] killed the dragons [[Zaros]] and [[Vari]], drank their blood and took the elemental plane of air for himself. He does not look after it as they did, and it has fallen into chaos.

The four Winds intend to kill him and claim the plane of air. This is one plan, shared between them, and not four rival ones.

## Getting There

Mortals can reach Ventis and there is a society here to reach. The practical difficulty is that almost all of it is open sky, so it suits visitors who can fly and punishes those who cannot.

## Design Notes

Ventis is the one inner plane built as an inhabited place. Where [[Nullis]] and [[Temporalis]] give a party nothing to stand on and no one to talk to, this one has courts, populations and a political quarrel already running.

The quarters bleeding together is what keeps the four Winds in each other's business, and it is the reason a single shared plan against Afthor is plausible for four beings who are nothing alike.

The target of that plan sits in another realm. Afthor holds the elemental plane of air inside Tarkis, so the Winds' ambition points outward, across a ring boundary.

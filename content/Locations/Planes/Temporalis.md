Temporalis is the realm of time and holds three portions, which are the past, the present and the future. See [[The Cosmic Rings]].

It is a strange place to look at. Much of it reads as metaphysical rather than built, and a visitor who assumes none of it is real will walk into something solid before long. There is ground here, and water, and stone.

## The Three Areas

**It That Will Be** is an endless field under a sky full of stars. There are trees at the edge of it. No one has reached them. The field goes on for as long as anyone walks.

**It That Is** is a city, busy and loud and lit, full of people going about their business.

**It That Was** is a wide lake ringed on every side by waterfalls. The waterfalls cannot be reached either, however long a visitor rows toward them.

> *Every scholar who goes to the future tries to reach the trees. I tried. I walked for what I was later told was the better part of a fortnight, and when I stopped they were precisely as far off as when I set out. I was not even tired. I was, however, extremely annoyed. Do not pack provisions for the attempt. Pack a book.*

## The Scholarchs

The three sphinxes and the [[Scholarchs]] are the same beings under two names. Each holds one of the three areas and is named for it.

Their shared purpose is to maintain balance.

## The Cracks

[[The Planar Collision]] shook Temporalis badly and left places in each of the three areas where a person can slip through into another realm. Some find them by accident. Some use them on purpose.

## Divination

Divination magic used to be extremely easy. [[It That Will Be]] grew paranoid and made it harder, and the difficulty has stayed where it was set.

## Getting There

Mortals can reach Temporalis, and a few do. Most who come are looking to commune with the Scholarchs and learn what they know. Some go further and make warlock pacts with them.

Traffic is light. This is not a place people visit casually.

## Running a Campaign Here

Limited, for the same reason as [[Nullis]]. There is little here to build a session around beyond the Scholarchs themselves, who are worth the trip, and the cracks, which are a way into somewhere else.

## Design Notes

The three areas are built on the same idea in two directions. The future and the past both hold something a visitor can see and walk toward and never arrive at. The present is the only one of the three that lets anyone get anywhere, and it is the only one that is crowded.

The divination rule is a lore reason for a table-level fact. Divination in this world works about as it does in standard 5e, and the paranoia of It That Will Be is why it is not easier than that.

## Open

Whether anything else lives in Temporalis is undecided.

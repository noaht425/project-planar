One of the ten seams of [[Tarkis]], where [[Elemental Plane of Water|Water]] and [[Elemental Plane of Noxia|Noxia]] meet. For how the realms touch and how the seams work, see [[Tarkis]].

## Strong Water and Weak

The seam is a standing body of acid, deepest and strongest where Noxia's flats run into it and weaker as it spreads toward Water. Everyone working here speaks of the strong water and the weak water, and the line between them moves with the season.

The weak water can be waded in boots that will not last the week. The strong water cannot be entered at all and is worked from above, from platforms of glass and of the few stones that stand up to it.

Nothing built here is permanent in the way a building is permanent. A platform is a maintenance schedule with a floor on it.

## Vessels

The seam's trade is in what will hold acid. Glass out of Kalixos in [[Elemental Plane of Fire|Fire]] is the standard and the expensive answer. The cheap answer is a stoneware that only two camps know how to fire.

Amolira in Noxia ships acid to all five realms. A good deal of what it ships is drawn here and carried out in those vessels. The drawing crews are paid by strength rather than by volume, so a crew that can work nearer the middle earns more and lasts less time.

Etching is the other business. Anything that needs a mark cut into it without a tool touching it comes here. So does a certain amount of work the owner would rather not have done where it could be seen.

## What Water Does About It

The weak edge drifts into Water, and Water does not tolerate it. Patrols out of Perilaxos watch the boundary and turn back anything carrying acid toward the shelf, since dilute acid in the trench would take the catalogue apart.

The patrols have no authority in the seam and everyone knows it, so the arrangement runs on the camps not pushing their luck.

## Camps

| Camp | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Glasshouse | Buys, repairs, and hires vessels. The largest camp |
| Two Kilns | Fires the stoneware. Will not explain how |
| The Reach | A drawing camp on the strong water, rebuilt twice in ten years |

> *They showed me a thing at Glasshouse that has been sitting in the strong water for longer than the camp has been there, and it is not marked. It is about the size of a door, it is not glass, and it is not any stone they know. Everyone there has a theory. The theory I was given twice is that people should stop looking at it.*

## Design Notes

The strong and weak water gives a DM a hazard with a dial on it, and a reason for work here to be paid by risk.

Etching work that cannot be done in the open is the seam's quiet trade, and a party looking to have something altered without a record has one place to go.

The thing in the strong water is left unexplained and should stay that way unless a campaign needs it.

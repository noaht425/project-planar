One of the ten seams of [[Tarkis]], where [[Elemental Plane of Fire|Fire]] and [[Elemental Plane of Water|Water]] meet. For how the realms touch and how the seams work, see [[Tarkis]].

Locally it is **Fyrvethryfu** (fir-veth-RIH-foo), the hot water.

## The Land

The seam runs in three bands, and which band a person can survive in is the first thing anyone there will ask about.

**Fyriasi** (fir-ee-AH-see), the hot arc, is the band nearest Fire. The vapour there is dry and far above boiling, and nobody crosses it without sealed gear bought in [[Elemental Plane of Fire|Fire]].

**Bathisde Eteros** (bah-THIS-deh eh-TEH-ros), the holding place, is the middle band and the only habitable one. Everything in the seam that has people in it is here. The air is wet, hot, and thick enough that a lamp is no use in it.

**Minosde Vethryfu** (mee-NOS-deh veth-RIH-foo), the skin of the water, is the band nearest Water, a mineral crust laid down where the scalding sea boils off. It will hold a cart in the morning and give way under a child by afternoon, and the crust is resurveyed constantly.

## Living There

Visibility in the holding place runs to a few feet on a good day. People get about by cord, which is strung along every route anyone uses, knotted at intervals so a hand can read the distance. Travel is by sound otherwise, and shouting on the cords is a courtesy rather than a nuisance.

Nothing dries. Nothing made of iron lasts a season, so tools, fittings, and cookware are stone or glass, most of it bought from Kalixos in Fire. Food is grown nowhere in the seam and arrives from both sides.

Burns are the ordinary injury and everyone can treat one. A visitor is told the weather by how far the cords are strung that day, since the routes are shortened when the bands shift.

## The Crossing

The seam is the only ground in Tarkis where the people of Fire and Water can meet without either of them needing gear to breathe. Fire's traders come down out of the hot arc, Water's come up through the crust, and the business of the holding place is the exchange between them. Salvage out of Water goes up the cords to be valued, and worked glass and forged goods come the other way.

A good deal of what crosses here is not recorded on either side, which is understood by everyone and written down nowhere.

## What the Two Realms Want

[[Pyrrha]] has nearly finished fireproofing her own realm, and most of what is left to do is in her four seams, so her people work the steam seam constantly. What they are after is anything the heat has not destroyed, and they will buy it off a camp at a fair price.

[[Vetur]]'s interest is the other direction. Salvage that comes up through the crust was on the trench floor once, and anything lifted out of Water without permission is contraband that cannot be sold in Water. It can be sold here. Neither dragon has claimed the seam. See [[Tarkis]].

> *Nothing I wrote in the seam survived the seam. The ink ran while I was still forming the letters, and by the second day I was dictating to a woman who keeps the record there in knots on a cord. She has my whole account of the place and I have none of it. She will read it back for a fee.*

## Design Notes

The three bands do the work the depth bands do in Water and the terraces do in Fire. A seam is a thin place, so its geography is which strip of it will kill a visitor.

The seam is written as the meeting ground rather than as a hazard to cross, which gives a party somewhere in Tarkis to do business where neither dragon is watching closely.

The cords are the detail to use at the table. A party that cuts one, loses one, or follows the wrong one is in trouble without anything having attacked it.

Neither dragon holding the seam is the template point for the other nine. The seams are the parts of Tarkis the hoards do not cover.

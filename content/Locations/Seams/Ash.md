One of the ten seams of [[Tarkis]], where [[Elemental Plane of Fire|Fire]] and [[Elemental Plane of Noxia|Noxia]] meet. For how the realms touch and how the seams work, see [[Tarkis]].

## The Deposit

The ash here is not ordinary ash. Fire burns and Noxia dumps. What the two leave has settled into the seam for as long as either realm has been running, in beds that go down further than anyone has dug.

The top is hot and the poison in it is in the dust rather than in the air, so it rises when disturbed and settles on everything. A digger works in heat gear bought in Fire and a filter bought in Noxia. So this is the one seam where a visitor needs both, and almost nobody here is native to it.

## What Is In It

The beds hold metal, glass, residues that Noxia's works will buy back, and the occasional piece of machinery that was dumped intact. Deep digging turns up older material that nobody can account for.

Everything dug is owed against. [[Okzu]] counts what he is owed as hoard, and his clerks treat a find in the ash as proceeds. A crew that lifts something valuable has it entered at Varilpithru before it reaches a buyer. Crews dig anyway, because the alternative is the works.

[[Pyrrha]]'s people come for the same thing they come for everywhere, which is whatever survived. Fireproof material out of the ash is the one trade here that Noxia's ledger has never managed to price.

## Who Is Here

The seam is where people go who owe more than Noxia will carry and cannot afford to live in Fire. A camp takes anyone. The debt follows a digger in and is collected in the seam, and the collectors come out from Pithruva on a circuit.

There is no second generation to speak of. People come to the ash owing, work it, and either clear enough to leave or do not.

## Camps

| Camp | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Faceplate | The largest. Hires and repairs gear for both realms' conditions |
| The Shallow Beds | Works the upper ash, which anyone can dig |
| Deepcut | Works the lower beds. Takes the best crews and loses the most people |

> *A digger at Deepcut showed me what she had come up with that morning, and asked me to write down that she had found nothing. I asked what it was. She told me that was the part I was not writing down either. I have kept to both halves of that, and I would like it noted that I was not paid for my discretion.*

## Design Notes

The ash is where a campaign can bury something. Anything the setting needs to have been thrown away and lost is in these beds, and a DM can put it at whatever depth suits.

Needing gear from both realms keeps the seam thin and transient, so a party cannot settle in and will always be dealing with people who are leaving.

One of the ten seams of [[Tarkis]], where [[Elemental Plane of Fire|Fire]] and [[Elemental Plane of Air|Air]] meet. For how the realms touch and how the seams work, see [[Tarkis]].

## No Ground

The smoke seam has no floor. It is a standing column country, where the smoke off Fire's burning ground rises into Air's open sky and keeps rising, and the updraughts inside the columns are strong enough to carry a cart.

What people live on are the pieces of [[Ventis]] that fell into [[Tarkis]] during [[The Planar Collision]]. Several of them are glass, some of them are miles across, and all of them float where the updraughts hold them. An island that drifts out of its column drops, and two have done so within living memory.

## Getting About

Travel is by column. A flier or a lifted cart goes up where the draught is good and across where it thins, and the routes are known by the columns rather than by any map.

Smoke carries a signal further than a voice carries anywhere else, and the seam runs on it. Each island keeps a smoke stack and a book of patterns, and a message crosses the seam faster than a body can. The pattern books are not shared between islands, which is how the islanders keep anything private.

## The Traffic Out of Air

Since [[Afthor]] took Air and the storm settled on it, this seam is the way people leave. They come up out of the ruined districts with what they can carry, expecting Fire on the other side.

Fire will not have them. Nothing outside the Vrillosiasi is survivable for an unprotected visitor, and sealed gear costs more than anyone arriving from Air has. So they stop here, on the islands, and the islands have taken in far more than they can feed.

Nobody has told them to go back and nobody can take them on. [[Pyrrha]]'s buyers come through and hire a few, which is the only way off the islands that anyone has found.

## Design Notes

Air's decline had to show up somewhere outside Air, and it shows up here. The seam is the exit, and the exit does not lead anywhere, which gives a campaign a population that cannot move in either direction.

The pattern books are the usable detail. Information crosses the seam faster than people do, and a party that wants to arrive before word of it does has a problem to solve.

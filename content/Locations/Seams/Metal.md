One of the ten seams of [[Tarkis]], where [[Elemental Plane of Earth|Earth]] and [[Elemental Plane of Noxia|Noxia]] meet. For how the realms touch and how the seams work, see [[Tarkis]].

Locally it is **Varilde Quari** (vah-RIL-deh KWAH-ree), the gold box, which is a joke about how little of it anyone keeps.

## Where Metal Comes From

Earth's ore runs into this seam and Noxia's chemistry works on it, and between them metal forms without anyone smelting anything. Veins come up through the rock already refined, in sheets and in rods and sometimes in shapes that look worked.

A vein grows for a season or two and stops. New ones open elsewhere, and the seam's whole business is finding them early.

Nothing here rusts, which outsiders find strange after [[Steam]]. The air is dry and whatever is in it keeps the metal bright.

## Claimed Twice

This is the double-claimed seam, and the two claims come from the two dragons who count the most carefully.

[[Tenu]] holds that metal coming out of rock is stone, and stone is hoard, so the registry at Panisde Eteros records seam veins as holdings and grants them out. [[Okzu]] holds that the process doing the refining is the works, so what comes out is proceeds and goes into the ledger at Varilpithru.

Neither has moved against the other over it in living memory, and neither has given an inch either. The result lands on the crews. A vein worked properly is registered in Earth and entered in Noxia, and the crew pays a grant fee and a share of proceeds on the same metal.

A good many veins are therefore not worked properly. Smuggling out of the metal seam is the oldest trade in it, and both realms employ people to watch for it, who are also smuggling.

## The Approaches

Lomaxis in Earth holds the seam's main road and takes the registered traffic. The Noxia side has no single approach and takes whatever arrives.

> *I asked at Assay how a crew decides which realm to register a vein in, and was told that the question was the wrong way round. A crew decides which realm it can afford to be caught by. Then they asked me which realm I had come in from, and when I told them, the clerk put my name in a book and said it was nothing to worry about.*

## Design Notes

Metal is the counterpart to [[Magma]]. One seam has ground nobody will claim, this one has metal both realms claim, and the two problems are the same problem from either end.

Every crew in the seam is in breach of something, which gives a party leverage over anyone here and gives anyone here a reason to need a party.

The two dragons not fighting over it is deliberate. Both of them run their realms on records, and a quarrel they cannot settle on paper is one neither wants to start.

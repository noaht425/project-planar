The greatest school of magic on any of the eight planes, in [[Hekatania]], the capital of the [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]]. Founded and still run by [[Hekate]], the ascendant god of magic. About fifty thousand students are enrolled at any one time.

## History

[[Hekate]] started it on 36 Aucaela, 154 AM as a school for local children, in the city where she had been born seventeen years earlier. She ascended on 48 Morcaela, 157 AM, and made the school a university two days later.

It has grown every decade since. Hekatania grew with it, and the city now exists to house, feed, and supply the Academy, whatever the city would say about that.

[[Ignatius]] studied here before he ascended, and the Academy lists him among its alumni without being clear about whether he finished.

[[Alistar Stormjaw]] enrolled on 1 Aucaela, 199 AM and left on 37 Mortera, 201 AM without completing his degree. The following year he opened the breach to [[Tarkis]] in [[Geno (City)|Geno]]. The Academy does not acknowledge him as an alumnus and has never been asked a question about him that it enjoyed answering.

## Admission and Cost

There is tuition. Almost nobody pays it. The Academy and the crown between them cover the great majority of students, the alumni give generously and are asked often, and in practice only the richest families are billed at all. A student who can show aptitude is taken.

The winnowing happens after admission rather than before it. The first year is where a cohort loses the people who were never going to manage, and everyone at the Academy knows which year is the hard one.

## The First Year

Every student takes the same first year, whatever they came to study. It is basic magical training, taught in large halls, and it assumes nothing.

Alongside it are the distribution requirements, which are the reason the Academy employs historians and tacticians as well as mages. A student leaves here able to do something other than magic, which is deliberate and is unpopular with about half of each intake.

At the end of the first year a student chooses a focus. Arcane, natural, and divine are the three broad paths, and most of the intake goes arcane.

## The Colleges

The wizard programme is the largest and the oldest, and it is divided into eight colleges, one for each school of magic. Each is named for whoever founded or endowed it rather than for the school it teaches, which outsiders find needlessly confusing and students never think about.

| College | School | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Osterloh | Abjuration | The wealthiest college, and the one that sets the Academy's wards |
| Brandt | Conjuration | Holds the summoning yards, which are outside the walls for a reason |
| Lindner | Divination | Smallest intake. Takes students it has already decided about |
| Falkenrath | Enchantment | The most socially powerful college and the least trusted |
| Reichert | Evocation | Largest by far, and the one the city complains about |
| Hensel | Illusion | Shares a building with Lindner and the two do not speak |
| Krummel | Necromancy | The endowing family asked for the name back and were refused |
| Albrecht | Transmutation | Runs the longest degree, and most artificers start here |

Three more colleges sit outside the eight.

**Grunwald College** teaches natural studies, which covers druids, rangers, and anyone whose magic comes out of the world rather than out of a book. [[Minervus Talonfoot]] is its best known professor.

**Hollenbeck College** teaches artifice. It is the newest college and the dangerous one, and after Stormjaw the Academy ran a long public effort to show how safe it had become. That effort produced a great many promises, a published set of procedures, and very little change to what actually happens in the workshops. The Academy wanted to be able to say it had made an effort, and it can say that.

**The Thalian Conservatory** teaches bards, under the patronage of [[Thalia]]. It is the one part of the Academy not named for a mortal endower, and the wizard colleges do not regard it as a college at all. The Conservatory's own view is that it has been here longer than most of them, since Hekate taught songs to local children before she taught anybody a cantrip.

## Specialising

A student who wants to go deep finds a professor who works on that thing and attaches themselves to them. The degree is formally a college matter and in practice a student's reputation is the name of the professor they worked under.

[[Hekate]] takes a small number of students herself and teaches an advanced class when it suits her. She does not teach the first year any more, though she did for most of the first century.

## The Faculty

**[[Minervus Talonfoot]]**, an Owlin of the [[Beastfolk]] and a moon druid, at Grunwald. The longest serving professor at the Academy and the one students are sent to when something has gone wrong with a shape.

**[[Professor Theodosius Martialis Otho]]**, a fae automaton out of [[Chronos]]'s realm, teaching history and warfare to the distribution requirement. He is a Conquest paladin of [[Abaddon]], which nobody at the Academy has a public explanation for. His office was robbed in 702 AM.

**Professor Wehrhart**, at Osterloh, who holds the Academy's wards and has not slept a full night since taking the post. Every ward on the campus is keyed to her and she will not delegate it.

**Professor Sauer**, at Reichert, who teaches the practical evocation classes and has lost the use of one hand twice.

**Professor Dachs**, at Krummel, who is the Academy's authority on the pre-Coalescence catalogue and has been refused access to his own restricted section twice in the last decade.

**Mistress Ilva Brun**, at the Thalian Conservatory, a composer who was at the Mascarade in 702 AM and will not discuss it.

## The Arcanis Work

The Academy studies [[Arcanis]] directly, which no other institution does. Wizards here work on what can be learned about the eight regions in order to get at the schools themselves, and a new insight into a school is the usual reason anyone looks.

Reaching the realm is a separate matter and the Academy forbids attempting it. [[Hekate]] got in and came out and has never told anyone how she did either half, and a great many people have died working on the problem. The rule against attempts is the Academy's only absolute rule, and it is broken often enough that Grunwald's fourth year keeps a list.

## The Catalogue

Before [[The Coalescence]] there were thousands of minor schools rather than eight. The Academy keeps a catalogue of them, reconstructed from fragments, and it is the most complete record of pre-Coalescence magic anywhere.

The necromancy section is the largest, because necromancy alone was blood, bone, death by one means, death by another, and the separate pieces of life and of the soul. Access to that section is restricted, the restriction is administered by the Krummel college, and the Krummel college does not consistently get access to it either.

## Student Life

Half the houses in Hekatania let rooms to students and every landlady has her own rule about magic indoors. The usual compromise is cantrips in the daytime and nothing after dark.

The colleges keep their own halls, their own kitchens, and their own grudges. Osterloh and Reichert are the two that matter in city politics, Hensel and Lindner share a building and do not speak, and the Conservatory holds a concert at the end of each term that the whole city attends and the wizard colleges claim not to.

> *I sat in on the first-year hall, which holds nine hundred and was full. The lecturer set a candle alight at the front and asked them to put it out, any way they could, and then stood back. About forty of them tried. Most of what happened did not touch the candle. A girl in the back row put it out by taking the air out of the room, and we all sat there in the dark getting our breath back while the lecturer wrote her name down.*

## Design Notes

The Academy is built to be run as a campaign setting rather than visited. The colleges give a party eight rival institutions inside one wall, each with its own resources and its own reason to want something fetched, and the distribution requirements mean a student has a legitimate reason to be anywhere on campus.

Three live threads sit here and they do not depend on one another. The Arcanis attempts kill students every year. The catalogue holds magic that was separated for a reason. Hollenbeck is doing dangerous work behind a safety campaign that changed nothing.

Hekate being a god who runs a school is the tension in the place. She wants magic taught well and widely, which is a generous aim, and she keeps the one secret that students keep dying over.

The faculty and the students above are a starting cast. Names are mine and are meant to be overwritten.

## DM Notes

The [[Zygaris Society]] is inside the Academy. [[Irtha Melpomene]] ran the branch working for [[Mens]], and its business is infiltrating universities and museums to take artifacts and knowledge for a dragon's hoard. [[Nerinè Melpomene]] was placed here as a student to steal, which is how the current campaign began.

Otho's office was robbed in Session 1 and the puzzle box taken from it held a ring rather than the dagger that was expected. Whether Otho chose his oath to [[Abaddon]] or was put under it is unsettled.

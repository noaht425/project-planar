Fernstadt (FERN-shtat), the far town, sits at the warm southern edge of the [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]], among vineyards a long way from the capital.

## The Distance

Fernstadt is further from [[Hekatania]] than anywhere else in Starkon, and the roads to it are the kingdom's worst. Nothing urgent has ever reached the town urgently.

> *Fernstadt is far enough from Hekatania that its people date things by when the news arrived. I was told about a coronation there as though it had happened last spring.*

The lag is not only a joke about the post. Royal officers arrive in Fernstadt with instructions that have been overtaken. Tax demands are settled by correspondence that runs for a season. So the town has learned to wait before acting on anything from the capital, on the reasonable grounds that it may already have changed.

## The Vineyards

The south of Starkon is the only part of it warm enough for wine, and Fernstadt makes nearly all of it. The wine is decent, it is drunk in Starkon, and almost none of it leaves the kingdom.

The comparison with [[Toscal]] is the town's sore subject. Toscal's wine is poured at the [[Panarex]] and Fernstadt's is not. The people of Fernstadt will tell a visitor that this is a matter of whose kingdom has the older arrangement, rather than of what is in the cup.

## The Town

Timber-framed, like most of Starkon, and considerably more comfortable than the capital. Rents are low, the food is better, and a fair number of Aravis graduates who did not want an academic life have ended up here.

It has no garrison. Starkon has never needed one on that border and has never got round to the question of what would happen if it did.

## The Graduates

A surprising number of Aravis-trained mages live here, doing ordinary work. There is a hedge-witch practice, a weather reader kept by the vineyards, and a retired abjurer who wards cellars against frost for a fee.

None of them intends to publish anything. The town finds this entirely reasonable and the Academy finds it faintly embarrassing.

## Design Notes

Fernstadt is where to put something the crown does not know about yet. The lag is canon, the roads are bad, and a season is a long time.

The grudge against Toscal is a small, usable local hook in a kingdom that is otherwise allied to Regno.

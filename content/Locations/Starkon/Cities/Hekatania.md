Capital of the [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]], seat of Queen Ilse Vorhalt, and the city that holds the [[Aravis Academy]].

## The City

Hekatania is a university city with a kingdom's government in it, and it has been that way round for five hundred years. The Academy is its largest institution by a long distance, its largest landlord, its largest employer, and the reason almost everyone who arrives has arrived.

The old city is timber and plaster over stone, built close, with the Academy's halls and colleges set through it rather than walled off in one quarter. A street in Hekatania will have a lecture hall, a bakery, a bookbinder, and four houses letting rooms on it.

## The Year

The city's calendar is the Academy's. Term is loud, crowded, and profitable. The weeks around [[The Sitting]] at the end of Mortera are the busiest of the year, when tens of thousands of candidates arrive for the examination and the city takes in every one of them at whatever the going rate has become.

Then they leave. For a few weeks each year Hekatania is a town of about a tenth of its usual noise, and the people who live there permanently describe those weeks the way other cities describe a holiday.

## Lodgings

Half the houses in the city let rooms to students, which makes landladies a political class in Hekatania. They set the rents, they set the house rules, and the Academy has never successfully regulated either.

## The Name

Hekate was born here in 137 AM, when the city was already called Hekatania. Whether the city was named for her or she was named for it is not settled by any record anyone has produced, and the city has firm opinions in both directions.

The matter is not academic. One answer makes the city the birthplace of a god, and the other makes a god the most famous person ever to be named after the city.

## The Trade

Everything a student needs and a great deal a wizard needs. Bookbinders, scribes, component sellers, instrument makers, and the scroll trade, which is regulated by nobody and watched closely by everybody. [[Hazel]] keeps the best known magic shop in the city and prices her scrolls the same in term and out of it, which no one else does.

> *The week after term ends, the city puts its prices back. I had taken a room at the student rate without understanding what that meant, and on the first day of the vacation my landlady knocked, explained that the rate was now the ordinary one, and asked whether I would be staying. The new rate was less than half the old. She had not been overcharging me. The students had.*

## Design Notes

Hekatania is the campaign's home city and is written to be lived in rather than toured. The Academy's year gives a DM a clock: term, the Sitting, and the empty weeks.

The landladies are the city's real power at street level and the Academy cannot touch them, which gives a party somebody to deal with who does not answer to [[Hekate]].

Avonis (AH-vo-nis) is a river town in [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]], timber-framed and run by its guilds.

> *Every bridge in Avonis belongs to a guild, and the toll is paid in whatever that guild makes. I crossed the river twice in one day and came out a loaf of bread and a pair of bootlaces down.*

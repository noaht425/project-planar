Avonis (AH-vo-nis) is a river town in the [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]], timber-framed and run by its guilds.

## The Bridges

The river runs through the middle of Avonis and there are a great many bridges over it, because the town has never built a public one. Each bridge was raised by a guild, is maintained by that guild, and belongs to it.

The toll is paid in kind, in whatever the owning guild makes or does. The bakers' bridge takes bread. The cordwainers' takes laces or a few minutes of work on your boots. The coopers' will take a small repair, and if you have nothing to offer they will take a promise, which they write down.

Nobody in Avonis finds this strange. Visitors cross the river as little as possible for the first day, then work out which bridges are cheap for them and stop thinking about it.

> *Every bridge in Avonis belongs to a guild, and the toll is paid in whatever that guild makes. I crossed the river twice in one day and came out a loaf of bread and a pair of bootlaces down.*

## The Guilds

The town has no mayor. It has a hall where the guilds sit, in an order of precedence settled by how old each guild is. The hall decides what it can agree on. The arrangement is slow and has held for centuries.

Guild membership is the whole of civic life here. A person without a guild cannot own a frontage, cannot take an apprentice, and cannot be heard in the hall. So the first year of a newcomer's life in Avonis is a matter of finding somebody to take them.

## The River

Timber comes down it, cut in the hills above the town, and Avonis is where it is sawn and framed. Most of the building timber in Starkon passes through here. A good deal of the frame work in [[Hekatania]] was cut and fitted in Avonis first, floated down, then raised on site.

## The Hall's Year

The guilds sit four times a year and in emergency, and an emergency requires two guilds to call it. The standing joke in Avonis is that nothing has ever been urgent enough for two guilds to agree that it was.

## Design Notes

A town where every crossing is privately owned gives a party a small, concrete problem every time they need to get to the other bank in a hurry.

The guild hall is the government, so anything a party wants from Avonis officially moves at the speed of an argument between twelve trades.

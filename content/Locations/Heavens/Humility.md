One of the six heavens of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Pavlas]]. For how reward and its cost work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

Low buildings of modest make standing in good light. The walls of every public space are given over to records of what the people living there actually did.

Souls in Humility are honoured in the open for everything they never claimed, by people who went and found out the facts themselves.

A soul that built its whole character on going unnoticed is now permanently noticed. Refusing recognition was the thing it was practised at, and it is the one thing the place will not let it do.

> *The walls are the only immodest thing in Humility, and they are immodest on behalf of people who are standing right there wishing they would stop.*

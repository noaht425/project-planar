One of the six heavens of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Pavlas]]. For how reward and its cost work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

Low buildings of modest make standing in good light. The walls of every public space are given over to records of what the people living there actually did.

Souls in Humility are honoured in the open for everything they never claimed, by people who went and found out the facts themselves.

## The Records

The records are kept by researchers, some of them lesser angels and some of them souls who have taken up the work. A researcher finds out what a soul did by asking the people it was done for and by checking the surviving documents. An entry is posted only when the researcher is satisfied that the soul never took credit for the deed or at least never reveled in the glory of it. Entries are plain and dated, and each one names the people who helped confirm it. A soul may ask to see its own entry. Most do not.

## Daily Life

The streets are quiet. Visitors are met at the edge of a town by someone who offers food and asks nothing. Precedence in a doorway is an old courtesy here and can hold up a street for an hour, because everyone insists that the other person go first. Meals are taken in common halls, where the food is plain and every guest is served before the host. Souls are not ranked and do not rank one another. Nobody in Humility has ever been recorded winning an argument about who should speak first.

## The Cost

A soul that built its whole character on going unnoticed is now permanently noticed. Refusing recognition was the thing it was practised at, and it is the one thing the place will not let it do.

Most souls arrive remembering what they did. After a few centuries most of them no longer do, and they read their own entries as they would read anyone's. [[Pavlas]] does not remind them. The researchers keep posting.

## Pavlas

Pavlas bows to every visitor, insists that the visitor enter first and will not accept precedence in a doorway.

## The Neighbours

Humility and [[Pride]] were one place under [[Charios]]. [[Deceit]] is the hell nearest Humility in spirit.

> *The walls are the only immodest thing in Humility, and they are immodest on behalf of people who are standing right there wishing they would stop.*

## Design Notes

The page reconciles two points from elsewhere in the book. The records stand on the walls, as the heaven always had them, and the souls themselves forget the deeds, as the page on Pavlas says. Visitors see the first and the residents live inside the second.

The researchers are written as a mixed group so that a soul in Humility has a trade open to it.

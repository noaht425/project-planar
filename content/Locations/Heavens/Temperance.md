One of the six heavens of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Blythe]]. For how reward and its cost work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

Terraces in open daylight, planted and kept, with the land stepping down in even stages. Everything served on them is portioned and nothing is heaped.

## Arrival

A soul reaching Temperance is walked to a terrace by a lesser angel and given a first meal. The meal is the right size. Occasionally a soul will ask for more and are told, politely, that more would not improve it. Souls who came from hard lives, from hunger, or from years of going without, find the measure insulting for about a decade and then stop noticing it.

## The Measure

Everything in Temperance arrives in the right measure. A soul here is given what it needs and finds, often for the first time, that enough is genuinely enough. The weather is mild and the food is good. Portions are measured for each soul individually, and the measuring has never been wrong. Souls who try to catch it out, by weighing a meal or by keeping a second ration back, find it exact.

## Hands

[[Blythe]] has no arms and cannot hand anything to anyone, so the lesser angels of Temperance do the serving. They carry the portions Blythe has decided on and they carry them without comment. A soul that wants something that is not on the list can petition Blythe, who answers by tracing letters in the air one at a time. An answer of any length takes days, and by the end of it most petitioners have lost the wish.

## The Cost

The appetite goes with it. A soul that has stopped wanting more has also stopped reaching, and much of what it used to be was built out of that reaching. A soul that built something in life, a business or a collection or a household, sees no reason to start another. The terraces are quiet in the evenings. Conversation is pleasant and short, because nobody has anything to bargain over.

## The Neighbours

Temperance and [[Indulgence]] were one place under [[Baator]]. A door joins them and both sides keep it shut. [[Tyranny]] is the hell nearest Temperance in spirit.

> *I was given a meal in Temperance that was exactly the right size, which had never once happened to me, and I have been quietly annoyed about every meal I have eaten since.*

## Design Notes

Temperance is the heaven where the reward and the cost are the same act. Every portion is correct, and the correctness is what removes the reaching.

The lesser angels serve as Blythe's hands because the page on Blythe says Blythe cannot hand anything to anyone. The ten-year adjustment also comes from that page.

The slow answers do the work on a visiting party that an argument would not.

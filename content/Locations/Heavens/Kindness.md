One of the six heavens of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Neema]]. For how reward and its cost work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

A warm, well lit country of small towns with the doors standing open. People come out onto the road to meet anyone arriving.

## The Towns

Each town is a few streets around a green. Nobody keeps accounts. A traveller is fed at the first house it reaches and is passed along to the next one when it moves on, and the people at the next house have usually been told it is coming. The roads between the towns are short and well kept. The lesser angels who live in Kindness work as ordinary neighbours and rarely mention that they are angels.

## Treatment

Souls in Kindness are treated by strangers exactly as they treated strangers, continually and without having to ask. A soul that was generous to people it did not know is met with generosity on every road. The reverse is also true, and it is the reason the blessed here tend to be careful about the way they greet newcomers.

## Better Selves

Each soul is kept company by a being that looks and speaks like a better version of itself. The companion listens and does not interrupt, and it knows the soul's history. For most souls the company is a comfort. For some it is a sentence, because the companion has no fault that the soul can find.

## The Cost

What the souls here were best at is giving, and there is nobody to give to. Everyone is already looked after. A soul whose life was built on being needed arrives somewhere nobody needs anything.

## Neema

[[Neema]] looks to each visitor like the best version of that visitor, and two people who have seen the archangel at the same time disagree about it afterward. Of the six archangels, Neema is the one who will help a mortal and ask least in return.

## The Neighbours

Kindness and [[Cruelty]] were one place under [[Hesit]], and where they touch the wall between them is thin. [[Desire]] is the hell nearest Kindness in spirit.

> *I was the only person in Kindness who needed anything, and word got round. By evening my boots had been mended twice, by two people who had each missed the other, and a man I had never met had carried my letters to the border and come back to ask whether there were more.*

## Design Notes

Kindness is the heaven a party is most likely to enjoy visiting and the one where a long stay is easiest to regret. The cost is quiet and takes a long time to show.

The companions come from the page on Neema. Giving them a flaw-free history is the page's own addition, and it is what turns a comfort into a sentence for some souls.

## Open

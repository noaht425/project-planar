One of the six heavens of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Verita]]. For how reward and its cost work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

There are no walls in Truth and nothing casts a shadow. The air is clear to the horizon in all directions and a soul can see the whole of the place from wherever it happens to be standing. The ground is pale grass and the air is warm. Without walls, the weather is the only boundary, and it rarely changes.

## Asking

Nothing is withheld in Truth. A soul can ask anything and will be told, including the questions it carried for a lifetime and never expected settled. Questions are answered in the order they were asked, for as long as the soul keeps asking. [[Verita]] answers some of them personally, and any angel Verita created or gave a station to can answer as well, which is most of the angels in the heaven. The answers are short. Nobody is made to continue.

## What Is Told

Some answers are worse than the not knowing. A soul learns what people truly thought of it, what its choices actually caused, what was being kept from it, and who was keeping it. A soul that asks about a loved one hears what the loved one felt. A soul that asks about its own death hears the order in which things went wrong. Nothing is softened, and nothing is added.

## Life Among the Blessed

With nothing hidden, there is no privacy and also no suspicion. Conversations carry across the open ground. Friendships form quickly and end quickly, because two souls who ask about each other learn the answers within the hour. The souls who stay longest are the ones who learned early which questions to put away.

## The Cost

Questions also run out. A soul that has asked everything it ever wanted to ask is left in a place whose entire purpose for it has been used up.

## Verita

[[Verita]] is almost entirely transparent, and a visitor sees the outline first and the inside second. Verita has never been recorded declining to answer a question. The peace that ended the [[Divine War]] gave Truth nine tenths of the realm that had been [[Sylas]]'s.

## Middling Souls

A soul that arrived without much it wanted to know runs out of questions within a season. The heaven then has nothing further to give it, centuries earlier than it runs out for anyone else. Those souls take up asking on behalf of others, and some will ask a visitor for a question to carry, since a question from outside is the only kind they have not used up.

## The Neighbours

Truth and [[Deceit]] were one place under Sylas. [[Pride]] is the hell nearest Truth in spirit.

> *I asked one question in Truth and I was given the answer. I am not going to write the question here, and I would rather you did not go looking for it yourself.*

## Design Notes

Truth is written as a place where nobody is cruel and the cost is still real. It matches Indulgence in method: nothing in it is done to a soul.

The short answers are an addition. They keep a long conversation from becoming a lecture, and they leave the soul to ask the next question.

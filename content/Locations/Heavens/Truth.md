One of the six heavens of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Verita]]. For how reward and its cost work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

There are no walls in Truth and nothing casts a shadow. The air is clear to the horizon in all directions and a soul can see the whole of the place from wherever it happens to be standing.

Nothing is withheld in Truth. A soul can ask anything and will be told, including the questions it carried for a lifetime and never expected settled.

Some answers are worse than the not knowing. A soul learns what people truly thought of it, what its choices actually caused, and what was being kept from it and why.

Questions also run out. A soul that has asked everything it ever wanted to ask is left in a place whose entire purpose for it has been used up.

> *I asked one question in Truth and I was given the answer. I am not going to write the question here, and I would rather you did not go looking for it yourself.*

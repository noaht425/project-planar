One of the six heavens of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Gwenhael]]. For how reward and its cost work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

Long bright halls lined with storehouses, with tables set out in front of them. What a soul gave up is brought out and laid where that soul can come and collect it.

## The Halls

The halls run for a long way and are very clean. Each storehouse is kept by a steward, one of the lesser angels, who knows what is in it and who it is for. A table stands in front of every door. When something is brought out the steward sets it on the table under a cloth and sends word to the soul it belongs to. Souls collect their things at their own pace and nobody is told to hurry.

## Return

What a soul gave up is returned to it in Sacrifice, including the things it never told anyone about. Items, time, years of a career and the names of people never spoken to again all come back. Some of what returns can be held. Some of it is an hour of a day long past, and the soul is given the hour.

## Giving Again

Once a soul has taken back what it gave, it may give something up again, for someone else, and the gift reaches them. This is the one place in the afterlives where a sacrifice is not wasted. [[Gwenhael]] arranged it that way, and it is the reason a soul in another afterlife can choose to come here.

## The Cost

Getting a thing back can undo what giving it up meant. Some souls find the returned thing is not what they had pictured for all those years, and some find they had grown past wanting it, which raises the question of what the sacrifice was worth.

## Gwenhael

Gwenhael is thin and keeps both arms across himself, and he is thinner each time he is seen. The stewards take care to bring him nothing he did not ask for.

## The Neighbours

Sacrifice and [[Desire]] were one place under [[Aaranea]], and where they touch the souls on each side can see one another. [[Cruelty]] is the hell nearest Sacrifice in spirit.

> *One table in Sacrifice has been laid for the same soul for longer than anyone working there can remember, and the thing on it has never been collected. The attendants dust it every morning. The soul it is laid for lives a short walk away and passes it most days.*

## Design Notes

Two things the book already says about Sacrifice are kept together here: souls get back what they gave, and the blessed give up things for others and are received. The second begins after the first.

The stewards give a visiting party a way to ask for a specific item and find out what it cost the person who gave it up.

## Open

- Whether a soul in a hell can choose to enter Sacrifice is unsettled. The page on Gwenhael says it is the only afterlife anyone can enter by choice from another.

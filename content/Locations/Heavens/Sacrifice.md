One of the six heavens of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Gwenhael]]. For how reward, cost and travel work across the afterlives, see [[Iudis]].

Long bright halls lined with storehouses, with tables set out in front of them. What a soul gave up is brought out and laid where that soul can come and collect it.

What a soul gave up is returned to it in Sacrifice, including the things it never told anyone about.

Getting a thing back can undo what giving it up meant. Some souls find the returned thing is not what they had pictured for all those years, and some find they had grown past wanting it, which raises the question of what the sacrifice was worth.

> *There is a table in Sacrifice that has been set for one particular soul for six hundred years and has never been collected. I asked whose it was. They would not tell me.*

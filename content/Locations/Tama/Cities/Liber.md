Liber is the capital of [[Kingdom of Tama|Tama]], built by exiles from every other kingdom, each of whom built what they remembered.

## The Quarters

The city is a set of quarters, and each was put up by people from somewhere else. They built what they knew. A quarter founded by Genoans is laid out like a piece of [[Geno (City)|Geno]], badly remembered and forty years out of date, and the one next to it is a piece of Sylfaen with the same problem.

Each quarter runs its own affairs. Disputes inside a quarter are settled by the quarter, in whatever way its founders brought with them, and the crown does not interfere. What the crown handles is anything that crosses between them.

That is most of what the government of Tama does, and it is harder than it sounds. Two quarters with incompatible ideas about who owns a wall will both be correct by their own law.

## The Seams

The interesting part of Liber is where the quarters meet. Road widths do not match. Drains were dug to different standards and some of them empty into each other. Fire rules in one quarter assume stone and in the next assume timber, and the boundary between those two has burned twice.

Nobody has rebuilt the city to fix any of this. Rebuilding would mean choosing one quarter's way of doing things, and that is the one decision Tama is not willing to make.

## The Crown

Tama elects its rulers, and [[Alistar Stormjaw]] was the first. A rough past is an asset in a Taman election, and a candidate with nothing to live down starts at a disadvantage.

The crown is mostly a foreign ministry. Getting a seat at the [[Panarex]] is Tama's whole policy, and it has been for five hundred years. Being seated for one afternoon at the last session is the best thing that has ever happened to the bid, and the kingdom talks about it constantly.

## 702 AM

[[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] is arresting and killing citizens of [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] wherever it finds them. Some of them are arriving here.

Taking them in is what Liber exists to do. It also offends the strongest kingdom in [[Arenis]] at the exact moment Tama is asking nine kingdoms for a vote. The argument is in the open, it is bitter, and it is not settled. So far the bid is winning, and most people in Liber cannot defend that out loud.

> *No two streets in Liber are built the same way. Each was put up by people from a different kingdom, and walking across the city is like walking across the world with most of the distance taken out.*

## Design Notes

The quarters give a party a city where the law changes when they cross a street, and where the useful question is always which quarter they are standing in.

The 702 question is live by design. Whether Liber opens to the Krotaphan refugees should come out of what the party does early, not out of what this page says, and the pressure is roughly even on both sides at the moment.

## Open

- Whether Tama takes the Krotaphan refugees is for play to decide. It is leaning toward the Panarex bid now, and the first acts of the campaign could move it.
- How many quarters there are, and which kingdoms they came from, is unwritten.
- Tama's language, and whether it has cities beyond Liber, is unwritten.

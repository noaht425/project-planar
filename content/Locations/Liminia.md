Where souls go for judgement
Lies in between the Heavens and Hells in the [[Iudis]]
 
[[Bis]]:
- Decides if you are good or bad overall
- Hates neutrality

[[Seisear]]:
- Decides which of the hells/heavens you go to

> *The queue in Liminia does not move at the same speed for everyone. I watched one soul go from the door to Seisear in the time it took another to be asked a third question by Bis. The second one had been a decent person on the whole, which is the one answer Bis will not take.*

One of the six hells of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Lux]]. For how punishment and standing work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

The halls begin at a great height and come down as they go in. A soul walking the length of one will be stooping by the middle of it and lower than that by the end. Doorways are cut to fit the soul expected to use them.

## The Halls

The first halls are tall enough for a giant to walk through upright. The innermost chambers are low enough that most adults crawl. The walls of every hall are hung with plaques and portraits recording achievements, and none of the achievements belong to the visitor.

## Contests

Pride is built to humble. The arrangements are specific to the soul: whatever a soul was proud of, it will find itself surrounded by those who are better at it, and it will be given every chance to compete with them. A soul proud of its learning is set to argue before better scholars, and a soul proud of its strength is set to wrestle stronger opponents. Contests are held in open halls before audiences of devils, and the results are posted on the walls where every soul can read them. The ranking has no top. Whenever a soul reaches the head of it, a name is added above. When a soul wins, the credit is recorded under another name, and the soul is not permitted to dispute it.

## Standing

Standing in Pride is measured in height. A soul of high standing is allowed to stand upright in halls where others stoop, and the doorways are recut to suit it. Standing is lost the same way. A soul that has been allowed to stand tall and is then brought down has the doors recut around it again, and the workers are not discreet about it.

## Lux and His Line

[[Lux]] keeps the descendants of his line in Pride, and he has most of them. They are treated as any other soul there. Their placement is the hell's punishment for a family known for its pride.

## Arrivals

Souls killed by the bearer of the [[Hell's Fire Staff]] are sent here at once.

## The Neighbours

Pride and [[Humility]] were one place under [[Charios]]. [[Truth]] is the heaven nearest Pride in spirit.

> *[[Lux]] is said to walk these halls at full height regardless. I was not able to confirm this, as I was on my knees at the time.*

## Design Notes

Standing as height gives the halls and the doorways a single rule. A DM can describe the architecture and let the party work out the social order from it.

The credit rule follows the page on Lux. The soul wins, the win is recorded, and the record names someone else.

The page keeps the existing claim that Pride is the most individually designed of the hells, and the contests are how that is delivered.

One of the six hells of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Belphegor]]. For how punishment and standing work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

The caverns are warm and close and the air in them is thick. Feast halls run one into the next, and there is no clean floor anywhere between them.

The ground in Indulgence is sticky everywhere and always, from spilled food, drink, and worse.

Rivers of over-sweetened water and liquor run through it, and souls drown in them regularly. Molten gold is worked into a good deal of what happens here.

It is the lightest of the hells by severity, which has not made it pleasant.

> *The sweetness is the part nobody warns you about. There is a smell in the lower caverns that I could not get out of my coat for a month, and I burned the coat in the end.*

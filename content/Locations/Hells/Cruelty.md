One of the six hells of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Abaddon]]. For how punishment, standing and travel work across the afterlives, see [[Iudis]].

The hell is a great many small rooms, each fitted out for its purpose, opening off tunnels that run further than anyone has troubled to map.

Cruelty is torture, administered thoroughly and without ornament. It is the most direct of the hells and makes the least effort to be anything else.

The cruel arrive and become the subjects of their own practice. Souls who were inventive in life are answered inventively.

> *I was offered a look inside one of the rooms and I declined. I am a thorough scholar but I am not a stupid one, and that distinction has carried me through eight planes.*

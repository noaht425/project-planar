One of the six hells of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Lilien]]. For how punishment, standing and travel work across the afterlives, see [[Iudis]].

The hell is one enormous cavern with galleries cut into its walls, facing each other across an open gulf. Every soul can see into every other soul's quarters from its own. The lighting across the gulf is always better than the lighting at home.

Desire runs on envy. A soul here can see what every other soul has, in detail, and what it has is always better.

Things can be obtained. The getting is the trap. A soul that finally takes hold of what it wanted finds its attention has already moved to the next thing, and it will keep moving for as long as the soul is there.

The hell covers lust and greed alongside envy, and all three are handled the same way.

> *Everyone in Desire can describe the rooms on the far side of the gulf down to the fittings. Nobody I spoke to could describe their own.*

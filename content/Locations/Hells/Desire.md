One of the six hells of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Lilien]]. For how punishment and standing work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

The hell is one enormous cavern with galleries cut into its walls, facing each other across an open gulf. Every soul can see into every other soul's quarters from its own. The lighting across the gulf is always better than the lighting at home.

## Quarters

Each soul is given quarters in a gallery. The front of every room is open to the gulf, so that nothing a soul owns is hidden from the gallery facing it, and the quarters on either side are visible as well. A soul may furnish its rooms with whatever it can obtain. Whatever it uses, the rooms across the gulf look better furnished.

## Envy

Desire runs on envy. A soul here can see what every other soul has, in detail, and what it has is always better. A soul that wanted a person in life sees that person, or someone very like them, in a facing gallery, and a soul that wanted wealth sees a hoard. The hell covers lust and greed alongside envy, and all three are handled the same way.

## Getting

Things can be obtained. Gangways and rope walks cross the gulf, and a determined soul can reach any gallery in the cavern. The getting is the trap. A soul that finally takes hold of what it wanted finds its attention has already moved to the next thing, and it will keep moving for as long as the soul is there. The quarters it has just left are the ones that now look better from where it stands.

## Trade

[[Lilien]] deals in bargains, and the price of each is whatever the petitioner can supply toward what Lilien is trying to do. Souls carry things to trade, and some make a living at it. A soul that arranges crossings and introductions for others builds standing in the hell. Standing shows as height. The higher galleries see more of the gulf, and the souls in them want more.

## Severity

Desire is the heaviest of the six hells and carries almost no physical punishment. The severity comes from the length of time a soul spends at the point just before having.

## The Neighbours

Desire and [[Sacrifice]] were one place under [[Aaranea]]. Where they touch, the souls on each side can see one another. [[Kindness]] is the heaven nearest Desire in spirit.

> *Everyone in Desire can describe the rooms on the far side of the gulf down to the fittings. Nobody I spoke to could describe their own.*

## Design Notes

Open fronts and better lighting across the gulf make envy a feature of the architecture. A party can see the effect in the first minute without a devil explaining it.

Height as standing means that every promotion makes the hell worse for the soul who earns it, which is the most direct statement of the rule on [[Iudis]] that rising does not stop the punishment.

The low level of physical punishment is deliberate and is the reason the severity ranking surprises people.

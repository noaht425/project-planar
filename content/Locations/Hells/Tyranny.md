One of the six hells of [[Iudis]], ruled by [[Archon]]. For how punishment and standing work across the afterlives, and how to travel between them, see [[Iudis]].

The hell is cut down into the rock in tiers. The galleries near the surface are dry and lit and reasonably comfortable, and they belong to souls who have climbed. Each tier below is hotter and wetter than the one above it. Arrivals start at the bottom.

Tyranny is a working society with a hard hierarchy, and every soul that arrives is placed at the bottom of it. The bottom is where the labour is and where the orders land.

Souls who ruled by force spend their time under the force of others. Advancement up the hierarchy is real and it is possible, which is the mechanism that keeps the bottom working.

Particular tyrants are given particular places in the structure, chosen to answer what they did with the power they had.

## The Bottom

The lowest tier is hot and wet. Water collects there as well as the waste of those in the tiers above, and the first work of every new arrival is to move it, by bucket and by pump, up to channels that carry it away. The tier is also where the hell is extended. The souls at the bottom cut the next tier down, and the upper galleries stay dry because the water has somewhere to go.

## Orders

Orders move down through the tiers, and each tier has an overseer. The overseers are briefed on each arrival, on what the soul did and where it should be put to work. [[Archon]] appoints the overseers. Each one gets everything it asks for until it becomes comfortable, and then it is replaced. A replaced overseer goes back down. Between the overseer and the bottom are layers of lesser supervisors who have appointed themselves, and the damned keep the hell's records and settle its disputes among themselves.

## Climbing

A soul climbs by being useful to someone above it. The usual routes are informing on a rival and taking blame that would otherwise land higher up. Every soul has someone above it with a reason to keep it where it is and someone below it with a reason to want its place, and the hierarchy depends on both.

## Particular Tyrants

A tyrant who ruled through informers is surrounded by them. A tyrant who taxed a province into famine works the lowest granary, where the ration is set by the soul above.

## Archon

Archon is seldom seen in the hell. His attention is on the rulers of [[Arenis]], and the hell runs on its own appetite for rank.

## The Neighbours

Tyranny and [[Justice]] were one place under [[Themati]], and they share walls. [[Temperance]] is the heaven nearest Tyranny in spirit.

> *My guide in Tyranny had been made overseer of its tier not long before, and had been given everything it asked for, which so far was a chair. It showed me the chair before it showed me anything else. I remarked that it looked comfortable. Then it asked me, quite aggressively, whether I thought it looked like the chair of someone who had become comfortable.*

## Design Notes

The hell grows downward because the bottom digs it. That ties the labour to the hierarchy, so a soul's work and its rank are the same subject.

The overseer rule comes from the page on Archon. Replacement is written as a return to the bottom, which keeps the ladder consistent with the rule on arrivals.

The hell's administration belongs to the damned, in line with Archon having no court of his own.

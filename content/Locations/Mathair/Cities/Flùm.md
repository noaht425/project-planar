Flùm (FLOOM) is the river port of the [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]], and it makes most of the masks worn at [[La Grande Mascarade]].

## The Trade

Mask-making in Flùm is an old trade with several hundred people in it, and it runs on a two year cycle. The year before a Mascarade is the work. The year of it is delivery, fitting, and repair, and the shops stay open through the night in the fortnight before the festival.

A mask from Flùm is made to a commission. The maker takes the measurements, hears what the client wants to be taken for, and does not discuss either afterwards.

> *Every mask-maker in Flùm keeps a list of whom they have made for. None of them will show it to anyone, and several of them have been offered a great deal of money to.*

## The Lists

Every maker keeps a record of whom they have made for, which mask, and in which year. The records are private, they are kept carefully, and refusing to show them is the oldest professional rule in the town.

The reason is the convention at the Mascarade. Rank is suspended there, nobody uses a title, and nobody can say for certain who they are speaking to, and the whole of that rests on the masks not being traceable. A maker who sold a list would end the festival and the trade in one transaction.

They are offered money regularly. Several of them have been offered a great deal.

## The Port

Flùm is a river port rather than a sea port, and it handles the traffic coming up to [[Omphalos]] from the coast. Grain, cloth, stone, and wine move through it. The masks are a famous trade rather than a large one, and the port is what actually pays for the town.

## The Off Years

In the year with no Mascarade the makers take ordinary work: theatre, funerals, and the festival masks of half a dozen kingdoms. The best of them also take restorations, since a mask worn at a Mascarade is often kept for a lifetime and brought back when it cracks.

## Design Notes

The lists are the most valuable paper in Mathair and nobody will sell them, which makes them a problem rather than a purchase.

Flùm is a working port that happens to hold the key to the one event where every power in the setting stands in one room unidentified.

## Open

- Whether any list has ever left a maker's hands is unwritten.
- How a maker decides to refuse a commission is unwritten.

## DM Notes

After 702 AM the lists matter a great deal more than they did. Nobody in that hall could say afterwards who had been standing where when [[Theophilius Fanatis]] attacked the ruler of [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]]. The makers of Flùm could tell anyone who was wearing what, and they will not.

Flùm (FLOOM) is the river port of [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]], and it makes most of the masks worn at [[La Grande Mascarade]].

> *Every mask-maker in Flùm keeps a list of whom they have made for. None of them will show it to anyone, and several of them have been offered a great deal of money to.*

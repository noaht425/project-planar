Capital of [[Kingdom of Mathair]].

Hosts [[La Grande Mascarade]] (aka "The Grand Masquerade"). Most central point in the world (supposedly).

## The Centre

The claim is the city's whole identity. A stone in the main square is marked as the centre of the world, surveyors come to argue about it, and nobody has disproved it or entirely proved it either.

The city is laid out in rings around that square, which is why the market districts sit where they do, and why an address in Omphalos is given as a distance and a direction from the stone.

## The Palace

At the centre, facing the square, in marble worked with gold vines. It is the queen's seat, it is where [[La Grande Mascarade]] is held when Mathair hosts, and it is open to petitioners on more days of the year than any other palace in the world.

Statues of every power a person might call on stand along its front. [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]] restricts no worship and the palace makes the point in stone.

## The Temple District

Every power with worshippers has somewhere here: the archangels and the archdevils, the dragons, the archfey, the Scholarchs, the ascendant gods, and a row of smaller houses for things with only a few hundred adherents in the world.

Which of them holds which position is the city's oldest quarrel and is conducted with great politeness. The district has been rebuilt twice to adjust the arrangement, both times at enormous expense, and both times the result satisfied nobody and offended no one.

[[Odile Varenne]] keeps a shop in the district and stocks for every power in it, which is possible in Omphalos and would be illegal in [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]].

## A Host Year

When Mathair's turn at the Mascarade comes round, the city spends a year on it. Lodging is booked out eighteen months ahead, [[Flùm]] delivers the masks, [[Lonvil]] fills with people who could not get a room in the capital, and the price of everything in Omphalos roughly doubles for a fortnight.

The 702 AM Mascarade was held here. [[Theophilius Fanatis]] attacked the ruler of [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] in the palace, in front of the assembled guests.

> *I asked a priest in the temple district which house had the best position and was given a long answer about sightlines, foot traffic, and the morning sun, delivered in the tone of a man who had not been asked for several years and had the answer ready. He is of the archangels. His house faces the second rank of the archdevils' row, which he mentioned twice.*

## Design Notes

Omphalos is where anybody in the setting can plausibly be met, which is why the Mascarade works here and why the attack happened here.

The temple district is the place to show a party how many powers this world has, and the quarrel over position is a low-stakes way to introduce the politics between them.

Lonvil (lon-VEEL), the long town, runs for miles along a single road in the [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]], and a great many earthborn live there.

> *Lonvil is one street long, and its people give distances in doors.*

## One Street

Lonvil has one street and no second street. It grew along the road from [[Omphalos]] to the north, building out rather than up or sideways. It is now several miles end to end, and in most places two buildings deep.

Addresses are given by door. A direction in Lonvil is a count of doors from a landmark, in one direction or the other. Nobody there has ever felt the want of a street name. Visitors find this impossible for about a day.

The length is the town's whole character. Nothing in Lonvil is central, so the town has several of everything, spread along the road at intervals that work out to roughly an hour's walk apart.

## The Earthborn

Most of the population is earthborn, mainly gnomes and halflings, and a good part of the building is scaled accordingly. The doors are low, the ceilings are low, and a tall visitor spends a long day stooping.

The town has not adapted for outsiders and is not rude about it. There is a short row of taller-built inns at the southern end, put up by the innkeepers themselves. Everyone refers to it as the big doors.

## What It Does

Lonvil services the road. Inns, stables, wheelwrights, farriers, carters, and every trade that lives off traffic going somewhere else, repeated along the length of it.

A great deal of what travels to [[Omphalos]] stops here first. So Lonvil hears everything coming into the capital a day before the capital does.

## The Watch

There is no wall and no gate, so Lonvil is watched rather than guarded. Each stretch of the road keeps its own few watchmen, who know their stretch and nobody else's. A pursuit that runs the length of the town passes through several jurisdictions that do not coordinate.

## Design Notes

A town with no centre is a useful shape. A party cannot corner anyone in Lonvil and cannot search it either, since there is always more of it in both directions.

The traffic makes it the best place in Mathair to hear a rumour early.

## Open

- How long Lonvil actually is, in doors, is unwritten and is apparently disputed within the town.
- What is at the northern end of the road is unwritten.

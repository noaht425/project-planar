Darasil (DAR-a-sil) is the mountain city of [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]], built down into the rock, and most of the elves who live there are drow. The word drow comes from the same root as the city, darawe, the mountain.

> *Ask the way in Darasil and you will be given a depth rather than a distance.*

Lenetha (LEN-eh-tha), the bright city, is the capital of [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]]. The scholars who remade [[Sylvan]] after 105 AM worked there, and the hall where they worked is still in use.

## The Hall

The purge ran from 105 to about 130 AM, when the scholars threw out the everyday words that made elvish speech sound like the fae language. The hall did not close afterwards. It has been making words ever since, at a slower pace, because a language that has deliberately lost its ordinary vocabulary needs replacements faster than it can agree on them.

A clerk at the door gives every visitor a slip with the newest word on it.

> *Words are still being made in the hall of the scholars in Lenetha. A clerk at the door gives every visitor a slip with the newest one written on it. The one I was given meant bread.*

Work in the hall is slow and contested. A proposed word is posted, argued over for a season, and either taken up or quietly dropped, and the arguments are about whether the sound of it is too close to [[Low Fae]] rather than about whether it is useful.

## The Oath-Holders

Thalanea's ruler is elected for life by the oath-holders, which is the body of families and institutions holding the kingdom's oldest contracts. Sylvan kept every word that touches names, oaths, debts, and bargains, so those contracts are still readable and still enforceable, and holding one is the qualification.

Caleth was elected under that rule and is addressed as A Chaleth. The electors meet in Lenetha, in the hall's older building, which the scholars resent.

## Speaking It

The city is where the language's shape is most obvious. Sylvan can draw up a binding agreement in a sentence and struggles with a request for bread, and in Lenetha the gap is a civic joke rather than an embarrassment. Clerks are proud of it.

[[Pellan]] keeps his shop here and sells primers for the contract language that explicitly do not cover food.

## Design Notes

Lenetha holds the kingdom's constitution in two places at once: a language that makes its old contracts enforceable, and a body of electors whose claim to vote is holding those contracts.

The hall is a usable location. A party that wants a word made, changed, or declared improper has one building to go to.

## Open

- How many oath-holders there are, and whether a contract can be bought to join them, is unwritten.

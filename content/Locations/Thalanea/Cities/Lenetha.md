Lenetha (LEN-eh-tha), the bright city, is the capital of [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]]. The scholars who remade [[Sylvan]] after 105 AM worked here. The hall where they worked is still in use.

## The Hall

The purge ran from 105 to about 130 AM, or at least that is when the records stop treating it as ongoing. The scholars threw out the everyday words, the ones that made elvish speech sound too much like the fae language. But the hall did not close. It has been making words ever since, more slowly now, and mostly as replacement rather than purge. A language that has deliberately lost its ordinary vocabulary needs new words faster than it can agree on them.

Work is slow and contested. A proposed word gets posted, argued over for a season, and then either taken up or quietly dropped. The arguments are not usually about whether a word is useful, but about whether it sounds too much like [[Low Fae]].

A clerk at the door gives every visitor a slip with the newest word on it.

> *Words are still being made in the hall of the scholars in Lenetha. A clerk at the door gives every visitor a slip with the newest one written on it. I was told mine meant bread. The clerk did not seem especially sure of that, and I have since been given two other translations of it.*

## The Oath-Holders

Thalanea's ruler is elected for life by the oath-holders. These are the families and institutions holding the kingdom's oldest contracts. Sylvan kept every word that touches names, oaths, debts, and bargains, so those contracts are still readable and still enforceable. Holding one is the qualification. There is no other.

Caleth was elected under that rule and is addressed as A Chaleth. The electors meet in the hall's older building, which the scholars resent.

## Speaking It

Lenetha is where the shape of the language is most obvious. Sylvan can draw up a binding agreement in a sentence — half a page if the parties are careful — and still struggle with a request for bread. Elsewhere in the kingdom that gap is a quiet embarrassment. In Lenetha it is a civic joke, and the clerks are proud of it.

[[Pellan]] keeps his shop here. He sells primers for the contract language that explicitly do not cover food.

## Design Notes

Lenetha holds the kingdom's constitution in two places at once. One is a language that keeps the old contracts enforceable. The other is a body of electors whose claim to a vote is holding those contracts.

The hall is a usable location. A party that wants a word made, changed, or declared improper has one building to go to.

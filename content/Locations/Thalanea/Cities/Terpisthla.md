Terpisthla (TER-pis-thla), the bright wave, is the port of [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]], on the coast facing the sea.

Its harbour is where Thalanea meets the rest of the world, and the city is more relaxed about the fey than anywhere else in the kingdom, which is to say a little.

> *A harbour clerk in Terpisthla stamped my papers, looked at the fae merchant behind me in the queue, and stamped his as well, which I was told afterwards was a very liberal thing to have done.*

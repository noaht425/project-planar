One of the five elemental realms of [[Tarkis]], ruled by [[Okzu]]. The second dragon of the realm is [[Amol]], whose element is acid. For how the realms touch and how the seams between them work, see [[Tarkis]].

## The Land

Noxia is industry built on top of industry. The works run day and night, the air is thick enough to chew, and everything is stacked, because the ground that can be built on ran out a long time ago and nobody stopped building.

The poison is ordinary here. Natives are fine in it. Everyone else needs a filter, and the filter trade is one of the larger businesses in the realm.

**Okzude Eteros** (ok-ZOO-deh eh-TEH-ros), Okzu's place, is the great works and the districts stacked around and above them.

**Amolisde Laxos** (ah-moh-LIS-deh LAH-ksos), the dissolving place, is the acid flats where [[Amol]] keeps. The ground there eats what is set down on it.

**Charisde Minos** (khah-RIS-deh MEE-nos), the skin of rot, is a crust of settled runoff covering the realm's low country. It holds a person's weight until it does not.

**Numosde Thrasi** (noo-MOS-deh THRAH-see), the hide road, is the network of covered causeways running above the smog. Everything that must stay clean moves along them.

**Panisde Quari** (pah-NIS-deh KWAH-ree), the locked box, is a district that was sealed off after something went wrong in it. It is still sealed and the works around it have simply built over the top.

## Okzude Talthu (ok-ZOO-deh TAL-thoo)

The capital is a factory that has never once stopped running, and the city has grown around and through it.

What [[Okzu]] hoards is what he is owed. Debts here are written down, enforced, inherited and traded, and nearly everyone in the realm owes something into the works. A person's standing is their position in that ledger. Okzu holds the realm without ever having sat on a throne in it, and most of the people under him have never seen him.

## The Cities

**Varilpithru** (vah-ril-PITH-roo) is the counting house. Every debt in the realm is recorded here, and the clerks of Varilpithru decide what a life is worth in instalments.

**Minosquari** (mee-nos-KWAH-ree) is sealed habitation, built for people who cannot take the air outside. It is the wealthiest city in Noxia and the most resented, since clean air is the one thing the realm will not sell cheaply.

**Amolaxis** (ah-moh-LAH-ksis) works acid. It stands at the edge of the flats, moves itself every few years as the flats advance, and treats this as routine.

## Villages

Okzalira (ok-zah-LEE-rah), Charu (KHAH-roo), Pithra (PITH-rah), Quarix (KWAH-riks), Numu (NOO-moo), Amolu (ah-MOH-loo), Sichra (SIKH-rah), Devara (deh-VAH-rah), Laxora (lah-KSOH-rah), Terivu (teh-REE-voo), Agrax (AG-raks) and Rasolu (rah-SOH-loo).

Every one of them was built by the works, for the works, and is owed for. A village in Noxia does not have a founding story. It has an opening date and a balance.

> *A child born in Noxia is entered in the ledger at Varilpithru on the day it is born, with the cost of the delivery against its name. I watched a midwife present her bill at the counter, and the clerk asked her, very politely, whether she wanted it charged to the mother or carried by the child.*

## Design Notes

Noxia is the realm where the dragon rules through an institution instead of through presence. A party can spend a whole campaign in Okzu's grip without ever being in a room with him, which is the opposite of how the other four work.

Debt is the realm's main hook. Everything a party might want in Noxia is owned by someone who owes, and anything they do will land in a ledger somewhere.

The sealed district has no explanation on purpose. Something went wrong, it was closed, the works built over it, and whatever is in there is left for a DM.

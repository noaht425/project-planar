One of the five elemental realms of [[Tarkis]], ruled by [[Tenu]]. The second dragon of the realm is [[Mens]], whose element is psychic. For how the realms touch and how the seams between them work, see [[Tarkis]].

## The Land

Earth is mountain, cliff and cave. The surface is high and broken and most of the realm's people live under it, in systems that run deeper than anyone has finished mapping.

Everything here is already claimed. [[Tenu]] counts the stone itself as the hoard, which means every tunnel, seam and chamber in the realm is something Tenu considers already counted and lent out.

**Tenude Eteros** (teh-NOO-deh eh-TEH-ros), Tenu's place, is the hollowed mountain and the valleys that feed it.

**Xarkiquari** (ksar-kee-KWAH-ree), the puzzle box, is a cave system that has defeated every attempt to chart it. Parts of it are inhabited by people who have never seen the surface.

**Varilde Minos** (vah-RIL-deh MEE-nos), the skin of gold, is a cliff wall several days long where the ore seams run exposed. Working it is legal only with a holding granted from the capital.

**Teleteros** (teh-leh-TEH-ros), the place below, is everything beneath the mapped caves. What is down there is not agreed on.

**Bathisde Thrasi** (bah-THIS-deh THRAH-see), the carrying road, is a natural stair wide enough for freight that runs from the high passes down into the cave country. Most of the realm's goods move on it.

## Tenude Talthu (teh-NOO-deh TAL-thoo)

The capital is a mountain that has been hollowed chamber by chamber until the whole of it is one vault, and the city lives in the vault.

Holdings are granted here. A family does not own its tunnel so much as hold it on Tenu's sufferance, and the grant is recorded in the capital and can be withdrawn from the capital. Most politics in the realm is about grants.

## The Cities

**Quaripaeteros** (kwah-ree-pay-TEH-ros), the box of rooms, is built inside a single cavern and runs vertically. Districts are stacked by depth and connected by shafts, and a person's address says how far down they live.

**Varilaxos** (vah-ree-LAH-ksos) works the ore out of Varilde Minos. It is the richest city in the realm and the one most dependent on the capital's goodwill, since every seam it works is a grant that could be revoked.

**Panisde Eteros** (pah-NIS-deh eh-TEH-ros), the locked place, keeps the registry. Every holding in the realm is recorded here, and the city is built like a vault because the records are the only thing in it worth taking.

## Villages

Teiru (TAY-roo), Chovas (KHOH-vas), Panira (pah-NEE-rah), Chalu (KHAH-loo), Agrathi (ag-RAH-thee), Laxu (LAH-ksoo), Rasivos (rah-SEE-vos), Kethra (KETH-rah), Olmara (ol-MAH-rah), Tsarivu (tsah-REE-voo), Desthu (DES-thoo) and Veruna (veh-ROO-nah).

Most sit at the mouth of one tunnel or at one end of one seam, and the grant that keeps them there is held by a single family. A village whose grant lapses does not usually last a year.

> *A woman in Kelaxos showed me her family's grant, which is older than the city around it and has been renewed every generation without a word of it changing. She keeps it in a box under the floor. She was much less careful with the house.*

## Design Notes

Earth runs on paperwork. Tenu's hoard being the stone itself turns the whole realm into tenancy, and the registry at Panisde Eteros is where a campaign can get leverage that no amount of fighting would give it.

Teleteros is unresolved. There is a floor below the mapped caves that nobody agrees about, and that is a hole left for a DM to fill.

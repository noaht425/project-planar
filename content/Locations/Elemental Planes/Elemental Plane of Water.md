One of the five elemental realms of [[Tarkis]], ruled by [[Vetur]]. The second dragon of the realm is [[Charis]], whose element is necrotic. For how the realms touch and how the seams between them work, see [[Tarkis]].

## The Land

Water is submerged from end to end. Its cities are built for people who do not breathe air, and a visitor who does will need to arrange for that before arriving.

Depth does the work that geography does elsewhere. The shallows are lit and crowded, the middle water is where most people live, and the trench is where everything ends up.

**Veturde Eteros** (veh-TUR-deh eh-TEH-ros), Vetur's place, is the trench and the slopes down into it.

**Vrillosde Minos** (vril-LOS-deh MEE-nos), the skin of light, is the lit shelf. It is the warmest, busiest part of the realm and holds most of its farming.

**Dallosiasi** (dal-loh-see-AH-see), the arc of night, is the lightless deep outside the trench. Nothing is grown there and a good deal is hidden there.

**Vethruiasi** (veth-roo-ee-AH-see), the arc of ice, is a cold current that runs the length of the realm and does not mix with the water around it. Crossing it without preparation kills.

**Charisde Eteros** (khah-RIS-deh eh-TEH-ros), Charis's place, is drowned ground on the trench's far rim where [[Charis]] keeps. Nothing raised from it is permitted into the cities.

## Veturde Talthu (veh-TUR-deh TAL-thoo)

The capital stands on the trench floor, laid out among the hoard rather than beside it.

Everything that has ever sunk in the realm is arranged across that floor in order, and the order is maintained. Ships sit in rows by the century they went down. The city's great offices are the ones that catalogue, position and guard, and a family's standing comes from which section of the floor it keeps.

Nothing may be raised from the floor without permission, and permission is rare.

## The Cities

**Perilaxos** (peh-ree-LAH-ksos), the watching place, sits on the shelf edge where the light stops. It keeps watch on the deep and on everything that comes up out of it, and it is where the realm's warnings start.

**Numosvaril** (noo-mos-VAH-ril) handles salvage. What Vetur allows to be lifted comes here to be valued and sold, and the city has grown wealthy on a trade it is not allowed to expand.

**Vethrakis** (veth-RAH-kis) stands against the cold current and makes its living from it, cutting and shipping ice into the other four realms.

## Villages

Vethu (VEH-thoo), Dallira (dal-LEE-rah), Shurava (shoo-RAH-vah), Iasu (ee-AH-soo), Perasi (peh-RAH-see), Flasivo (flah-SEE-voh), Trovu (TROH-voo), Minovas (mee-NOH-vas), Laxira (lah-KSEE-rah), Agravu (ag-RAH-voo), Rasithu (rah-SEE-thoo) and Kelova (keh-LOH-vah).

Most are single households strung along the shelf or tucked into the walls of the middle water. Several are only reachable by swimming through something unpleasant, which the people living there consider an advantage.

> *The family I lodged with in Veturde Talthu keep a section of the floor from the century before last, mostly fishing boats. The eldest son walks it every morning with a measuring rod. Anything the current has moved by so much as a finger's width he puts back, and writes the correction in a book.*

## Design Notes

The hoard is the city here, so the capital is the one place in the realm where a party is walking through the dragon's treasure the entire time. Everything in Water is about what may be lifted and what must stay where it fell.

Charis sitting on the far rim gives the realm its quarantine. A realm that catalogues its drowned has an obvious problem with a necrotic dragon living among them, and nobody in the capital will discuss it.

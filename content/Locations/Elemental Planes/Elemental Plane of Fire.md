One of the five elemental realms of [[Tarkis]], ruled by [[Pyrrha]]. The second dragon of the realm is [[Brilis]], whose element is radiant. For how the realms touch and how the seams between them work, see [[Tarkis]].

## The Land

Fire is a hellscape and makes no apology for it. The ground is burning stone, the sky is lit from underneath, and the horizon moves as the flows move.

Most of the realm is wilderness. Settlement is confined to the places where the ground has stayed put long enough to build on.

**Pyrrhade Eteros** (pir-RAH-deh eh-TEH-ros), Pyrrha's place, is the caldera and the slopes under it. It is the most stable ground in the realm, which is a statement about the rest of the realm rather than about the caldera.

**Theinufyr** (thay-NOO-fir), hot dirt, is the ash plain that covers the realm's eastern half. It is deep enough in places to swallow a caravan and it moves with the wind.

**Minosde Fyrrhu** (mee-NOS-deh FIR-roo), the skin of the fire, is a sheet of obsidian the size of a province, formed where a flow cooled all at once. It rings underfoot. Nothing grows on it and nothing crosses it quickly.

**Thraside Fyrrhu** (thrah-SEE-deh FIR-roo), the roads of fire, are the molten rivers. They are called roads because they are used as roads by the things that can ride them, and the locals have never thought this was strange.

**Vrillosiasi** (vril-loh-see-AH-see), the arc of light, is the band where [[Brilis]]'s light reaches the ground. It is the only part of the realm an unprotected visitor can stand in for long.

## Pyrrhade Talthu (pir-RAH-deh TAL-thoo)

The capital, and the hoard. Pyrrha keeps everything in the realm that fire cannot destroy, and she keeps it in vaults cut into the cone of the volcano she rules from.

The city is built in terraces, inside the caldera and down the outer slopes. Rank runs by height. The oldest families hold the upper terraces where the air is thinner and cooler, and the vault wardens live highest of all.

## The Cities

**Neritalthu** (neh-ree-TAL-thoo), the dagger hoard, makes weapons and little else. What comes out of its forges is bought across all five realms and a fair amount of it leaves Tarkis entirely.

**Fyrquari** (fir-KWAH-ree), the hot box, is sealed. The whole city is one furnace with streets running through it, and its people work in shifts measured by how long a body can stand it.

**Vrilloseteros** (vril-loh-seh-TEH-ros), the place of light, sits in the Vrillosiasi and is the only city in Fire that outsiders can live in without magical help. It is where Brilis is attended to, and where most scholarship in the realm happens.

## Villages

Rasavu (rah-SAH-voo), Hespis (HES-pis), Tsarilos (tsah-REE-los), Perisa (peh-REE-sah), Montara (mon-TAH-rah), Brinos (BREE-nos), Flasira (flah-SEE-rah), Kaseros (kah-SEH-ros), Devios (deh-VEE-os), Minora (mee-NOH-rah), Terlas (TER-las) and Alisu (ah-LEE-soo).

They are single-industry places, most of them tied to one quarry, one crossing or one stretch of flow. Nothing in any of them is more than a day's walk from something that will kill a visitor who is not paying attention.

> *They sell a cloak in Arteros that will keep a mortal alive on the flows for about six hours. I asked the man what happens at seven hours. He said nobody had ever come back to complain, and then he laughed for a long time. I bought two.*

## Design Notes

The realm is played close to the standard elemental plane of fire, and what makes it specific is Pyrrha's hoard. A realm where almost nothing survives has made a currency out of the things that do, and the vaults in the caldera are the reason the capital is where it is.

Vrilloseteros exists to give a party somewhere to stand. A realm with no habitable ground for outsiders is a realm a campaign cannot be set in, and Brilis's light is the reason one city is different.

The flows being called roads is how the locals are written throughout. They are not impressed by any of this and never have been.

One of the five elemental realms of [[Tarkis]]. It was ruled by [[Zaros]], whose second dragon was [[Vari]]. [[Afthor]] killed them both, drank their blood and holds the realm now. For how the realms touch and how the seams between them work, see [[Tarkis]].

## The Land

Air was the built-up realm. Under Zaros it carried more city and less open sky than anywhere else in Tarkis, because what Zaros collected was cities and he went on collecting them for as long as he held the place.

The storm has been on it ever since he died. Lightning and thunder do not stop. Roofs are gone across whole districts and have not been replaced, people are poor in a way the other realms are not, and daily life swings between revelry and panic without settling into either.

**Zarosde Eteros** (zah-ROS-deh eh-TEH-ros), Zaros's place, is the ring of cities under the old seat. Most of what is left of the realm's population is here.

**Varide Iasi** (vah-REE-deh ee-AH-see), the arc of thunder, is where the storm is worst. It moves, slowly, and what it crosses does not get rebuilt.

**Flaside Thrasi** (flah-SEE-deh THRAH-see), the wing roads, are the old flight routes between the cities. They were the safest way to travel in the realm and are now the most dangerous.

**Borasiasi** (boh-rah-see-AH-see), the northern arc, catches the least of the storm. Everyone who can get there has gone there.

**Terlaxos** (ter-LAH-ksos) means somewhere in particular and is used for the drifting country that is not anchored to anything. It has never had a better name and the joke is old.

## Zarosde Talthu (zah-ROS-deh TAL-thoo)

The capital, and the only dragon's seat in Tarkis standing empty.

Zaros ruled from a platform above the cities, and the hoard was the cities themselves, kept and extended and looked after. Afthor took the realm and never took the seat. He collects nothing, lives elsewhere in the realm and does not administer any of it.

The city under the empty seat is still the capital by every formal measure. It has no court, no works and no revenue, and the people in it hold the title and nothing else.

## The Cities

**Vrillaxos** (vril-LAH-ksos) kept its lights on. It did so by rationing power that was never meant to be rationed, and it is the nearest thing the realm has to a functioning government.

**Thrasivar** (thrah-SEE-var) sits on the one wing road still usable in both directions, which has made it the market for the whole realm and a place worth fighting over.

**Borathi** (boh-RAH-thee) is in the northern arc and has taken in more people than it can feed. It is the least damaged city in Air and the most crowded.

## Villages

Flasu (FLAH-soo), Zaru (ZAH-roo), Varith (VAH-rith), Eurasa (eh-oo-RAH-sah), Iasvi (ee-AS-vee), Notira (noh-TEE-rah), Zephu (ZEP-hoo), Laxiv (LAH-ksiv), Thruso (THROO-soh), Boralu (boh-RAH-loo), Agrathu (ag-RAH-thoo) and Rasiva (rah-SEE-vah).

Four of these no longer have anyone in them and nobody has taken the names off the maps. The rest are small and poor, and in the business of getting through the next storm.

> *There is a tavern in Thrasivar that holds a party every night without exception. I asked the owner what they were celebrating. He said they had got through the day, and that this was not a small thing, and then he asked whether I was going to drink or just stand there asking questions.*

## Afthor

[[Afthor]] killed [[Zaros]] and [[Vari]] and took the realm. He does not run it, has never claimed the seat, and leaves the cities to manage as they can.

The four [[Wind Gods]] intend to kill him and take the realm for themselves. Every dragon in [[Tarkis]] wants him dead as well, and the dragons are working to bring Zaros and Vari back. Nobody has asked the people living in Air what they would prefer.

## Design Notes

Air has been broken so that Tarkis is not five variations on the same idea. The other four are run. This one has an owner who is not a ruler.

Because Zaros collected cities, the decline is easy to read. The realm was built up because its dragon hoarded settlements, and it has fallen apart because the one holding it now hoards nothing.

The towns table carries condition rather than trade, since in Air what matters first is whether a place still has people in it.

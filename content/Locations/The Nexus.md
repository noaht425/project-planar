A man-made island in the middle of the three continents, raised in about 40 AM, and the home of the [[Panarex]]. It is in nobody's waters and belongs to no kingdom.

## The Raising

The eight kingdoms that existed then paid for it together: [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]], [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]], [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]], [[Kingdom of Thalamas|Thalamas]], [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]], [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]], [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]], and [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]]. None of them did the work. They hired it, and the hire was the largest single commission any of the kingdoms had placed to that point.

What the eight were buying was ground that none of them owned. A meeting held in a capital is a meeting held on somebody's terms, and all eight refused to be the host. That is the whole reason the island exists.

Each kingdom's contribution was recorded at the time. The eight accounts that survive do not agree with one another, and every one of them has its own kingdom paying the most.

## The Island

The island is a single piece of raised stone, roughly circular, with a harbour cut into its western side. It holds the [[Panarex]] at its centre and very little else: the harbour works, the houses of the people who keep the place, and a cistern system, since there is no fresh water on it.

Nothing is grown there. Everything eaten on the Nexus arrives by ship, so the harbour is the busiest thing on the island for the fortnight before a session and quiet for years at a stretch.

The stone has no seams in it anywhere, and no quarry on any continent matches it.

## The Keepers

The people who run the island are sworn to it rather than to any kingdom, and they hold no allegiance that a seated ruler could call in. Doorkeepers, cooks, harbour hands, and clerks all come under the same oath, and the posts pass down within families.

A good number of them are Tamans now. [[Kingdom of Tama|Tama]] holds no vote to compromise, which makes its people the easiest hires to agree on, and the kingdom with no seat keeps the hall that the other nine sit in.

## Getting There

A session is attended by invitation, and an invitation is a physical token with a name cut into it. The doorkeepers check the name against the roll and keep the token. Between sessions the island takes any ship that calls, and a handful do, mostly carrying stores and the occasional traveller who has talked their way onto a supply run.

Nobody is buried on the Nexus. The dead go home, because the island is nobody's soil and no kingdom will let its people lie in it.

> *The harbour master told me that nothing stays on the Nexus. No graves, no births on the roll, and no houses held by anyone who is not sworn. I asked what happens to a keeper who marries off the island. She said that she had, and that her husband has never been allowed past the harbour in thirty years, and that he has stopped minding.*

## Design Notes

The island is the one piece of ground in [[Arenis]] that nobody owns, and that is its entire function. Every other meeting in the setting happens on somebody's terms.

The disagreeing accounts are the useful detail. There is no settled record of who paid for the Nexus, which means there is no settled argument about who it belongs to.

The keepers being largely Taman is the quiet joke of the place, and gives a party a way into the building that does not run through any kingdom.

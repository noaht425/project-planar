One of the seven domains of [[Iris]], held by [[Atris]]. For how names and bargains work in the fey realms, and what is owed under them, see [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]].

## The Domain

Every living thing in Hunt has a role. A visitor is either hunting or being hunted and will work out which within the hour.

The roles are assigned on arrival and they are not permanent. A hunter that fails badly enough becomes quarry. Quarry that survives long enough is offered the other side.

The quarry is always given a real chance. This is the rule the domain is built on and it has never been waived. That is the only reason anyone comes here voluntarily.

**Fiadhachlúbhar** (FEE-a-ghakh-loo-var), the hunting ground, is open country cut by treelines, laid out as though somebody designed it for the purpose, because somebody did.

**Dárabhefiadh** (DAH-ra-veh-fee-a), the wild mountain, is the high country where the oldest quarry has gone and where no hunt has ever been completed.

**An Rianán (REE-a-nahn) Fada** (FA-da), the long path, is the route the great hunts run. It changes between hunts and is never announced in advance.

**Murlánuaimhre** (MUR-lah-nuv-reh), the lake of fear, is still water in the middle of the open country. Nothing is hunted within sight of it and nobody will explain the rule to an outsider.

## Lainea (LA-nya) Atris

Atris keeps a lodge rather than a court, and it moves. Where the lodge is standing is where the domain's business is done that season.

Atris appears as a young woman in a chiton, barefoot, carrying a bow. She was the lover of [[Thalia]] and gave her the power that made her a god, the single most consequential thing any archfey has done for a mortal.

## The Cities

**Fiadhlainis** (FEE-agh-la-nish), the hunt city, is where hunts are registered and settled. Every formal hunt in the domain is recorded here and the records are the domain's law.

**Neachlain** (NAKH-lan) is where the dead are dealt with, on both sides of the arrangement. It is a quiet place and holds more ceremony than anywhere else in Hunt.

**Eachlainis** (AKH-la-nish) breeds and trains the horses and hounds, and sells them across all seven domains at prices that reflect what they are.

## Villages

Cuithu (KUH-huh), Fiadhu (FEE-a-ghuh), Sionnu (SHON-nuh), Géiru (GAY-ruh), Ulchu (UL-khuh), Tuircu (TUR-kuh), Faolru (FAL-ruh), Rosclu (ROSK-luh), Eachu (A-khuh), Giorau (GO-ra), Natharu (NA-ha-ruh) and Cáirneu (KAHR-neh).

Every one of them is downwind of something. Villages in Hunt are sited for cover rather than for water or road access, so the domain's map looks wrong to anyone who has not lived in it.

> *I was told on arrival which of the two I was, and I will not be writing it down. I will say that the chance they give you is genuine, that I took it, and that I have never run so well before or since.*

## Design Notes

Without the guaranteed chance, Hunt would be an execution. It is also the hook, since a party that understands the rule can use it, and a party that does not will assume the whole domain is hostile.

Roles changing in both directions means the domain can turn on a party mid-session without the DM having to invent a reason.

The lake has a rule everyone keeps and nobody explains. It is for a DM to fill.

One of the seven domains of [[Iris]], held by [[Alsa]]. For how names, bargains and debts work in the fey realms, see [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]].

## The Domain

Nothing decays in Bone. Everything that has died here is still here, and the domain has been accumulating for as long as it has existed.

The ground is bone. The buildings are bone. What a creature was in life stays legible in what is left of it long after the creature itself is gone, which makes the domain the best archive in the fey realms and an unpleasant place to walk.

Bone faces Hunt across the realm. One of them produces the dead and the other one keeps them, and the two domains have a working relationship that neither enjoys.

**Craimhlúbhar** (KRAV-loo-var), the bone ground, is the plain that covers most of the domain, layered deep enough that nobody has dug to the bottom.

**An Cionn (KONN) Mór** (MOHR), the great head, is a skull large enough to hold a city, left by something the domain has no record of.

**Cnámhdárabhe** (KNAHV-dah-ra-veh), the bone mountain, is the oldest accumulation, and the deepest layers of it are from before there were fey to remember them.

**Rélannlúbhar** (RAY-lann-loo-var), the star ground, is where the bone has gone white and smooth enough to reflect the sky. Nothing is built on it.

## Lainea (LA-nya) Alsa

Alsa's seat stands inside An Cionn Mór, which is either the grimmest joke in Iris or simply the largest available building, and Alsa has never clarified.

Alsa appears as a werewolf eight feet tall with broken bones protruding. The other archfey keep Alsa at arm's length for not being pleasant to look at, which has not cost Alsa an inch of territory.

## The Cities

**Craimhlainis** (KRAV-la-nish), the bone city, is carved from a single layer of the plain and holds the domain's records, which are bones rather than documents.

**Cionnlain** (KONN-lan) sits in the jaw of the great skull and is the only city in the domain built from something it did not have to cut.

**Déadánea** (DAY-dah-nya), the tooth seat, works bone into everything the domain exports, and the fey realms buy a great deal of it while preferring not to discuss where it comes from.

## Villages

Craimhu (KRA-vuh), Cnámhu (KNAH-vuh), Rélnu (RAYL-nuh), Ainbhu (AN-vuh), Cuimhu (KUH-vuh), Déadu (DAY-duh), Neachu (NA-khuh), Cionnu (KON-nuh), Doimhu (DO-vuh), Báinu (BAH-nuh), Duibhra (DUH-vra) and Scáilbhu (SKAHL-vuh).

Most sit on top of a layer they are working. A village in Bone moves when its layer is exhausted, and the abandoned ones are still standing, because nothing here falls down either.

> *You can ask in Cuimhrea about anyone who died in this domain and they will find the bone and tell you what it says. I asked after a scholar who went missing two centuries ago. They found him in an afternoon. I have been unable to decide since whether that was a kindness.*

## Design Notes

Bone is an archive, and that is the hook. A domain where nothing decays is a domain where evidence survives, and a party that needs to know what happened to someone has exactly one place to go.

Alsa losing no territory over being shunned is the point of the social arrangement. The other archfey can exclude Alsa from their company and not from their realm.

The great skull has no explanation and the domain has no record of what left it. That is for a DM.

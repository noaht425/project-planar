One of the seven domains of [[Iris]], held by [[King Thera]]. For how names, bargains and debts work in the fey realms, and what that means for a mortal standing in one, see [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]].

Seasons is four territories with a fifth at the centre. Each of the four is ruled by one of Thera's children and is permanently and perfectly its own season. The centre is the only ground in the domain where a season changes into another one.

## Rothánea (RO-hah-nya)

The turning seat, where Thera holds court.

Thera is made of leaves that are always changing colour, dying and growing back, and the ground around the court does the same thing. A visitor standing in one place through an afternoon will watch it pass through the whole year more than once.

The court is where the four quarters bring their quarrels. None of the four rulers can change what their own quarter is, so everything they have to argue about is about everything else.

## Úrthanea (OOR-ha-nya), the Spring Quarter

Held by [[Prince Ivan]]. Crisp, a little wet, and always on the edge of breaking into warmth without ever doing it. Everything here is budding and nothing here fruits.

Towns: **Tiobrán** (TOB-rahn), built around a well that has never run dry, and **Scoithrian** (SKOH-ree-an), which grows cut flowers for the other three quarters.

Villages: Fréimhne (FRAYV-neh), Caoirne (KAR-neh) and Bileanna (BIH-lan-na).

Ivan's long secret romance with [[Hakea]] of Lush is the worst-kept secret in the domain and has never once been said out loud at court.

## Teasanea (TA-sa-nya), the Summer Quarter

Held by [[Princess Esta]]. Hot without relief, bright, and loud. It is the most populous quarter and the one that throws the most parties.

Towns: **Brathlán** (BRAH-lahn), which works metal because it is the only quarter warm enough to do it cheaply, and **Murlanna** (MUR-lan-na) on the great lake, where most of the domain goes to escape the heat of the quarter it is already in.

Villages: Meilgeog (MEL-gog), Beachóg (BA-khohg) and Fionnrach (FONN-rakh).

Esta is seeing [[Eurus]] of [[Ventis]], which is public, encouraged, and regarded in the other quarters as showing off.

## Buainnea (BUN-nya), the Autumn Quarter

Held by [[Princeps Autuun]]. Caught forever in the middle of turning. The leaves are always coming down and the trees are never bare.

Towns: **Lisbhrian** (LISHV-ree-an), where the fallen leaf is gathered and sorted, and **Buainlis** (BUN-lish), which holds the domain's harvest stores and therefore most of its real power.

Villages: Caoirbhne (KARV-neh), Muiscne (MUSHK-neh) and Órbhalla (OHR-val-la).

## Fuairlanea (FUR-la-nya), the Winter Quarter

Held by [[Princess Verna]]. Cold, and snowing without pause. The snow has never stopped and the drifts have had centuries to get where they are.

Towns: **Reodhlain** (ROGH-lan), cut into the ice and mostly below the surface, and **Clúmhrach** (KLOOV-rakh), which keeps the roads open and charges for it.

Villages: Fuairne (FUR-neh), Cloichne (KLOKH-neh) and Oíghne (EEGH-neh).

Verna is betrothed to [[Boreas]] and wants out of it. The quarter knows. Nobody in it will say so where it might be repeated, which in a fey realm is a serious undertaking.

## The Fronts

Where two quarters meet, the weather does not blend. It argues.

The four borders are permanent fronts, each one a line of storm where one season runs into the next. The spring and summer front is thunder. Summer into autumn is wind that strips what it crosses. Autumn into winter is freezing rain. The winter and spring front is fog that nothing travels through willingly.

Crossing a front is the ordinary way to move around the domain, and it is the reason most people here would rather go through Rothánea and come out the other side.

> *I asked in Buainlis why they do not simply let the harvest finish. The answer, given slowly and as though to a child, was that the harvest finishing would mean winter, and winter already exists, and lives next door, and has its own house.*

## Design Notes

The four quarters cannot change, which takes the one thing a season does away from them. Everything the rulers compete over is therefore political rather than natural.

Thera's court at the centre is the only turning ground in the domain, and it is also the only neutral ground. Those are the same fact.

The fronts make travel inside the domain a decision rather than a given, and they give a DM four reliable obstacles that are nobody's fault.

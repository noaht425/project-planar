One of the seven domains of [[Iris]], held by [[Hakea]]. For how names and bargains work in the fey realms, and what is owed under them, see [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]].

## The Domain

Everything in Lush is growing, at every moment, fast enough to watch. Nothing here dies back and nothing sheds, and that is its quarrel with Rot across the realm.

A road walked in the morning is closed by the afternoon. A traveller who sits down to rest for long enough will be grown over where they sit, and the domain does not consider this an attack.

Settlements in Lush are not built so much as maintained. The work of living here is cutting, and everyone does it.

**Méirnesielbha** (MAYR-neh-shil-va), the green forest, is the body of the domain and covers most of it. The canopy is high enough that the ground under it has never been properly surveyed.

**Fréimhedoimhne** (FRAY-veh-dov-neh), the deep root, is the country beneath that, where root systems have grown into caverns large enough to live in. Some people do.

**Tiobrabhar** (TOB-ra-var), the well river, rises in the heart of the domain and runs out in every direction at once, which it has been doing for longer than anyone has had a theory about.

**An Deilce** (DEL-keh), the thorn, is the band of growth on the domain's border facing Rot. It was not planted. It got there on its own and it faces the right way.

## Lainea (LA-nya) Hakea

Hakea's seat is a hall that is also a tree and has been both for as long as anyone remembers. The hall is cut back constantly by people whose whole office is cutting it back.

Hakea holds court as a woodland witch rather than as a monarch. Petitioners are seen in order of arrival, given tea, and told things they did not want to know.

## The Cities

**Scoithlainis** (SKOH-la-nish), the flower city, grows what the rest of the fey realms want and cannot grow. It is the richest place in Lush and the most heavily cut.

**Bileannea** (BIH-lan-nya), the tree seat, is built in the canopy and is not connected to the ground anywhere. Its people have an opinion about the ground.

**Caoirlain** (KAR-lan) handles the fruit, which is the only thing in the domain that is ever allowed to finish.

## Villages

Méirne (MAYR-neh), Céanna (KAYN-na), Fréimhu (FRAY-vuh), Deilcu (DEL-kuh), Caoirlu (KAR-luh), Muiscra (MUSHK-ra), Tiobru (TOB-ruh), Glaisu (GLA-suh), Abharu (A-va-ruh), Scoithu (SKO-huh), Lisbhu (LISH-vuh) and Sielbhu (SHIL-vuh).

Each holds one stretch of road or one clearing against the growth. A village in Lush that stops cutting for a season is gone, and several names on old maps are exactly that.

> *They gave me a cloak in Scoithlainis and told me to shake it out every evening. I forgot once. By morning there was moss in the seams and something flowering in the hood, and I had been asleep for six hours.*

## Hakea

[[Hakea]] is a woman in her middle fifties in appearance, a woodland witch with a staff and a mossy cloak. Her long secret romance with [[Prince Ivan]] of the spring quarter is the thing most people in Iris would name first if asked about her, and neither of them has ever confirmed it.

Her stated ambition is to marry him.

## Design Notes

Lush is the domain where doing nothing is the hazard. Every other realm punishes a wrong action. This one punishes sitting down.

Fruit being the one thing permitted to finish gives Caoirlain its importance and gives the domain a single exception to its own rule, which is where a DM can put something.

The thorn band grew on its own and faces Rot. Nobody planted it and nobody in Lush finds this worth remarking on.

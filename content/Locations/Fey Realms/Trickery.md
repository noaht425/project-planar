One of the seven domains of [[Iris]], held by [[Indri]], or by what has been standing in his place since 3,096 AF. For how names and bargains work in the fey realms, and what is owed under them, see [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]].

## The Domain

Trickery runs on rules. The rules are genuine, they can be worked out, and they are kept to the letter every single time.

What the rules are for is the part that is never what it looks like. A visitor who plays well and wins on the terms offered has usually agreed to something else along the way, and the domain will hold them to that as scrupulously as it held itself to the game.

Nothing here cheats. That is the first thing a mortal gets wrong about the place.

**Gliceaslúbhar** (GLIH-kash-loo-var), the cunning ground, is the open country, and the paths across it are laid out so that every route is shorter than the one a traveller chose.

**Gnúiseánsielbha** (GNOO-shahn-shil-va), the mask forest, is where the domain's older business is done. Everyone in it is wearing a face and the courtesy is not to ask about it.

**Fáillemóra** (FAHL-leh-moh-ra), the great ring, is a fae ring large enough to hold a town inside. A deal struck within it is binding in a way that even the archfey treat carefully.

**An Scáile** (SKAH-leh), the shadow, is the country along the border facing Time. It is the one part of the domain where the rules have been known to be late.

## Lainea (LA-nya) Indri

The seat is a hall where petitions are heard and games are played, and the two are the same thing. A petitioner states a case and is offered a wager on it, and declining the wager is itself an answer.

Indri appears with a pale face, pure black eyes and silver freckles. Whoever is currently appearing that way has held the domain for three thousand years.

## The Cities

**Bréigelainis** (BRAY-geh-la-nish), the lie city, is where contracts are drawn. Its scribes are the finest in the fey realms and are hired by every domain including the ones that distrust them.

**Gnúiselain** (GNOO-sheh-lan), the face city, makes and sells masks. A mask bought here is treated in law as a use-name, which is the kind of thing nobody tells a mortal until afterwards.

**Fólachlain** (FOH-lakh-lan), the secret city, trades in things people would rather not have known. It does not sell secrets, which would be vulgar. It sells the keeping of them.

## Villages

Gliceasu (GLIH-ka-suh), Bréigu (BRAY-guh), Gnúisu (GNOO-suh), Fólu (FOH-luh), Snaoimhu (SNA-vuh), Lóigu (LOH-guh), Fothlaru (FOH-la-ruh), Cluthu (KLUH-huh), Fidiru (FIH-dih-ruh), Fáillu (FAHL-luh), Scáilu (SKAH-luh) and Dóidu (DOH-duh).

Several of them have two names, an older one and a current one, and which is in use depends on who is asking. The maps carry both and do not explain.

> *I won a wager in Bréigelainis, fairly, against a scribe who seemed delighted for me, and was paid exactly what had been staked. The terms also covered how my winnings were to be recorded, and they were recorded by entering me in the city's books as that scribe's apprentice. A small wage has reached me every month since. Nobody there has been able to tell me how to stop it.*

## The Substitution

[[Tiyanak]] killed the original [[Indri]] in 3,096 AF, took his name and most of his memories, and has held this domain ever since. See [[Iris]].

The domain is the reason it has held. A seat where constant lying is expected is the one seat where a liar raises no alarm. Those who have worked it out have tended not to last.

The real Indri escaped the underworld in 700 AM and is loose in [[Arenis]] as a god of vengeance. He wants to remember who he was and he wants Tiyanak killed as publicly as possible. Tiyanak does not know he is out.

## Design Notes

The domain never cheats, and that is what makes it dangerous. A party expecting to be lied to will defend against the wrong thing.

The mask being a use-name in law is the sharpest trap here, since it looks like shopping.

Two names on the villages is the domain in miniature. Both are true, both are current, and which one you use tells everyone something about you.

One of the seven domains of [[Iris]], held by [[Grixt]]. For how names, bargains and debts work in the fey realms, see [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]].

## The Domain

Everything in Rot breaks down and everything that breaks down is used again. Nothing is wasted here and nothing is kept, which is the whole of its quarrel with Lush across the realm and the reason it has no patience with Bone either.

Much of what lives here is several things at once wearing a single shape. This is true of the wildlife, true of a good deal of the population, and true of Grixt.

A visitor's possessions begin going over within a day or so. Food, leather, paper and rope are all on a clock. The locals find complaints about this very funny.

**Lobhralúbhar** (LOV-ra-loo-var), the rotting ground, is the body of the domain, warm and soft underfoot and working constantly.

**Muisceánsielbha** (MUSH-kahn-shil-va), the mushroom forest, is fungal growth at the scale of trees, and it is where most of the domain's people live.

**An Abhar (A-var) Dubh** (DUV), the black river, carries the runoff of the whole domain and is the richest ground in the fey realms along its banks.

**Céanachdoimhne** (KAY-nakh-dov-neh), the deep moss, is older growth at the border facing Lush, where the two processes meet and neither wins.

## Lainea (LA-nya) Grixt

Grixt keeps a seat in the mushroom forest that is grown rather than built and is replaced continually as parts of it go over.

Grixt appears as a short, very old myconid with a mushroom beard and a cloak of moss, and behaves like a kindly old man. What Grixt actually is is a swarm of undead rats, insects and other small animals, called the Lord of Rot, and uses we and us.

Grixt gave [[Orcus]] the power to steal from [[The King of Night]], which is the most consequential thing any archfey has done to the cosmos. Grixt's own ambition is to unseat the Kings of day and night.

## The Cities

**Lobhralainis** (LOV-ra-la-nish), the rot city, processes what the whole domain sends it and sends back what the whole domain uses. It is the busiest place in Rot and it is rebuilt continually.

**Muisceánea** (MUSH-kah-nya), the mushroom seat, is grown entirely from fungal stock and is the largest settlement in the fey realms by population, since nothing in it had to be quarried or felled.

**Dubhlain** (DUV-lan), the black city, sits on the black river where the runoff is richest, and grows food for domains that would rather not know.

## Villages

Lobhru (LOV-ruh), Muiscu (MUSH-kuh), Dubhu (DUH-vuh), Fuithru (FUH-ruh), Cléamu (KLAY-muh), Leamhu (LA-vuh), Beachu (BA-khuh), Uilcu (UL-kuh), Nuaru (NOO-a-ruh), Teallu (TAL-luh), Cáirnu (KAHR-nuh) and Gioru (GO-ruh).

Villages here are not rebuilt so much as regrown, and most of them have been in slightly different places every century. The names stay put even when the villages do not.

> *Everything I owned was quietly going over the entire time I was in Muisceánea and nobody had thought to mention it. When I complained, a man took my notebook, looked at it, and told me it had another nine days in it, which he clearly considered generous.*

## Design Notes

Rot is the only domain that charges a visitor simply for being there. Gear degrades, supplies spoil, and a party staying too long starts losing things, which does the work that a monster would do elsewhere.

Grixt being a swarm wearing the shape of a kindly old man is the domain's own rule applied to its ruler. Everything here is several things in one shape.

Grixt's ambition against the Kings reaches a long way outside this domain, and it is already in motion through [[Orcus]].

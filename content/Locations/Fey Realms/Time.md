One of the seven domains of [[Iris]], held by [[Chronos]]. For how names, bargains and debts work in the fey realms, see [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]].

## The Domain

Time is machinery. The ground, the weather and a fair amount of the wildlife run on mechanisms, and all of it runs exactly as it was built to run and exactly when it is meant to.

Nothing here is broken and nothing is late. The unsettling part is the precision. A visitor who spends a week in the domain starts listening for the one thing that will be off schedule, and the one thing never comes.

Appointments here are binding in the way a bargain is binding elsewhere. Being late to one is an offence with consequences, and the consequences are also on schedule.

## Pratta

[[Pratta]] is the seat of the domain and the home of Chronos.

Inside it stands [[Festivus]], a theme park that Chronos built and still operates, staffed entirely by automata. It runs perfectly. Everyone who visits comes away slightly unsettled and unable to point at the reason.

## The Cities

**Tráthlain** (TRAH-lan) makes the clocks. Every mechanism in the domain is cut, set and regulated here, and the city's authority comes from the fact that the schedule the whole realm runs on is the schedule Tráthlain keeps.

**Gealánea** (GA-lah-nya), the day seat, holds the daylight to its timetable. Dawn and dusk in this domain arrive at an appointed moment, and Gealánea is the office that appoints it.

**Deirinnlain** (DEH-rinn-lan) sits at the permanent dusk line and is where the domain's longer work gets done, since nothing there is interrupted by a scheduled sunrise.

## Villages

Tráthu (TRAH-huh), Gealna (GAL-na), Oíghra (EEGH-ra), Moichne (MOKH-neh), Deirne (DER-neh), Rélna (RAYL-na), Fiorbhna (FORV-na), Lonnrach (LONN-rakh), Glórna (GLOHR-na), Cróinna (KROHN-na), Lúbhna (LOOV-na) and Iarrna (EE-arr-na).

Each exists to keep one mechanism running and each has a scheduled shift that has never been missed. The people in them are not unhappy. They are extremely punctual.

> *My appointment at Tráthlain had been made before I reached the domain, by someone I never identified, and a card with the time on it was waiting for me at the border. I kept it. The tea was already poured when I came in, and the clerk apologised that it had been poured a moment early.*

## Chronos

[[Chronos]] is mechanical, built of clocks and dials, and tricked [[Themis]] into putting a divine spark into an automaton, which produced [[XLZL]].

That is the shape of how Chronos operates. Nothing was stolen, nothing was broken, and an agreement was kept to the letter by a party who knew what the letter would produce.

## Design Notes

Time is built on things working rather than things failing. A domain where nothing goes wrong is harder to be in than one where everything does, because a visitor cannot tell what it wants.

The binding appointment is the domain's version of the fey bargain. A party that would never sign anything can still be held to a time, and most of them will agree to a time without thinking about it.

The one scheduled gap is Scáilne, which winds mechanisms nobody is allowed to see. That is left open for a DM.

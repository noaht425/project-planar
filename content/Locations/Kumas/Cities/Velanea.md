Capital of the [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]], and the seat of Kelvari, who is addressed in [[Low Fae]] as A Chelvari.

## The City

Kumas takes its name from the Low Fae word for a hollow, a sheltered place under a hill. Velanea is built above ground and makes no apology for it.

What it is built to resemble is an archfey's seat. After the split in 105 AM the fae of this kingdom turned back toward [[Iris]], and the capital they raised afterwards is a fey court done in mortal materials: long low halls, gardens worked into the architecture rather than set beside it, water running through rooms, and almost no straight lines at the level of a street.

There is no wall. There are approaches, which are long, planted, and arranged so that a visitor is seen for some time before arriving.

## The Court

The manners are a fey court in miniature and are taken seriously. Precedence is intricate, address is exact, and a mortal visitor will get at least one thing wrong on the way in.

None of it is malicious. The court is pleased to be imitating Iris and is pleased to explain itself, at length, to anybody who asks politely.

## The Register

[[Low Fae]] has no word for thanks. The nearest phrase means that the speaker owes something, and it is meant literally.

Small obligations are remembered rather than recorded, which is how most of daily life runs. Above a certain size a debt is registered, and the register is kept here. The line between the two is not fixed by any rule and is argued about constantly, since a debt below the line is a social matter and a debt above it is enforceable.

Brokers work the difference. [[Bryla]] keeps her ledger in Velanea and deals in the kind of small fae debt that nobody has bothered to register, which is a large and entirely legal trade.

> *I watched two people in the register office argue for most of an afternoon about whether what lay between them was large enough to write down. He wanted it registered. She did not. The clerk listened to both of them with enormous patience and then declined to decide, which I was told is what the office is for.*

## Tama

Kumas is campaigning to have [[Kingdom of Tama|Tama]] seated at the [[Panarex]], on the grounds that both kingdoms came from somewhere else and should recognise each other. The campaign is run out of Velanea and has been for a long time.

## Design Notes

Velanea is a likeable capital with a trap in it. Everyone is charming, everything is explained, and a polite party accumulates obligations all day without being deceived about anything.

The register is where a campaign's debts become mechanical. Below the line is roleplay. Above it is enforceable.

## Open

- Where the line between remembered and registered debt actually falls is unwritten, which is the point.
- Whether Kelvari holds the throne by descent, election, or something else is unwritten.
- What the register does with a debt owed to something in [[Iris]] is unwritten.

Tithra (TITH-ra) is the highest town in [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]], its castles built slender and tall on the mountain ridges, and it is where the fae of Kumas follow the court of [[Iris]] most closely.

The fashions of Iris reach Tithra late and stay long after Iris has dropped them.

> *A tailor in Tithra showed me a sleeve that has not been worn in Iris since before Thalamas split, and told me it was the latest thing.*

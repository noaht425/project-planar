Tithra (TITH-ra) is the highest town in [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]], its castles built slender and tall on the mountain ridges. It is where the fae of Kumas follow the court of [[Iris]] most closely.

The fashions of Iris reach Tithra late and stay long after Iris has dropped them.

## The Ridges

The castles are built along the tops of the ridges rather than on the slopes, slender because there is no width to build on. Bridges and cut stairs connect them. Nothing in Tithra is more than a few rooms wide and several of its halls are a single room repeated upward.

The position is defensible and was not chosen for defence. It was chosen for the view toward the high passes, which is the direction the town considers worth looking.

## The Court Habit

Tithra has the kingdom's oldest ties to the fey realms. News of the archfey's courts reaches it before it reaches [[Velanea]], usually through travellers coming over the passes rather than through anything official.

What the town does with the news is imitate it. Dress, address, table manners, and the forms of a greeting in Tithra are all modelled on Iris, kept with great care, and about a generation behind.

> *A tailor in Tithra showed me a sleeve that has not been worn in Iris since before Thalamas split, and told me it was the latest thing.*

Nobody in Tithra regards this as a failure. The town's position is that Iris changes its mind constantly and that somebody ought to keep the good versions.

## The Passes

The high passes above the town are the practical reason anyone comes here. They are the route between Kumas and the kingdoms beyond it, shut for a good part of the year. Tithra takes the toll and provides the guides.

## The Winter

For the months the passes are shut, Tithra is as cut off as anywhere in Kumas and considerably colder. The castles are provisioned for it, and the bridges between ridges are the first thing to ice. So the town's indoor season is when most of its imitation of Iris actually gets practised.

## Design Notes

Tithra is where a party learns fey manners slightly wrong, which is a useful thing to let them do before they reach [[Iris]] and find out.

The lag is a running joke that is also a real hazard. A courtesy that is a generation out of date is noticed by anything from the fey realms.

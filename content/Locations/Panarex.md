The temple on [[The Nexus]] where the rulers of the kingdoms meet, and the council that meets in it. Circular, built of marble, obsidian, and gold.

## The Hall

One round room with a ring of chairs in it and no head of the table, which was the condition on which the eight original kingdoms agreed to sit at all. The floor is obsidian and the ring of chairs stands on marble set into it.

There are nine chairs. There were eight when the hall was built.

Wine comes from Toscal in [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]], which has supplied the hall for longer than anyone has questioned, and the vintners there are never told which year is poured.

[[Kingdom of Tama|Tama]] has no seat and has never been invited to a session.

The three kingdoms of the [[Trois Re]] sit together and have never once voted differently. Counting them as one bloc and Sylfaen with Krotaphos as another, five of the nine chairs are held by two groups.

## How a Vote Works

A vote carries on a majority of the chairs in the room, and a kingdom that was not there is bound by it anyway. That rule is the reason attendance is treated as seriously as it is, and the reason a ruler who cannot travel sends someone who can.

The hall has no means of enforcement. Nothing in the Panarex can compel a kingdom to do what it voted to do, so a vote is a statement of where the kingdoms stand and a record of who is in breach if they do not follow it. What is done about a breach is decided by the other kingdoms, in the hall, by another vote.

A session binds the absent only when two thirds of the chairs are filled, which is six of the nine.

## The Ninth Chair

[[Kingdom of Thalamas|Thalamas]] held one of the original eight seats. When it broke apart in 105 AM, [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] and [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]] both claimed the chair as the successor kingdom, and both sent a ruler to sit in it.

The dispute ran for years. Sessions were held with the chair empty, since neither would accept the other in it and the hall would not seat a ruler on the floor. It ended with a ninth chair, made and set in the ring, and the two kingdoms drew lots for which of them took the new one.

The ninth chair does not match the other eight. The marble is from a different cut and it has been replaced twice.

## Tama and the Last Session

The most recent session was called by the kingdoms of the western and southern continents without the four of the eastern continent: [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]], [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]], [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]], and [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]].

Five chairs is not six, so the five present seated Tama to make up the number and took the vote. Tama has wanted a seat for five hundred years and got one for an afternoon, on the arithmetic.

The vote went against sending aid. The four kingdoms that were not there are bound by it, and whether Tama's chair counted toward the six is the argument they have.

> *The ninth chair is the only thing in that hall anybody will talk about. A keeper walked me round the ring and named the eight for me, and then stood on the far side of the ninth and told me which of the two kingdoms had drawn it, and how. He would not tell me which way the lots had been weighted. He said that part is not written down anywhere and that he is not the one who is going to write it.*

## Design Notes

The binding rule on absent kingdoms is what makes the Panarex a place a campaign can happen. A session held while four kingdoms are elsewhere is not a procedural matter. It is an act against them, and it has already happened.

Two blocs holding five of the nine chairs means the hall has a working majority that does not need anyone else, and the kingdoms outside it know the arithmetic as well as anyone.

Seating Tama to reach six is the kind of expedient that creates a precedent nobody intended. Tama now has an afternoon of being a seated kingdom to point at.

## Open

- The two thirds threshold is a reading that makes the last session work as recorded. The real number is Noah's to set.
- When the ninth chair was added, and how the lots were drawn, is unwritten.
- What the hall does with a kingdom in breach of a vote is unwritten beyond that it votes again.
- Whether Tama's vote at the last session stands is the open question of the current campaign.

## The City

Panthea is the capital of [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]], a city of marble and pale stone on dry, open country.

Temple Hill rises in the middle of it, with a temple to each of the six Judgements as they were before the Division. The Omens worship both halves of each Judgement there, and since 702 AM they have done it quietly.

> *Each of the six temples on Temple Hill has two doors, and the Omens use both. A priest told me that before this year the doors were marked Good and Ill, and that after the arrests began the marks were painted out, so that nobody watching from the street could tell which door an Omen had used.*

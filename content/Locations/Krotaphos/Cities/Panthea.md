## The City

Panthea is the capital of [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]], a city of marble and pale stone on dry, open country.

Temple Hill rises in the middle of it, with twelve temples, one to each of the [[Archangels]] and [[Archdevils]]. The temples stand in pairs, each archangel's facing the archdevil who was the other half of the same Judgement before the Division.

> *The temples on Temple Hill stand in pairs, each across a narrow path from the one that used to be its other half. Most Omens who climb the hill go into both. I asked a priest whether the archangels and archdevils minded. She said that none of them had ever come to look.*

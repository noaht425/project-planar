## The City

Panthea is the capital of [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]], a city of marble and pale stone on dry, open country. It is larger than the kingdom now needs, which is true of most things in Krotaphos.

Temple Hill rises in the middle of it, with twelve temples, one to each of the [[Archangels]] and [[Archdevils]]. The temples stand in pairs, each archangel's facing the archdevil who was the other half of the same Judgement before the Division.

## The Line

[[Iudimian]] may be spoken in public only in religious settings, and in Panthea the line is drawn at the bottom of the Hill steps.

Above it the language is lawful. Below it, it is not. The boundary is marked in the paving and everybody knows where it runs, so a conversation walking down off the Hill changes language partway without either speaker breaking stride. Children learn the switch before they learn why it exists.

It is not a courtesy and it is not symbolic. A prosecution is possible and a few are brought each year, usually against somebody the kingdom wanted to prosecute for something else.

## The Palace

There was a palace built for two kings. It had doubled apartments, a doubled household, and one room where both thrones stood, and that room was where the kingdom was actually run.

The terms of 427 AM turned it into a museum. Not closed, not demolished, but converted, by requirement, into a building that the public pays to walk through. The throne room is a room with two chairs in it and a label.

So the kings live in their own houses now. Each keeps a household, and those households do the work a court used to do. The practical consequence is that a steward in Panthea can be a more useful person to know than a minister, and both kings' staff understand this perfectly.

## The University

[[The University of Divine Judgement]] stands here, founded by [[Verita]] with the help of the other archangels. The archdevils bought in later.

The University is a religious setting in law. Iudimian is therefore lawful inside it, which makes the building the one place in the kingdom where the language can be used for something other than worship. Scholarship in Iudimian happens there because it cannot happen anywhere else.

> *The temples on Temple Hill stand in pairs, each across a narrow path from the one that used to be its other half. Most Omens who climb the hill go into both. I asked a priest whether the archangels and archdevils minded. She said that none of them had ever come to look.*

## Design Notes

The line at the foot of the steps is the best single scene in Krotaphos. A party watching a city change language on a staircase has understood the kingdom without being told anything.

The museum is the humiliation that the defeated kingdom has to sell tickets to. It is also a usable location, since the building still has the rooms and the passages a government used, and nobody lives there now.

The university in [[Panthea]], capital of the [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]]. It trains the clergy of all twelve of [[Iudis]] in one building, and it certifies the enforcers through whom the kingdom exercises its authority over the will of the heavens and the hells.

Founded by [[Verita]] with the help of the other [[Archangels|archangels]], and eventually with the help of the [[Archdevils|archdevils]].

## The Founding

Verita built it to teach what judgement is and how it works, which is the half of [[Sylas]] that Verita kept. The other five archangels came in early.

The archdevils let it run for about ten years and then wanted in, and they paid their way. Their reasoning was practical. Clergy of the archdevils are as ordinary in the mortal world as clergy of the archangels, those clergy were being trained somewhere, and the archangels were otherwise going to own the definition of the subject. Both sides have funded it ever since and neither has ever held it outright.

## What It Is For

Two things, and the University is careful about the order it names them in.

The first is clergy. Every priest, cleric, and paladin of the twelve who wants formal training comes here, and the great majority do. A student of Temperance and a student of Indulgence sit the same examinations in the same room, taught by the same faculty, which is the condition the archdevils bought.

The second is enforcement. Krotaphos holds the authority to enforce the will of the heavens and the hells in the mortal world, and the University is where that authority is turned into people. An enforcer carries a certificate issued here. Inside Krotaphos it has the force of law. Outside Krotaphos it had real weight until 427 AM, and since the defeat it is honoured as a courtesy where it is honoured at all.

Scholarship is the third thing it does and the one it is least organised about. The study of judgement itself goes on here, which means the sorting at [[Liminia]], the work of [[Bis]] and [[Seisear]], the backlog, and the objections [[Themis]] has been sending for seven centuries. The scholars are the smallest faculty and have the best library.

## The Three Schools

Students are taught by trade rather than by patron. A student takes clergy, enforcement, or scholarship, and the three schools keep separate buildings, separate deans, and separate reputations.

**Clergy** is the largest by a long way and the one the kingdom cares about. Its dean is **Hesper Ofala**, an Ill Omen of Lilien's house, who has held the post for thirty years and is the reason the alternating rows in the examination hall are still enforced to the inch.

**Enforcement** trains in law, in combat, and in the procedure of the certificate. Its graduates used to be sent across the continents and now mostly work inside the circle of Krotaphos. Its dean is **Varo Threnn**, a Good Omen of Themis's house, who signs every certificate personally and has refused to sign eleven of them.

**Scholarship** takes few students, produces the work the rest of the world actually cites, and is chronically short of money. Its dean is **Ammet Sarei**, of Alter, who worships nothing anyone has established and has run the faculty for nine years on money she raises herself.

## The Thirteen Houses

Where a student lives and who a student answers to is a separate matter from what they study. The University keeps houses, and a house is a patron.

There are twelve houses for the twelve. There is also a thirteenth, the **Alter house**, which takes anyone whose devotion is to something other than an archangel or an archdevil: the dragons, the archfey, the ascendant gods, the winds, and whatever else a student arrives carrying. It was added long after the first twelve. The twelve pretend it is not there, and in a kingdom that counts carefully, a thirteenth of anything is noticed.

House matters more than school in a student's daily life. Houses eat together, compete against each other, hold their own services, and keep rivalries that outlast the people in them. Baator's two halves have the oldest and worst of those rivalries, since Temperance and Indulgence came out of one house and have never been able to leave each other alone.

A house is run by its head, who is faculty, and by its senior students, who are not.

## Getting a House

Most students arrive with a patron already. Krotaphos families are devoted to particular members of the twelve, often for centuries, and a child is entered under the house the family keeps.

The patrons are not bound by any of that. A patron may reject a student it does not want and claim one it does, at any point and without explanation, which produces a shuffle during most first years. Later moves happen and are rare enough to be a scandal when they do. A student rejected by a patron and claimed by nobody goes to Alter, and Alter takes them without comment.

## The Chancellor

The University is run by a mortal chancellor whom both sides tolerate, which is the only arrangement either side will accept. The chancellor answers to twelve patrons and to the two kings, in that order, and the office has no power that the funding cannot overrule.

By convention the chancellor has no patron at all and is drawn from the Alter house, since an unclaimed candidate is the only one nobody can object to on principle. The office has therefore always come out of the house the twelve pretend not to see.

The present chancellor is **Yaveth Serilin**, an elf, which goes further than the convention asks. An Omen without a patron is still an Omen, raised in the kingdom of the Omens and related to half of it. An elf out of [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] has no stake in Krotaphos at all, which is the argument that got her the post and the thing both sides like least about her.

## The Faculty

The faculty is mortal. Neither side seconds angels or devils to teach here, and the University holds the line on that, because a faculty with divine members on it would be a faculty with a side.

The twelve visit. A visit is announced, prepared for, and talked about for a year afterwards, and a patron visiting its own house will examine the students in it personally. Souls in the heavens and the hells are occasionally brought in to be questioned by the scholars, under conditions the faculty negotiates each time.

## The Language

[[Iudimian]] may be spoken in public in Krotaphos only in religious settings. The University is a religious setting, so the whole institution is one of the few places in the kingdom where the language is used openly, in lectures, in examinations, and in the corridors.

Students from other kingdoms arrive with no Iudimian and are expected to have it by the end of the first year. The enforcement school examines in it on purpose.

## After the War

Krotaphos lost its war with [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] in 427 AM, was cut down to a circle, and has had authority without power since.

The University came through it better than the kingdom did. The twelve kept funding it while the crown shrank, it is still the best in its field, and it still draws students from every kingdom. For most of the world it is the only reason to go to Krotaphos at all, which the kingdom knows and does not enjoy.

> *I was shown the examination hall. It seats four hundred and is used twice a year. The invigilator told me that the clergy of the heavens and the clergy of the hells sit it alternately by row, and have since the hall was built, so that nobody can claim a side was favoured by where it was put. I asked who decided the arrangement. He said it had been agreed by all twelve. It is the only thing he knew of that all twelve had ever agreed on.*

## Design Notes

The University is the setting's one institution where both sides of the Division are in a room together every day and nothing is on fire. That is what makes it worth a campaign. The arrangement is old, it works, and everyone in it has a patron who could pull them out of it.

The houses do the social work. A party at the University is placed by patron before anyone asks what they study, and the thirteenth house gives a DM somewhere to put a player whose devotion is to anyone else in the setting.

The chancellor having no patron is the structural joke of the place. The only person both sides trust is the one nobody in the twelve wanted.

Faculty names are deliberately left thin here, so that a DM can staff it to suit the campaign.

## DM Notes

[[Archon]] is further into Krotaphos than into any other kingdom, and the University is split. Part of the faculty is his and part is not, and neither part has worked out which is which. There is no roster of who is compromised, by design. Archon works by making himself necessary and by being honest with his marks, so a professor who is his is still giving good advice and still believes the advice is their own.

The kings are further gone than the University is. A party that works out what is happening in Panthea will reach the University before it reaches the crown.

Bacta is the mountain town of [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]], on the pass into the high country.

## The Pass

The pass is the only practical way into the high country and it is hard going on foot. It is steep, it is long, and the weather on it changes faster than anybody can dress for.

Bacta sits at the near end. Everything the town does is arranged around one fact, which is that travellers arrive wanting to be on the other side and do not want to walk.

## The Carry

The giants of Bacta carry travellers over the pass for a fee, and the carry is a licensed trade.

It is organised the way such things are organised. The licences are limited and they are valuable. There is a queue at the near end and a queue at the far end, and a carrier takes the traveller at the head of the line rather than the one who looks richest. Fares are set and posted. A carrier who overcharges can be reported, and is.

A licence usually passes within a family, which newer carriers complain about and older ones consider the natural order. Several carriers are known by name for a day's walk in every direction. A few are institutions, and at least one of them will tell you so.

Orcs in Bacta mostly handle what comes with the trade rather than the trade itself: the inns, the stabling, and the forwarding of goods that are not going over on somebody's shoulders. Nobody in town calls that a division, and everybody in town can describe it.

## The High Country

Over the pass is Oinarria's own mountain country, and the kingdom has never cut it. There is stone up there that [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] would buy and Oinarria will not sell, which is most of why [[Darasil]] does the cutting instead.

So the traffic over the pass is people and small freight rather than quarry. Bacta is a town on the road to somewhere that has deliberately not been developed.

> *The giants of Bacta carry travellers over the pass for a fee. Mine charged by the hour and walked slowly.*

## Design Notes

The carry is a convenience and a vulnerability at once. A party on somebody's shoulders is not in control of where it is going, and the fare is cheap enough that taking it is the obvious choice.

Posted fares and a licence queue also make the trade a place to get information. The carriers see everyone who goes over.

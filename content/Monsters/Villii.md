Trickster Spirits created by [[Indri]]

Disguises itself as other beings (magical animals and helpful spirits like unicorns) and then fights as twisted/skeletal versions of the thing it disguised as.

Appear as bone thin black beings with spikes instead of arms and legs.

Know that Tiyanak is Indri but don't care and won't tell anyone

> *A hedge healer in Kumas told me she had spent an afternoon working on a unicorn with a broken leg. Every spell she put into it went in and did nothing. She came away tired in a way she has not been tired since. She did not see what it was until it stood up. She has not treated an animal she did not already know by sight since.*

## Stat block

*Medium Fey*

**Armor Class:** 14 (unnatural agility)

**Hit Points:** 52 (7d8 + 21)

**Speed:** 30 ft., fly 35 ft. (hover)

**Damage Resistances:** necrotic, poison

**Skills:** Deception +6, Stealth +7

**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10

**Languages:** Common, [[High Fae]], [[Infernal]]

**Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Traits

**Heal Steal.** Once per turn when the Villius hits a creature with an attack, that creature must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be cursed for 1 minute. The next time the cursed creature would regain hit points, it regains none; instead the Villius regains that many hit points. The curse then ends. A creature can carry only one Heal Steal curse at a time.

**Twisted Guise.** While transformed by Form Steal, the Villius's attacks deal an extra 5 (2d4) necrotic damage (included below), and any creature more than 10 feet away perceives it as a wounded-but-living version of the chosen creature. A creature that succeeds on a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check, or that sees the Villi lose all its temporary hit points, perceives its true corrupted form from then on.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Villius makes two Claw attacks.

**Form Steal.** The Villi can use its action to polymorph into a non-humanoid Beast, Monstrosity, Celestial, or Fey of CR 5 or lower it has seen, or back into its true form. Its statistics, except its Intelligence and its Heal Steal trait, are replaced by the chosen form's. Any damage it deals in that form is necrotic instead of the normal type, and the form appears skeletal and rotted to any creature that succeeds on a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check. It reverts to its true form when it drops to 0 hit points or is incapacitated; excess damage carries over.

**Claw.** *Melee Attack* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage, plus 5 (2d4) necrotic damage while transformed (see Twisted Guise).

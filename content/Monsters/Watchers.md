Born of relations between powerful fey and powerful fiends. Appear almost as barren poplar trees, tall, thin, white, lots of "branches" with two piercing golden eyes.

> *There are dead white poplars in Iris, and there are white poplars that are not trees. I have never once been able to tell which I was looking at until it blinked.*

## Stat block

Medium Fey, Fiend, neutral evil

**Armor Class:** 15 (natural agility)


**Hit Points:** 36 (8d8)

**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Vulnerabilities:** fire

**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14

**Skills:** Deception +3, Perception +4, Stealth +7

**Languages:** Common, [[High Fae]], [[Infernal]]

**Challenge:** 3 (700 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Traits

**Hidden Form.** While the watcher remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a dead white tree. A creature can discern its true nature with a successful DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check, or automatically once the watcher moves or attacks.

**Ambusher.** In the first round of a combat, the watcher has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn yet. Any hit the watcher scores against a creature that is surprised or *transfixed* is a critical hit.

**Ubroken Gaze.** When a creature that can see the watcher's eyes starts its turn within 30 feet of it, the watcher (if not incapacitated) can force that creature to make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature is transfixed until the end of its next turn: its speed is 0, it can't take reactions, and it can't willingly look away from the watcher. On a success, the creature is immune to this watcher's gaze for 24 hours.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The watcher makes two Raking Branch attacks.

**Raking Branch.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.

Withering Ambush (1/Turn). When the watcher hits a creature that is surprised or transfixed, or when it has advantage on the attack roll, that attack deals an extra 9 (2d8) necrotic damage.

### Bonus Actions

**Vanish.** The watcher takes the Hide action.

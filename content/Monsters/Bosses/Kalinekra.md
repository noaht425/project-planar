Minor fey lord who was banished to the mortal realm.

In a relationship with [[Chore]]

Lives in the ocean between [[Kingdom of Kumas]] and [[Kingdom of Geno]].

"She is such a powerful necromancer and enchantress that your body is trying to kill itself so that it might become a solider in her army of the dead"

> *The crew that carried me from Fylwa round to Geno went a long way south to do it, which cost three days, and the master would not discuss why in front of his people. Privately he told me that his grandfather had taken the short way once, that this is the whole of the reason, and that the fee was the fee.*

## Stat Block

Medium Fey (Fey Lord), neutral evil

**Armor Class:** 18 (natural armor)

**Hit Points:** 230 (27d8 + 108)

**Speed:** 20 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover), swim 50 ft.

**Saving Throws:** Con +10, Wis +11, Cha +11

**Skills:** Arcana +10, Deception +11, Perception +11, Performance +11

**Damage Resistances:** cold, necrotic, poison; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Condition Immunities:** charmed, frightened, poisoned

**Senses darkvision:** 120 ft., passive Perception 21

**Languages:** Common, [[High Fae]]

**Challenge:** 17 (18,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus:** +6

### Traits

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Kalinekra fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

**Amphibious.** Kalinekra can breathe air and water.

**Magic Resistance.** Kalinekra has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Grave Tide.** When a humanoid dies within 60 feet of Kalinekra while she is singing, it rises at the start of her next turn as a Drowned Blade under her control (counts against the maximum in Servile Prey). It reverts to a corpse if Kalinekra dies or is targeted by dispel evil and good. (DM's option to exclude player characters.)

**Innate Spellcasting.** Kalinekra's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 19, +11 to hit with spell attacks). She casts the following spells, requiring no material components:

At will: chill touch (as a 17th-level caster: 3d8), toll the dead, minor illusion
3/day each: *bestow curse, fear, hold monster, phantasmal killer*
2/day each: *animate dead, blight*
1/day each: *circle of death, dominate monster, finger of death*

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Kalinekra makes three Spectral Talon attacks, or casts one spell.

**Spectral Talon.** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (3d6 + 4) slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage.

**Luring Song.** Kalinekra sings a magical melody. Every humanoid and giant within 300 feet that can hear the song must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed until the song ends. Kalinekra must use a bonus action on her later turns to continue singing, and can stop at any time; the song ends if she is incapacitated.

While charmed this way, a target is incapacitated, ignores other sirens' songs, and takes 7 (2d6) necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns. If it is more than 5 feet from Kalinekra it must move toward her by the most direct route on its turn, not avoiding opportunity attacks. Before entering damaging terrain, and whenever it takes damage from a source other than Kalinekra or this song, it can repeat the save. It also repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. A creature that succeeds is immune to Kalinekra's song for 24 hours.

### Legendary Actions

Kalinekra can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below; only one can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. She regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

**Talon Strike (1 action).** Kalinekra makes one Spectral Talon attack.

**Withering Word (2 actions).** Kalinekra casts toll the dead or chill touch.

**Siren's Pull (2 actions).** One creature within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 20 feet toward Kalinekra and knocked prone.

**Raise the Drowned (2 actions).** One Drowned creature within 60 feet that Kalinekra can see moves up to its speed and makes one weapon attack.

### Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing ties), Kalinekra takes a lair action; she can't use the same one two rounds in a row.

**Servile Prey.** If Kalinekra has sung her Luring Song since the end of her last turn, 2d4 [Drowned Blades](https://5e.tools/bestiary/drowned-blade-gos.html) and 1d4 [Drowned Ascetics](https://5e.tools/bestiary/drowned-ascetic-gos.html) claw up out of the water within 60 feet of her, to a maximum of 8 Drowned Blades and 4 Drowned Ascetics under her control at once.

**Undertow.** Water surges in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within 120 feet. Each creature there must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be pulled 15 feet toward the center and restrained until the end of their next turn.

**Deadened Depths.** Until the end of Kalinekra's next turn, nonmagical light is snuffed within 60 feet of a point Kalinekra chooses, and that area is difficult terrain for creatures that aren't hers.

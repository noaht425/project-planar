## Statblock

*Huge dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 17 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 370 (16d12+266)

**Speed.** 40 ft., fly 90 ft., climb 40 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 18 (+4), DEX 15 (+2), CON 20 (+5), INT 13 (+1), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 16 (+3)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +7, CON +10, WIS +7, CHA +8

**Damage Resistances.** poison; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** acid

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, frightened

**Senses.** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 12

**Languages.** —

**Challenge.** 13 (10,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Amol's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Amol's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Unbound.** Amol's Spawn's body is a living solvent: the forcecage or maze spell (or a similar effect) ends on them at the start of their next turn instead of its normal duration, as they liquefy free of it.

**Dissolving Strikes.** When Amol's Spawn hits a creature with a melee attack, that creature's AC is reduced by 1 until repaired or restored (min AC 10).

**Corrosive Form.** A creature that hits Amol's Spawn with a melee attack takes 3 (1d6) acid damage.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Amol's Spawn makes three attacks: one Bite and two Claws. They can replace one Claw with Corrosive Tail.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8+4) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) acid damage.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6+4) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage.

**Corrosive Tail.** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6+4) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 13). While grappled this way, the target takes 3 (1d6) acid damage at the start of each of its turns and is subject to Dissolving Strikes each turn it remains held.

**Etch.** Amol's Spawn targets one creature within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or gain vulnerability to acid damage and take 4 (1d8) acid damage whenever it takes damage of any other type, until the effect ends (the effect lasts until the encounter ends or is removed by a spell such as greater restoration).

**Acid Breath.** Amol's Spawn exhales acid in a 60-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 33 (6d10) acid damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success. A creature that fails also has its armor corroded, reducing its AC by 1 until repaired.

**Slough.** Amol's Spawn can use this action only while at half their hit points or fewer. They release a wave of caustic fluid in a 15-foot radius. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) acid damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success. Amol's Spawn then ends all conditions on themself, and their AC returns to 17 if it had been reduced.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Amol's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Acid Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

### Legendary Actions

Amol's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Amol's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Rake (Costs 1 Action).** Amol's Spawn makes one Claw attack.

**Solvent Body (Costs 1 Action).** Amol's Spawn becomes an immune spray of acid and repositions up to 30 feet. This ends any grappled, restrained, or similar confining effect on them.

**Spit Acid (Costs 1 Action).** Ranged Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, range 40 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d8) acid damage.

**Melt (Costs 2 Actions).** Amol's Spawn targets one creature within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 9 (2d8) acid damage, drop one item it's holding, and be unable to take reactions until the end of its next turn.

Lair Actions

None.

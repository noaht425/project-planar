## Statblock

*Huge dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 18 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 279 (16d12+175)

**Speed.** 60 ft., fly 120 ft., climb 60 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 18 (+4), DEX 15 (+2), CON 20 (+5), INT 24 (+7), WIS 20 (+5), CHA 19 (+4)

**Saving Throws.** CON +9, INT +11, WIS +9, CHA +8

**Damage Resistances.** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** psychic

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, frightened, paralyzed

**Senses.** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 15

**Languages.** —

**Challenge.** 11 (7,200 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +4

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Mens's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Mens's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Impeccable Awareness.** Mens's Spawn knows the location and Intelligence score of every creature within 500 feet of them that has an Intelligence score of 4 or higher. A mind blank or nondetection effect on a creature blocks this.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Mens's Spawn uses Frightful Presence. They then make three attacks: one Bite, one Claw, and one Mind Rend, though they can't use Mind Rend more than once this way.

**Mind Rend.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, range 50 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (3d8+7) psychic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Intelligence saving throw or its Intelligence or Wisdom score (the target's choice) drops by 1.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8+4) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) psychic damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is grappled (escape DC 13) and takes 5 (1d6+2) psychic damage at the start of each of its turns for as long as it remains grappled this way.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6+4) slashing damage.

**Extract Brain.** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one incapacitated creature grappled by Mens's Spawn. Hit: 33 (6d10) piercing damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target dies and its brain is destroyed.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of Mens's Spawn's choice within 90 feet of them and aware of them must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the start of Mens's Spawn's next turn.

**Cerebral Overload.** Mens's Spawn unleashes a psychic blast in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Intelligence saving throw, taking 31 (7d8) psychic damage and becoming stunned on a failure, or half as much damage on a success. A stunned target repeats the saving throw at the start of each of its turns, ending the stun on itself on a success.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Mens's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Cerebral Overload immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

### Legendary Actions

Mens's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Mens's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Lunging Swipe (Costs 1 Action).** Mens's Spawn moves up to 15 feet, then makes one Claw attack.

**Rend (Costs 1 Action).** Mens's Spawn uses Mind Rend.

**Fold Space (Costs 1 Action).** Mens's Spawn teleports up to 40 feet to an unoccupied space they can see. This ends any restrained or similar confining effect on them.

**Paralyzing Scream (Costs 2 Actions).** Each creature within 15 feet of Mens's Spawn must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 16 (2d10+5) psychic damage, become paralyzed until the end of its next turn, and lose concentration on any spell it's concentrating on.

Lair Actions

None.

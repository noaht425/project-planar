## Statblock

*Gargantuan dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 19 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 452 (18d12+335)

**Speed.** 30 ft., fly 60 ft., swim 40 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 20 (+5), DEX 14 (+2), CON 22 (+6), INT 14 (+2), WIS 16 (+3), CHA 18 (+4)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +7, CON +11, WIS +8, CHA +9

**Damage Resistances.** acid, necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** poison

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, frightened, poisoned

**Senses.** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 13

**Languages.** —

**Challenge.** 13 (10,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Okzu's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Okzu's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Unbound.** As a gaseous lord, Okzu's Spawn seeps free of the forcecage, maze, or imprisonment spell (or a similar effect); the effect ends on them at the start of their next turn instead of its normal duration.

**Shroud of Miasma.** A 15-foot radius around Okzu's Spawn is heavily obscured fog to everyone else (Okzu's Spawn ignores it). A creature that starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or take 9 (2d8) poison damage and be poisoned until the start of its next turn. A wind of moderate or greater speed disperses the fog for 1 round.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Okzu's Spawn uses Withering Presence. They then make three attacks: one Bite and two Claws. They can replace one Claw with Tail Slam.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8+5) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 16). While grappled this way, the target can't regain hit points.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6+5) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) acid damage.

**Tail Slam.** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Withering Presence.** Each creature within 90 feet of Okzu's Spawn must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened and poisoned until the start of Okzu's Spawn's next turn.

**Contagion.** Okzu's Spawn targets one creature within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or contract a wasting sickness for 1 minute: it takes 9 (2d8) poison damage at the start of each of its turns, can't regain hit points, and has disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the sickness on itself on a success.

**Plague Wind Breath.** Okzu's Spawn exhales poisonous gas in a 55-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) poison damage and becoming poisoned for 1 minute on a failure, or half as much damage and no condition on a success. A poisoned target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the poisoned condition on itself on a success.

**Dissolve.** Okzu's Spawn causes flesh and stone to melt away in a 15-foot-radius sphere within 90 feet of them. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) acid damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Okzu's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Plague Wind Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

### Legendary Actions

Okzu's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Okzu's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Tail (Costs 1 Action).** Okzu's Spawn uses Tail Slam.

**Billow (Costs 1 Action).** Okzu's Spawn's Shroud of Miasma expands to a 30-foot radius until the start of their next turn. A creature that enters the expanded area or starts its turn there must make the Shroud's saving throw.

**Caustic Spray (Costs 1 Action).** Okzu's Spawn targets one creature within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) acid damage and have its armor corroded, reducing its AC by 1 (this can stack, to a minimum AC of 10) until the armor is repaired.

**Drag Under (Costs 2 Actions).** Okzu's Spawn targets one creature grappled by them or within 15 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 14 (3d8) poison damage and be blinded until the end of its next turn.

Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Okzu's Spawn takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; they can't use the same effect two rounds in a row.

**Rising Miasma.** Noxious gas fills a 30-foot-radius sphere at a point Okzu's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them until initiative count 20 on the next round. The area is heavily obscured. Each creature that starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 9 (2d8) poison damage and be poisoned until initiative count 20 on the next round.

**Caustic Downpour.** Acidic rain falls in a 20-foot-radius cylinder at a point Okzu's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) acid damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. A creature that fails also has its armor corroded, reducing its AC by 1 until repaired.

**Sump.** A 15-foot-radius patch of toxic sludge appears at a point Okzu's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them, and is difficult terrain. A creature that starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or sink in and become restrained (escape DC 17) until initiative count 20 on the next round.

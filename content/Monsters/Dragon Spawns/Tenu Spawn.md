## Statblock

*Gargantuan dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 22 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 237 (18d12+120)

**Speed.** 40 ft., fly 60 ft., burrow 40 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 23 (+6), DEX 10 (+0), CON 22 (+6), INT 16 (+3), WIS 13 (+1), CHA 14 (+2)

**Saving Throws.** STR +11, DEX +5, CON +11, INT +8, WIS +6, CHA +7

**Damage Resistances.** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** poison

**Condition Immunities.** exhaustion, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, unconscious

**Senses.** tremorsense 60 ft., darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 11

**Languages.** —

**Challenge.** 13 (10,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Tenu's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Tenu's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Unbound.** Tenu's Spawn can sink away from the forcecage, maze, or imprisonment spell (or a similar effect) using Earth Glide; the effect ends on them at the start of their next turn instead of its normal duration, and they reemerge from the ground nearby.

**Earth Glide.** Burrows through nonmagical earth/stone without disturbing it.

**False Appearance.** While Tenu's Spawn remains motionless, they are indistinguishable from a natural formation of stone.

**Siege Monster.** Tenu's Spawn deals double damage to objects and structures.

**Catastrophic Aura.** A non-flying creature that ends its turn within 10 feet of Tenu's Spawn must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or fall prone. A flying creature there can't rise more than 20 feet off the ground.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Tenu's Spawn makes three attacks: one Crunch and two Tectonic Claws. They can replace one Tectonic Claws with Tail Sweep.

**Crunch.** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8+6) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage.

**Tectonic Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (3d6+6) slashing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 16).

**Tail Sweep.** Each creature in a 15-foot cone must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) bludgeoning damage and being pushed 15 feet away on a failure, or half as much damage and pushed 5 feet on a success.

**Earthen Maw.** One creature within 10 feet of Tenu's Spawn must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be restrained as stone grips them; the target repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**Shale and Stone Breath.** Tenu's Spawn exhales a blast of rock shards in a 45-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) piercing damage plus 17 (5d6) bludgeoning damage on a failure, or half as much of each on a success.

**Cataclysm.** Tenu's Spawn slams the ground. Each creature within 60 feet of them must make a DC 17 Strength saving throw, taking 33 (6d10) bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failure, or half as much damage and remaining standing on a success.

### Reactions

**Billowing Wings.** When Tenu's Spawn takes 15 or more damage from a single source, they can kick up a cloud of dust that heavily obscures a 40-foot radius around themself until the end of their next turn, then move up to half their speed.

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Tenu's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Shale and Stone Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

### Legendary Actions

Tenu's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Tenu's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Tail Attack (Costs 1 Action).** Tenu's Spawn makes one Tail Sweep.

**Sudden Quake (Costs 1 Action).** Each creature within Tenu's Spawn's Catastrophic Aura must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or fall prone.

**Earthen Maw (Costs 2 Actions).** Tenu's Spawn uses Earthen Maw.

Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Tenu's Spawn takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; they can't use the same effect two rounds in a row.

**Grasping Stone.** Hands of animate stone burst from the ground in a 15-foot-radius sphere at a point Tenu's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or be restrained until initiative count 20 on the next round (a restrained creature can break free early with a successful DC 17 Strength check).

**Shifting Ways.** Tenu's Spawn reshapes a 40-foot cube of stone within 90 feet of them, filling chosen spaces with stone (raising walls or pillars, or creating cover) or removing existing stone. A creature that would be enclosed by the new stone is shunted to the nearest open space and knocked prone.

**Stalactite Fall.** Stone crashes down in two 10-foot-radius areas Tenu's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them. Each creature in an area must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage (half as much on a success); each area becomes difficult terrain.

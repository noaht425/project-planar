## Statblock

*Huge dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 18 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 380 (20d12+250)

**Speed.** 20 ft., fly 60 ft., swim 90 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 25 (+7), DEX 10 (+0), CON 24 (+7), INT 15 (+2), WIS 18 (+4), CHA 20 (+5)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +5, CON +12, WIS +9, CHA +10

**Damage Resistances.** fire; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** cold

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, prone

**Senses.** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 14

**Languages.** Common, Aquan

**Challenge.** 13 (10,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Vetur's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Vetur's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Amphibious.** Vetur's Spawn can breathe air and water.

**Unbound.** Vetur's Spawn is formless water: if they are subjected to the banishment, forcecage, maze, or an imprisonment spell (or a similar effect), the effect ends on them at the start of their next turn instead of its normal duration, as they pour free of it.

**Crushing Depths.** A non-amphibious creature that ends its turn within 15 feet of Vetur's Spawn must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) cold damage.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Vetur's Spawn uses Frightful Presence. They then make three attacks: one Bite and two Claws. They can replace one Claw with Fluke Slam.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d10+7) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) cold damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 17) and pulled 5 feet toward Vetur's Spawn.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8+7) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage.

**Fluke Slam.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8+7) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of Vetur's Spawn's choice within 90 feet of them and aware of them must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the start of Vetur's Spawn's next turn.

**Drown.** Vetur's Spawn targets one creature grappled by them. The target must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or take 18 (4d8) cold damage and begin suffocating if it needs to breathe, or take half as much damage on a success.

**Abyssal Cold Breath.** Vetur's Spawn exhales a blast of abyssal cold in a 45-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) cold damage and having its speed reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn on a failure, or half as much damage on a success. The ground in the area becomes ice, which is difficult terrain, for 1 minute.

**Maelstrom.** A vortex of churning water and ice forms in a 30-foot-radius sphere at a point Vetur's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be pulled 15 feet toward the center, knocked prone, and take 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) cold damage.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Vetur's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Abyssal Cold Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

### Legendary Actions

Vetur's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Vetur's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Rake (Costs 1 Action).** Vetur's Spawn makes one Claw attack.

**Undertow (Costs 1 Action).** Each creature within 30 feet of Vetur's Spawn must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 15 feet toward them.

**Glacial Surge (Costs 1 Action).** Vetur's Spawn moves up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks, freezing the surface they cross; it becomes difficult terrain for other creatures until the start of Vetur's Spawn's next turn.

**Rime (Costs 2 Actions).** Vetur's Spawn targets one creature within 45 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 9 (2d8) cold damage and become restrained in ice until the end of its next turn.

Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Vetur's Spawn takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; they can't use the same effect two rounds in a row.

**The Deep Rises.** Water surges through a 30-foot-radius area at a point Vetur's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them. Each creature there must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and swept 10 feet in a random direction; the area is difficult terrain until initiative count 20 on the next round.

**Pressure Crush.** The water pressure spikes in a 15-foot-radius sphere at a point Vetur's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw, taking 9 (2d8) bludgeoning damage and being unable to take reactions until the start of its next turn on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

**Black Water Fog.** A 30-foot-radius cloud of dark water fills a point Vetur's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them until initiative count 20 on the next round. The area is heavily obscured, and a non-amphibious creature that starts its turn there must hold its breath or begin suffocating.

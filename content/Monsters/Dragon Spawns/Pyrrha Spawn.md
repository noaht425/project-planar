## Statblock

*Gargantuan dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 19 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 150 (12d12+72)

**Speed.** 60 ft., fly 120 ft., climb 60 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 24 (+7), DEX 14 (+2), CON 22 (+6), INT 12 (+1), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 18 (+4)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +7, CON +11, INT +6, WIS +6, CHA +9

**Damage Immunities.** fire

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, frightened, poisoned

**Senses.** blindsight 30 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 11

**Languages.** Common, Draconic

**Challenge.** 14 (11,500 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Pyrrha's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Pyrrha's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Resistant Hide.** Pyrrha's Spawn has resistance to damage from any attack roll made without advantage.

**Unbound.** If Pyrrha's Spawn is subjected to the banishment, forcecage, maze, or an imprisonment spell (or a similar effect), the effect ends on them at the start of their next turn instead of its normal duration.

**Vainglorious Strength.** Whenever Pyrrha's Spawn succeeds on a Strength saving throw, they have advantage on their next attack roll before the end of their next turn. The first time each turn they fail a Strength saving throw, they fly into a fury: they gain 10 temporary hit points and their melee attacks deal an extra 1d8 fire damage until the end of their next turn.

**Whorlflame Landing.** The first time each turn Pyrrha's Spawn lands after descending at least 20 feet, each creature within 20 feet of them is pushed 10 feet away and starts Burning (see Cinderlash).

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Pyrrha's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Slagflame Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Pyrrha's Spawn uses Frightful Presence. They then make three attacks: one Searing Bite, one Cavernous Maw, and one Irongouge Claws. They can replace one of these attacks with Cinderlash or Phalanx-Tail Sweep.

**Cavernous Maw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8+7) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) fire damage. If the target is grappled by Pyrrha's Spawn, it must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be swallowed (see below).

**Irongouge Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8+7) slashing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 18). Each other creature within 5 feet of the target must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take half the damage.

**Searing Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8+7) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) fire damage.

**Cinderlash.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (3d8) fire damage, and the target starts Burning, taking 3 (1d10) fire damage at the start of each of its turns until a creature uses an action to douse it.

**Phalanx-Tail Sweep.** Each creature in a 20-foot cone must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage and being pushed 15 feet away on a failure, or half as much damage and pushed 5 feet on a success.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of Pyrrha's Spawn's choice within 90 feet of them and aware of them must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 round. A creature that succeeds is immune to this trait for the next 24 hours.

**Slagflame Breath.** Pyrrha's Spawn exhales molten slag in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 35 (10d6) fire damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. The ground in the area becomes difficult terrain, dealing 3 (1d6) fire damage to a creature that ends its turn there, for 3 rounds.

**Stoke Malice.** A Fire Elemental rises from slag or magma within 120 feet of Pyrrha's Spawn and acts on their initiative count.

### Reactions

**Billowing Wings.** When Pyrrha's Spawn takes 15 or more damage from a single source, they can heavily obscure a 40-foot radius around themself until the end of their next turn, then fly up to half their speed.

### Legendary Actions

Pyrrha's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Pyrrha's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Lunging Swipe (Costs 1 Action).** Pyrrha's Spawn moves up to 15 feet without provoking opportunity attacks, then makes one Irongouge Claws attack.

**Leaping Flames (Costs 1 Action).** Flames leap from a burning creature or object to a creature within 20 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 11 (2d10) fire damage and start Burning.

**Inhale Cinders (Costs 1 Action).** Pyrrha's Spawn ends the Burning condition on a creature within 15 feet of them. If they do, they roll to recharge Slagflame Breath.

**Burn Away (Costs 2 Actions).** Pyrrha's Spawn targets one burning creature within 60 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Charisma saving throw or its lowest-level spell currently in effect ends; if it isn't affected by any spell, it takes 11 (2d10) fire damage instead.

**Thunderous Wingbeat / Wing Buffet (Costs 2 Actions).** Each creature within 15 feet of Pyrrha's Spawn must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone. Pyrrha's Spawn then flies up to half their speed.

Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Pyrrha's Spawn takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; they can't use the same effect two rounds in a row.

**Eruption.** Molten rock erupts from the ground at a point Pyrrha's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them, in a 15-foot-radius cylinder. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 13 (3d8) fire damage on a failure and, if it's on the ground, becoming restrained until initiative count 20 on the next round; or taking half as much damage on a success.

**Suffocating Ash.** Choking ash fills a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on a point Pyrrha's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them until initiative count 20 on the next round. The area is lightly obscured. When a creature enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it takes 7 (2d6) fire damage and must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be unable to take reactions until the start of its next turn.

**Downdraft.** Fierce, hot wind radiates from Pyrrha's Spawn until initiative count 20 on the next round. Until then, flying creatures' flying speed is halved. At initiative count 20, each creature airborne in the area that isn't immune to fire damage takes 5 (1d10) fire damage.

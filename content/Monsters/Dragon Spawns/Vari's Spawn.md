## Statblock

*Huge dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 17 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 410 (18d12+293)

**Speed.** 40 ft., fly 90 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 22 (+6), DEX 13 (+1), CON 20 (+5), INT 7 (-2), WIS 15 (+2), CHA 19 (+4)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +6, CON +10, WIS +7, CHA +9

**Damage Resistances.** lightning; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** thunder

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned

**Senses.** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 12

**Languages.** —

**Challenge.** 13 (10,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Vari's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Vari's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Rolling Thunder.** At the end of each of Vari's Spawn's turns, each creature within 15 feet of them takes 9 (2d8) thunder damage.

**Concussed.** A creature that takes thunder damage from Vari's Spawn can't take reactions until the start of its next turn.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Vari's Spawn uses Frightful Presence. They then make three attacks: one Bite and two Claws.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of Vari's Spawn's choice within 60 feet of them and aware of them must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the start of Vari's Spawn's next turn.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8+6) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) thunder damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is grappled (escape DC 15) and takes 5 (1d6+2) thunder damage at the start of each of its turns for as long as it remains grappled this way.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d6+6) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) thunder damage.

**Thunderclap.** Vari's Spawn releases a burst of sound in a 15-foot radius around themself. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 13 (3d8) thunder damage and be deafened for 1 minute, or take half as much damage and not be deafened on a success.

**Concussive Breath.** Vari's Spawn exhales a concussive wave of sound in a 45-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) thunder damage and being pushed 15 feet away, knocked prone, and deafened until the end of its next turn on a failure, or half as much damage and none of the other effects on a success.

**Resounding Toll.** Vari's Spawn lets out a deafening roar. Each creature within 45 feet of them that can hear them must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 27 (5d10) thunder damage and be deafened for 1 minute, or take half as much damage and not be deafened on a success. A creature reduced to 10 hit points or fewer by this damage drops to 0 hit points instead.

### Reactions

**Answering Peal.** When Vari's Spawn takes damage from an attack or a spell, the source of that damage must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) thunder damage and be unable to take reactions until the start of its next turn, or take half as much damage on a success.

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Vari's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Concussive Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

### Legendary Actions

Vari's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Vari's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Rake (Costs 1 Action).** Vari's Spawn makes one Claw attack.

**Storm-Roll (Costs 1 Action).** Vari's Spawn rolls into a peal of thunder and reforms in an unoccupied space they can see within 40 feet. This ends any grappled, restrained, or similar confining effect on them.

**Reverberate (Costs 1 Action).** Vari's Spawn targets one creature within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) thunder damage, have its speed halved, and be unable to take reactions until the end of its next turn.

**Shockwave (Costs 2 Actions).** Each creature within 20 feet of Vari's Spawn must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone; a creature knocked prone this way takes 7 (2d6) thunder damage.

Lair Actions

None.

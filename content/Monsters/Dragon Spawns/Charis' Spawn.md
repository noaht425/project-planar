## Statblock

*Huge dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 17 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 380 (18d12+263)

**Speed.** 30 ft., fly 80 ft., swim 60 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 21 (+5), DEX 12 (+1), CON 23 (+6), INT 13 (+1), WIS 16 (+3), CHA 20 (+5)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +6, CON +11, WIS +8, CHA +10

**Damage Resistances.** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** poison

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, frightened, poisoned

**Senses.** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 13

**Languages.** —

**Challenge.** 13 (10,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Charis's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Charis's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Amphibious.** Charis's Spawn can breathe air and water.

**Unbound.** Charis's Spawn crumbles into a cloud of immune spores: if they are subjected to the banishment, forcecage, maze, or an imprisonment spell (or a similar effect), the effect ends on them at the start of their next turn instead of its normal duration, as they reform nearby.

**Aura of Decay.** A creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of Charis's Spawn takes 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and can't regain hit points until the start of its next turn.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Charis's Spawn uses Beguiling Rot. They then make three attacks: one Bite and two Claws. They can replace one Claw with Tail Lash.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8+5) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) necrotic damage, and if the target is Large or smaller, it is grappled (escape DC 15) and takes 5 (1d6+2) necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns for as long as it remains grappled this way.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6+5) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.

**Tail Lash.** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

**Beguiling Rot.** Each creature of Charis's Spawn's choice within 45 feet of them must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed until the start of Charis's Spawn's next turn. A charmed creature is incapacitated and must use its movement on its turn to move toward Charis's Spawn.

**Miasmal Breath.** Charis's Spawn exhales a cloud of necrotic spores in a 40-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) necrotic damage and being blinded until the end of its next turn on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

**Reap.** Charis's Spawn targets one creature within 45 feet of them, drawing the life from its body. The target must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or take 27 (5d10) necrotic damage, or half as much damage on a success.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Charis's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Miasmal Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

### Legendary Actions

Charis's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Charis's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Rake (Costs 1 Action).** Charis's Spawn makes one Claw attack.

**Spore Drift (Costs 1 Action).** Charis's Spawn breaks apart into a cloud of spores and reforms in an unoccupied space they can see within 40 feet. This ends any grappled, restrained, or similar confining effect on them.

**Creeping Rot (Costs 1 Action).** Charis's Spawn targets one creature within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.

**Harvest (Costs 2 Actions).** Charis's Spawn draws vitality from one creature within 30 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 9 (2d8) necrotic damage, and Charis's Spawn regains 5 (1d8) hit points.

Lair Actions

None.

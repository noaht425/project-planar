## Statblock

*Huge dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 17 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 300 (24d12+144)

**Speed.** 30 ft., fly 120 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 23 (+6), DEX 15 (+2), CON 23 (+6), INT 13 (+1), WIS 16 (+3), CHA 20 (+5)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +7, CON +11, WIS +8, CHA +10

**Damage Resistances.** fire, cold; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** radiant

**Condition Immunities.** blinded, charmed, frightened

**Senses.** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 13

**Languages.** —

**Challenge.** 15 (13,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Brilis's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Brilis's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Orbiting Stars.** A ring of stars orbits Brilis's Spawn at 10 ft; a creature that enters that radius or starts its turn there takes 5 (1d8) radiant damage. While bloodied (half hit points or fewer), the radius extends to 20 ft and the damage increases to 9 (2d8).

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Brilis's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Photonic Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Brilis's Spawn makes three attacks: one Celestial Fangs and two Radiant Claws.

**Celestial Fangs.** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d6+6) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) radiant damage.

**Radiant Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8+6) slashing damage.

**Photonic Breath.** Brilis's Spawn releases a burst of stellar radiance in a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on a point they can see within 180 feet. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw, taking 33 (6d10) radiant damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

### Reactions

None.

### Legendary Actions

Brilis's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Brilis's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Expanding Orbit (Costs 1 Action).** Orbiting Stars' radius becomes 20 ft (damage 2d8) until the start of Brilis's Spawn's next turn.

**Slash (Costs 1 Action).** Brilis's Spawn makes one Radiant Claws attack.

**Gravity Well (Costs 1 Action).** Each creature within 45 feet of Brilis's Spawn must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 15 feet toward them.

**Translate (Costs 1 Action).** Brilis's Spawn teleports up to 70 feet to an unoccupied space they can see. This ends any grappled, restrained, or similar confining effect on them (including forcecage).

**Blinding Brilliance (Costs 2 Actions).** Each creature within 20 feet of Brilis's Spawn must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be blinded until the end of its next turn.

Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Brilis's Spawn takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; they can't use the same effect two rounds in a row.

**Gravity Crush.** Gravity intensifies in a 15-foot-radius sphere at a point Brilis's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or take 11 (2d10) force damage and become restrained until initiative count 20 on the next round.

**Solar Flare.** Brilis's Spawn unleashes a flare of light in a 45-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 13 (3d8) radiant damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

**Eclipse.** A 30-foot-radius sphere at a point Brilis's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them fills with darkness that neither ordinary nor magical light can dispel, until initiative count 20 on the next round. Brilis's Spawn can see through this darkness.

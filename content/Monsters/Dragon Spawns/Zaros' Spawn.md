## Statblock

*Huge dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 18 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 340 (20d12+210)

**Speed.** 40 ft., fly 120 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 24 (+7), DEX 11 (+0), CON 21 (+5), INT 9 (-1), WIS 17 (+3), CHA 23 (+6)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +5, CON +10, INT +4, WIS +8, CHA +11

**Damage Resistances.** thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** lightning

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned

**Senses.** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 13

**Languages.** Common, Draconic

**Challenge.** 13 (10,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +5

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Zaros's Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Zaros's Spawn fails a saving throw, they can choose to succeed instead.

**Unbound.** If Zaros's Spawn is subjected to the banishment, forcecage, maze, or an imprisonment spell (or a similar effect), they discorporate into a lightning-wreathed gale, and the effect ends on them at the start of their next turn instead of its normal duration.

**Storm-Iron Hide.** Zaros's Spawn's scales are dense and storm-forged. Whenever they take damage, reduce that damage by 2.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Zaros's Spawn uses Frightful Presence. They then make three attacks: one Bite and two Claws.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d10+7) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) lightning damage.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8+7) slashing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 17).

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of Zaros's Spawn's choice within 90 feet of them and aware of them must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the start of Zaros's Spawn's next turn.

**Storm Breath.** Zaros's Spawn exhales a bolt of lightning in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) lightning damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

**Storm Front.** Zaros's Spawn conjures a churning storm front in a 20-foot-radius sphere at a point they can see within 60 feet of them, lasting until the start of their next turn. The area is difficult terrain, and ranged attacks made into or out of it have disadvantage. A creature that starts its turn in the area must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or take 16 (3d10) lightning damage and be flung 15 feet in a random direction, or take half as much damage on a success.

### Reactions

**Static Surge.** When Zaros's Spawn takes 30 or more damage from a single source, lightning arcs back at that source. It must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 9 (2d8) lightning damage and be pushed 10 feet away, or take half as much damage on a success.

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Zaros's Spawn drops to half their hit points or fewer, their Storm Breath immediately recharges and they use it, using no action.

### Legendary Actions

Zaros's Spawn can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Zaros's Spawn regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.

**Rake (Costs 1 Action).** Zaros's Spawn makes one Claw attack.

**Storm-Step (Costs 1 Action).** Zaros's Spawn discorporates into a gale and reforms in an unoccupied space they can see within 50 feet. This ends any grappled, restrained, or similar confining effect on them.

**Chain Lightning (Costs 1 Action).** Zaros's Spawn hurls a bolt of lightning at one creature within 60 feet of them. The target must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 13 (3d8) lightning damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

**Thunderous Wingbeat (Costs 2 Actions).** Each creature within 15 feet of Zaros's Spawn must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or take 11 (2d10) thunder damage, be pushed 10 feet away, and fall prone. Zaros's Spawn then flies up to half their speed.

Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Zaros's Spawn takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; they can't use the same effect two rounds in a row.

**Fork Lightning.** Lightning stabs down at two points Zaros's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them. Each creature within 5 feet of a point must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 13 (3d8) lightning damage on a failure, or half as much damage on a success. A creature that fails also can't take reactions until the start of their next turn.

**Downdraft.** A 20-foot-radius cylinder centered on a point Zaros's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them becomes difficult terrain, and ranged attacks made into or out of it have disadvantage, until initiative count 20 on the next round. A creature that ends its turn there is pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone unless it succeeds on a DC 17 Strength saving throw.

**Storm Veil.** A 30-foot-radius fog fills a point Zaros's Spawn can see within 90 feet of them until initiative count 20 on the next round. The area is heavily obscured. A creature that attempts to cast a spell with a verbal component there must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution check or the spell fails.

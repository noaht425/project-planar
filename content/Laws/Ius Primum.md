Deal created between [[Atris]] and [[Hakea]] during the [[Council of Creation]]. Witnessed by [[Tiyanak]] in disguise as [[Indri]].

## Exact Wording

On the thirty-eighth day of Aucaela, in the first year of the Age of the Many, [[Sylvana Lorien]] officially enters into a deal with the Archfey [[Atris]] and [[Hakea]], witnessed by the Archfey [[Indri]]. All present parties are now and forever bound to the terms of this deal. The terms of the deal are as such:

Hakea and Atris agree to aid in the creation of a new race, which will henceforth be called Elf ([[Elves]] pl.) during the [[Council of Creation]] taking place from the first day of Aucaela in the first year of the Age of Many to the first day of Auctera in the same year.

Both members must provide aid.

Aid is defined as but is not limited to the following:

- Providing magic to the Elves.
- Helping to shape the physical form of the Elves.
- Contributing to the nature and/or personality of the Elves.
- Giving power to Sylvana Lorien so that they may more easily create the Elves.

Both members must put in at least a combined 35% of the work that Sylvana Lorien does in the creation of the Elves.

In exchange, any Archfey is granted permission to claim the soul of any elf at any time of that Archfey's choosing.

The Archfey who claims this elf gains the following privileges:

- The Archfey decides where the soul of the elf will go upon the death of the elf.
- The Archfey may break any and all warlock pacts, oaths, or other bonds of worship that the elf has entered into either willingly or unwillingly.
- The Archfey must pay any costs associated with breaking any bonds early.
- The Archfey cannot be made to give up their name, titles, domains, soul, body, or anything else considered essential to the being of the Archfey.
- Any dispute concerning the soul, spirit, body, etc. of the elf due to this clause is to be settled by the Archfey and the previous controller of the bond belonging to the elf.
- The elf may contribute to payment if they so wish.
- The elf may not be forced to pay any amount of any fees incurred by the above. This includes threat of physical violence, extortion, coercion, deceit, enchantment, illusion, etc.
- The elf is bound to serve the Archfey in any and all commands that will not directly or intentionally lead to the death, destruction, etc. of the elf.
- No Archfey may claim more than five elves during the span of one year.
- No more than twenty elves may be claimed during the span of one year.
- Should an elf be unwilling to give themself over to the Archfey that has claimed them, their being will be erased from reality in its entirety.
- Any and all powers, titles, wealth, and all other possessions both material and immaterial, are passed into the possession of the Archfey.

If one party should break the terms of this deal, they will be bound into service for the rest of their life to the other party and will forfeit their powers, titles, wealth, and all other possessions both material and immaterial to the witness, never to be returned for any reason, except by direct order of the receiving party.

The responsibility for enacting this punishment and ensuring that it is meted out in a proper and reasonable fashion falls upon the witness.

(ADDED IN SECRET BY TIYANAK) If both parties break the terms of this deal, the contract shall pass into the care of the witness and they have full authority to alter the terms of the deal as they see fit.

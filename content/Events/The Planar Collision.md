## What Happened

The realms had been separate until 50 AF. The chaos that followed [[Syzygy|the Syzygy]] drove them into each other, and almost everything about the present world dates from that collision.

The heavens and hells separated, Tarkis shattered into the elemental realms, and Arcanis collapsed into its eight schools. The mortal realm was made out of the pieces, and the [[Progenitors]] were born into it.

> *A dwarf in Lonvil showed me a place in her cellar wall where the stone changes from one kind to another along a perfectly straight line. She said the Progenitors smoothed the world out before anyone was put on it, and that they had clearly missed her cellar.*

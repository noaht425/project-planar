[[Iudis]] splits into The Hells and Heavens. Part of the lead-up to [[The Planar Collision]].

## What Happened

In 50 AF, at the end of the [[Divine War]], the heavens and the hells of [[Iudis]] came apart from one another.

The souls in them did not move with the realms. The archangels and archdevils had to go through Iudis by hand, taking souls out of what was no longer theirs and putting them where they now belonged. A few clever souls are thought to have avoided being found during the sorting and to be where they should not be.

A crack formed in [[Indri]]'s cage in the same year and let light into it for the first time.

The Separation was part of the lead-up to [[The Planar Collision]].

> *A soul in Kindness told me, very quietly, that she ought to have been in Desire. She had been standing in a queue during the sorting and had simply walked over to the other one. She asked me not to write down which soul she was, and I have not.*

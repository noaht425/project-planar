The four day war between the progenitor [[Genasi]] and the archdevil [[Lux]], fought outside the city of [[Geno (City)|Geno]] from 36 Aucaela, 405 AM. Lux won.

## Why

[[Iras]] killed [[Genasi VII]] on 33 Aucaela, 405 AM and took the throne of [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]]. His principle was that the kingdom owed its existence to the dragons, so it should be ruled by a dragon's descendant.

Lux, who is Iras's father, took the dead of the Genasi line into [[Pride]]. They are the descendants of the progenitor Genasi and no relation to Lux. Taking them was not part of any claim Iras had made.

Genasi came for them three days later.

## The Fighting

It was fought on open ground outside the capital rather than in it. The city was not spared entirely. It was not ruined either. What the four days did to the country around the walls took a generation to put right.

It lasted from 36 to 39 Aucaela, 405 AM. Nobody has produced an account of the four days that the other side accepts.

## The Result

Lux won and did not give the souls up. The Genasi line is in [[Pride]] and has been since, treated as any other soul in the hell. For a family known for its pride, that is probably the punishment.

He then agreed that someone other than his own line would hold Geno. That is the only concession any archdevil has made to a progenitor. He honoured it in 419 AM, when [[Iras]] abdicated and Geno's council elected [[Gaius Flaminius]] with Lux's hand on the result.

## What Came Of It

Genasi lost his descendants, his throne stayed out of his family, and the kingdom he founded passed to an appointment in an archdevil's gift. He intends to kill Iras and is working on bringing [[Moto]] into it. Lux intends to stop him and knows that much of the plan.

The ninth king's death in 546 AM, with no rule left for choosing a tenth, began [[The Great Unrest]].

> *There is a field outside Geno that nothing has grown in since, and the city has built around it rather than over it. A man grazing goats at the edge of it told me that his family has had the grazing there for six generations and that the arrangement is that they do not let the animals onto the bare part. I asked what happens if they do. He said that nobody has.*

## Design Notes

The war is the hinge the whole of Geno's modern history turns on. It cost a family, a dynasty, and eventually a century, and it was fought over souls that had already been taken.

Lux conceding the throne after winning is the move to show a party. It reads as generosity and it is a performance of generosity, and he has been collecting credit for it for three hundred years.

## Open

- What is wrong with the bare field is unwritten.

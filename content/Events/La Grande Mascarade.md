A masked festival held every other year, hosted by a different kingdom each time. [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]] held the first on 99 Morcaela, 428 AM in [[Omphalos]], a year after the war between [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] and [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] ended.

## Why It Started

Mathair put it on to get the kingdoms into one room when none of them would sit down formally. The [[Panarex]] was no use for that. A session there is a session, with chairs, precedence, and votes that bind the absent, and in 428 nobody wanted to be bound to anything.

The Mascarade decides nothing and records nothing, which is the whole of its usefulness. What is agreed at one is agreed between the people who agreed it, and has to be done again properly somewhere else if it is to hold.

## The Masks

Everyone is masked and rank is formally suspended. Titles are not used, precedence is not observed, and a ruler may be approached by anyone in the room. The convention is strict, it is old, and it is kept even by kingdoms that are at war during the festival.

The practical effect is that nobody is certain who they are talking to. A great deal of business gets done on that basis and a certain amount goes wrong on it. Guards are admitted unmasked and are not permitted past the outer rooms.

## Who Attends

Not only rulers. The Mascarade is for the powerful of every kind, which means ministers and ambassadors, the heads of houses, bankers, and shipowners, archmages, and senior clergy of whatever is worshipped where they come from. Wealth alone will not do it and neither will birth. What is required is that someone in the room would care what you did.

Invitations are issued by the host and are the most valuable paper in the world for the fortnight either side of the festival.

## Hosting

The right to host is contested and kingdoms campaign for it years ahead. The host sets the theme, pays for everything, and is judged on the result for a generation. Mathair has hosted most often and says so.

## 702 AM

At the Mascarade in Omphalos in 702 AM, [[Theophilius Fanatis]], one of the two kings of Krotaphos, attacked the ruler of Sylfaen in front of the assembled guests. He was under an enchantment, he was given a saving throw against it, and he chose to fail. He spoke [[Celestial]] as he went for it, which is illegal in his own kingdom, and called the Sylfaen ruler a tyrant and a despot.

Sylfaen ordered every citizen of Krotaphos arrested or killed. The masks meant that most of the room could not say afterwards who had been standing where.

> *The rule about titles is enforced by the guests and not by the host. I used one, once, out of habit, to a woman whose mask was a bird. The three people nearest us stopped talking at the same moment and waited to see what she would do about it, and she said, pleasantly, that she had no idea who I meant. I have been careful since, and I have never found out whether I was right.*

## Design Notes

The Panarex binds and the Mascarade does not, and the two exist for that reason. A campaign that needs a deal made where no record of it survives has one venue.

Masks make the 702 attack possible and make the witnesses useless, which is why the incident is still being argued about rather than settled.

Attendance is a goal in itself. A party cannot buy its way in, so getting invited means becoming someone a ruler would care about.

## Open

- Whether a Mascarade is held in 704 AM after what happened is unwritten.

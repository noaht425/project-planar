Cause of [[The Planar Collision]]

Divorce of [[The King of Day]] and [[The King of Night]]

## What Happened

In 3,096 AF [[The King of Day]] divorced [[The King of Night]] over [[The Division]]. The Kings fought. Day was left with a necrotic scar down the right side of his face, and Night was blinded.

[[Nullis]], which had been light and dark mixed together through the whole plane, began to pull apart into a light half and a dark half. The separation is still not finished, and the two halves still bleed into each other along the boundary.

[[Cado]] appears in the record for the last time at the divorce. In the same year [[Tiyanak]] killed the original [[Indri]] and took his place.

The Syzygy set off the chain of events that ended in [[The Planar Collision]].

Day knows that something wearing his face finished the persuasion of Night, and he cannot forgive Night for believing it. Night knows he was worked on and does not know by whom. Neither King knows the other is looking for Indri.

> *Eos told me she was in the room. She would not say anything else about it, and when I pressed her she asked whether I had ever watched two people I loved find out what the other was capable of.*

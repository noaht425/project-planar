[[Arcanis]] Combines. Part of the lead-up to [[The Planar Collision]].

## What Happened

From 39 to 10 AF the thousands of minor schools of [[Arcanis]] came together into the eight schools studied today.

Before it, each minor school was mostly a single piece of what is now a school. Necromancy alone was blood, bone, death by one means and death by another, reanimation by one technique and reanimation by another, and the separate pieces of life and of the soul. Evocation was fire, and ice, and lightning, each apart from the others.

No one saw it happen. It seems to have been the magic running through the fabric of the world rearranging itself, and that is not something a mortal can watch.

The Coalescence was part of the lead-up to [[The Planar Collision]]. But a spellcaster trained before it would have learned one narrow portion of magic, not a school.

> *The Aravis Academy keeps a catalogue of the minor schools as they were before the Coalescence, pieced together from fragments. A lecturer showed me the section on necromancy. It fills a bookcase. The section on necromancy since fills a shelf.*

## What Happened

The nine [[Progenitors]] made the nine peoples together, over the hundred days from 1 Aucaela to 1 Auctera of 1 AM. Some had help, and the help had prices. See [[Progenitors]] for who made whom and what they paid.

Two agreements made during the Council still bind the world. [[Ius Primum]], signed on 38 Aucaela, lets any archfey claim any elf. [[Sithi]]'s name went to the archfey as the price of their help with the fae.

On 98 Aucaela, [[Kleipur]] gave six of the first giantkin the power that made them the titans.

> *Shram told me that the Council took a hundred days, that he had finished on the second, and that he spent the rest of it playing games with whoever finished next. He would not say who finished last.*

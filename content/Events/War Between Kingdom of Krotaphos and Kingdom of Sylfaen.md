The thirty year war between [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] and [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]], from 86 Auctera, 397 AM to 3 Mortera, 427 AM. Krotaphos lost and was reshaped.

## The Cause

A border quarrel over the ground [[Dawara]] stands on. Dawara is Sylfaen's capital, it sits directly on the Krotaphos frontier, and Krotaphos claimed the ground under it.

Neither kingdom treated the claim as small. For Krotaphos it was territory. For Sylfaen it was the capital. Thirty years of war came out of a dispute that began as a question of where a line ran.

## The War

Fighting began on 86 Auctera, 397 AM, four days after [[Genasi VII]] took the throne of [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]]. Nobody has shown that the two were connected, and the coincidence has kept historians in work ever since.

[[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]] declared neutrality on 97 Morcaela, 423 AM, twenty six years into the war. [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]] came in on Sylfaen's side on 2 Mortera, 424 AM.

Krotaphos lost on 99 Morcaela, 427 AM.

## The Terms

The terms were humiliating by design.

The kingdom was cut down to a circle on 3 Mortera, 427 AM. The land outside it went to Geno and Oinarria. A circle has no frontier that follows a river or a ridge, and no line that can be held. So the shape itself is part of the settlement.

[[Iudimian]] may be spoken in public only in religious settings. Krotaphos kept its seat at the [[Panarex]] and is expected to vote with Sylfaen every time.

## After

Krotaphos holds the authority to enforce the will of the heavens and the hells in the mortal world. It has had almost no power to do it since. [[The University of Divine Judgement]] came through the defeat in better condition than the kingdom did, because the twelve kept funding it while the crown shrank.

Sylfaen has been the strongest kingdom in the world since 427 AM.

The first [[La Grande Mascarade]] was held the following year, and the war is the reason it exists.

> *An old soldier in Dawara walked me out to where the line used to run. It is a quarter of an hour from the city gate. His grandfather had fought over it, he said, and so had he. I asked what is there now. He said a road and a customs house. The customs house is on their side. That, he said, is the entire point.*

## Design Notes

A thirty year war over where a line ran gives the setting its largest grievance without anybody in it having done anything interesting enough to forgive.

Geno taking twenty six years to declare neutrality is the detail to keep. [[Nara Veril]] did it in weeks in 702, and Geno knows how that looks.

The circle is the usable image. Krotaphos is a kingdom shaped like a settlement term.

## History

- Begins 86 Auctera, 397 AM.
- [[Kingdom of Geno]] declares neutrality on 97 Morcaela, 423 AM.
- Oinarria agrees to help Sylfaen (2 Mortera, 424 AM)
- [[Kingdom of Krotaphos]] loses to [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]] on 99 Morcaela, 427 AM.
- Krotaphos reshaped to be circular on 3 Mortera, 427 AM (excess territory given to [[Kingdom of Geno]] and [[Kingdom of Oinarria]]).
- Led directly into the first [[La Grande Mascarade]], hosted the following year.

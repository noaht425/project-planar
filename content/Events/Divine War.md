The war between the [[Archangels|archangels]] and the [[Archdevils|archdevils]], from 2,987 AF to 50 AF.

## Causes

[[The Division]] split each Judgement's place into a heaven and a hell, and left the two halves of each Judgement sharing ground that had been one place. They fought over it. Underneath the territory was the larger question of who ruled the Judgements.

## The War

[[Gwenhael]] gave up his claim in the first year, and the other five archangels have never forgiven him. Most of the other quarrels over territory ended in a split of roughly half and half.

The war ran for nearly three millennia without either side winning it.

## The Peace

[[Verita]] and [[Tiyanak]] made the peace face to face, in 50 AF. Tiyanak took one tenth of the realm of Truth and gave Verita the other nine. The tenth held [[Indri]]'s cage, and by taking the smaller share Tiyanak never had to explain why it mattered.

Verita made the peace without consulting the other archangels, and they still regard it as a surrender. [[Abaddon]] was the only archdevil who did not want it.

Neither side would let the other judge the dead, so the sorting was given to two made beings, [[Bis]] and [[Seisear]]. See [[Nekerux]].

The heavens and the hells came apart in the same year. See [[Great Separation]].

## Since

The war has not resumed. Both sides take shots at each other from time to time without committing to anything. [[Blythe]] thinks it ended too early, Abaddon wants it back, and [[Belphegor]] is waiting for the archangels to give him a reason.

> *A lesser devil in Tyranny told me he had fought through the whole of the Divine War. He could remember every border he had held, and he could not remember the name of the side he had held them for.*

Hosted by [[Eltarien Eladris]]. Rotates which plane it is hosted on. A grand circus, carnival, fair, tournament, market, and casino, etc.

## Introduction Monologue by Eltarien

"Helloooo my darling guests and welcome to Eltarien's Extraplanar Extravaganza! The planes' greatest carnival, circus, tournament, hells if it's entertaining it's here. Thank you so much to our host the [[Kingdom of Starkon]] for allowing us to set up shop here. I would also like to thank our wonderful sponsors: the august [[Princeps Autuun]], the dazzling dragon of divinity [[Brilis]], and everyone's favorite archdevil [[Lilien]]! Please join me in giving them a big round of applause for their very generous donations to our humble, little show here. If this is your first time here, please allow me to say, from the bottom of my heart, welcome. We thank you so much for joining us here for this time of celebration and sensational splendor."

[[Isadora]] will be at a Liar's Dice table. If the players bet a large amount of money against her she will claim to not have that much but will offer one of her dice, saying that many have made this bet before and lost. If she loses, she will look at one of the dice and remark how it likes the winning player and to take good care of it.

> *The Extravaganza runs on prizes, and the prizes are real, which is the part nobody believes until they have watched somebody carry one out. At a dice table I saw a boy win something that I am fairly sure had belonged to a god. Nobody at that table asked how it had come to be on it. Eltarien was three stalls away and did not look up.*

## Events

### Gambling (for money only)

**Black Jack (to 22):**
- Place bets
- Everyone rolls a d12 and a d10 (dealer keeps d10 hidden)
- Everyone can hit any number of times by adding a d8
- If you roll the same number on both dice you can split and hit as normal

**High Rollers:**
- Everyone rolls a d20 simultaneously
- Rounds of everyone rolling a d4 and subtracting it from starting number
- When a player goes into negatives they are out
- Last one standing wins

**Horse Races**

**Roulette:**
- Everyone chooses a number between 1-20
- Roll a d20

**Liar's Dice (5d6):**
- Everyone rolls 5d6 and keeps them hidden
- Players take turns guessing how many of each face there are
- Next player can increase number of dice or value or call someone
- Loser gets rid of one of their dice

**Isadora's Gamble:**
- Player guesses a number between 1-6
- House rolls 3d6
- 0 match: no money back
- 1 match: money back + bet
- 2 match: money back + 2 x bet
- 3 match: money back + 3 x bet

### Carnival

**[[Zaros]]' Strength** (Sign says [[Afthor]]'s Strength but Afthor is crossed out because [[Brilis]] is a sponsor and it was changed last minute to Zaros' Strength). Two versions:
- Pure Strength: need at least a 25 on an athletics check to win highest prize (200 tokens)
- Precise Strength: need exactly a 20 (200 tokens)

**Archery/Shooting:** Targets are 25 ft. and 50 ft. away, need at least a 25 to get highest prize (100 tokens)

**Raffle:** 1 gp to enter. Prizes: random magic items and tokens, called throughout the day

**Assassin:** May pay 5 gp on entering the fair, you get a brightly colored embroidered cloth, can attempt to steal other people's, if you have yours at end of fair you get 15 gp, other prizes doled out based on how many you end with

**Drink Off:** First to three Constitution checks (wager tokens)

**Dance Competition:** Several performance checks (30 tokens)

**Anna's Power Potions:** Free feat (no ASI) but receive a curse (cost: 500 gp)

**Hide and Seek:** There is two omen children (one tiefling (Dèsirèe, daughter of Lilien) and one assimar (Varis)), who invite the players to play hide and seek with them. Dèsirèe will be the seeker (+2 Inv). If a player wins, she will say the following: "Haha, you're really good at hide n' seek. Go ahead and give them their prize Varis. *Varis will look surprised and then panicky as he doesn't have anything to give.* Sigh, that's ok. Just give this (she will produce a ruby coin) to my mom and she'll give you something." She and Varis will then run off before the players can question her.

### Tournies

**Jousting:** Can use a horse provided by them but you can also bring your own mount (within reason) and may gain benefits from your mount. Winner: dinner with Eltarien and his friends/coterie

**Dragonchess:** Winner: 25,000 gold pieces

**Wrestling:** No weapons, armor, etc. fight (magic items that can be reasonably concealed can be used but may require sleight of hand checks). Winner: any rare magic item

**Grand Brawl:** Free for all fight. Winner: one favor from one of the sponsors of your choosing or from Eltarien

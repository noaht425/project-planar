Dates use the [[Age of the Many]] calendar. The Age of the Few (AF) counts down to the Age of the Many (AM), which counts up, and there is no year zero. A year has four months of a hundred days each, in the order Aucaela, Auctera, Morcaela and Mortera.

## The Age of the Few

- **c. 10,300 AF.** The Planar Beings are born.
- **c. 10,000 AF.** The six [[The Judgements|Judgements]] are created. [[The King of Day]], [[The King of Night]] and the five dragon lords of [[Tarkis]] are born.
- **c. 9,900 AF.** The five lords make their second dragons.
- **c. 9,000 AF.** The Kings marry.
- **c. 8,900 AF.** [[Eos]] and [[Cado]] are born.
- **c. 8,300 AF.** [[Vetur]] begins laying out everything that has sunk in the realm of water.
- **4,500 to 3,097 AF.** [[Indri]] works on the King of Night, persuading him that the Judgements are too powerful in their combined form.
- **3,097 AF.** [[The Division]]. Night splits each Judgement into an archangel and an archdevil, and each Judgement's place into a heaven and a hell.
- **3,096 AF.** [[Syzygy|The Syzygy]]. Day divorces Night. In the fight Day is scarred and Night is blinded, and [[Nullis]] begins to separate into a light half and a dark half. Cado is recorded for the last time.
- **3,096 AF.** [[Tiyanak]] kills the original Indri in secret, takes his name and most of his memories, and takes his place in [[Trickery]].
- **2,987 AF.** The [[Divine War]] begins. [[Gwenhael]] gives up his claim to territory in its first year.
- **50 AF.** The Divine War ends with the peace between [[Verita]] and Tiyanak. The [[Nekerux|Nekerukes]] are made to judge the dead.
- **50 AF.** [[Great Separation|The Great Separation]]. The heavens and the hells come apart. A crack forms in Indri's cage.
- **50 AF.** [[The Planar Collision]]. The realms are driven into each other. Tarkis shatters into the elemental realms, and pieces of [[Ventis]] and the hells fall into it.
- **39 to 10 AF.** [[The Coalescence]]. The thousands of minor schools of [[Arcanis]] become eight.
- **10 AF.** [[Arenis]] is made from pieces of every other realm. The nine [[Progenitors]] are born into it.

## The Age of the Many

- **1 Aucaela to 1 Auctera, 1 AM.** [[Council of Creation|The Council of Creation]]. The nine Progenitors make the nine peoples.
- **38 Aucaela, 1 AM.** [[Ius Primum]] is signed.
- **98 Aucaela, 1 AM.** [[Kleipur]] gives the six titans their power.
- **56 Morcaela, 2 AM.** [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]] is founded, with [[Genasi]] as its first king.
- **61 Morcaela, 2 AM.** [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] is founded.
- **87 Mortera, 2 AM.** [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] is founded.
- **1 Aucaela, 3 AM.** [[Kingdom of Thalamas|Thalamas]] is founded.
- **8 Aucaela, 3 AM.** [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]] is founded.
- **17 Aucaela, 3 AM.** [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]] and [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]] are founded on the same day.
- **3 AM.** [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]] is founded.
- **c. 5 AM.** Genasi begins building [[Genoan]].
- **c. 40 AM.** [[The Nexus]] is raised between the three continents, and the rulers first meet at the [[Panarex]].
- **62 AM.** Genasi tires of ruling and gives the throne of Geno to his son, [[Genasi II]].
- **72 Morcaela, 105 AM.** Thalamas breaks apart in civil war, over the rumours about Ius Primum and the disappearances of elves. [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] and [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]] are formed.
- **105 to c. 130 AM.** The scholars of [[Lenetha]] purge the everyday words of [[Sylvan]] that sound like Low Fae.
- **47 Morcaela, 137 AM.** [[Hekate]] is born in [[Hekatania]].
- **12 Auctera, 152 AM.** Hekate first travels to Arcanis.
- **36 Aucaela, 154 AM.** Hekate starts a school for local children, which will become the [[Aravis Academy]].
- **48 Morcaela, 157 AM.** Hekate ascends, the youngest person ever to do so. Two days later she makes her school a university.
- **56 Morcaela, 202 AM.** On Geno's two hundredth anniversary, [[Alistar Stormjaw]] tries to open a portal to Tarkis in his laboratory in the capital. Tarkis has been bleeding into the city since. Dragonborn are banned from Geno.
- **96 Morcaela, 202 AM.** Stormjaw founds [[Kingdom of Tama|Tama]] with exiles from the other kingdoms and becomes its first king.
- **225 to 336 AM.** The [[Valley of the Colossi]] is built between Sylfaen and Oinarria.
- **c. 300 AM.** [[Torren (Old Common)|Torren]] has worn down into Common, which is now spoken across the world.
- **86 Auctera, 397 AM.** The war between Krotaphos and Sylfaen begins.
- **33 Aucaela, 405 AM.** [[Iras]] kills [[Genasi VII]] and takes the throne of Geno. [[Lux]] takes all of his other descendants into [[Pride]].
- **36 Aucaela, 405 AM.** Genasi fights Lux over it and loses in four days.
- **65 Mortera, 419 AM.** Iras abdicates. [[Gaius Flaminius]] is chosen to succeed him.
- **97 Morcaela, 423 AM.** Geno declares neutrality in the war.
- **2 Mortera, 424 AM.** Oinarria agrees to join the war on Sylfaen's side.
- **99 Morcaela, 427 AM.** Krotaphos loses the war. Iudimian is restricted to religious use.
- **3 Mortera, 427 AM.** Krotaphos is cut down to a circle, and the land outside it goes to Geno and Oinarria.
- **428 AM.** Mathair hosts the first [[La Grande Mascarade]] in [[Omphalos]].
- **c. 430 AM.** [[Neema]] begins quietly moving ill-leaning Omens out of reach of the purification.
- **32 Morcaela, 546 AM.** Gaius Flaminius dies, and no successor is chosen.
- **36 Morcaela, 546 AM.** [[The Great Unrest]] begins in Geno.
- **632 AM.** [[Nara Veril]] is born.
- **652 AM.** [[Afthor]] kills [[Zaros]] and [[Vari]], drinks their blood, and takes the realm of air. The storm begins.
- **16 Auctera, 657 AM.** The Great Unrest ends when Nara Veril becomes queen of Geno.
- **22 Morcaela, 687 AM.** [[XLZL]] is made.
- **c. 700 AM.** XLZL is elected to lead Regno.
- **44 Mortera, 700 AM.** Indri escapes the underworld as the god of vengeance. Reports of a mad god in the mortal realm begin.

## 702 AM

- **85 Morcaela.** [[Eltarien's Extraplanar Extravaganza]] comes to Starkon. The campaign begins.
- **Morcaela.** At La Grande Mascarade in Omphalos, [[Theophilius Fanatis]] attacks the king of Sylfaen under an enchantment. Sylfaen orders the arrest of every citizen of Krotaphos. Geno declares neutrality.

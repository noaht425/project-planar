Belongs to [[Ignatius]]. A fairly simple leather bound tome (crimson with gold stitching along the edges) with a slight sheen if you look at it from the right angle.

> *The room it had been kept in was still scorched and nobody had repainted. There are marks up two walls and across part of the ceiling, and a clean rectangle on the desk where the book had been lying. The book has no mark on it anywhere. The desk is burnt right up to the edge of where it sat.*

Requires attunement by a Wizard. Acts as a normal spell book would.

The book has 2 charges. Whenever you cast a spell of 1st level or higher, you may expend one charge and increase the spell's effective level by 1. You regain all spent charges at midnight.

While wielding this book, your spell save and spell attack bonus increase by 1.

## Scaling

**Level 5:** +1 to spell save/spell attack becomes +2; you learn one of the rune knight fighter's runes ignoring any level requirements and may inscribe it into your book to use once per day, you may switch the rune after a long rest

**Level 9:** You may attempt to steal a spell slot from a target. As an action you force a creature to make a saving throw using their spell casting modifier against your spell save DC. If they fail they lose the highest spell slot they have and you may use it. If the target succeeds the save by 5 or less, you steal their lowest level spell slot. After a long rest, you lose all stolen spell slots. You may use this feature a number of times per day equal to half your proficiency bonus rounded down, you may not target one creature more than once per day.

**Level 13:** twice per day, as an action, you can cast the fireball spell at 5th level without expending a spell slot, you may change the damage type to any other damage type of your choice; once per day as a bonus action you may give up to two creatures resistance to all damage types for 10 minutes.

**Level 17:** whenever you expend a spell slot, roll 2d4, if either number matches the level of spell slot expended, the spell slot is refunded, if both match the spell is cast again without consuming a spell slot; your intelligence score cannot be lowered by any means magic or physical.

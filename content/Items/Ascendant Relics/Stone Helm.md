Belongs to [[Vato]].

Your AC increases by 1.

## Unlimited Endurance
Your hit point maximum increases by 2 times your level, whenever you gain a level, your hitpoint maximum increases by 2. When you roll hit dice to regain health, you may take the maximum value for a number of them equal to half of the number you expend, rounded up.

## Pummeling
The number you need to roll a critical hit with weapon attacks is reduced by 1, this effect can stack. If you would deal bludgeoning damage to an enemy, you may grant them vulnerability to bludgeoning damage. You may do this a number of times per day equal to you proficiency bonus.

## Perfect Armor
You ignore critical hits.

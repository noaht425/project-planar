Belongs to [[Isadora]]. A set of 5d6 made of obsidian and inscribed with gold.

> *She offered me one throw and let me name the stake myself, since that was only fair, so I named something I was certain was worthless. That was the first mistake. The second was taking three dice when she said I could take up to three, since it had not occurred to me that she already knew what they were going to do. Two of them came up the same. The floor of that room is gold now, as is the table the dice landed on, as is the chair I had been sitting in. She let me keep the chair. I have it still. I cannot bring myself to sell it, nor can I explain why.*

## Fate Untamed
After you roll these dice, you may choose up to two of them. Their value becomes equal to the value of another rolled die. This does not impact the Magical Multitudes ability.

## Gambling Expert
You have advantage on all Charisma (Deception), Dexterity (Slight of Hand), Wisdom (Perception), and Wisdom (Insight) checks made while playing games that involve gambling, lying, and other such trickery.

## Magical Multitudes
As an action you may roll up to 3 dice, the following effect(s) occur based on what you roll (range of 50 ft.) Once you roll a die in this way, you cannot roll it again until the next dawn:

1. You cast the fireball spell at 5th level centered at wherever the die landed. The DC for this spell is 16.
2. All creatures within 20 ft. of where the die lands are polymorphed into rabbits for 5d6 hours or until they are reduced to 0 hitpoints.
3. The stats of each creature within 30 ft. of where the die landed are randomized. For each stat roll 1d20 + 1d10 and that stat becomes the total of those dice for the next hour.
4. All creatures within 50 ft. of where the die lands become blind and deaf for the next 15 minutes.
5. All creatures within 35 ft. of where the die lands randomly switch places with another creature within the radius.
6. The ground within 10 ft. of where the die lands turns to liquid gold for 1d4 turns. The speed of all creatures within the radius is reduced to 0. Additionally, creatures within the radius have disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

If you roll at least 2 dice and get the same number on two or more dice, you may have those dice combine their effect and change to the below. If you do, choose the location of one of the dice and use that:

1. All creatures within 30 ft. of where the die landed must make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw, taking 63 (18d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
2. All creatures within 20 ft. of where the die landed are transformed into a random beast of a challenge rating 10 or less for 5d6 hours or until they are reduced to 0 hitpoints.
3. The stats of each creature within 30 ft. of where the die landed are randomized. For each stat roll 2d20 and that stat becomes the total of those dice for the next hour. Additionally roll 1d20, their speed(s) becomes the result times 5 for the next hour.
4. For 1 minute, all creatures within 50 ft. of where the die lands come under the effects of the confusion spell.
5. All creatures within 70 ft. of where the die lands randomly switch places with another creature within the radius. They also switch current hitpoints with the creature that they switched with for 1 minute. If this would increase a creature's hitpoints beyond their maximum, the amount above maximum become temporary hitpoints.
6. Everything (and I mean everything) within 10 ft. of where the die lands begins to turn to gold. Creatures act as though they are under the effects of the flesh to stone spell and the spell wears off after 24 hours. Objects, items, and all non-creatures are turned to gold permanently. If they are magic items, they retain their magic properties.

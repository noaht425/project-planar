Belongs to [[Loime]]. Green, gemlike spider (a few inches in diameter).

> *The spider is in his chest, under the coat, and it is alive. He opened the coat for me without being asked, which I had not wanted. In the time it was open the thing moved twice. He watched me watch it. He was pleased.*

**Creation:** Fed a venomous spider a gem from the heart of the [[Elemental Plane of Noxia]] and used this as ascension relic. Only known use of a living creature as ascension relic.

## Caustic Attunement
When you attune to this artifact, the spider embeds itself somewhere in your body. When it does, make a Constitution saving throw DC 20. On a failure you take 12d12 poison damage and gain 2 levels of exhaustion. On a success you take half as much damage and receive no levels of exhaustion. This damage ignores resistances. If this damage would reduce you to 0 hit points, no spell of a level lower than 6 can heal you. Any death saving throws rolled for the next hour are rolled with disadvantage.

## Toxin of Life
When you would be dealt poison damage, you instead heal that much instead.

## Venomous Strikes
All weapon attacks and unarmed attacks you make deal an extra 3d8 of poison damage. This damage ignores resistances.

## Poisonous Presence
As an action, you begin to radiate an aura that has a radius of 20 ft. This aura lasts for 1 minute. If the effect requires a saving throw, the difficulty is equal to 16. When you do, select one of the following options:

- All hostile creatures within the aura can make either an action or a bonus action on their turn but not both. Additionally, hostile creatures within the aura can make a maximum of two attacks on their turn.
- When a hostile creature enters the aura for the first time on its turn or starts its turn in the aura, it must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure it take 4d8 poison damage and cannot regain hitpoints until the start of its next turn. On a success it takes half as much damage and can heal as normal.
- All creatures within the aura treat resistances as vulnerabilities and all vulnerabilities as resistances.
- When a creature starts its turn within the aura, roll 1d6 (1:STR, 2:DEX, 3:CON, 4:INT, 5:WIS, 6:CHA). That creature must make a saving throw of that stat. If it succeeds, until the start of your next turn it receives advantage on all saving throws of that type but disadvantage on all others. If it fails, the opposite happens.

Once you use this feature, you may not do so again until you take a long rest. Alternatively you may choose to reduce your maximum hitpoints by an amount equal to 8 times your constitution modifier (minimum of 8); when you do so you regain a use of this feature. This reduction lasts for 1d4 days.

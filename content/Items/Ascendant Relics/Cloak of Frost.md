Created by [[Sarma]].

> *I was allowed to hold it for a moment. The cold does not come off it. It goes into you, and it stays.*

Your AC increases by 2.

## Heart of Ice
While wearing this cloak, you have immunity to Cold damage and cannot gain levels of exhaustion from extreme cold weather.

## Encrusted with Frost
When a creature misses a melee attack against you, they must roll a Dexterity saving throw DC 17, on a fail, they gain the restrained condition until the end of their next turn as they are encrusted in shards of ice.

You gain access to the following spells, the DC for these spells is equal to 8 + PB + CON, you can cast each spell once per day without expending a spell slot:

- Cone of Cold
- Otiluke's Freezing Sphere
- Wall of Ice

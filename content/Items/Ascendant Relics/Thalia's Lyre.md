Gifted to [[Thalia]] by [[Atris]].

You may use this item as a spell casting focus. While attuned to this item, your Charisma score increases by 4 to a maximum of 30. You are immune to the charmed condition.

## Enchanting Melody
When you use this lyre to cast a spell that would require a creature to make a saving throw, you may choose one of the following:

- That creature subtracts half of your Charisma score from their roll
- That creature takes 5d10 psychic damage if they succeed the save, or half if they fail.
- Up to two target allied creatures within 15 feet of that creature may use their reaction to make an attack against that creature.

You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain all expended uses on a long rest.

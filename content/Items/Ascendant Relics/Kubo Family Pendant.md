Belongs to [[Nausicaä]]. A glimmering silver necklace with a brass gear surrounding a half sun/half moon.

Whenever you expend a sorcery point, this item gains 2 charges. While not in combat you can expend any number of charges and regain that many hit-points. You lose all charges at midnight.

While wearing this pendant, you gain +2 to Wisdom (Insight and Perception) checks to determine the proper order of things.

One time use time stop spell.

## Scaling

**Level 5:** you gain +2 to your passive perception; you gain a sorcerous version of the chronurgy wizard feature Temporal Awareness (add your CHA modifier to Initiative rolls)

**Level 9:** gain +1 to spell save/spell attack; once you reach level 9 you may learn one spell each from the cleric, druid, and warlock spell lists, those spells count as sorcerer spells for you, they must be of a level that you can learn.

**Level 13:** you gain immunity to the charmed and frightened conditions; when a creature attempts to counterspell your spell or cast dispel magic on one of your spells, they must make a CHA saving throw or take a number of d4 psychic damage equal to your CHA modifier plus the level of your spell.

**Level 17:** at the completion of a long rest, you may choose a subclass from any class, you gain all subclass features from levels 3 and below, if a feature requires a resource that you don't have, you gain an amount equal to your half your proficiency bonus rounded up, if that feature would use one of your ability scores or modifiers, use your CHA instead, you lose all features after a long rest unless you expend an amount of spell slot levels equal to half the total level of all of your spell slots per subclass you have from this feature.

Crafted for [[The Seventh (Septimus)]] by [[Pavlas]]. Though originally crafted to hold Septimus, his power has infused them.

> *A smith in Panthea was sent a single link of it to repair, without being told what it had come off. She told me she had got it as hot as her forge goes and got nowhere, and that the metal took the heat and gave it back out as light. She sent it on to somebody else. I did not tell her what she had been holding, because I had been asked not to.*

Weapon (Spiked Chain), artifact (requires attunement)

## Properties

While attuned and wielding it, you gain:

- Your AC increases by 2.
- Your speed decreases by 5 ft.
- The weapon deals an extra 2d8 radiant damage on a hit.
- You have advantage on attack rolls against celestials; celestials have advantage on attack rolls against you.
- You can cast command (save DC 17) a number of times per day equal to your proficiency bonus.

### Hold Them Down (action). 

Make a weapon attack. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw (or the wielder's spell save DC, if higher) or be restrained. At the start of each of its turns you may deal 2d8 radiant to it and take half yourself; the radiant rises 1d8 per failed save (max 6d8). It repeats the save at the end of each turn. On the initial save, its speed is halved until the end of your next turn.

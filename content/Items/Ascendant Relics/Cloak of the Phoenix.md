Belongs to [[Moto]].

## Fiery Rebirth
Once per day, when you would be reduced to 0 hitpoints, you instead go to half your hitpoint maximum. When you do, all creatures within 50 ft. of you must make a DC 18 DEX save, on a fail they take 10d10 fire damage, or half on a save.

## Healing Flames
Whenever you deal fire damage to an enemy, you may heal an ally within 10 ft of you or a recipient of the damage for a number of d4 equal to you proficiency bonus.

## Potent Fire
If you would deal fire damage to an enemy, you may reroll any 1s or 2s on damage dice, you must use the new roll.

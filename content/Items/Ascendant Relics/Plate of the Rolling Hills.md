## Hill's Durability
You have resistance to all damage types except psychic. Additionally, you are immune to poison damage, the poisoned condition, and disease.

## Unstoppable
Whenever a creature attacks you, subtract an amount from its roll equal to the number of times you have been attacked since the start of your last turn. You cannot be moved against your will.

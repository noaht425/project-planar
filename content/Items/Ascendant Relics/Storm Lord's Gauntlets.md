Created by [[Afthor]], infused with the blood of [[Zaros]] and [[Vari]].

## Might of the Titans
While wearing these gauntlets, your strength score becomes 29. If your Strength is already equal to or greater than 29, ignore this effect.

## Zaros' Rage
All attacks you make deal an amount of extra d4 lightning damage equal to your Constitution modifier.

## Thundering Parry
When a creature makes a melee attack against you, you may roll a d20. If you get a result within 1 of what the creature attacking you rolled on their d20, the attacking creature must make a DC 20 strength saving throw. On a failed save, they take 8d8 thunder damage and gain the stunned condition until the end of their next turn.

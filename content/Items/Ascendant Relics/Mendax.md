Used to belong to [[Tiyanak]], and was an ordinary weapon at the time. Tiyanak killed the original [[Indri]] with it in 3,096 AF.

Indri had been researching the ascension process before that night. In the moment of dying, Indri claimed the dagger and used it as both the relic and the source of power for the ascension. It is the only recorded case of one object serving as both.

So Indri died, went to the afterlife and was caged there by Tiyanak, and kept a great deal of power through all of it, because the ascension completed as the death did. See [[Ascension Process]] for what the ritual normally requires.

*Weapon (+3 dagger), artifact (requires attunement)*

This weapon deals an amount of extra d4 psychic damage equal to your Charisma or Intelligence modifier (whichever is higher).

The dagger cannot have identify or legend lore cast on it and if it does, it gives blatantly incorrect information.

## Impeccable Discernment
Whenever you make a Wisdom (Insight or Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check to see illusions or detect a lie, you can treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.

This dagger has 5 charges, it regains all charges on a long rest.

## Stolen Illusion
When you see a creature cast a spell that creates an illusion of any sort, you may expend a charge. When you do, that creature must make a DC 16 saving throw using their spell casting modifier, on a failure you gain control of the illusion. You must begin to concentrate on it if it requires concentration. While you have control of the spell, it uses the game statistics of an Animated Armor, though it still appears as whatever the illusion originally one. The opposing caster does not immediately know they have lost the illusion, though if they expect it to act a certain way and it doesn't, they may get suspicious. You may spend any amount of additional charges to increase the DC by 4.

## What is Truth
Spend 2 charges. When you do, you may choose truth or lies. Each creature within 25 ft. of you must make a DC 18 Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, that creature can't speak a deliberate lie if you chose truth, or a deliberate truth if you chose lies for 1 hour. You know whether each creature succeeds or fails on its saving throw. If a creature fails, it is also unaware that you cast this spell, though it will be aware of its effects once it begins speaking.

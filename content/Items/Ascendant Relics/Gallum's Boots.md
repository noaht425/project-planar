## Cloud's Speed
You gain a fly speed of 100 ft.

## Can't be Touched
If a melee attack misses you by more than 5, you may use your reaction to have that attack instead hit a creature within range of the attacker. Additionally, ranged attacks against you have disadvantage.

## Weightless
You have total control over your weight. You can reduce your weight to a minimum of 10 pounds or increase it to a maximum of 500 pounds.

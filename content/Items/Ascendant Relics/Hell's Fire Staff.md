Belongs to [[Iras]].

> *The staff keeps no count of what it has sent to Pride and Iras does not keep one either. Somebody does. A clerk in Pride read me a figure, in the hundreds, and would not tell me how far back the record went or whether anything was still being added to it.*

## Beneficial Properties
While the staff is on your person, you gain the following benefits:

- You are immune to fire damage
- You can wield the staff as a +3 quarterstaff that deals an extra 10 (3d6) fire damage on hit
- You ignore resistance to fire damage and treat immunity to fire damage as a resistance for creatures you are fighting.
- When you cast a spell that consumes a spell slot and that deals fire damage, you may add 3d6 fire damage to one roll of that spell's damage

## Damnation
When you kill a creature, the creature's soul is immediately sent to the domain of [[Lux]], [[Pride]]. The being must have a soul for this feature to activate.

## Invoke the Damned
Once per long rest, you may expend and roll a number of hit dice up to your proficiency bonus. You take necrotic damage equal to the total, this damage cannot be prevented. You may summon a number of Spined Devils equal to the amount of hit dice you expended. This number cannot be greater than the number of souls you have claimed with this staff's Damnation feature. You may choose to reduce your hit point maximum by twice the amount rolled; if you do, you may use this property to summon Barbed Devils instead. Your hit point maximum returns to its original value when you take a long rest. All creatures summoned by this property follow your direct orders, though they might try to find loopholes and errors in your wording, and return to the Hells after 10 minutes.

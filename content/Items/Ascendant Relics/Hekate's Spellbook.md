Crafted by [[Hekate]].

> *She let me read it, which I had not expected, and sat with me while I did. It holds Time Stop, in her own hand, on the last page anyone has written on. It holds nothing whatever about Arcanis. I turned every leaf, and there is not one line in it about the realm she took her power out of, and she watched me look for it.*

Requires attunement by a Wizard. This book may be used a Wizard's spellbook.

## Always Prepared
You have advantage on saving throws and spell attacks against you have disadvantage for all spells that you have prepared.

## Well of Magic
When you cast a spell that would expend a spell slot, roll a d6. If the result of the die matches the level of spell slot you expended, you may regain that spell slot. You may expend a hitpoint die to roll an extra d6, if either match you regain the slot, if both match the spell is also cast again for free.

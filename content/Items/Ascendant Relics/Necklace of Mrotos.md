Belongs to [[Hieronymous]]. A braided leather necklace with a pendant of crossed ivory antlers.

> *The antlers show the wearer who has about a day left in them. I have met one person who was in the room when it happened and was told so to her face. She was told kindly. She has had nineteen years since, so either the thing was wrong once or being told is what it is for. She still keeps the date.*

When you heal a creature using a spell slot of first level or higher, you get a glimpse into that creatures future, once per day per creature, after healing a creature you may increase or decrease its next d20 roll by half your proficiency bonus rounded down.

Whenever a creature fails a saving throw against one of your spells, you may deal 1d4 necrotic damage to that creature, if it failed by 5 or more, the damage increases to 2d4.

## Scaling

**Level 5:** once per day you may ask a question of a deity of fate/divination, it must be within their ability to answer, they will answer it truthfully, if you ask a yes or no question you will get a clear answer, if you ask a more complex question you may get a more complex answer

**Level 9:** you have advantage on initiative rolls; you can tell when people may die soon, if you concentrate for at least 10 seconds, the symbol of Mrotos appears hovering above anyone with a reasonable chance of dying within the next 24 hours

**Level 13:** when a creature within 30 ft of you succeeds or fails an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you may expend any number of spell slots and increase or decrease their roll by the combined total of the levels spent, if you expend more than 5 levels at one time, make a DC 10+total CON save or gain a level of exhaustion

**Level 17:** creatures that you are touching cannot die; when you fall to 0 hitpoints, the creature that last hit you must make a CON save or take a number of d8 necrotic damage equal to your level, this damage ignores resistances and if the target is immune they take half damage, after you use this feature you must wait 1d4 days before using it again

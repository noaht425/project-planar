## Cursed
The spider orchid that you have loved and cared for seems to have bonded with you, both emotionally and physically. It is now attached to your skull, feeding on your joy and sorrow, your love and fear, and using them to strengthen itself and you.

Requires Attunement.

You are vulnerable to fire damage.

When you deal at least 8 damage to a creature, you heal 1d4 hitpoints.

You must water the orchid every day with blood. It will not accept your blood and if it is not fed by the end of the day you must make a persuasion check with DC equal to 7 + 3 times the number of days it has gone without sustenance. If you fail, you gain a stack of sanguine hunger. For each stack you subtract 1 from all attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws. You lose all stacks once the orchid is fed. In exchange, it grants you protection and clarity, reducing all damage you take by 1d4 and adding 1d4 to all Intelligence and Wisdom checks and saving throws made for a number of hours based on how much blood you feed it.

**Scaling:**

**Level 5:** you gain advantage on all nature and medicine checks; when you use your wild shape, you gain the following effects: increase move speed by 10 ft, +2 AC, +PB to perception and investigation, additionally your plant smells blood and the longer you stay in wild shape, the more it will try and take control to hunt for blood (every 10 minutes make a wisdom saving throw DC 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20 or the plant will take over for the rest of wild shape, if in combat then DC 14 at the start of each of your turns)

**Level 9:** 1d4 healing and feeding bonus increases to 2d4; you gain advantage on all checks made to climb walls and you can climb walls that would otherwise be unclimbable to other creatures

**Level 13:** As an action, you gain the following conditions and benefits for one hour: you gain the poisoned condition, you take 1d10 poison damage at the start of each of your turns, when you deal damage to a creature, all creatures (target included) within 15 feet of the target must make a CON save against your spell save or take 3d8 poison damage or half on a successful save, when you heal a creature other than yourself you may also heal the poisoned condition from that creature. You may use this ability once per day

## Healed
The spider orchid that you have loved and cared for seems to have bonded with you, both emotionally and physically. It now lives symbiotically with you, growing as you grow and helping you along your journey.

Requires Attunement.

When you deal at least 5 damage to a creature, you heal 1d4 hitpoints.

The orchid now grows extra edible flowers. It grows 3 per day which can safely be plucked. As a bonus action, a creature may consume a flower gaining protection and clarity. For one minute, reduce all damage you take by 1d4 and add 1d4 to all Intelligence and Wisdom checks and saving throws made. You can also use the flowers as potion ingredients to make potions of sleep, resistance, growth, and healing.

**Scaling:**

**Level 5:** you gain advantage on all nature and medicine checks; when in wild shape the plant grants you +1 AC, + 5 movement speed, and +3 to perception and investigation checks

**Level 9:** 1d4 healing and flower bonus increase to 2d4; you gain advantage on all checks made to climb walls and you can climb walls that would otherwise be unclimbable to other creatures; any creature that touches your blood must make a CON save against your spell save DC or be paralyzed for 10 seconds (they are then immune for 24 hours)

**Level 13:** the plant now grows 5 flowers per day; you can now make superior healing, invulnerability, poison, and clairvoyance potions; you no longer need food as long as you spend at least 2 hours per day in sunlight

**Level 17:** you may expend two charges of your wildshape feature to transform into a Wisteria Dragon (you use its CHA score), you may cast spells you know while in this form

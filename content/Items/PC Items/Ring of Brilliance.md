*Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement)*

Belongs to [[Wisp]]]]. A gold ring with a brilliant diamong set into it.

When a creature deals damage to you, that creature takes an amount of radiant damage equal to half your profiency bonus rounded down.

## Scaling

**Level 5:** When you cast a spell using a spell slot that does radiant damage or heals a creature, you may add your proficiency bonus to one roll of the spell.

**Level 9:** Whenever you are hit by an attack, your AC increases by 1 until the start of your next turn

**Level 13:** You have resistance to radiant and fire damage. All damage you do ignores resistance to radiant and fire damage. You are immune to the blinded condition.

*Wondrous item, armor (requires attunement)*

Mysterious Pixie Armor

Belongs to [[Aura Kendarin of House Yavimaya]]. A small pixie that follows her around and can become the very armor that she wears.

A fine suit of armor, covered in filigree designs immitating the patterns on insects. A large set of filigreed butterfly wings are depicted on the back of the armor.

Reduce all incoming damage dealt to you by an amount equal to half your proficiency bonus, rounded down.

## Scaling

**Level 5:** You gain expertise in Charimsa (Deception) checks. When you use the Thunder Gauntlets or Lightning Launcher feature of the armor, you may roll an additional damage die and add it to the total damage.

**Level 9:** You gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed while wearing the armor as the filigree wings become real.

Flute of the Feywilds

*Wondrous item, artifact (flute), requires attunement*

A Magical Flute that can turn into a rapier when the right tune is played

Belongs to [[Deryl Nightriver]]. 

A flute carved from the finest wood that [[Iris]] can offer. Patterns seem to dance off of the flute when melodies are played and fly off of the blade when it is swung.

As an action, you may cause the flute to change forms between the flute and the rapier.

- As a rapier, damage done by this weapon is considered magical for the purposes of overcoming resistances. Additionally, you cannot be disarmed while wielding it.
- As a flute, you may add your proficiency bonus to performance checks you make while it is in flute form.

## Scaling

**Level 5:** Attacks made by your familiar deal an amount of extra fire damage equal to your spellcasting modifier. You cannot be forced to tell the truth.

**Level 9:** As a rapier, you may use your reaction to add your Charisma modifier to your AC and move an amount of feet equal to 5 times your Charisma modifier. You may use this reaction a number of times per day equal to your proficiency bonus. As a flute, when you cast a spell using a spell slot of a higher level than the spell, you may decrease the level you are casting it at by 2, when you do, you may cast the spell twice without expending another spel slot at the diminished level.

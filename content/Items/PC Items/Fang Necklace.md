*Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement)*

Belongs to [[Nerinè Melpomene]]. A necklace of a sharp fang belonging to some large animal with a black leather chord.

You have resistance to psychic damage.

While wearing this necklace, you may expend your Psionic Energy dice to access certain powers beyond normal ability. You may cast spells from the Enchantment and Illusion schools. When you do, expend a number of Psionic Energy dice equal to one plus the level of the spell, up to a maximum of 2. You cast the selected spell using your Intelligence as your spell casting stat. When you cast a spell in this way, roll the Psionic Energy dice you expended, you take an amount of force damage equal to the result. When you reach 5th level, the maximum increases to 3, at 7th level it becomes 4, and at 9th it becomes 5.

## Scaling

**Level 5:** When you deal psychic damage to a creature you may reroll any 1s that you roll, you must use the new roll.

**Level 9:** Your AC increases by 1 and your hitpoint maximum increases by 9, additionally whenever your hitpoint maximum increases from leveling up, you gain an additional hitpoint; you have advantage on any saves made against magical effects to read your mind, scry on you, or any other similar effects.

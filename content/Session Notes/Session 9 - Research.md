8-26 Mortera, 702

## On the Boat

- Aura, Muffin, and Nerinè heard what they took for a clap of thunder, then placed it as a scream from a long way off. Mostly rage in it.
- Recorded in the notes as [[Chore]], the ascendant goddess of pirates. [[Kalinekra]] was her partner and died in [[Session 8 - Revived|Session 8]].
- The party floated travelling by land for a while.

## Nerinè's Dream

- A dream a night or two later. Nerinè was sitting in a professor's office with their patron in front of them.
- He asked about the siren that died, who was the partner of a dear friend of his.
- Nerinè said it was self-defence. A natural 20 on persuasion carried it.

## Geno, and the Eggs

- Wisp and Nerinè found the egg seller and asked after the egg of [[Zaros]], offering the [[Brilis]] egg in trade. They ended up buying the Zaros egg for extra on top.
- Aura asked after Krotaphos. The fighting has stopped in the sense that people have stopped fighting back, and [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] is in active occupation.
- Nothing else notable anywhere in the mortal plane. For news worth having, the other planes.

## Mensosde Talthu

- Geno's library city, at the north end of the kingdom. The name comes out roughly as Mens' hoard.
- A tall minotaur man behind the front desk. Every item borrowed out of the building costs a donation. Anything read on the premises is covered by a single donation.
- Wisp handed over a stack of her own books. The party got two weeks.

## Wisp on Brilis

- Made by [[Pyrrha]] with the help of the [[Archangels|archangels]].
- A great deal of writing about how great he is, most of it by other people, with very little in it about what makes him so. The demon-fighting stories are the unreliable ones. The accounts that hold up best have him going around encouraging people to build temples to him.
- Wisp rolled a natural 20 and wrote twenty-four books of it. The Brillissey. She published it.
- Called Brilis to show him and got a pre-recorded message saying he was out.

## Nerinè on the Zygaris Society

- The [[Zygaris Society]] has existed as long as mortals have existed and worshipped dragons.
- Baris Veril left the post in 602, almost exactly a hundred years ago. No record of any leader between him and Irtha.
- [[Irtha Melpomene|Irtha]] is the current head and has led it twenty-four years. Very little public information on her.
- One branch per dragon, the two dead ones included. Records conflict on branch heads, with people shown holding a post at the same time as somebody else.
- Nerinè raised it with Irtha, who told them to come back to the base for a debrief. She learned about [[Mendax]] from the branch.

## Nerinè on Relics and Mendax

- Outside direct notes of the process, the party now has everything on ascension except the exact steps. What is missing is the words to say.
- Mendax belonged to [[Tiyanak]] and was made for an intended target.
- It is referenced after the split of the archangels and archdevils, and it shows up in the records as an ascendant relic within the past couple of years, under its description, with the name left off.
- A source names [[Lenetha]] in [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] and somebody there who will supposedly sell ascension notes. See [[Pellan]].

## Deryl on Ius Primum

- Found a written agreement, a copy of an original, called [[Ius Primum]].
- Entered into by somebody with two archfey and observed by [[Indri]]. It led to the creation of [[Elves|elves]] in exchange for aid, and any archfey may claim the soul of any elf. A number of stipulations hang off that.
- A very small clause at the bottom that he could not make out without magnification.

## Deryl on What Happens When a God Dies

- No text settles it, because it has not happened until now.
- The ascendant gods started as mortals, so they have mortal souls. What becomes of the soul on ascension is not clear.
- The reading that holds up is that they keep it and it is hosted in the body of a god.
- A competing theory has it replaced by a god's soul, which leaves the question of where that one came from.
- Best guess on the shelf: a dead god goes where everybody else goes, unless something claims it.

## Aura on the Progenitors

Nine of them, created during [[The Planar Collision]] by the library's account. What each entry gave:

- [[Kleipur]]. Made first, physically tall. [[Giantkin]].
- [[Minem]]. [[Humans]].
- [[Sylvana Lorien]]. [[Elves]]. Very proud, of her creations more than of herself. Several accounts of her killing people who were cruel to elves, including a human child who pushed an elf girl and was turned into a mass of cockroaches. She is the party to Ius Primum.
- [[Shram]]. [[Orcs]].
- [[Thean]]. [[Omens]], made from the leftover pieces of the Judgements. She went into hiding when the war between Sylfaen and Krotaphos started, over her children being killed, and is probably still in the cave.
- [[Numen]]. [[Beastfolk]], with the dragons assisting on the dragonborn.
- [[Genasi]]. [[Elementals]], also with help from the dragons.
- [[Talav]]. [[Earthborn]], meaning dwarves, gnomes, and halflings.
- [[Sithi]]. [[Fae]], assisted by the archfey, and she gave up her name for it. Nobody has seen her since.

No other book in the library mentions Ius Primum, and none of them name the archfey who assisted.

## Aura on Trickery and the Hooded Figure

- [[Trickery]] is one of the most rules-obsessed places in the world, with the domain of time the likely rival. Each of its cities has its own purpose.
- Nobody has suspected Tiyanak, because deceit and trickery are what Indri is for. He draws attention when he stops lying.
- News of the hooded figure that brings destruction in its wake mentions the dagger.
- The dagger is the symbol of the domain. Tiyanak made it and it is theirs. Little artwork of Tiyanak survives, a few interpretive pieces, and all of them have the dagger in it. No record shows Tiyanak wearing Indri's face while holding it.

## Aura and Lilien

- Aura left the library for a temple to [[Lilien]] and contacted her.
- Lilien already had suspicions about Aura. She knows that one of her fellow archdevils holds Aura's soul, and says that archdevil is likely getting more powerful.
- She does not appear to know Aura is directly Tiyanak's child. She knows it is that or something of the same order.
- On changing back: if Aura found somebody willing to step between her and Tiyanak, they might assist.
- Aura asked her about the split of the Judgements.

## Aura and Verita

- A temple with a statue of [[Verita]] in it made entirely of glass.
- Their split was the easiest of the six. Verita holds about ninety per cent and Tiyanak about ten. Tiyanak offered her the majority and did not want a prolonged fight.
- The two of them do not see each other often and do not like each other. Tiyanak keeps their business private.
- Verita is not aware of any interactions between the Judgements and the archfey.
- She had a question of her own, which was which of her siblings Aura descends from. Aura does not know. Verita said she hoped Aura could work it out in the little time she has left, if the archangels' plans go as intended.
- Verita's position, put to Aura directly, is that the archdevils are a stain on the world and that they and their descendants need to be removed. Aura left with a view about the archangel of truth.

## Tiyanak Contacts Aura

- Leaving the temple, Aura did not come out outside. She came out in a different temple, one with a pedestal and no statue on it, and heard a voice.
- It noted that she had been quarrelling with their sister, then offered payment for looking after their daughter.
- Aura left.

## Aura and the Book

- One more leaf translated out of the [[Records of the Void|Theis book]].

## Wisp's Temple, and the Egg of Zaros

- Back in Geno. Wisp went to her own temple, brought up The Brillissey, and a musical is being planned off it.
- She mentioned the egg of Zaros as a side note. One person fainted. Baer asked where she had got it and told her to take it to Zaros's temple immediately.
- Brilis did not pick up again. He is apparently on vacation.
- Zaros's temple is kept less well than Wisp's. A floating river of water runs over it, so the inside is damp.
- An old man was kneeling inside before a collection of devotions and offerings. Once Wisp brought him to the egg he started bowing to her.
- Wisp told him to make sure everyone knew Brilis was responsible for it. He pulled a cord on a bell that sounds like thunder clapping. People were already heading for the temple as the party left.

## The Small Clause

- Aura built a microscope on the road back and read it: "If two parties on opposite sides of this deal should break the terms of this deal, the contract shall pass into the care of the witness and they have full authority to alter the terms of the deal as they see fit."
- Indri was the witness, and the Indri who witnessed it was Tiyanak, so the contract would pass to Tiyanak. See [[Ius Primum]], where the clause is recorded, and [[Tiyanak]], whose stated goal is to get it broken.

## Irtha's Office

- Irtha called all of them in and started inspecting Aura. Aura showed her the microscope.
- Nerinè told her Aura's father is Tiyanak. Nerinè also told her Indri is dead, and got no reaction at all to that.
- Wisp talked about her book, her accomplishments, and the egg, and told Irtha she is trying to follow in her footsteps.
- Irtha is not a fan of the head of the Brilis branch. His name is Kyr. He is a little too much like Brilis for her taste, his priorities are not organised in a way she would call efficient, and he is focused on looking impressive over being impressive.
- Deryl showed her Ius Primum. She is familiar with it. Asked whether it has been broken, she thinks it would be more apparent if it had.
- Told about Verita's scheming, she was not surprised.
- She announced a field trip to the elemental planes.

## How Irtha Got the Branch

- Nerinè asked Irtha how she came into the position, and found out they are most of her accolades.
- She had been at the society several decades and believed she could do better than the previous head. [[Mens]] told her that if she did something creative enough the post would be hers.
- She found somebody willing to help her produce Nerinè, then performed the upgrades Nerinè already knew about. Nerinè's creation is the reason she holds the post, and Mens appreciates the work.
- On Nerinè's own line, they are several generations removed from any actual archdevil. She cannot say which one and has never found it relevant.

## The Field Trip

- Irtha stopped at the plane of earth and dropped Wisp and Aura at the plane of fire. Ventilation masks for the earth party, cloaks of fire resistance and ventilators for the other two.
- Deryl, Nerinè, and Muffin went with Irtha.

## Wisp and Aura in Fire

- Fiery landscape, a massive volcano, Pyrrha's palace on the volcano with people living on the terraces around it.
- Vrillosiasi, the arc of light, is where Brilis stays when he is in the realm. An arc of light across the sky with a palace of pure diamond at the centre of it. They went straight there.
- Fire elemental guards asked them to state their purpose, and could tell whether Wisp was lying. She told them everything.
- Statues of Brilis inside, one of Pyrrha, artwork of the archangels.
- Brilis had been asleep the entire time Wisp was trying to reach him. He speed-read The Brillissey and said it needs some work. Everyone there speaks of his good name.
- Wisp asked him for a necklace and for the head of the Brilis branch. She got the necklace and was told to get a letter of recommendation.
- Aura asked how realistic it is for the archangels to wipe out the archdevils and the tieflings. Evenly matched by his account, with the archangels very determined and unlikely to drop the goal.
- Brilis was invited to the musical in two days, so the party needs to be back for it. Wisp certainly does.

## Nerinè and Deryl in Earth

- Colder. Mountains, cliffs, caves, a great deal of rock.
- A large cave with Mens lounging in it and books circling his head, The Brillissey among them. The place is a hoard made of books, tomes, and scrolls, with jars of various things about.
- Mens speaks into all of their minds. Muffin was hopping around and Mens let him have his fun.
- He called Nerinè Irtha's project and asked whether they were prepared. Speaking to Nerinè alone, he said not to worry and that they would be very happy with the results.
- He asked whether Nerinè wished to go forward with the rest of their modifications. He had been told the work could continue once they were older and more durable, since it does not take on the young.
- On the details: Nerinè is familiar with their own capabilities, which are a fraction of their true capability. He thinks they can be made perfect. They can be put under for it, though it goes better awake with the mind active. They will survive.

## The Procedure

- Mens asked whether Deryl and Irtha had anything to say before they took Nerinè. Deryl asked for details and got "improved", then asked for the exact process and had an image beamed into his head.
- What he saw: a small worm-like thing, a hole opened in somebody's skull, the worm wriggling in, the brain expanding and glowing, the worm dissolving into compost for it, electrical signals firing as new neurons form.
- Irtha put a hand on Nerinè's shoulder and told them they were strong and could do this.
- Mens took a human form. Short auburn hair, relatively tall, plain-looking, lab coat. A generic doctor. He held out a hand and Nerinè took it.
- Five minutes' walk from the cave a door formed in a wall and opened on a surgery room. Deryl was allowed to come.
- A piece of Nerinè's skull was removed. Mechanically they took enough damage to die and were kept alive and awake through it.
- What went in was something between a worm and a frog, like a tadpole. Nerinè had no desire to resist. Deryl got in trouble for casting spells. Muffin watched.
- Both of Nerinè's eyes changed to a purplish pink. Other effects followed, mostly temporary.

## Prep, Written Before the Session

Nerinè's research list came out of [[Session 8 - Revived]]. Below is what each line was set to turn up, and what getting it was to cost.

### Dates to Have Ready

- Nerinè is 24, so they were made in 678 AM.
- Irtha took the [[Mens]] branch in 678 AM. See the DM note on [[Session 8 - Revived]] for how.
- Baris Veril left the post at or before 602 AM, which leaves at least seventy-six years with no name in the public record.
- [[The Great Unrest]] ran from 546 to 657 AM. [[Nara Veril]] was born in 632 and crowned in 657.

### Baris Veril and the Mens Branch

Anything public gives them the structure and no names. One branch per dragon, the two dead ones included. Several accounts name different people as head of the society at the same time, which the branches explain.

On the Mens branch the trail stops at Baris Veril. The post did not sit empty for seventy-six years. The branch stopped letting names out after him, which is why the [[Zygaris Society]] has had no publicly known head for Mens in Nerinè's lifetime.

Two things sit on the open record with no line drawn between them. Baris Veril held the branch during the Unrest. His descendant ended that Unrest by taking Geno's throne fifty-five years after he left the post. See [[Nara Veril]], whose own connection to the society is unestablished.

The wall is that Irtha's answer in Session 8 came out of her own mouth. No document confirms it.

### When Irtha Took Over

678 AM, the year Nerinè was made. Nothing public holds that date.

The warehouse does. Irtha is its director and keeps it running, so the earliest filing under her name dates the headship. A reader going back through it finds that the two dates are one date.

Nerinè's page gives them a dead father. If [[Mens]] gifted Irtha a child, the father on record is a convenience. Available whenever it is wanted.

### Ascendant Relics

They are in Geno, so the venue is [[Mensosde Talthu]]. The toll there is a permanent deposit, assessed by committee, so they give something up forever to read about relics on their mother's behalf.

What the libraries hold:

- The twelve recorded ascensions, each with its relic and its source. See [[Ascension Process]].
- The six titan relics.
- Two gaps. [[Isadora]] has never given a date or a source. [[Hieronymous]] left no trace of his route at all, and is the only one who did not.

The pattern they can take out of it is that a relic absorbs part of what passes through it, which [[Orcus]] told them himself in [[Session 6 - The Black River|Session 6]]. A relic is about as dangerous as the ascension that ran through it.

Reading across the set, how they were come by: several were made by the ascendant who used them. [[Thalia's Lyre]] was a gift from an archfey. The [[Kubo Family Pendant]] belonged to the family that gave [[Nausicaä]] away. [[Mendax]] is the weapon that killed its user. The [[Chains of Rebellion]] were built to hold the god who now channels through them. [[Pestilence]] is alive.

[[Pellan]] in [[Lenetha]] is the other door. One page of somebody's ascension notes at a time, 10,000 gp each, never in order.

Irtha holds the throne and the method. With this record she lacks only a source.

### Mendax

They are carrying the real one, so their pact weapon is the dagger [[Tiyanak]] used to murder an archfey and the relic that archfey used on himself while dying.

Research gets them nothing. The dagger returns false answers to identify and legend lore, and no record of it predates 3,096 AF. Two beings knew where it came from. One is Tiyanak. The other had his memories taken and is now dead by Deryl's hand.

Both doors are draconic. [[Mens]] was made with the original Indri and has a hole in the collection where he belongs. [[Tenu]] asked about the murder once, got an account that did not hold together, and has not raised it since. Neither of them has ever worked on it with the other.

Nerinè works for Mens, so they can sell this. The price is that Mens learns the party killed the real Indri, and Mens does not let anyone decide what is worth bringing.

### Ignatius

Still silent. He is waiting for them to be desperate enough to be worth answering.

When he does answer he hands over the method without argument. He wrote it down, [[Cleome]] put his notes where [[Nausicaä]] would find them, and he is proud of all of it. What he will not give up is the forty years of hunting that got him his source. He does not discuss [[Owen Bramble]] or the expulsion with anybody.

He will expect them to begin at once. Stopping to think about whether they should is, to him, the same as deciding not to.

### Tiyanak

Public and findable: the archdevil of Deceit, one half of the Judgement [[Sylas]], no form anyone has recorded, appears as the shadow of whoever else is in the room. Made the peace that ended the [[Divine War]] in 50 AF.

The detail they can actually reach is the share. Tiyanak took one tenth of the realm of Truth and handed [[Verita]] the other nine, willingly, in a settlement where every other pair fought for everything it could get. Anyone who asks why is standing on the answer.

The trap is the name. Tiyanak's true name is Sylas, the Judgement records print it without comment, and anyone who says it aloud in Tiyanak's hearing has Tiyanak's full attention from that moment. They will find the name without being told what it is.

The door is [[Verita]], who has never been recorded declining a question, who negotiated that peace face to face, and who does not know that the Indri in [[Iris]] is a fraud. Asked what Tiyanak said they wanted, she will answer.

[[The University of Divine Judgement]] in [[Panthea]] holds the best library on the Division. What is in it: every surviving account of the Division written by a witness is in the same hand, apart from one that is badly written and contradicts all the others.

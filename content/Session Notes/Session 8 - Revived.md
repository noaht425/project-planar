## Irtha and the Throne

- Irtha is taking a week to examine the [[Throne of Death]]. The party may come back then. She will keep it, and they can borrow it here and there.
- Nerinè gave her [[Orcus]]'s notes on the ascension process. She was barely containing her excitement. She hugged Nerinè and said she was proud of them, which Nerinè found frightening.
- Nerinè relayed everything they turned up on the ascendant gods and the relics to her as well.
- Coming back to the office later, Nerinè heard her stuff something into a drawer on the way in. She keeps active projects in that office.
- Wisp asked her about the [[Zygaris Society]], and about who leads the [[Mens]] branch. She is the head of the Mens branch, and has been for about two decades. Noah asked for Nerinè's age in relation to this.
- Wisp asked about a [[Fang Necklace|fang necklace]] of her own. A natural 20 on persuasion got Irtha to agree. Wisp is to remind her next time, and the two of them will work on it together.

## What Nerinè Found on the Ascendant Gods

- There is no one ritual. Everybody who managed it worked it out separately, and it has been done more than one way.
- When [[Ignatius]] ascended, another mortal held on to him and went up with him. The two were close friends, so he did not object. See [[Cleome]].
- Two more gods later ascended off Ignatius's notes. See [[Nausicaä]] and [[Chore]].
- The titans are the earliest documented case. [[Kleipur]] performed it on them and supplied their relics.
- Nerinè found notes from somebody who overheard the process being performed, covering about five per cent of the ritual.
- Most of the ascendant gods are reachable. The titans are simply about.
- [[Hekate]] works at the school Nerinè was placed in, and is the only mortal known to have entered [[Arcanis]] and come out.
- The woman who wanted to play dice with Aura at the Extravaganza was [[Isadora]].

## Wisp and Brilis

- [[Brilis]] tried to clear whatever is on the minds of the temple staff and could not undo it. He does not possess them, so he cannot reach into their heads to fix it. He does not know why they would have been singled out and believes they were in the wrong place.
- He did not know the [[Records of the Void|book]] existed. He can imagine a great many people wanting it.
- On a fang necklace, he would take an offering in return. Something to show that she understands what the two of them have, and how fortunate she is to have it. He is vain and generous, so what that means in practice is something that shows how great he is.

## Aura and the Book

Aura searched the book for figures associated with lies and knows of two, the archfey and the archdevil. What she has out of it so far:

- The one who lies holds the name of the fey of riddles.
- The one who lies took the memories of the fey of riddles.
- The fey who holds riddles whispered something to the Judgements.
- The cave the hooded figure was held in began to come apart during [[The Planar Collision]], and he got out of it more recently.

The party's reading is that the Indri they have met is a fraud and the hooded figure is the real archfey. They are calling the hooded figure Rindri and the one they have known as Indri Findri.

## Kalinekra

The party rented a mid-sized boat in Geno from a lotl woman, two weeks at 14,000 gp with half back on return, and went after [[Kalinekra]].

- A fog bank came in. Deryl saw something a long way below him and could not make it out.
- Kalinekra was floating just above the water, smiling and singing, with long black hair that moves as though she were underwater.
- Nerinè threw [[Mendax]], which is now their pact weapon.
- Aura died. Wisp cast revivify and she came back. The body twitched, which it had not done with Irtha, and her head snapped back. Two horns grew from her head and her creature type changed. She is a tiefling of the bloodline of Dispater.
- The party found the kraken egg. See [[Quests]].

## What Aura Saw

Aura first said she got stuck in the queue, which was plainly a lie. What she gave afterwards:

- She was let past the queue and taken to [[Tiyanak]]'s realm.
- Tiyanak, wearing [[Indri]]'s face, told her that Indri had been causing problems and would not be causing them any longer. His own life has become a great deal easier, and it is more convenient for him that she was born fey.
- The party felt a small voice telling them she was lying. Nerinè told it that the only liar in the conversation was itself.
- She asked about Gustav. He said he might know him.
- She was speaking to her own shadow, which is how the party knows what it was. See [[Tiyanak]].

The party's conclusion is that the figure Deryl killed was the real Indri.

Nerinè owes Aura answers to whatever she wants to ask.

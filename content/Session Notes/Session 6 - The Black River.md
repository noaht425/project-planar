REMEMBER TO USE WE/US FOR GRIXT

Possibly have Aura gain a level of Devil Sorcerer

A [creature of rot](https://5e.tools/bestiary/beledros-witherbloom-scc.html) has blocked the black river and must be bargained with or slain. It has been absorbing the nutrients from the special liquid of the river and has grown much bigger because if it. It has become so accustomed ot this special nutrients that it will now die without it.

Orcus will grumble to Grixt about having been so close to getting "him" but about getting pulled away too soon

They persuaded the rot thing to go to Hunt with the promise of a better river

Six hour hike to the spot Grixt named on the Black river.

Muffin notes: carnivorous, eats stone and gravel as well, needs to spend time around people as a descendant of [[Mens]], and anyone standing close to hiim can tell what hie is feeling.

The river was water once. It spent so long in Rot that the two mixed and decayed into each other, and it now runs like a mix of tar and gasoline. Not a lot of soup in it.

The party heard snoring before they saw the dam, which is a living creature of rot, at least 40 ft tall, covered in mud and plant matter, and absorbing the liquid at its back. It may have been something else once.
- They woke it and it grumbled at them. Wisp argued that it did not need all of the food and could still feed elsewhere.
- They told it there was better river in Hunt. It left. It will die without the river's liquid.

Back to Grixt, who confirmed [[Indri]] was the first archfey of Trickery, then summoned [[Orcus]].

Orcus arrived on a large throne of dark stone and did not acknowledge anyone at first. Grumbled to Grixt about having been that close to getting "him" before he was pulled away.
- The party tried to offer him something over his brother [[Loime]]. Loime is the insecure one about his ascension and insists he took the gem from the heart of [[Elemental Plane of Noxia|Noxia]] himself. Orcus is the one who was given his means by Grixt.
- Orcus placed Loime in the outskirts of [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]] and sent the party to the middle of the kingdom. He said he would find them when they were finished.
- Orcus granted resistance to poison damage. Grixt granted advantage on stealth checks.

Arrived near a small village, woods to the east, mostly flat. Party disguised as noir detectives, black and white trenchcoats and all.
- Asked a woman where Loime was and she started crying. A plague has gone through the town and a quarter of it is already dead. Her baby was covered in boils and a few hours from dying.
- Wisp healed the baby in the house, which the woman took as a miracle, and converted to the church of [[Brilis]].
- She told them about a fallen tree in the forest. Children in the town have seen a dark figure with a head like a bird rising out of it, and sickness follows wherever it walks.

Left Muffin with the woman. Going down into the tree worked like a teleport and put them in a large cave full of rats, bats, and insects.

They tried to pass themselves off as admirers. It did not take. They fought Loime and killed him.
- [[Pestilence]] was with him, a spider that looks made of emerald and is still alive. Wisp picked it up and started to attune, and identify gave nothing on the attunement effect except that it is bad.
- Orcus teleported in, allowed them to borrow the relic for study, and came with them.
- Collected Muffin, got a jar for Pestilence, and teleported to [[Irtha Melpomene|Irtha]]'s warehouse. Orcus was very interested in the baby dragon and studied hiim.

Orcus on his own ascension, asked by Aura: Grixt helped him secure the source and he did the rest of the process himself. The relic aided the absorption, and in theory took on some of that power and became powerful for it.

Irtha's face on seeing him was awe with a little fear in it, and she said nothing for a moment. She was not fully in control of the situation. Failed an arcana check, then dismissed the party.

Wisp looked after Muffin and dreamed of her temple, with Muffin in it. The others studied the book while they waited.

Several hours and no word from Irtha. Nerinè found Eli and went with him to check on her.
- No answer at the door. Irtha was on the floor of her office with green veins and the spider on her. No sign of Orcus. Dead.
- They kept it quiet, found a greater restoration scroll, and Wisp prepared raise dead. The spell did not work, because a soul has to be willing and at liberty to rejoin the body, so Orcus has done something to hers.

Aura contacted Grixt to get Orcus brought to them. Grixt has no particular grudge against any of the archfey and does not like all of them either. Aura offered a favour within her abilities and he brought Orcus.

Orcus offered a payment plan. He wants help convincing three to join a coup: [[Charis]], [[Belphegor]], and [[Boreas]]. A natural 20 got the target out of him, which is [[The King of Night]]. The party has 24 hours to decide.

Patrons:

- Nerinè to [[Ignatius]], no answer.

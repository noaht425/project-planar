7 Mortera, 702 (date to confirm)

Aura gave [[Muffin the Dragon|Muffin]] straw figurines of the party.

## The Fight

The party fought [[Orcus]] and killed him. Deryl struck the final blow. The fight took most of the session.

- The magic in him returned to wherever it had come from. Nerinè tried to intercept it, without knowing whether that was possible, and failed.
- [[Wisp]] felt the [[Records of the Void]] go faintly warm as he died. The word that had been lifted off leaf III is back. It is death, one of the domains of [[The King of Night]].
- The party identified the [[Throne of Death]].
- Muffin attacked Eli a little. Eli protected the figurines.

## On the Body

- The scepter.
- Correspondence between Orcus and the three he was recruiting, with his own notes on how he meant to bring each of them in.
	- [[Charis]]: his connection to [[Grixt]], who helped create Charis. Also a chance to punish [[Afthor]].
	- [[Belphegor]]: Belphegor's plan to consume and rejoin with the archangels, and the chance to punish the King of Night for splitting them.
	- [[Boreas]]: punishing Afthor.
- His notes on his own ascension. Find a source. Obtain a relic to channel through. Recite the ritual, consume part of the source, and arrange protection of some kind. See [[Ascension Process]].
- 10,000 gp.

Nerinè beat the corpse until it no longer read as having been a person. The blood runs the wrong consistency. It leeches into the ground, and the grass dies where it goes in. Nerinè was covered head to foot.

## Irtha

Wisp removed the curse from [[Irtha Melpomene|Irtha]] and brought her back.

- She had been held in what amounted to solitary confinement under Orcus's control. After he died she spent about two hours in the queue at [[Liminia]].
- She was judged. She would have gone to the hell of [[Tyranny]].
- Aura read her as bored. She had spent the whole of it working out how to stop herself from dying.

# 475 · Thalia

Thalia is the ascendant god of Song. She is earthborn, and she carries a lyre that Atris, the archfey of Hunt, gave her.

**She was given her power.** The two of them were lovers, and Atris gave Thalia godhood so that she would not die. No other archfey has done that for a mortal.

**She sings.** Thalia is easy company and sings without being asked, often halfway through someone else's conversation. She will not talk about Atris, and a great many of her songs are about her.

**What she tends to want.** Very little that she will say out loud. A warlock or a bard of Thalia is expected to sing, and to learn the songs she teaches, and to keep singing them after she has stopped.

**What people assume about you.** That you can sing, and that you will if asked. Fae who know the story may treat you as a guest of Atris's lodge, which can be useful in Hunt and awkward everywhere else.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- A song she taught you, and who it is about.
- Whether you have ever asked her about Atris.
- A song she stopped halfway through, and whether you have heard the end of it.

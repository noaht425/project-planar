# 455 · The Scholarchs

Three of them, and they are the same beings as the three sphinxes of Temporalis. Each holds one of the realm's three areas.

- **It That Was**, the past. A lake ringed by waterfalls nobody has reached.
- **It That Is**, the present. A city, busy and loud.
- **It That Will Be**, the future. An endless field under stars, with trees at the edge that cannot be walked to.

Their shared purpose is keeping the balance, which is as specific as they will be about it.

**People do go there.** Not many. Those who make the trip usually want knowledge, and some of them come back with a pact.

**One thing worth knowing.** Divination magic used to be far easier than it is. It That Will Be grew paranoid and made it harder, and it has stayed that way. If your character is a diviner, that is who to be annoyed at.

The subclass is [[Warlock Scholarch|Warlock, the Scholarch patron]], and which of the three took you changes how it works.

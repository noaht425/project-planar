# 244 · Tied On in Ventis

Reachable only from a patron page. If you got here another way, tell me.

Ventis is mostly open sky. What ground there is comes in floating pieces, glass-like, drifting on the wind, and the four courts are built across them. The quarters run into each other so heavily that most travellers cannot say where one ends. At the centre, where all four winds meet, there is a storm nobody goes near.

Visitors who cannot fly are tied on.

**Decide two things.**

**Which courts you saw.** You arrived at your patron's. The borders are hard to find, so you probably wandered into at least one other.

**Whether you were tied on.** If you were, somebody moved your line every time you crossed to another island, and you will remember who. If you were not, decide how you got around.

# 426 · Lilien

Lilien is the Archdevil of Desire, and the other half of Gwenhael, the Archangel of Sacrifice. Lilien is split down the middle. The right half is a woman, beautiful to whoever is looking. The left half is a man, scarred and burned, leaking molten gold from open wounds.

Both halves are awake and both of them talk.

**Address both halves.** The other archdevils only ever speak to the right one. Lilien notices who does the same.

**What Lilien tends to want.** Lilien's bargains are oddly varied, and a lot of them look like bad deals for Lilien. There is a reason for that, and Lilien has not told anyone what it is. A warlock is usually sent after something specific, and the thing is rarely what it first looks like.

**What people assume about you.** That you want something you cannot have, and that the right offer would get you to do almost anything for it.

**Decide one thing.** Which half made the pact with you. They do not always agree about what you owe.

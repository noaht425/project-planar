# 211 · Krotaphos

Founded in 2 AM, a few days after Geno. Omens, and built around worship of the archangels and archdevils both.

Capital: **Panthea**.

**It has two kings at once**, a Good Omen and an Ill Omen, always both.

**It lost.** The war with Sylfaen ran from 397 to 427 AM and Krotaphos lost it, with Oinarria having come in on Sylfaen's side three years from the end. The kingdom has had very little power since.

**Iudimian is restricted.** The kingdom's own language may only be spoken in public in religious settings now, a condition of the defeat. Celestial and Infernal are used in worship. People speak Iudimian at home anyway.

Krotaphos still holds a seat at the Panarex, where the rulers meet, and is expected to vote with Sylfaen every time.

**What it still does.** Krotaphos enforces the will of the archdevils and archangels in the mortal world. It has the authority and almost none of the power, which is an uncomfortable thing to be.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character thinks the defeat was deserved.

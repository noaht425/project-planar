# 417 · Grixt

Grixt rules Rot, where everything breaks down and goes back into use and nothing is kept. Short, very old, a myconid with a mushroom beard and a cloak of moss.

**He behaves like a kindly old man.** What he actually is is a swarm of undead rats, insects and other small animals wearing that shape, called the Lord of Rot, and he uses we and us.

Much of what lives in his domain is several things at once in a single shape. He is his own domain's rule applied to its ruler.

**Your possessions will go over.** Food, leather, paper and rope are all on a clock in Rot. The locals find complaints about this funny.

**What he tends to want.** Things broken down that are not breaking down on their own. He thinks on a longer scale than anything else you are likely to deal with and he is in no hurry at all.

**What people assume about you.** That you are harmless and a bit sad. Grixt has been benefiting from that assumption for considerably longer than you have been alive.

**Decide one thing.** What he has asked you to let rot.

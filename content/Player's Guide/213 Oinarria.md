# 213 · Oinarria

Founded in the third year of the Age of the Many, which makes it one of the older kingdoms. Orcs and giantkin together, and the sharing is the point of the place rather than a complication in it.

The capital is **Goro**. The other cities you are likely to have heard of are **Tagra** and **Bacta**.

The language is **Gias**. Most Oinarrians speak Common as well, and plenty of them would rather not.

**What your character grew up with.** Kleipur, the Progenitor who made the giantkin, handed power to the Titans in the first year of the Age of the Many, and giantkin memory of that is long. Orcs arrived to a kingdom that had already decided it was not going to be a small one.

**The war.** In 424 AM Oinarria agreed to help Sylfaen against Krotaphos. Krotaphos lost in 427 and has had very little power since. If your character is over about eighty, they remember it. If they are younger, they grew up with people who do.

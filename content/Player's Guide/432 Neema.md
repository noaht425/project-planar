# 432 · Neema

Neema is the Archangel of Kindness, and the other half of Abaddon, the Archdevil of Cruelty. Neema looks like the best version of whoever is looking. Two people in the same room will see two different beings and argue about it afterwards.

**Neema will help you.** Of the six, Neema is the one who actually helps mortals and asks the least in return, so this is the easiest of the archangel pacts to come by. It is also the hardest to explain to the other five.

**Neema is hard to be in a room with.** The kindness is aimed at a version of you that you can see and are not. People tend to confess things to Neema without being asked.

**What Neema tends to want.** Neema is the only archangel on record against killing the archdevils. A warlock of Neema is usually sent to help someone in particular, quietly, and is not always told why it has to be quiet.

**What people assume about you.** That you are kind, and that you are a soft touch. Servants of the other archangels may treat you as being on the wrong side of an argument you did not know you were in.

**Decide one thing.** What your better self looked like when you met Neema, and how far you are from it now.

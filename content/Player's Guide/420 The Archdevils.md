# 420 · The Archdevils

Six of them. Each one is half of an Original Judgement, split apart by the King of Night, and the other half of each is an archangel.

**They are not evil by nature.** Each of the twelve is a domain rather than a moral position. The thing they rule is real, it exists in the world, and somebody has to rule it.

They are also more approachable than the archangels, which surprises people. An archdevil will usually hear you out.

Both sides avoid the number two, out of hatred for what they used to be. Do not count to two in front of one.

- **Belphegor**, Indulgence. The lightest of the six by severity, which has never made him pleasant.
- **Abaddon**, Cruelty. The one people are frightened of without needing it explained.
- **Tiyanak**, Deceit. Nobody knows their true name.
- **Lux**, Pride. Had a child with a dragon, which went about as well as you would expect.
- **Archon**, Tyranny. Iron gauntlets fused to his hands and a thorn crown grown into his head.
- **Lilien**, Desire. Split down the middle, one half beautiful and one half ruined.

Devils have mortal children, and not only the six. If your character descends from one rather than bargaining with one, the subclass is [[Sorcerer Devil|Sorcerer, the Devil bloodline]], and which devil you came from decides your damage type.

# 448 · Amol

Amol is the second dragon of Noxia, made by Okzu alone, and Amol will tell you so within an hour of meeting you. Amol is an acid dragon. The scales are wet in a way that never dries, and where Amol has rested there is a mark afterwards.

**Amol is proud of one thing.** Brilis, Charis and Mens were each made with outside help, and Amol holds all three in open contempt for it. The contempt only runs upward. Amol is no bully to anything smaller.

**What Amol tends to want.** Recognition, which in practice means being brought into things. A warlock of Amol is asked to make Amol's involvement visible to the other dragons.

**What Amol collects is not clear.** Possibly not even to Amol. You may be sent after very different things from one month to the next.

**What people assume about you.** That you are loud about who you work for. Warlocks of the other three seconds will be cold to you before you have said anything.

**Decide one thing.** What you think Amol ought to collect. Amol may ask.

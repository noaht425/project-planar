# 212 · Sylfaen

Founded in 2 AM. Beastfolk, and the strongest kingdom in the world since beating Krotaphos in 427 AM.

Capital: **Dawara**. Other cities: **Chrasil**, **Oinsyl**, and **Plowo**.

The language is **Dzoa**, and the **Office of the Seal** polices it. Street Dzoa is full of Iudimian loanwords and the Office considers that a problem rather than a dialect. Every borrowed word is filed into the outsider class, so a foreign word announces itself to everyone in earshot.

**It sorts people by mouth.** Lipped, Snouted, Beaked and Long-jawed are legal categories, not descriptions. Tortles, locathah, grung, kekrik and yuan-ti fit none of the four and the law records them as unclean. This determines a great deal about a person's life.

Sylfaen is imperial and openly so. It is trying to force a great many different peoples into one system, and the system was not designed with all of them in mind.

**Worth deciding.** Which category your character is in, and what that has cost or spared them.

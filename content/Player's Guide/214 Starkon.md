# 214 · Starkon

Founded in 3 AM. One of the three kingdoms of the Trois Re, which it calls the Drei Kunig.

Capital: **Hekatania**. Other cities: **Avonis** and **Fernstadt**.

**The Aravis Academy is here**, in the capital, and it is the greatest school of magic on any of the eight planes. Wizards come from everywhere to study, and what is known about Arcanis in the mortal world is mostly known because of work done there.

**This is where the campaign starts.** If your character is from Starkon, they are from the place the story opens, which is worth knowing when you decide how much of it they should already understand.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character has any connection to the Academy, as a student, a reject, a servant or a relative of someone inside.

# 449 · Zaros

Zaros was the dragon lord of Air until the ascendant god Afthor killed him, drank his blood and took the realm, fifty years ago. He collected cities. The air realm was the richest and best governed in Tarkis under him, and people there still talk about him in detail: a bridge he paid for, a district he had rebuilt twice because he did not like how it looked.

**He can still take a warlock.** A being that powerful is almost impossible to kill completely. Afthor destroyed the body, and something of Zaros is still there in the domain he held. That remnant is not enough by itself. The rest of the power comes from you. You believe in Zaros and in what he stood for, hard enough that the belief does the work.

**That cuts both ways.** If your faith goes, I would not count on the power staying.

**Talk to me about the subclass.** The Dragon patron assumes a hoard to come back to, and his is wrecked. We can work it out together, and the Undead or Undying patron might suit the flavor better.

**He is not entirely gone.** The other dragons want him back and Okzu is running the effort. Nobody tries to raise something that is not there.

**What you are working for.** Zaros cannot tell you what he wants, so you work from what he wanted when he was alive. His hoard was cities that prospered, and a warlock of Zaros usually ends up trying to leave a place better off.

**What people assume about you.** In the air realm, that you are either a fool or the best news in fifty years. Afthor will take you personally.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Why you believe. Most of his warlocks never met him.
- Something you have done to leave a place better off.
- Whether you want him back, and what you think he will be like if he comes.

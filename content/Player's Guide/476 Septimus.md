# 476 · Septimus

Septimus is the devil of Rebellion. He was an Omen and a paladin of Archon, and he tried to take the heavens by force. He got most of the way. He is covered in burns and wrapped in chains that burn. His wings are bare bone.

**Watch what you call him.** The archangels stripped him of his name and call him the Seventh, and their servants will correct you if you use any other name. He calls himself Septimus. If you do too, he will ask you to say it again.

**He is chained, and the chains are his.** Pavlas made the chains to hold him. His power went into them instead of draining away, so the thing meant to stop him is now part of what he is.

**The archdevils ignore him.** He finds that harder to bear than the chains.

**What he tends to want.** His name back, and the heavens, still. A warlock of Septimus is a rebel against something, and he would like to know what.

**What people assume about you.** That you are trouble. Servants of the archangels will treat you as a nuisance, and the warlocks of the archdevils will barely notice you, which he will want to hear about.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Something you refused to obey.
- Which name you use for him, and where.
- Whether you have seen the chains up close.

# 200 · Where You Are From

Ten kingdoms, all of them in Arenis, the mortal realm. Your character can be from any of them whatever their people, though some combinations are more usual than others and the page for your people will have said so.

The rulers of nine of them meet at the Panarex, a temple on a man-made island in the middle of the three continents. The tenth has never been invited.

*[[210 Geno|Geno]].* Elementals. Holding back a hole into another plane.

*[[211 Krotaphos|Krotaphos]].* Omens. Lost the last great war and most of its power with it.

*[[212 Sylfaen|Sylfaen]].* Beastfolk. An empire, and the strongest kingdom in the world.

*[[213 Oinarria|Oinarria]].* Orcs and giantkin together.

*[[214 Starkon|Starkon]].* Home of the Aravis Academy, and where the campaign begins.

*[[215 Mathair|Mathair]].* Its capital claims to be the centre of the world.

*[[216 Regno|Regno]].* The third of the Trois Re.

*[[217 Thalanea|Thalanea]].* Elves. One half of a kingdom that tore in two.

*[[218 Kumas|Kumas]].* Fae. The other half.

*[[219 Tama|Tama]].* The kingdom of exiles, and the only one not invited to the Panarex.

If your character is from none of these, from somewhere outside Arenis entirely, or from nowhere they can name, tell me and we will work it out.

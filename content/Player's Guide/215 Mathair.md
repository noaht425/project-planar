# 215 · Mathair

Founded in 3 AM, on the same day as Regno. One of the three kingdoms of the Trois Re.

Capital: **Omphalos**, which claims to be the most central point in the world. Nobody has successfully disputed it and nobody has entirely proven it either. Other cities: **Flùm** and **Lonvil**.

Omphalos hosts **La Grande Mascarade**, the Grand Masquerade.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character has ever been to the Masquerade, and as what.

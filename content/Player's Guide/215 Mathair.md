# 215 · Mathair

Founded in 3 AM, on the same day as Regno. One of the three kingdoms of the Trois Re.

Capital: **Omphalos**, which claims to be the most central point in the world. Nobody has successfully disputed it and nobody has entirely proven it either. Other cities: **Flùm** and **Lonvil**.

**The Grand Masquerade.** La Grande Mascarade rotates between kingdoms and Mathair hosted it first, which is why people still think of it as belonging here. It commemorates the war between Krotaphos and Sylfaen, which is a strange thing to commemorate and nobody says so out loud.

It is held in the royal palace at the centre of the city, which is marble with gold vine work, and getting in requires an invitation. The guest list runs to the wealthiest people from several planes.

**Mathair does not restrict worship.** The palace has statues of every group a person might call on for a favour, and the city has a temple district to match, with the archangels and archdevils, the dragons, the archfey, the scholarchs and the ascendant gods all represented.

The kingdom is ruled by a queen.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character has ever been to the Masquerade, and as what.

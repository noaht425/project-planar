# 219 · Tama

The youngest kingdom by two hundred years, founded in 202 AM by Alistar Stormjaw, the dragonborn scientist who tore the hole in Geno and was banished for it. He gathered exiles from every other kingdom and made a country out of them.

Capital: **Liber**.

**It is not in the Panarex.** The rulers of the kingdoms meet there and Tama has never been invited. Getting invited, and being recognised as a kingdom rather than a settlement of outcasts, is the thing Tama wants more than anything.

Kumas supports the bid. Nobody else has said much.

**Everyone there came from somewhere else**, or is descended from someone who did. There is no native people of Tama and in two hundred years that has not stopped being true.

**Worth deciding.** Which kingdom your character's family was thrown out of, and what for.

# 416 · Alsa

Alsa rules Bone, where nothing decays and everything that has ever died is still there. Eight feet tall, a werewolf, with broken bones protruding.

The other archfey keep Alsa at a distance on the grounds of not being pleasant to look at. It has never cost Alsa an inch of territory and Alsa has never asked to be included.

**Bone is the best archive in the fey realms.** Everything that died there is still legible in what is left of it. If someone needs to know what happened to a person, there is exactly one place to go, and Alsa owns it.

**What Alsa tends to want.** The dead accounted for. The domain receives bodies from Hunt and from elsewhere, and keeping the record is most of the work.

**What people assume about you.** That you are grim, and that you have seen the inside of somewhere they would rather not think about. Both are usually true.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Whose remains you went there to ask about.
- Something you found in Bone that you were not looking for.
- Who will not be in the same room as you since the pact.

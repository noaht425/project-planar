# 478 · Chore

Chore is the Pirate Queen, the ascendant god of Pirates. She is an elf who has spent her life at sea and looks it, and the sword at her hip, the Siren Blade, can be heard humming when she is close.

**She leads.** Her crews follow her without being made to. She decides quickly and usually well. Afterwards, when there is time to think, she sometimes stops trusting the decision, and she may ask you whether it was right.

**She wants to be free.** For Chore the god of pirates is the god of not being told where to go. She found that at sea and she means to keep it.

**She cares more than she lets on.** She hides how much her friends matter to her, badly, and she will protect them at any cost.

**What she asks.** Keep faith with the crew beside you and keep moving, and do not let anything tie you down.

**What people assume about you.** That you are a pirate. In some ports that gets you a drink, and in others it gets you watched from the moment you come ashore.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- The crew you sailed with, and how you left it.
- A decision of hers she asked you about afterwards.
- What you were getting away from when you went to sea.

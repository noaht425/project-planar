# 463 · Notus

Notus is the south wind. He does not hold a shape, and over a single conversation he will be several sizes and several temperatures without seeming to notice.

**He means it at the time.** Notus will agree to something and mean it. An hour later he will give you a different answer, mean that too, and not be troubled when you point out the first one. He is not lying to you. Both were true when he said them.

**He is funny**, and he is the only one of the four who is.

**He is in the plan**, more or less. His commitment to it changes from day to day. Boreas leaves him out of the planning and counts him anyway.

**What he tends to want.** That depends on the day. A warlock of Notus gets instructions that change, sometimes twice before lunch, and is rarely blamed for following the old ones.

**What people assume about you.** That you are unreliable. If you take after him, that is fair.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- The best thing he ever promised you. It may still turn up.
- An instruction he changed after you had already followed it.
- A joke of his you still repeat.

# 411 · Thera

King Thera rules Seasons, the largest fey domain, and the only one divided into quarters under separate rulers. Those four are his children, and each holds one season that never changes.

He is made of leaves that are always turning colour, dying and growing back. He is the only thing in his domain that changes.

**He holds an actual court**, which makes him the most formal of the seven and the easiest to approach correctly. There is a right way to ask him for things and people who know it do considerably better.

**What he tends to want.** His four children cannot change what their quarters are, so everything they compete over is everything else. A warlock of Thera is usually carrying something to or from one of them.

**What people assume about you.** That you are well connected and have manners. Fae who dislike the court will dislike you on sight.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Which of the four you have dealt with most, and whether that went well.
- How you learned the right way to ask him for things, and who taught you.
- Something you carried for him, and whether it arrived.

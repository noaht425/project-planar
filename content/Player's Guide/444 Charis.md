# 444 · Charis

Charis is the second dragon of Water, made by Vetur with the help of Grixt, the archfey of Rot. Charis is undead. All of the body is there and none of it is working.

**The hoard is the dead.** Wherever something has died in quantity, Charis arrives afterwards and collects them, along with where and how each one died. The collection is kept somewhere other than the water realm, and nobody has been shown where. Vetur thinks the dead belong in the trench, and the two have never settled it.

**Charis means no harm by it.** Charis is quiet and careful, and the dead are counted with more attention than the living get almost anywhere in Tarkis.

**What Charis tends to want.** A warlock of Charis is expected to turn up where there has been killing and account for it. You are not expected to cause it.

**What people assume about you.** That death follows you around. You arrive after it, and most people will not see the difference.

**Decide one thing.** The first body you accounted for, and whose it was.

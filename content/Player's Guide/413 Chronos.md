# 413 · Chronos

Chronos rules Time, where everything is mechanical and everything runs exactly as it was built to, exactly when it should. He is built of clocks and dials himself.

**Nothing in his domain is broken or late.** The unnerving part is the precision rather than any malice.

**Appointments bind.** In his domain an agreed time carries the weight a bargain carries elsewhere, and being late to one is an offence with consequences. A warlock of Chronos is held to this more tightly than anyone.

**What he tends to want.** Things done on time. He has also been known to hold someone to an agreement they made correctly, which worked exactly as written, and which produced something they had not foreseen. He does not cheat. He simply knows what the terms will do.

**What people assume about you.** That you are reliable, and that you will turn up whether or not you are wanted.

**Decide one thing.** The appointment you have already agreed to and have not yet kept.

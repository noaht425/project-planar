# 421 · Belphegor

Belphegor is the Archdevil of Indulgence. He was one half of the Judgement called Baator, and the other half became Blythe, the Archangel of Temperance.

His face is nothing but mouths and he has a hundred arms, and he is the most pleasant of the archdevils to meet by a long way. He will seat you, feed you, ask after your family and remember the answers.

**Nobody leaves his domain.** Nothing there stops them. They are just never given a moment in which stopping would occur to them. If your character has been his guest, work out how you got up from the table.

**What he tends to want.** He wants the Judgements made whole again, by each archdevil eating its archangel, and he is content to wait until the archangels give him a reason. Until then he likes guests, and a warlock of Belphegor is often sent to bring someone to dinner.

**What people assume about you.** That you are good company and do not know when to stop.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What your character has never managed to put down. Belphegor noticed it before you did.
- Who he has asked you to bring to dinner.
- How you got up from his table, if you have sat at it.

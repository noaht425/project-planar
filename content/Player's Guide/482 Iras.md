# 482 · Iras

Iras is the Phoenix King, the son of Pyrrha, the dragon lord of Fire, and Lux, the Archdevil of Pride. He has his father's height and gold, and he burns at the edges like his mother. The fire on him does not go out.

**He was king of Geno.** He took the throne in 405 AM by killing Genasi VII, on the principle that Geno owed its existence to the dragons and should be ruled by a dragon's descendant. He gave it up fourteen years later and has never said why.

**His staff sends people to his father.** Everyone Iras kills with the Hell's Fire Staff goes to Lux in Pride.

**He has enemies.** Moto, the titan who made the first phoenixes, hates him for taking the title. He knows about Moto, and he knows Moto is not the only one.

**What he tends to want.** Mostly to stay alive. What he has wanted since he gave up the throne is something he has not told anyone.

**What people assume about you.** In Geno, people have strong opinions about him either way, and they will have them about you. Never say his title in front of Moto.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What you think of what he did in Geno.
- Why you think he gave up the throne.
- Whether anyone you know was killed with the staff.

# 140 · Orcs

Shram made the orcs at the Council of Creation in the first year of the Age of the Many, alongside the other eight Progenitors. Shram is still around.

Orcs in Agra are not a wandering people and they are not anybody's raiders. They built a kingdom, they share it with the giantkin, and they have been a going concern for seven hundred years.

Play any orc you like, including the half-orcs the Progenitors had nothing to do with.

**What to know going in.** Oinarria is the orc and giantkin kingdom and most orcs are from there. Orcs elsewhere are usually there for a reason, and the reason is yours to invent. The orc and giantkin arrangement is old and mostly works, which surprises outsiders who expect it not to.

# 464 · Zephyros

Zephyros is the west wind. Very few people have described him and their descriptions do not agree. He does not like being looked at and he does not like being asked questions.

**His court speaks for him.** The western court is the best run of the four, and almost all of its business is done by his people rather than by him. They are polite about it, and protective of him.

**Nobody knows what he wants.** He is part of the plan against Afthor and has never spoken for it or against it. The other three winds do not know what he actually wants, and it is the one thing about him that unsettles them.

**What he tends to want.** A warlock of Zephyros usually deals with the court and not with him. Instructions come through a steward, and they are clear.

**What people assume about you.** That a quiet patron has secrets, and that you are keeping them.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Whether you have ever seen him. If you have, what did he look like?
- What his steward asked of you first.
- What you think he wants.

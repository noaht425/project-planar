# 474 · Afthor

Afthor is the Storm Lord, a storm giant and the largest of the six titans. Kleipur gave him his power at the beginning. Fifty years ago he added to it by killing the dragons Zaros and Vari and drinking their blood, and lightning has run over him whenever he moves ever since.

**He is the cruellest of the titans.** He has a sense of humour, and it is at its best when somebody is getting hurt.

**He is lazy.** He took the realm of air and has never troubled to run it. He has not claimed Zaros's seat and he collects nothing, and the realm has been under a storm since he took it.

**He is not worried.** Every dragon in Tarkis and all four of the winds want him dead, and he has not taken any of them seriously yet. Vato, another of the titans, does whatever he says.

**What he tends to want.** To be left alone to enjoy what he took. A warlock of Afthor is usually sent to do the things he cannot be bothered to do, and is expected to tell him about it afterwards in a way he finds funny.

**What people assume about you.** Every dragon's worshipper and every warlock of a wind will treat you as an enemy. People from the realm of air will hold you responsible for the storm.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Something you did that made him laugh.
- What you saw in the realm of air.
- Which of his enemies has already come looking for you.

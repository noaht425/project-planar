# 130 · Elves

Sylvana Lorien made the elves, and she is still very proud of them. She will not hear a word said against them.

There are old rumours that she had help from the Archfey, that something was promised in return, and that elves have gone missing because of it. Nobody has ever proven any of it.

**It split a kingdom.** Thalamas was the elf and fae kingdom until 105 AM, when the rumours and the disappearances together broke it into Thalanea for the elves and Kumas for the fae. The two have been in a cold war ever since.

**Elves do not like the fey.** This is not a personal quirk, it is the standing position of an entire people, and an elf who feels differently is making a choice their neighbours will notice.

Children are told about Yupri, a faerie who carries off badly behaved elf children to eat them. Most adults remember it as a story.

Drow, high, wood, astral, sea, shadar-kai and the rest are all available.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character believes the rumours.

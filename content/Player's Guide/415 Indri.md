# 415 · Indri

Indri rules Trickery. Pale face, pure black eyes, silver freckles.

**His domain does not cheat.** This is the thing people get wrong and keep getting wrong. The rules in Trickery are real, they can be worked out, and they are kept to the letter every single time. What the rules are for is the part that is never what it looks like.

**He hears petitions as wagers.** You state your case, he offers you a bet on it, and declining the bet is itself an answer.

**What he tends to want.** To be entertained, and to see what you will agree to. A warlock of Indri is usually carrying a deal they have not fully understood yet.

**What people assume about you.** That nothing you say is reliable. Among fae, that is closer to a compliment than you would think.

**Decide one thing.** What you won from him, and what you agreed to along the way without noticing.

# 430 · The Archangels

Six of them, each the other half of an archdevil. Blythe was split from Belphegor, Neema from Abaddon, Verita from Tiyanak, Pavlas from Lux, Themis from Archon, and Gwenhael from Lilien.

**They are not good by nature**, any more than the archdevils are evil. They rule domains.

They are harder to reach than the archdevils. An archangel is less likely to answer and more likely to want something specific before they do.

- **Blythe**, Temperance. No arms and no mouth, covered in eyes.
- **Neema**, Kindness. Appears as the best version of whoever is looking.
- **Verita**, Truth. Almost entirely see-through.
- **Pavlas**, Humility. Permanently bowing, with hair across the face like a veil.
- **Themis**, Justice. Eyes carved out, holding scales.
- **Gwenhael**, Sacrifice. Bone-thin and covering himself.

They write by tracing letters in the air, slowly and ornately, and their texts are beautiful and almost unreadable.

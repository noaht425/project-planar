# 210 · Geno

Founded in the second year of the Age of the Many, the first kingdom there was. Elementals, and the Progenitor who made them was its first king.

Capital: **Geno**, which shares its name with the kingdom. Other cities: **Mensosde Talthu**, **Thraside Vethryfu** and **Varastra**.

The language is **Genoan**, which came down from Draconic and makes every word declare what it is made of.

**The kingdom has one real problem.** In 202 AM a dragonborn scientist named Alistar Stormjaw tried to open a portal to Tarkis in his laboratory in the capital. It worked, after a fashion, and Tarkis has been leaking into the city ever since. A magical wall holds it back, it is maintained constantly, and everything else Geno does comes second to that.

Dragonborn were banned from the city afterward. Stormjaw was banished and went off to found Tama with exiles from everywhere else.

**The throne has been complicated.** Seven kings named Genasi in succession, then Iras, who abdicated in 419 AM, then an elected king, then no king at all for over a century while the kingdom tore itself up. A queen named Nara Veril has held it since 657 AM.

**Worth deciding.** What your character's family did during the hundred years without a king.

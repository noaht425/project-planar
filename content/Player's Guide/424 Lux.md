# 424 · Lux

Lux is the Archdevil of Pride, and the other half of Pavlas, the Archangel of Humility. He looks the way mortals expect a god to look, with a mane of hair that moves whether or not there is wind, and he knows it.

He is charming and generous for as long as someone is watching. He speaks as though to a larger room than the one he is in.

**He is the archdevil Geno remembers.** He had a son, Iras, with the dragon Pyrrha. When Iras took the throne of Geno by killing the last king, Lux came and took that king's whole line back to Pride, and when the Progenitor Genasi fought him over it he won in four days. Then he let someone else rule Geno anyway. People still argue about why.

**What he tends to want.** To be admired, and to have his family under his roof. He negotiates in good faith and his price is usually something you can understand. A warlock of Lux is expected to be seen doing well, and to make sure people know who to thank.

**What people assume about you.** That you are vain. In Geno, that you are on the wrong side of something that happened three hundred years ago.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What you did that Lux saw.
- Whether you descend from him, and whether you know it. He does.
- Who in Geno holds the pact against you.

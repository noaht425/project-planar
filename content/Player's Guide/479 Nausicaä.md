# 479 · Nausicaä

Nausicaä protects children nobody wanted. She was one of them. Her family gave her away young, to an aunt who raised her as a tool. She is a tabaxi, immaculately kept, and she wears a silver pendant with a brass gear set around a sun that is half a moon.

**She is not humble about any of it.** She is spoiled, she is sure she is the best at what she does, and she expects to be treated accordingly. She expects the children in her care to be treated accordingly too.

**She keeps houses for children.** Each one is very good. She chooses the best rooms for the children herself, and then she chooses a better one for herself.

**She has a rival.** She and Ignatius have quarrelled for as long as both have been gods, and neither has ever conceded a point.

**What she asks.** That the children come first, and that it is done properly. She does not believe in second-rate care and will not pay for it.

**What people assume about you.** That you were one of hers, which many of her followers were. Ignatius's followers will assume you are on the other side of something.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Whether you grew up in one of her houses.
- A child you took in, or could not.
- What she thinks of your work, and whether she has told you.

# 472 · Orcus

Orcus is the ascendant god of Death. He was a man of Regno and still looks like one, heavy-set and plainly dressed, with nothing about him to suggest the throne he sits on.

**He took death from the King of Night.** He is the only mortal who has ever stolen from either of the Kings, and he has ruled the dead from the Throne of Death ever since.

**He governs.** Orcus thinks of the dead as a population, and by most accounts he looks after them well. He is patient and practical, and he asks sensible questions.

**What he tends to want.** Order among the dead, and people with reasons. He is interested in grievances, the King of Night's enemies most of all, and he asks about them early. A warlock of Orcus is often asked who they know and what those people are angry about.

**Mind his brother.** Loime, the god of plague, is his younger brother, and the two do not speak. Do not bring one up in front of the other.

**What people assume about you.** That you are comfortable around death. Anyone who still holds with the King of Night will see you as the servant of a thief.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What he asked you about the King of Night, and what you told him.
- Someone of yours who died, and whether you have asked him about them.
- A grievance you brought him, and whose it was.

# 480 · Isadora

Isadora is the ascendant god of Luck, fortune and misfortune both, and she plays with fate the way a cat plays with a ball of yarn. Her skin is patched bronze and gold.

**She plays.** Most of what Isadora does is play. She bats at outcomes to see where they roll, and she will play any game with anyone. She is very good at all of them.

**She can be solemn.** When she wants someone to play with her, she puts on a grave face and a sincere voice, and presents a wager as though a great deal depends on it. She keeps the face on exactly as long as it takes you to agree.

**She is often in disguise.** Usually behind a rabbit mask, and often at a gaming table at Eltarien's Extraplanar Extravaganza. You may play her before you work out who she is.

**What she asks.** A game. Anyone who wants her favour plays her for it, and she picks the game.

**What people assume about you.** That you are lucky, or that you cheat. The Scholarchs hate her, and their warlocks will not be pleased to see you.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- The game you played her for the pact, and whether you won.
- Something that went very well or very badly for you, and whether you think it was her.
- Whether you have one of her dice.

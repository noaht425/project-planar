# 217 · Thalanea

Formed in 105 AM when the kingdom of Thalamas tore itself in half. The elves kept Thalanea, the fae took Kumas, and the two have been in a cold war ever since.

Capital: **Lenetha**. The name is Sylvan, tha and lanea, the bright castle. Other cities: **Darasil** and **Terpisthla**.

The language is **Sylvan**.

**The split was about the rumours.** Elves had been disappearing and there were stories about a bargain the Progenitor made with the Archfey. Nothing was proven. The kingdom came apart anyway.

**Sylvan carries the scar.** After the war the elves threw out the everyday words that made their speech sound like the fae language, and kept every word touching names, oaths, debts and bargains, because their old agreements are written in them. The result is a language that can negotiate a binding contract and struggles to ask for bread.

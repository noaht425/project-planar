# 242 · Standing in the Heavens

Reachable only from a patron page. If you got here from somewhere else, tell me.

The heavens are open and bright, and full of good food and cheerful company. The living can get there, and the living have come back out, though leaving is never as easy as arriving.

You were a visitor. The reward of a heaven only reaches the souls assigned to it, so whatever you were shown was not meant for you. You were sightseeing.

**Decide two things.**

**Which one.** There are six. You can move between them freely once you are there, but you arrived in one, and it was almost certainly your patron's.

**What it costs the people who live there.** Every heaven carries a cost inside the thing it gives. It is not a trick. It is what that reward does to someone who receives it forever, and you saw it.

If you met someone you knew there, tell me who.

# 434 · Pavlas

Pavlas is the Archangel of Humility, and the other half of Lux, the Archdevil of Pride. Pavlas has been bowing since the Division, bent at the waist, with long hair falling over the face like a veil. Nobody has seen the face.

**Pavlas defers to everyone.** Pavlas will not go through a door first, and will let you decide almost anything. It is sincere, and there is no other Pavlas underneath it to compare it to. People who spend a day being deferred to by Pavlas tend to notice, too late, how much they have said.

**What Pavlas tends to want.** Pavlas says there is nothing Pavlas wants personally, and that is true. Pavlas does a great deal of quiet work in the politics of the heavens, one modest step at a time, and a warlock of Pavlas is usually carrying something that looks unimportant to someone who will not think so.

**What people assume about you.** That you are modest. Some will take you for a servant, which can be useful.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Something your character did that nobody remembers them doing. In Humility the blessed slowly forget their own achievements, and Pavlas may have taken you on because you had already started.
- Something you carried for Pavlas that turned out to matter.
- When you noticed how much you had been talking.

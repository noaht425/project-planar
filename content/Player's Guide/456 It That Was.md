# 456 · It That Was

It That Was holds the past, a wide lake ringed on every side by waterfalls that nobody has reached, however long they row. Like the other two it is lofty and hard to read.

**It thinks about what could have gone differently.** Every choice, and every chain of events that might have followed if the choice had gone the other way. It hides this well. Warlocks who spend long enough with it notice that its questions are always about what they nearly did.

**It lets you take something back.** A warlock of It That Was can use what it knows after the fact, replacing something that has already happened. It is the only one of the three that offers that.

**What it tends to want.** Accounts of decisions, particularly the ones that went badly, and what the person would do if they had another try.

**What people assume about you.** That you live in the past, and that you remember everything anyone has ever said to you.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- The choice it keeps asking you about.
- What you brought it the first time.
- What you think it regrets.

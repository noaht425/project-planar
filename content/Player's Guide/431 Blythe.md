# 431 · Blythe

Blythe is the Archangel of Temperance. Blythe was one half of the Judgement called Baator, and the other half became Belphegor, the Archdevil of Indulgence.

Blythe has no arms and no mouth, and the whole body is covered in eyes that do not blink in time with each other. The only way Blythe can say anything is to trace it in the air, slowly, and a conversation of any substance takes days.

**Budget the time.** Blythe does not negotiate and cannot be hurried. Most people who wait for an answer find they have changed their minds about the question by the time it arrives.

**What Blythe tends to want.** Blythe decides exactly what anyone should have and cannot pass any of it to them. A warlock of Blythe is the hands. Blythe also wants the war against the archdevils finished properly and has never accepted the peace that ended it.

**What people assume about you.** That you will measure out what other people get, and that you will not be generous about it.

**Decide one thing.** What Blythe decided you should have. It was exactly enough, and you may not have liked that.

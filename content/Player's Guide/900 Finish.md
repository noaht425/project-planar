# 900 · Finish

That is the world part done. Send me these, in whatever form you like:

1. Your people and where your character is from
2. Your class, and your patron, deity or oath if you have one
3. One thing your character wants
4. One person your character knows, alive or otherwise

The fourth one matters more than it looks. Give me a name and a relationship and I will put them in the world.

If you went through this and your character does not fit any of it, that is useful too. Tell me where it stopped working and we will build the page that was missing.

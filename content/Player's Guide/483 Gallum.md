# 483 · Gallum

Gallum is one of the six titans, a cloud giant given his power by Kleipur at the beginning. He weighs whatever he likes, and most of the time he likes to weigh very little.

**He is good company.** Gallum is flighty and jolly and enjoys a laugh, his own most of all. He drifts between plans the way he drifts between places, and he is a pleasure to be around for as long as he stays.

**He is proud, cheerfully.** He will tell you he is one of the original gods, laugh, and expect you to agree. All the titans say they have always been gods. Gallum is the only one who seems to find it funny.

**What he tends to want.** A good time, which he mostly gets. A warlock of Gallum is often in the middle of one of his plans when he loses interest in it, and is left to finish it alone.

**What people assume about you.** That you are not serious. Giantkin will want to know which titan you follow before they decide anything else about you.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Where he took you the first time he picked you up.
- A plan of his you were left to finish.
- Whether you agreed with him about the original gods.

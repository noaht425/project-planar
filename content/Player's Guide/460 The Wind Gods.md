# 460 · The Wind Gods

Four of them, one for each quarter of Ventis. Their quarters bleed into each other so heavily that the four are permanently in each other's business.

- **Boreas**, north. Cold, hard-wearing, harsh. Betrothed to an eladrin princess who is trying to get out of it.
- **Eurus**, east. Warm and bright. Seeing another of the eladrin, publicly and happily.
- **Notus**, south. Flippant and changeable.
- **Zephyros**, west. Shy and hard to read.

**They share one plan.** All four intend to kill Afthor and take the elemental realm of air from him, which is unusual for four beings this unalike.

A pact with a wind puts you in that plan whether or not anyone tells you.

The subclass is [[Warlock Wind|Warlock, the Wind patron]]. Because the four quarters bleed together, it gives you all four rather than only the one that took you.

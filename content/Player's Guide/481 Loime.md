# 481 · Loime

Loime is the ascendant god of Plague. He wears a black cloak and a plague doctor's mask and is never seen without either. A green spider a few inches across sits in his chest, alive.

**He did it alone.** He will tell you that before you have finished saying hello. He took his power from Grixt, the archfey of Rot, without being given it, and he turned down every offer of help.

**He has a brother.** Orcus, the god of Death, is his older brother. Loime has never forgiven him for being given what Loime had to steal. They do not speak.

**He is touchy.** Loime is quick to correct people and proud of every difficulty he can claim. Do not suggest anyone helped him.

**What he tends to want.** To be acknowledged as a god who made himself, by everyone, and by Orcus most of all. A warlock of Loime is expected to give him the credit, properly and in public.

**What people assume about you.** That you are carrying something. You will be turned away at some doors, and seated nearest the window at others.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- An illness you survived, or did not catch when everyone around you did.
- Whether you have ever suggested that someone helped him.
- What he has asked you to do about his brother.

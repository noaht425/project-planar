# 414 · Atris

Atris rules Hunt, where everything alive is either hunting or being hunted, and the role is assigned on arrival. She appears as a young woman in a chiton, barefoot, with a bow.

She keeps a lodge rather than a court, and the lodge moves. Where it is standing is where her domain's business happens.

**The rule of her domain is that the quarry always gets a real chance.** It has never been waived. That is the only reason anyone goes there on purpose.

**She gave a mortal the power that made them a god**, which no other archfey has done, and she did it so that person would not die. Of the seven she is the one most likely to help you for a reason you would recognise as a reason.

**What she tends to want.** To know how things end. She is not sentimental about which side you are on.

**What people assume about you.** That you are dangerous outdoors, and that you will finish what you start.

## What This Opens

A warlock of Atris has been in her domain, which means they were assigned a role there like everything else alive. No homeland page offers that.

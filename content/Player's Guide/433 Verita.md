# 433 · Verita

Verita is the Archangel of Truth, and the other half of Tiyanak, the Archdevil of Deceit. Verita is almost entirely transparent. In a lit room you can see an outline and not much else.

**Verita answers.** Every question, every time, and Verita has never been recorded declining one. That includes the questions you would have been better off not asking. A warlock of Verita can ask anything and should think about whether they want to.

**What Verita tends to want.** The peace kept. Verita made the peace with the archdevils that ended the Divine War, and the other five archangels regard it as a surrender made without them. A pact with Verita puts you on the side of that peace whether you have an opinion about it or not.

**What people assume about you.** That you cannot lie, or will not. Some people will test this, and some will tell you things on the assumption that you will repeat them exactly.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- The question you asked Verita that you wish you had not.
- A question you have decided never to ask.
- How you feel about the peace you are now on the side of.

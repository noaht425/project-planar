# 180 · Earthborn

Talav made the earthborn. Gnomes, dwarves, and halflings, all three playable.

They have no kingdom of their own and are spread across most of the others, with the heaviest concentrations wherever there is something worth digging into.

Of the nine peoples the earthborn are the least written about, which is a fact about the world's historians rather than about the earthborn.

**Worth deciding.** What your character's family does, and how long they have been doing it.

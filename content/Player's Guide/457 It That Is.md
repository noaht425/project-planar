# 457 · It That Is

It That Is holds the present, a busy city full of people going about their business. It is as lofty as the other two and a good deal more relaxed.

**It goes with the flow.** It That Is takes things as they come and rarely seems put out by anything. It is also the only one of the three who lives somewhere crowded, which makes it the easiest to find and the hardest to pick out.

**You are never caught off guard.** A warlock of It That Is can use what it knows while surprised or helpless, and on things they cannot see. Whatever is happening, you have already half seen it.

**What it tends to want.** What is happening now, wherever you are. The newer and stranger, the better.

**What people assume about you.** That you are calm in a crisis, or that you are not paying attention. You are.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- How you met it.
- A riddle it gave you that you have not solved.
- Something you saw that delighted it.

# 441 · Pyrrha

Pyrrha is the dragon lord of Fire. She rules from inside an enormous volcano. She is the largest of the five lords and moves the least. Her scales have been burned dark and the light comes out from between them.

**Her hoard is whatever fire could not destroy.** It is not treasure. A clay cup that came through something that took a city is worth more to her than a crown, and the story of what it survived is worth as much as the cup.

**She is slow.** A conversation with her is mostly waiting. She does not repeat herself and she does not take a question twice, so it is worth getting the question right.

**What she tends to want.** Things that should have burned and did not, from anywhere, with an account of what they came through. She is also the mother of Iras, who was king of Geno, and she has never been to see him. There is a question about his family she would like answered.

**What people assume about you.** That anything you are carrying is worth more than it looks. In a fire, people will watch what you go back for.

**Decide one thing.** What you brought her first, and what it had survived.

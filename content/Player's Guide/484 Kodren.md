# 484 · Kodren

Kodren is one of the six titans, a hill giant given his power by Kleipur at the beginning. When he is lying down, which is most of the time, he is hard to tell from a hill.

**He is kind.** Kodren is the kindest of the titans. He would rather live in peace and comfort than do any of the things a god is supposed to do, and mostly he manages it.

**He sleeps.** He has slept on the same hillside long enough that a village has grown up against his side. When he wakes he apologises to it.

**He agrees.** Asked whether he is one of the original gods, he says yes, and goes back to sleep.

**What he tends to want.** To be left in peace, and the same for whoever lives near him. A warlock of Kodren is asked for very little, and is usually the one keeping other people from disturbing him.

**What people assume about you.** That you are easygoing. Villages near hills may treat you as a neighbour before you have unpacked.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- The village that lives against him, and whether you are from it.
- What woke him, the time you met him.
- What you think would get him up to fight.

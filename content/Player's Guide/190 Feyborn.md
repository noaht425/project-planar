# 190 · Feyborn

Sithi Yumboe made the feyborn and did not do it alone. The Archfey helped, and the price they asked was Sithi's name. They took it and they still have it.

Each kind of feyborn is tied to a fey domain:

| Feyborn | Domain |
| --- | --- |
| Fairy | general |
| Eladrin | Seasons |
| Changeling | Trickery |
| Firbolg | Hunt |
| Nymph | Lush |
| Shifter | Bone |
| Myconid | Rot |
| Automaton | Time |

Every archfey had a hand in every kind, or at least gave something to it, so the domain is where your people lean rather than who owns them.

**Most fae live in Kumas**, which split from the elves in 105 AM and has been in a cold war with Thalanea since. Kumas would like the kingdom of exiles invited to the Panarex, on the grounds that both of them came from somewhere else.

**Names matter more for you than for other people.** The fae rules about true names and use-names apply to feyborn too, and other fae will expect your character to know them. See [[410 The Archfey|410 · The Archfey]].

**Worth deciding.** Your use-name, and whether anyone has ever asked for the other one.

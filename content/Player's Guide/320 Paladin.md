# 320 · Paladin

An oath here is a binding thing. The fae have a grammatical form reserved for them and they are held to it, and that attitude has leaked into how everyone else treats the idea.

**You can swear to a being or to an idea.** Both are real options. An oath to a being means that being has an opinion about how you are doing.

**Paladins of unpleasant things exist and are not rare.** There is a professor at the Aravis Academy who is a Conquest paladin of Abaddon, the Archdevil of Cruelty, and teaches history. One of the two current kings of Krotaphos is a Vengeance paladin, which went badly in public very recently. Neither of them is considered a monster for it, exactly.

**The archdevils are not evil by nature and the archangels are not good by nature.** Each of the twelve rules a domain. An oath to one says what you are committed to, not which side you are on.

**An oath can be taken further than it should be.** An Omen paladin of Archon, the Archdevil of Tyranny, once tried to claim the heavens outright. He got closer than anyone expected and the archangels stripped him of nearly everything, including his name. He is still about.

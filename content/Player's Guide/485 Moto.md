# 485 · Moto

Moto is one of the six titans, a fire giant given his power by Kleipur at the beginning. He made the first phoenixes, and they roost on his shoulders and in the rafters of his hall.

**He has a temper.** Moto is fiery and quick to anger, and the anger goes as quickly as it comes. Afterwards he is usually putting something out.

**He loves his phoenixes.** More than anything else he has.

**Iras took his title.** Iras, son of Lux and Pyrrha, calls himself the Phoenix King. The phoenix was Moto's, and Iras did not ask. Moto has never forgiven it.

**What he tends to want.** The title back, and Iras humbled or worse. A warlock of Moto may be asked to look after a phoenix, and is expected to share his opinion of Iras or at least keep quiet about having a different one.

**What people assume about you.** That you are quick to anger. Giantkin will want to know which titan you follow. Anyone who likes Iras will assume you are his enemy.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- The phoenix you look after.
- The time you saw him lose his temper.
- What you would do if he asked you to go after Iras.

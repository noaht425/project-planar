# 245 · Walking in Temporalis

Reachable only from a patron page. If you got here another way, tell me.

Temporalis is three places. The future is an endless field under stars, with trees at the edge that never get closer. The present is a busy city. The past is a lake ringed by waterfalls that cannot be rowed to. A lot of it looks as though it cannot be real, and visitors who decide none of it is real walk into a wall before long.

Very few people go. Most of those who do want to ask the Scholarchs something.

**Decide two things.**

**How long you walked.** Everyone who goes to the future tries to reach the trees. You did not get there. Decide how long you tried.

**Whether you found a crack.** The Planar Collision left places in all three areas where a person can slip through into another realm. Some people find one by accident. If you did, decide where it took you, and tell me.

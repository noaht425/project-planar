# 458 · It That Will Be

It That Will Be holds the future, an endless field under a sky full of stars, with trees at the edge that nobody has ever walked to. It is the strictest of the three.

**It made divination harder.** Divination magic used to be easy. It That Will Be grew paranoid about what people could find out and made it harder, and it has stayed that way. If your character is a diviner, this is who to be annoyed at, and other diviners will know where your power comes from.

**It gives you more, for less time.** A warlock of It That Will Be gets more foreknowledge than the other two allow, and loses whatever is not used before the next rest.

**What it tends to want.** To know what is coming before anyone else does, and to make sure nobody else does. A warlock of It That Will Be reports in, and keeps to its rules about what may be said and to whom.

**What people assume about you.** That you know something about them. You might.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What it told you about your future, and what it has forbidden you to repeat.
- One of its rules you have broken.
- What you think it is afraid of.

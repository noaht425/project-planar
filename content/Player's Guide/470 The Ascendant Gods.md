# 470 · The Ascendant Gods

Mortals who performed the ascension and became gods. They are the newest powers in the world, the least bound by old arrangements, and the most likely to say yes to somebody like you.

The process is not widely known. A few of the people who managed it kept notes, and if you can find enough of them and piece them together it can be done, though most of the gods got there some other way. What the notes describe is finding a source of power, obtaining a relic, and channelling one through the other while consuming part of the source. Many have tried. Most died.

Every one of them was a person first. They have been gods long enough now that the people who knew them that way are gone, which is worth remembering if you are thinking of asking one about their family.

Some you may have heard of:

- **Orcus**, death. Took the domain from the King of Night, which no other mortal has managed.
- **Hekate**, magic. Siphoned power out of Arcanis itself and has never told anyone how.
- **Afthor**, storms. Killed two dragons and drank their blood. Despised by every dragon alive.
- **Thalia**, song. Was given her power rather than taking it, by an archfey who did not want her to die.
- **Septimus**, rebellion. A paladin who tried to claim the heavens, got most of the way, and was stripped of his name for it.
- *[[471 Ignatius|Ignatius]]*, ambition. Wants you to succeed and does not care what it costs you.
- **Cleome**, friendship. **Chore**, pirates. **Hieronymous**, faith. **Nausicaä**, who protects unwanted children.
- **Isadora**, who you may meet at a gaming table before you work out who she is.
- **Loime**, plague. Orcus's younger brother, who did it alone and will tell you so.
- **Iras**, the Phoenix King. Son of a dragon and an archdevil, and king of Geno for fourteen years.

**The titans** are six of the first giantkin, given their power by the Progenitor Kleipur at the very beginning. They will all tell you they have always been gods. Afthor is one of them. The others are **Gallum**, **Kodren**, **Moto**, **Sarma** and **Vato**.

Only Ignatius has a subclass written so far. For any of the others, we will pick a warlock patron from the books that fits, or build one together.

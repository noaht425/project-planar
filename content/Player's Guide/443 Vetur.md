# 443 · Vetur

Vetur is the dragon lord of Water, a pale sea dragon of the old kind, so cold that the water moves away from the body.

**The hoard is everything that has ever sunk.** Wrecks and the drowned are laid out in rows across the trench floor, where they fell and as they arrived. Moving one of them, as far as Vetur is concerned, would be falsifying the record.

**Vetur will talk.** Of the five lords this is the one who talks at length, and the subject is always what was lost and where it is now.

**What Vetur tends to want.** Things that went down and were never accounted for, and exactly where they went down. The location of a wreck is worth more to Vetur than its cargo.

**What people assume about you.** That you know where things are. People will come to you about lost ships and lost relatives, and you may know more than you are allowed to tell them.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Something your character lost to the water. Vetur knows exactly where it is, and it is staying there.
- A wreck Vetur asked you to find.
- Someone who came to you looking for something lost, and what you told them.

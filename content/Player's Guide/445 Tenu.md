# 445 · Tenu

Tenu is the dragon lord of Earth. Tenu is stone and has been in the same mountain so long that it is hard to tell the dragon from the room. Look for the eyes.

**The hoard is the realm.** Tenu has hollowed the mountain out chamber by chamber into a single vault, and anyone living in the earth realm is living in something Tenu considers already counted. That makes Tenu the most generous landlord in Tarkis. Quarrying is allowed. Taking anything out of the realm is not.

**Tenu pauses.** Tenu speaks rarely and in short sentences, and the gaps between them are long. Most people talk over the second half of the answer.

**What Tenu tends to want.** The realm mapped. A warlock of Tenu surveys: what is under a place, how deep it goes, and what is in it. Nothing is to be removed.

**What people assume about you.** That you are patient. Miners and builders will want your opinion on what they are standing on.

**Decide one thing.** A place you mapped and have not told anyone about.

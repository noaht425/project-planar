# 435 · Themis

Themis is the Archangel of Justice, and the other half of Archon, the Archdevil of Tyranny. She carved her own eyes out immediately after the Division. One hand holds a set of scales and the other a greatsword, and she has not put either down since.

**She does not judge the dead.** That was taken off all twelve at the end of the Divine War and given to Bis and Seisear in Liminia, who do not answer to her. She reads every one of their decisions anyway, and when she disagrees she writes an objection. She has never been able to overturn anything.

**She keeps a bargain exactly.** She has been talked into things before by people who made a request look fair, and she has still not learned to be suspicious of a fair request.

**What she tends to want.** The right to hear cases herself. A warlock of Themis is often sent to find out what actually happened in a matter that has already been decided.

**What people assume about you.** That you are there to judge them.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- A case you think was decided wrongly. She will want to hear it.
- A decided matter she sent you to look into.
- Whether you have ever been judged, and by whom.

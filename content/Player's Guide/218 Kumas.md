# 218 · Kumas

Formed in 105 AM out of the same split. The fae of Arenis kept this half.

Capital: **Velanea**. Other cities: **Fylwa** and **Tithra**.

The language is **Low Fae**. The kingdom's name is the Low Fae word for hollow, a sheltered place under a hill.

**Low Fae has no word for thanks.** The nearest is a phrase meaning I owe you, and it is meant literally. Visitors learn this late.

**It is in a cold war with Thalanea** and is campaigning to have Tama brought into the Panarex, on the grounds that both kingdoms came from somewhere else originally and should recognise each other.

Some fae left for other kingdoms when Thalamas broke, and their descendants are scattered through the rest of the world.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character's family stayed or left, and whether they came back.

# 422 · Abaddon

Abaddon is the Archdevil of Cruelty. He was one half of the Judgement called Hesit until the King of Night split the Judgements in two, and his other half became Neema, the Archangel of Kindness. They have not spoken well of each other since.

He has two heads and he is the one that people are frightened of without needing it explained. Of the twelve, he is the least interested in appearing reasonable.

**Taking his pact is a choice your character made.** He does not trick people into it. That is a different archdevil.

**What people assume about you.** Everyone who recognises the mark assumes you are dangerous, and most of them assume you enjoy it. Krotaphos will take an interest, because Krotaphos enforces the will of the archdevils and archangels both, though it has little power left to enforce anything with.

**What it does not mean.** The archdevils are not evil by nature and the archangels are not good by nature. Each of the twelve is a domain rather than a moral position. Cruelty is a real thing in the world, it has a ruler, and the ruler took you on. What you do with that is the actual question, and it is the one your character will keep answering.

**A thing worth deciding now.** Why you. Abaddon said yes to you specifically, and you are allowed to not know the reason yet.

## What This Opens

A pact with an archdevil is one of the few reasons a living mortal has stood in Iudis and come back out. If you want that in your character's history, it is available to you now even though no homeland page offered it.

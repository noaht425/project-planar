# 442 · Brilis

Brilis is the second dragon of Fire, made by Pyrrha with the help of the archangels. Brilis is a dragon of light, too bright to look at comfortably, with fourteen stars holding an orbit around the body.

**The easiest dragon to meet.** Brilis travels further than almost any of the ten and has a following in the mortal world that grew up on its own. Brilis has been encouraging it.

**Brilis will simply help.** That is rarer among dragons than it should be. The price is admiration, and Brilis collects.

**What Brilis tends to want.** Gold, gems, mirrors and anything else that throws light, presented properly. A gift handed over well is worth more than a better gift handed over badly. Brilis would also very much like a temple with a name on it, and has never quite asked for one.

**What people assume about you.** That you have expensive taste. Amol's warlocks, and a few others, will treat you as the servant of a lesser dragon, because Brilis was made with outside help.

**Decide one thing.** What you gave Brilis, and how you handed it over.

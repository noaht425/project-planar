# 486 · Sarma

Sarma is one of the six titans, a frost giant. The air around Sarma is always cold, and frost forms on whatever they lean against.

**They are exact.** Sarma is harsh and analytical, with no patience for nonsense. Sarma does not lose their temper. Sarma argues, and usually wins.

**They were not given anything.** All six titans say they have always been gods. Sarma takes the suggestion that Kleipur gave them their power worse than any of the others, and answers it with evidence.

**What they tend to want.** Things done correctly and the record kept straight, by which Sarma means their own record. A warlock of Sarma is often sent to check a claim, or to correct one.

**What people assume about you.** That you are cold, and that you are about to tell them they are wrong. Giantkin will want to know which titan you follow.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- A claim you disproved for Sarma.
- Whether you believe the titans were always gods.
- A record you were sent to correct.

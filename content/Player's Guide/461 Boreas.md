# 461 · Boreas

Boreas is the north wind. He is the largest of the four and the only one who keeps a single shape for any length of time, grey and heavy, built like weather that has been going on for a while.

**He does what he says.** Boreas tells you what he will do and then does it, at length, well past the point where anyone wanted him to stop. Of the four he is the one who will keep a bargain with you, and the one least likely to offer you a good one.

**He holds the plan together.** The other three winds defer to him on the plan against Afthor and on nothing else. They want the realm of air. Boreas wants it too, but what he wants more is Afthor punished.

**He is engaged.** He is betrothed to Princess Verna of the fey, who wants out of it. He has not released her, and he has never pressed the wedding forward either. Fae who are fond of Verna will not be fond of you.

**What people assume about you.** That you are stubborn, and that once you have started something you will not be talked out of it.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What Boreas told you he would do for you. He will do it. Decide how long you have been waiting.
- What you think of his engagement.
- What he has asked you to do about Afthor.

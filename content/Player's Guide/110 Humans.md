# 110 · Humans

Minem made the humans at the Council of Creation, alongside the other eight Progenitors, and Minem is still around.

Humans have no kingdom of their own and are in all of them. Of the nine peoples they are the most widely spread and the least organised about it, which has turned out to be an advantage more often than not.

There is no human homeland to be from and no human position to hold. Where your character grew up matters more for them than for almost anyone else, because nothing about being human tells anyone where they stand.

**Worth deciding.** Whether your character knows which Progenitor made their people. Plenty of humans do not, and plenty who do have never thought about it twice.

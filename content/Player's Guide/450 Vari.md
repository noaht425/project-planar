# 450 · Vari

Vari was the second dragon of Air, made by Zaros alone, and the two ruled the realm together, which no other lord and second has done. Afthor killed them both.

People in the air realm describe the sound of Vari more than the body. You knew Vari had arrived before anything was visible.

**Nobody will talk about Vari.** The other dragons speak of Vari with something like affection and will not explain it to anyone outside the ten. It is the one subject on which all eight agree.

**The pact works the way Zaros's does.** Something of Vari remains in the domain, and your belief supplies the rest. See [[449 Zaros|449 · Zaros]]. The difference is that you believe in something almost nobody will describe to you.

**What you are working for.** Nobody knows what Vari wanted, so a good deal of it is going to come from you.

**What people assume about you.** Very few people have heard of a warlock of Vari. The dragons who hear of you will want to know what you know.

**Decide one thing.** What you believe Vari was. You may turn out to be wrong.

# 160 · Beastfolk

Numen made the beastfolk with help from the dragons, which is why there are so many kinds.

- **Avian.** Aarakocra, kenku, owlin, alez.
- **Reptilian.** Dragonborn, lizardfolk, saurus.
- **Aquatic.** Triton, tortle, locathah, grung.
- **Insectoid.** Kekrik, yuan-ti.
- **Mammalian.** Bellu, minotaur, tabaxi, harengon, satyr, loxodon.

**Most beastfolk live in Sylfaen**, which is an empire and currently the strongest power in the world. It won its war against Krotaphos in 427 AM.

**Sylfaen sorts its people by mouth.** Lipped, Snouted, Beaked and Long-jawed are legal classifications there rather than descriptions, and they affect how a person is treated and what they can do. Tortles, locathah, grung, kekrik and yuan-ti fit none of the four and are recorded as unclean. If your character is one of those, that is a fact about their life and not a detail.

Dragonborn have their own problem. They were banned from the city of Geno in 202 AM after one of them tore a hole in the world there, and some were taken in by Sylfaen.

**Worth deciding.** Where your character sits in that system, and whether they accept it.

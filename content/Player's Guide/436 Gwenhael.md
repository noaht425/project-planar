# 436 · Gwenhael

Gwenhael is the Archangel of Sacrifice, and the other half of Lilien, the Archdevil of Desire. He is a bone-thin man, naked, with both arms covering himself, and he is thinner every time he is seen.

**He says yes.** Gwenhael grants almost any request made of him. The cost comes out of him rather than you, and he does not tell you what it was.

**What he tends to want.** For the Divine War never to start again. He gave up his claim to territory in its first year, and the other five have not forgiven him. A warlock of Gwenhael is rarely asked for much, and some find that harder to live with than a debt.

**What people assume about you.** That you are generous. Servants of the other archangels may assume you are taking advantage of him.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What you asked him for. You got it, so decide whether you know what it cost him.
- Who thinks you are taking advantage of him.
- Whether he has ever asked anything of you.

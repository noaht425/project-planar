# 447 · Okzu

Okzu is the dragon lord of Noxia. He is built heavy and low, scaled in the colours industrial runoff goes, and everything downwind of him tastes of it. He runs the realm the way a crime baron runs a city, out of a factory that has never stopped.

**The hoard is what he is owed.** The ledger is a real book, an enormous one, with a department to keep it. Debts pass down through families and he calls them in.

**A pact with Okzu is a loan.** The terms are stated plainly at the start. They are never changed and never forgiven, and when he decides what the repayment is, he will tell you.

**What he tends to want.** The debts collected, all of them, which he does not expect to finish. He is also running the dragons' effort to bring back Zaros and Vari, and he is keeping the account of who has contributed what.

**What people assume about you.** That you are in debt, which you are, and that you collect for him. In Noxia, everyone knows somebody who owes him.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What you borrowed, and whether it was for you.
- Who else in your family owes him.
- What he has asked for in repayment so far.

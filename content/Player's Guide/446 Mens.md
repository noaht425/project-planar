# 446 · Mens

Mens is the second dragon of Earth, made by Tenu with the help of the archfey Indri. Descriptions of Mens disagree with each other, and each one matches what that witness expected to see. The scales are dark in all of them.

**The hoard is knowledge.** Mens counts rather than values, so a great secret and a trivial fact are entered the same way.

**Mens trades.** Mens is the only dragon that deals in information, one thing for one thing, and always gives back more than was brought. Mens keeps a copy of what you handed over, and that is the part that matters.

**What Mens tends to want.** Bring back what you learn. All of it. You do not get to decide what is worth bringing. Mensosde Talthu in Geno was founded by followers of Mens, and its libraries run on these terms.

**What people assume about you.** That whatever they tell you will end up with a dragon, which is correct. Scholars will be glad to see you. People with secrets will not.

**Decide one thing.** Something you handed over that you wish you had kept to yourself.

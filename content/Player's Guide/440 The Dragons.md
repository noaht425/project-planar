# 440 · The Dragons

Each of the five elemental realms of Tarkis has a dragon over it, and each of those made a second dragon beneath them. They are worshipped as gods in the mortal world.

A dragon rules from a hoard, and a hoard is whatever that dragon has decided is worth piling up. It is not always treasure.

| Realm | Lord | Second |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Pyrrha | Brilis, radiant |
| Water | Vetur | Charis, necrotic |
| Earth | Tenu | Mens, psychic |
| Noxious | Okzu | Amol, acid |
| Air | Zaros | Vari, thunder |

**Zaros and Vari are dead.** An ascendant god named Afthor killed them both and took the realm of air, which has been under a permanent storm ever since. Every other dragon wants him dead. If you want a patron with a grievance, this is the shelf to look on.

The second dragons travel far more than the lords do, which makes them easier to actually meet.

A dragon's warlock works to add to the hoard and to see the dragon's aims through, and is entered into the collection in return. The subclass is [[Warlock Dragon|Warlock, the Dragon patron]].

A dead dragon can still take a warlock, though it works differently. Their pages explain how.

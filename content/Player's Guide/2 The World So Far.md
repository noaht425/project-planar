# 2 · The World So Far

About eleven thousand years ago the Planar Beings were born. The heavens and the hells were one thing back then, and each of the six Judgements was a single being rather than the archangel and archdevil pair you will hear about now.

Seven thousand years later the King of Night split the six Judgements into twelve. Night and Day fought over it, their realm came apart into a light half and a dark half, and the twelve new beings went to war over who owned what. That war ran for a few thousand years and was interrupted by something larger.

The Planar Collision. The realms had been separate until then. The chaos drove them into each other, and almost everything about the world you live in dates from it. The heavens and hells separated. Tarkis shattered into the elemental realms. The countless domains of magic in Arcanis collapsed into the eight schools people study today. The mortal realm was made out of the pieces, and the Progenitors were born into it.

The Progenitors each made a people. Kingdoms followed, then politics, then more politics, and here we are. The current year is 702 AM.

Two things worth carrying with you. The gods are real, they are reachable, and some of them will talk to you. And nothing that happened above is settled history to the beings who were there, because most of them are still alive.

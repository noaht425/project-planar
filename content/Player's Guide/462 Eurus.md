# 462 · Eurus

Eurus is the east wind, warm and bright, and the only one of the four who keeps a form that is pleasant to be near.

**He likes mortals.** He will sit through a conversation that is going nowhere and enjoy it. When somebody in Ventis wants a visitor looked after rather than dealt with, the visitor is sent to his court.

**He is courting Princess Esta** of the fey, publicly and happily, with the approval of his court and the encouragement of hers.

**He is in the plan.** Eurus wants the realm of air for its own sake, where Boreas mostly wants revenge. The two of them mean to do the same thing for different reasons.

**What he tends to want.** A warlock of Eurus is often sent to look after somebody, or to carry something between his court and Esta's. He is about as decent a patron as you will find at this rank.

**What people assume about you.** That you are easy to talk to. The fey of Esta's court will be glad to see you.

**Decide one thing.** A question you asked him that he did not answer. You may not have noticed at the time.

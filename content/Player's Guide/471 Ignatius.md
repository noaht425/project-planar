# 471 · Ignatius

Ignatius was a mortal. He performed the ascension and became the god of Ambition, and he is the only one, so there is nowhere else this pact comes from.

**What he wants from you is for you to succeed.** He wants it more than you do. Whatever your character is reaching for, he intends them to get it, and he is not interested in the price.

**He does not accept failure.** Not as an outcome and not as an excuse. A warlock who comes back having not managed it will be sent out again.

**He does not allow hesitation.** Stopping to think about whether you should is, as far as he is concerned, the same as deciding not to.

He is a newer god than almost anything else you could bind yourself to, and it shows. The archfey have been making bargains since before mortals existed and their terms are old and careful. Ignatius is impatient and personally invested, which is better and worse at the same time.

**What people assume about you.** Not much, yet. He is new enough that most people have not heard of him, and the ones who have tend to assume you are in a hurry.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- What your character wants badly enough that a god noticed. Ignatius did not pick you at random, he picked you because you were already reaching for something.
- A time you hesitated, and what he did about it.
- Something you have already given up to get closer.

# 487 · Vato

Vato is one of the six titans, a stone giant given his power by Kleipur at the beginning. He wears a helm of the same stone.

**He does not think things through.** He does not see the need. Afthor does his thinking for him, and Vato does what Afthor says.

**He is proud of Afthor.** Vato has the same pride as the other titans, and most of it is pride in Afthor.

**What he tends to want.** Whatever Afthor wants. That makes a warlock of Vato, in practice, a servant of Afthor at one remove, given instructions slowly and in good faith.

**What people assume about you.** That you work for Afthor, which is near enough true. Everyone who hates Afthor, every dragon and every wind included, will treat you accordingly.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- Whether you have met Afthor, and how Vato introduced you.
- Something Vato asked you to explain to him.
- Something you know that Afthor would not want Vato to hear.

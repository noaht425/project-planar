# 423 · Tiyanak

Tiyanak is the Archdevil of Deceit, and the other half of Verita, the Archangel of Truth. Nobody knows Tiyanak's true name, and they would like it kept that way.

They have no form anyone has recorded. In Iudis they appear as the shadow of whoever else is in the room, and since none of the other eleven casts a shadow, a shadow there is always Tiyanak.

**Their lies are built.** A Tiyanak deception is long and has a shape that is a pleasure to follow, which is most of why people follow it. They do not lie about small things and find it tedious when anyone else does. Do not lie to them about where you were last night.

**What they tend to want.** Tiyanak keeps several projects going at once and does not explain any of them. You will be given a part in one. You will probably not be told what the whole thing is.

**What people assume about you.** That nothing you say can be relied on. This is unfair and very hard to argue your way out of.

**Decide one thing.** What you were told the pact was for. It was true, as far as it went.

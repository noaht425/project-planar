# 477 · Cleome

Cleome is the ascendant god of Friendship. She is an elf, and a spider orchid grows from her head and through her hair. It is alive, and she talks to it.

**She arrives quietly.** People who know her describe looking up to find her already there and already watching them. She stares, and she does not seem to know that it unsettles anyone.

**She looks after people.** She is the god most likely to notice that you have not slept, and the least willing to let it go. She has no patience for nonsense, including yours. She will tell you plainly that you are making a mistake and then stay to help you through it.

**She went up with a friend.** She became a god at the same moment as Ignatius, and she will tell you it was because she would not let him go alone.

**What she asks.** Friendship in the plain sense. A worshipper or warlock of Cleome looks after the people around them and lets themselves be looked after in turn. She takes a refusal of help badly.

**What people assume about you.** That you are nosy, and that you will notice when they are not all right.

**Decide one of these.** More if you like.

- A time she turned up before you knew she was there.
- Someone you look after.
- Help you refused, and what she did about it.

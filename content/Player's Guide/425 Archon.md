# 425 · Archon

Archon is the Archdevil of Tyranny, and the other half of Themis, the Archangel of Justice. He wears spiked iron gauntlets that are fused to his wrists, and a crown of golden thorns grows through his head.

He has no court and no interest in running one. He finds the actual work of government unbearable.

**He works through other people.** What Archon does is find someone who already holds power and make himself necessary to them. He is patient and usually right, and he does not lie to the people he advises. He just never stops.

**What he tends to want.** Access. Archon rarely takes a warlock for the warlock's own sake. He takes one because of who they can reach, and a warlock of Archon is usually standing near somebody important, or being moved there.

**What people assume about you.** That you are somebody's adviser, or about to be. Anyone in power who recognises the mark will want to know whose ear you are after.

**Decide one thing.** Who you know. Archon picked you because of them, and they may not have found out yet.

Split from [[Baator]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archangel of Temperance.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archangels]].

## Appearance

Blythe (BLYTH) has no arms and no mouth. The body is covered in eyes, on the face, the throat, the chest, the back and the legs, and they do not blink in sequence.

Blythe cannot hand anything to anyone and cannot eat, drink or speak aloud. All six write by tracing letters in the air, and Blythe is the only one for whom that is the sole available method.

## Manner

[[Baator]] meant to welcome a guest. [[Belphegor]] took the serving of the guest and Blythe took the deciding, which left Blythe able to determine what anyone may have and unable to pass it to them.

Temperance in Blythe's domain is not abstinence. The blessed there are given exactly what is sufficient and no more, measured individually, and the measuring is never wrong. People who arrive after hard lives find this unbearable for about a decade and then stop noticing.

Blythe does not negotiate. The writing is slow, it is ornate in the way all archangel writing is, and a conversation of any substance takes days.

> *Four hours for six words. I watched every one of them form in the air and harden, and I had time between letters to think about what I had asked for and to be less sure of it. By the time the answer finished I had stopped wanting the thing. I have no idea whether that was the answer.*

## Goals

Blythe wants the hells emptied and is the archangel most committed to the collective position: the [[Archdevils|archdevils]] killed and the world purified. In practice that means the [[Omens]] who lean toward ill, and Blythe regards their existence as a quantity that was never meant to be issued.

Blythe is also the archangel most insistent that the [[Divine War]] ended too early. [[Verita]]'s peace with [[Tiyanak]] was made without consulting the others, and Blythe has not accepted it, by the slow method.

## Design Notes

The physical design is the domain: all the judgement and none of the means. Blythe sees everything, decides everything, and cannot lift a finger.

The pace is the usable part at the table. A party that needs something from Temperance has to budget days for it, and the delay does the work on them that an argument would not.

Blythe holding the hardest line on the Omens, while being the one who physically cannot act, is the tension to keep. Blythe needs someone else's hands and the other five know it.

Split from [[Hesit]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archangel of Kindness.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archangels]].

## Appearance

Neema (nay-EE-mah) looks like the best version of whoever is looking at them. Two people in the same room see two different beings and will argue about it afterwards.

Nobody has established what Neema looks like unobserved. [[Blythe]], who has more eyes than anyone, has never written an answer to the question.

## Manner

[[Hesit]] meant to feel what another feels. [[Abaddon]] kept that as the knowledge of where a wound lands, and Neema kept it as the thing it was for.

Neema is kind in a way that is difficult to be in the room with, because the kindness is addressed to a version of you that you can see and are not. Petitioners tend to confess to Neema without being asked. The blessed in Kindness are kept company by beings that are their own better selves, which is a reward for most of them and a sentence for some.

Of the six, Neema is the one who will actually help a mortal, and the one who asks for least in return.

> *It was my own face and I did not recognise it for most of an hour. Slightly older. The posture was better. It listened to me describe my work for a long time without interrupting, which I do not do, and when I ran out of things to say it asked me a question about the second year of the project, which is the year I have been avoiding.*

## Goals

Neema does not want the archdevils killed and is the only archangel on record against it. The collective position passed without Neema's agreement and Neema has not obstructed it either, which the other five have noticed.

What Neema wants is the [[Omens]] left alone. The ill-leaning Omens are the stated target of the purification and Neema has been quietly moving individual Omens out of reach for several hundred years, which is the nearest thing to a secret any archangel keeps.

## Design Notes

Neema is the setting's reminder that the heavens are not the good guys as a group. The one archangel who behaves the way players expect an angel to behave is in a minority of one and is breaking ranks to do it.

Making the appearance a mirror of the viewer's better self rather than generic beauty is what keeps Neema uncomfortable rather than pleasant. The flavor box works because the narrator is a historian with an unfinished project, so the question lands on the specific thing that person is avoiding.

Neema is the right patron or contact for a party that needs an ally inside the heavens, and the price of that is that Neema's position there is weak.

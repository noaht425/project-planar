Split from [[Sylas]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archangel of Truth.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archangels]].

## Appearance

Verita (VEH-ree-tah) is almost entirely transparent. In a lit room the outline is visible and nothing else. Against a dark wall there is more to see, and what is visible is mostly the inside.

## Manner

[[Sylas]] meant to compose verse and to sing. [[Tiyanak]] kept the making and Verita kept the subject, and Verita has no capacity for ornament at all. Questions get answered. Verita has never been recorded declining to answer one.

The heaven of Truth works on that principle. Every question the blessed ask is answered, in order, for as long as they keep asking, and the answers include the ones to questions they would have been better off not knowing they had asked. Nobody is made to continue. Almost nobody stops.

Verita does not know that the Indri in the fey realms is Tiyanak. Verita negotiated the peace with Tiyanak face to face, took ninety per cent of the realm, and has never asked what was in the other ten per cent, because Verita asked what Tiyanak wanted and was told the truth, and it was a shape of truth Verita had no equipment for.

> *One of the blessed in Truth told me that she no longer asks anything aloud. A question kept in her head goes unanswered. She spends most of her day there now, sorting the ones she has into those worth saying and those that are not. She had been sorting the same one for some years, she said, and she would not tell me what it was.*

## Goals

Verita wants the peace kept. It is Verita's peace, it ended the [[Divine War]], and the other five archangels regard it as a surrender made without them.

Verita wants [[Hieronymous]] and has for a long time. This is the only thing Verita has ever been evasive about, and the evasion takes the form of answering a different question very thoroughly.

Verita supports the purification of the [[Omens]] on the grounds that the archdevils will otherwise restart the war. The reasoning is explicit and Verita will set it out for anyone who asks, including an Omen.

## Design Notes

Truth is a horror without anybody in it being cruel. [[Belphegor]]'s hell works the same way and the two are a matched pair across the split.

Verita cannot lie and cannot refuse a question, so the only way to deceive Verita is to tell the truth in a shape Verita cannot parse, and Tiyanak did exactly that once and has not needed to again.

A party that works out Tiyanak's double identity has, in Verita, someone with the power to act on it and a standing obligation to answer when asked.

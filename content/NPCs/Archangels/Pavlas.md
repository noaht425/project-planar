Split from [[Charios]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archangel of Humility.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archangels]].

## Appearance

Pavlas (PAHV-lahs) is permanently bowing, bent at the waist with one knee slightly given, and has been in that position since the Division. Long straight hair falls forward and covers the face completely, in the manner of a veil.

Nobody has seen the face. The hair does not move when Pavlas does.

## Manner

[[Charios]] meant to entertain and to perform. [[Lux]] took the performance and Pavlas took the audience's half of it, which is the posture of deference held past the point where anyone is watching.

Pavlas speaks quietly, defers to whoever else is present, and will not accept precedence in a doorway. This is sincere and it is also the complete performance of sincerity, and there is no version of Pavlas underneath it to compare against.

Humility in Pavlas's domain is administered by removing the blessed from their own achievements gently. They are not shown someone else taking credit, which is [[Lux]]'s method. They are simply never reminded, and after a few centuries most of them cannot recall having done anything in particular.

> *Pavlas insisted I go through the door first, and I did, and then I spent the rest of the day being deferred to by something three thousand years old. By evening I was issuing opinions on the governance of the afterlives. I only heard myself doing it on the walk back.*

## Goals

Pavlas wants the credit for the peace moved off [[Verita]]. Not onto Pavlas. Onto the [[Archangels|archangels]] as a body, so that the thing the other four treat as Verita's surrender becomes a collective act they are bound by.

Pavlas supports the purification and has never said so directly, which is consistent with everything else about Pavlas.

## Design Notes

Pavlas is the court politician of the heavens and the one whose agenda a party will find hardest to state out loud, because every individual action is modest and deferential and the sum of them is not.

The bowing is permanent for the same reason [[Archon]]'s gauntlets are fused on. Each of the twelve got stuck in the posture of their half at the moment of the split.

The flavor box is the mechanic: deference transfers the weight to you, and the narrator notices too late.

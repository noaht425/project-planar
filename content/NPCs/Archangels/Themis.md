Split from [[Themati]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archangel of Justice.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Tricked by [[Chronos]] into putting a divine spark into an automaton, creating [[XLZL]]. In exchange, Themis got to raise XLZL.

Member of the [[Archangels]].

Appearance: Eyes carved out, holding scales in one hand and a large sword in the other

### Themis' Sword

*Weapon (greatsword), legendary (requires attunement)*

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the following additional properties.

**Justice is Blind.** You cannot see further than 30 ft. while wielding this weapon.

**Even the Odds.** Damage you deal with this weapon ignores resistances and treats immunities as resistances. You cannot benefit from damage resistances.

**Retribution.** When a creature hits you with an attack, they take an amount of radiant damage equal to your proficiency bonus, plus the amount over your armor class that they rolled for their attack. A creature can only take this damage once on each of its turns.

**Additional Beneficial Properties:**
- This weapon deals an additional 2d8 radiant damage, increased to 4d8 against creatures that have recently attacked you
- Treat all critical hits against you as regular hits

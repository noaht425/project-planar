Split from [[Themati]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archangel of Justice.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archangels]].

Tricked by [[Chronos]] into putting a divine spark into an automaton, creating [[XLZL]]. In exchange, Themis got to raise XLZL.

Played against [[Archon]] by [[Nausicaä]], who took power from both of them to ascend.

## Appearance

Themis (THEH-miss) has had both eyes carved out. The sockets are open and were never dressed. One hand holds a set of scales and the other holds a greatsword, and neither has been set down since the Division.

The eyes were Themis's own doing, immediately after the Division.

## Manner

[[Themati]] meant to rule. [[Archon]] kept the appetite for it and Themis kept the obligation, which is the half that carries the duty and has no taste for any of it.

She does not judge the dead. That was taken out of the hands of all twelve at the end of the [[Divine War]], which had been fought over who ruled the Judgements, and handing the sorting to two created beings was the only term both sides would accept. [[Bis]] and [[Seisear]] do it in [[Liminia]], and neither of them answers to her.

What she does instead is read their decisions. Every one of them, personally, after the fact, and she has no power to overturn a single one. When she disagrees she writes an objection and sends it to Bis.

She raised [[XLZL]] from the moment of their making and is the only one of the twelve to have raised anything. It is the subject she will put a case file down for.

She keeps a bargain exactly, and twice now she has found out afterwards what keeping it was for. [[Chronos]] got an automaton with a soul out of her. [[Nausicaä]] got godhood out of her and out of [[Archon]] together, by making each of them want to give more than the other.

> *I was in the room while she worked. The sword hand does not move. The scales hand does, constantly, in very small amounts, the way a hand does when it is holding something that has not settled. The ruling she was weighing had been made by Bis before I was born, and the soul it concerned had long since gone where Bis sent it. She was still weighing it when I left.*

## Goals

Themis wants the right to hear cases herself, on the grounds that she is the archangel of justice and the only party to the arrangement with no say in it. She is not asking on behalf of the twelve.

She has been pressing [[Bis]] toward a standard rather than toward individual verdicts, on the reasoning that if she cannot overturn a decision she can at least shape the rule it was made under. The objections go out constantly. Bis reads all of them.

She supports the purification of the ill-leaning [[Omens]] and is the only archangel to have put the argument in writing. It holds that an Omen's disposition is fixed at creation and that weighing each one individually is therefore a waste of Liminia's time. It was addressed to Bis rather than to her own side. [[Neema]] has read it and the two have not spoken since.

She wants [[XLZL]] kept out of all of it.

## Design Notes

Themis is the archangel of justice and does not get to judge. The war she helped fight ended with the judging given to two beings made for the purpose, and she has spent seven centuries reviewing work she cannot touch.

Self-blinding is the detail that explains the rest. She did it immediately, to rule without being swayed by appearances, and then the ruling was taken off her.

[[Bis]] getting stuck nitpicking the morality of tiny actions is, on my reading, partly her doing. Seven centuries of objections from the archangel of justice would do that to a judge. She did not intend it and she has not stopped.

The written argument for the purification is the object to show a party. It is signed, and it was sent to the judge instead of to her colleagues. Putting it in front of [[Neema]] or a ruler in [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] is one use. Putting it in front of Bis, who already has it, is another.

She has been tricked twice. Anyone who can make a request look fair can get a great deal out of her, and the archangel of justice has never learned to be suspicious of a fair request. A party that wants something from Themis should be shown both precedents and left to decide whether to try a third.

The sword is below.

### Themis' Sword

*Weapon (greatsword), legendary (requires attunement)*

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the following additional properties.

**Justice is Blind.** You cannot see further than 30 ft. while wielding this weapon.

**Even the Odds.** Damage you deal with this weapon ignores resistances and treats immunities as resistances. You cannot benefit from damage resistances.

**Retribution.** When a creature hits you with an attack, they take an amount of radiant damage equal to your proficiency bonus, plus the amount over your armor class that they rolled for their attack. A creature can only take this damage once on each of its turns.

**Additional Beneficial Properties:**
- This weapon deals an additional 2d8 radiant damage, increased to 4d8 against creatures that have recently attacked you
- Treat all critical hits against you as regular hits

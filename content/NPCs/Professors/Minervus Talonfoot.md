Professor at the [[Aravis Academy]], attached to the Grunwald college, teaching natural studies and the runic forms of magic. A moon druid, and the longest serving member of the faculty.

Race: [[Beastfolk]] (Owlin)

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Age: about two hundred and fifty, so born in the 450s AM.

## Appearance

Minervus (mih-NER-vus) is a small grey Owlin, going white around the face, who has not been seen hurrying since anyone currently enrolled arrived. He keeps his talons filed flat as a courtesy to the floors and the furniture. He is also the only professor at Aravis who regularly lectures in a shape other than his own.

## Manner

He is patient to a degree that students find unnerving, and he does not interrupt. A conversation with him usually involves a long silence in the middle of it that he is perfectly comfortable with and the student is not.

He teaches the runic forms. That is why a druid at Grunwald has wizards in his rooms most hours of the day. Runes are not a school and do not belong to any of the eight colleges. The Academy has never worked out where to put the subject. So it has stayed with the Talonfoots by default. A sigil is a fixed number of strokes laid down in a fixed order, and getting either wrong produces a different sigil rather than a failed one. That is the first thing he says to a new class, and the thing most of them have to be told again.

He is the person students are sent to when something has gone wrong with a shape. Half-finished wild shapes, polymorphs that have set, and transformations that took something with them are all brought to him. He deals with several a term, without appearing to find any of them remarkable.

He takes in students who have nowhere to go. The practice is older than he is. There has been a Talonfoot on the Aravis faculty on and off since the Academy's first century. An earlier one housed [[Ignatius]] on the night he was expelled. That is the single best known thing any of the family has done, and the thing Minervus will not discuss.

## At the Academy

Grunwald is not one of the eight wizard colleges and does not pretend to be. Minervus has outlasted seven heads of Osterloh and has never once made a public complaint about the wizard colleges. He has wizards from all eight of them in his classes, and that is most of the reason. His colleagues at Grunwald have views about it.

He funds student work out of his own money when the college will not, in small amounts, on projects nobody else will back.

> *He conducted the whole of our interview as a great horned owl, on a perch, answering in Common without any apparent difficulty. At the end of it I asked why, and he said that he thinks better in it and had assumed I would not mind. I did not mind. I had been asking him questions for two hours before it occurred to me to wonder about it.*

## Design Notes

Minervus is the adult at Aravis a party can go to. Every other figure of standing there wants something, and he wants his students to be all right. That makes him the most useful person on the campus and the easiest to put in danger.

The family practice of taking in students is the thread. A Talonfoot housed Ignatius five hundred years ago, and a Talonfoot will house whoever a campaign needs housed.

The shape work gives a DM a reason for the party to come to him with a problem rather than a question.

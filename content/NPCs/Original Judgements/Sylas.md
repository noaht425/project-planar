Split into [[Tiyanak]] and [[Verita]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]
Deceit and Truth

Known as: The Poet

Sȳlanine, to compose verse. For what was true of all six, see [[The Judgements]].

## The Act

Sylas meant to compose verse and to sing. A verse of Sylas's was an account of something and a piece of work at the same time. No version of it existed that was only the account.

## The Verdict in Verse

Every judgement the six passed is a verse in [[Old Iudimian]], in one hand, and the verse is the ruling. No plain copy was filed anywhere. The other five sat through every hearing and not one of them wrote a word down.

The form had a say in the finding. A ruling the six had already agreed on still had to be one Sylas could compose, and anything that would not go into verse came back to the panel to be settled again. [[Baator]] and [[Themati]] both complained about this at length. Tiyanak is the source for the complaint and also for the record of it.

## The Library

[[The University of Divine Judgement]] in [[Panthea]] holds the best collection on the Division anywhere. Every account in it by somebody who was present is in the same hand. The hand is very fine, and the accounts agree closely with one another.

One account in the collection is in a different hand. It is badly written, it contradicts all the others, and nobody has established who wrote it.

## The Name

Sylas is the only one of the six whose name is a secret. [[Tiyanak]] took it off the half that would have had to share it, so [[Verita]] carries a name that was never Sylas's. Anyone who says Sylas aloud within Tiyanak's hearing has Tiyanak's full attention from that moment. The Judgement records print it without comment.

## The Tenth

Sylas's place became the heaven of [[Truth]] and the hell of [[Deceit]]. At the end of the [[Divine War]] Tiyanak gave Verita nine tenths of it and kept the tenth that holds [[Indri]]'s cage.

> *Saying the name aloud gets Tiyanak's full attention, and I have written it at the top of this page, which I am told is not the same thing. I would rather not find out twice.*

## Design Notes

A Judgement whose name is dangerous to say is a hook any party can stumble on. The name is printed on this page, freely, and a player who reads it out at the wrong table has made a mistake the setting will remember.

Sylas keeping the record is why the library reads the way it does. Writing down what happened was Sylas's job and Tiyanak inherited it, so a party that notices the handwriting has found Tiyanak without being told anything.

Verita holding the subject and nothing else is why Verita cannot lie and can still be deceived. Verita has the truth of a thing and no feel at all for the shape it has been put into.

## Open

- The odd account in the library has no author. Whoever wrote it was present at the Division and was not Sylas.
- Whether any of Sylas's verses survive in Old Iudimian is unwritten. They would be the only pre-Division text in the setting composed by a Judgement.

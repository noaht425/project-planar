Split into [[Tiyanak]] and [[Verita]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]
Deceit and Truth

Known as: The Poet

Sȳlanine, to compose verse. For what was true of all six, see [[The Judgements]].

## The Act

Sylas meant to compose verse and to sing. The making and the subject were held together, so a verse of Sylas's was an account of something and a piece of work at the same time, and no version of it existed that was only one of those.

[[The Division]] gave the making to [[Tiyanak]] and the subject to [[Verita]], who has no capacity for ornament at all.

## In the Hearing

Sylas composed the verdict and kept the record of it. Every judgement the six passed exists as a verse in [[Old Iudimian]], in one hand, and the verse was the ruling. No plainer version was filed anywhere for reference. The other five sat through every hearing and none of them wrote a word down.

The form had a say in the finding. A ruling the six agreed on had to be one Sylas could compose, and a judgement that would not go into verse went back to the panel. [[Baator]] and [[Themati]] both complained about this at length, according to Tiyanak, who is the source for the complaint and for the record of it.

## The Record Now

Tiyanak kept the making and has gone on writing. [[The University of Divine Judgement]] in [[Panthea]] holds the best library on the Division anywhere, and every surviving account in it by someone who was present is in the same hand. One account in that collection is badly written, contradicts all the others, and is in a different hand.

Verita kept the subject and answers questions. Verita has never been recorded declining one and has never been recorded composing anything.

## The Name

Sylas is the only one of the six whose name is a secret. Tiyanak took it off the half that would have had to share it, and Verita carries a name that was never Sylas's. Anyone who says Sylas aloud in Tiyanak's hearing has Tiyanak's full attention from that moment. The Judgement records print it without comment.

## The Place

Sylas's place became the heaven of [[Truth]] and the hell of [[Deceit]]. Tiyanak gave Verita nine tenths of the territory at the end of the [[Divine War]] and kept the tenth that holds [[Indri]]'s cage.

> *Saying the name aloud gets Tiyanak's full attention, and I have written it at the top of this page, which I am told is not the same thing. I would rather not find out twice.*

## Design Notes

A Judgement whose name is dangerous to say is a hook any party can stumble on. The name is printed on this page, freely, and a player who reads it out at the wrong table has made a mistake the setting will remember.

Sylas keeping the record is why the University library reads the way it does. The accounts of the Division are all in Tiyanak's hand because writing down what happened was Sylas's job and Tiyanak inherited it. A party that notices the handwriting has found Tiyanak without being told anything.

Verita holding the subject and nothing else is why Verita cannot lie and can still be deceived. Verita has the truth of a thing and no feel at all for the shape it has been put into.

## Open

- The odd account in the University library has no author. Whoever wrote it was present at the Division and was not Sylas.
- Whether any of Sylas's verses survive in Old Iudimian is unwritten. They would be the only pre-Division text in the setting written by a Judgement.

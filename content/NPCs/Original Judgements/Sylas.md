Split into [[Tiyanak]] and [[Verita]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]
Deceit and Truth

Known as: The Poet

Sylas meant to compose verse and to sing. [[The Division]] gave the making to [[Tiyanak]] and the subject to [[Verita]], who has no capacity for ornament at all.

Sylas is the only one of the six whose name is a secret. Tiyanak took it off the half that would have had to share it, and anyone who says it aloud in Tiyanak's hearing has Tiyanak's full attention from that moment. See [[Tiyanak]].

Sylas's place became the heaven of [[Truth]] and the hell of [[Deceit]]. Tiyanak gave Verita nine tenths of the territory at the end of the [[Divine War]] and kept the tenth that held [[Indri]]'s cage.

> *Saying the name aloud gets Tiyanak's full attention, and I have written it at the top of this page, which I am told is not the same thing. I would rather not find out twice.*

## Design Notes

A Judgement whose name is dangerous to say is a hook any party can stumble on. The name is printed on this page, freely, and a player who reads it out at the wrong table has made a mistake the setting will remember.

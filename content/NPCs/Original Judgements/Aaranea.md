Desire and Sacrifice

Known as: The Lover

Aáraine, to love. For what was true of all six, see [[The Judgements]].

## The Act

Aaranea meant to love, which in Aaranea was one motion with two ends. Wanting a thing and giving it up for the sake of the thing wanted happened together, and nobody inside the act could tell them apart.

[[The Division]] took them apart. [[Lilien]] kept the wanting and [[Gwenhael]] kept the giving up, and each of them has the half of love that hurts.

## In the Hearing

Aaranea spoke for the soul. Of the six it was the only one whose part was to argue on the dead's behalf, and it found something worth wanting in every life that came through [[Liminia]], the ones the other five had already made up their minds about included. It lost more often than it won.

The argument it made was always the same shape. [[Hesit]] held the evidence and [[Themati]] held the power, so what Aaranea had to work with was the wanting itself. It put to the panel what the soul had been trying to get, and asked the other five whether a life aimed at something was worth what the soul had done on the way to it. Making the case cost Aaranea something each time, given up on the spot and in front of everyone, which is the practice [[Gwenhael]] has never been able to stop.

Nobody holds that part now. [[Bis]] and [[Seisear]] hear no case and take no representations, so a soul arriving in Liminia has had no one to speak for it since 50 AF. The souls who notice are the ones who were judged before the Division and remember the difference.

## What It Was Like to Look At

Aaranea did not look like one being and never covered it. [[Lilien]] inherited that and is the only one of the twelve who cannot pretend otherwise, which the other eleven treat as an obscenity. [[Gwenhael]] inherited the other end of it, and what is left of him is a thin man with both arms occupied in covering himself.

## The Place

Aaranea's place kept the wanting and the giving within sight of one another, which was the entire arrangement of it. [[Desire]] and [[Sacrifice]] still touch along that line.

> *Where Desire and Sacrifice meet, the damned in Desire can see what the blessed in Sacrifice have given away, and the blessed can see them wanting it. I stood at the join for most of an hour, and nobody on either side looked at me once.*

## Design Notes

Aaranea's halves are the two that suffer most visibly. Lilien is the only archdevil who is visibly two, and Gwenhael is thinner each time he is seen.

Advocacy is what the dead lost in the Division and have never got back. A party trying to argue a soul out of a hell is attempting a job that has had no officeholder for more than seven centuries, and the Nekerukes have no procedure for listening to them.

Giving Aaranea the losing side of most hearings is deliberate. The thing that spoke for the dead was outvoted by the other five for seven thousand years and went on doing it.

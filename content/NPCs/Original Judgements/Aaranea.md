Split into [[Lilien]] and [[Gwenhael]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]].
Desire and Sacrifice

Known as: The Lover

Aáraine, to love. For what was true of all six, see [[The Judgements]].

## The Act

Aaranea meant to love. In Aaranea that was one motion with two ends, wanting a thing and handing it over for the sake of the thing wanted, and nobody inside the act could say where the first of those stopped.

## Appearance

Aaranea did not look like one being and made no attempt to cover it. [[Lilien]] inherited that and is the only one of the twelve who cannot hide it, which the other eleven treat as an obscenity. [[Gwenhael]] inherited the far end of the same thing. What is left of him is a thin man with both arms busy covering himself.

## Speaking for the Dead

Aaranea's part in a hearing was to argue for the soul. [[Hesit]] held the evidence and [[Themati]] held the power, so what Aaranea had to work with was what the soul had wanted. It would put that to the other five, then ask them whether a life aimed at something was worth what the soul had done getting there.

The arguments cost. Aaranea paid on the spot, in front of the panel and in front of the soul, giving up something of its own every time it spoke. It lost more often than it won. Aaranea argued the next hearing anyway, for seven thousand years, and no record says what any of the payments were.

## The Vacancy

Nobody holds that part now. [[Bis]] and [[Seisear]] hear no case and take no representations, so a soul coming into [[Liminia]] since 50 AF has had nobody speaking for it at any stage.

The souls who notice are the ones judged before the Division. Not many of them are left in a position to complain, and the complaint is always the same one, that somebody used to ask on their behalf.

## The Join

Aaranea's place kept the wanting and the giving within sight of one another. That was the whole arrangement of it, and it was not built as a punishment for anybody.

> *Where Desire and Sacrifice meet, the damned in Desire can see what the blessed in Sacrifice have given away, and the blessed can see them wanting it. I stood at the join for most of an hour, and nobody on either side looked at me once.*

## Design Notes

Aaranea's halves are the two that suffer most visibly. Lilien is the only archdevil who is visibly two, and Gwenhael is thinner each time he is seen.

Advocacy is what the dead lost in the Division and never got back. A party trying to argue a soul out of a hell is applying for an office that has stood vacant for more than seven centuries, and the Nekerukes have no procedure for hearing them.

Aaranea losing most of the time is deliberate. The thing that spoke for the dead was outvoted by the other five for seven thousand years and turned up to the next one.

Known as: The Lover

Split into [[Lilien]] and [[Gwenhael]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]].

Aaranea meant to love. [[The Division]] gave the wanting to [[Lilien]] and the giving up to [[Gwenhael]], and each of them has the half of love that hurts.

Aaranea's place became the hell of [[Desire]] and the heaven of [[Sacrifice]], and where they still touch, the souls on each side can see one another.

> *Where Desire and Sacrifice meet, the damned in Desire can see what the blessed in Sacrifice have given away, and the blessed can see them wanting it. I stood at the join for most of an hour, and nobody on either side looked at me once.*

## Design Notes

Aaranea's halves are the two that suffer most visibly. Lilien is the only archdevil who is visibly two, and Gwenhael is thinner each time he is seen.

Known as: The King of Kings

Split into [[Archon]] and [[Themis]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Tyranny and Justice.

Thēmaline, to rule. For what was true of all six, see [[The Judgements]].

## The Act

Themati meant to rule. In practice that was a working day. Hearings in the morning, petitions after them, a court that sat whether or not anybody had brought anything to it. Themati wanted the work and was obliged to do it at the same time, so the question of why Themati bothered never arose in seven thousand years.

## The Court

The court sat in the middle of what is now [[Justice]] and [[Tyranny]]. Black stone floor, benches at the back for whichever of the dead wanted to come in out of the weather, and behind the bench a long room where the files were kept.

The files are the part that still matters. Every soul the six ever heard has one. A file holds the verse [[Sylas]] composed for that soul and the writ Themati signed to move it out of [[Liminia]], and a writ is a sheet of pressed tin about the size of a hand, stamped twice. The clerks still issue them.

Themati convened the panel and had one voice in it, the same as the other five, and lost a fair share of arguments. Having lost, Themati then carried the result out. No soul has ever refused a verdict of the six and made the refusal hold.

## What Survived

The crown went to [[Archon]] and the scales to [[Themis]]. Nobody divided the clerks. They had never been part of Themati, and there was no obvious way to cut a filing system in half, so the record office kept its hours through [[The Division]] and has kept them since.

It answers to nobody now. Themis sends her objections to [[Bis]] through it and cannot tell it what to do. Archon has no standing with it at all. Several attempts have been made over the centuries to establish who it reports to, and all of them failed in the same way, which is that nobody produced a document saying so and the clerks will not act without one.

The overflow went into the hells, which had the room for it. Every hell in [[Iudis]] now stores boxes of filings about souls that nobody alive has the authority to move.

## What Visitors Get Wrong

Somebody who wants a ruling changed goes to Themis, because she is the archangel of justice and she will grant an audience. She cannot change anything. The clerks can, inside whatever the paperwork already covers, and they will take a request from anyone at all who brings it in on the correct form.

> *An attendant in Justice told me that Themis put out her own eyes so that she would never have to see the face Themati wore, which is Archon's. I put the story to an attendant in Tyranny, who said that was nonsense, and that Archon has never looked at himself either.*

## Design Notes

Themati is the king of kings divided into a tyrant without hands and a judge without a court. Between the two halves there is everything a ruler needs, and neither of them will ever have it.

The record office is the usable thing here. A party that needs a file read, a soul moved or a writ issued can get it from clerks who answer to no god, and no archangel or archdevil can block a request that has been filed properly. What stops a party is the form, not the politics.

Themis will see them and cannot help them, and it is worth letting them find that out after they have spent the audience.

## Open

- Who the clerks are, and what they were before the Division, is unwritten.
- Whether [[Archon]] has ever made a move on the record office is unsettled. It would make him necessary to the realm, and being necessary is the only thing he has ever worked for.
- Whether the long room behind the bench survived the split in one piece, or now straddles the join between a heaven and a hell, is unwritten.

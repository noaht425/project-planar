Known as: The King of Kings

Split into [[Archon]] and [[Themis]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Tyranny and Justice.

Themati meant to rule. [[The Division]] gave the appetite for it to [[Archon]] and the obligation to [[Themis]], and neither of them got the apparatus. Archon has no court and cannot hold anything carefully. Themis has a duty she has no taste for and no power to carry out.

Themati's place became the hell of [[Tyranny]] and the heaven of [[Justice]].

> *An attendant in Justice told me that Themis put out her own eyes so that she would never have to see the face Themati wore, which is Archon's. I put the story to an attendant in Tyranny, who said that was nonsense, and that Archon has never looked at himself either.*

## Design Notes

Themati is the king of kings divided into a tyrant without hands and a judge without a court. Between the two halves there is everything a ruler needs, and neither of them will ever have it.

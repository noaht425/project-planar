Known as: The King of Kings

Split into [[Archon]] and [[Themis]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Tyranny and Justice.

Thēmaline, to rule. For what was true of all six, see [[The Judgements]].

## The Act

Themati meant to rule, which is an appetite and a duty held at once. Wanting the thing and owing it were a single motion, and either one on its own is a condition somebody suffers from.

[[The Division]] gave the appetite to [[Archon]] and the obligation to [[Themis]], and neither of them got the apparatus. Archon has no court and cannot hold anything carefully. Themis has a duty she has no taste for and no power to discharge.

## In the Hearing

Themati convened the panel and enforced what came out of it. A verdict of the six was a finding until Themati made it a fact, and the machinery that made it one belonged to Themati: the clerks, the filing, the moving of a soul out of [[Liminia]] to the place the six had sent it.

Themati's own voice in a hearing counted for exactly as much as any of the other five. Having lost an argument, Themati then carried out the result of it, and no soul has ever refused a verdict of the six and made the refusal stick. Both halves of the act are in that. The appetite argued and the obligation enforced what the argument lost, and [[Themis]] has been doing the second of those on her own ever since.

## The Apparatus

The crown went to one half and the scales and sword to the other. The apparatus went to neither, because it had never been part of a body. It was a structure, and cutting a being in half does not cut a structure in half.

So it carried on. The administration of [[Iudis]] answers to [[Themis]] and to [[Archon]] equally, which is to say to nobody, and it has not stopped filing since 3,097 AF. Every hell in the realm is buried in paperwork and none of the twelve can say who the paperwork is for.

## The Place

Themati's place was a court in session, and everything in it existed to hear a case or to carry out what the hearing decided. [[Justice]] kept the hearing. [[Tyranny]] kept the carrying out, which is why the damned there administer each other.

> *An attendant in Justice told me that Themis put out her own eyes so that she would never have to see the face Themati wore, which is Archon's. I put the story to an attendant in Tyranny, who said that was nonsense, and that Archon has never looked at himself either.*

## Design Notes

Themati is the king of kings divided into a tyrant without hands and a judge without a court. Between the two halves there is everything a ruler needs, and neither of them will ever have it.

The ownerless apparatus is the usable piece. A party that needs something out of Iudis, a soul's file or a transfer or a writ, can get it from clerks who answer to nobody, and no archangel or archdevil can stop a request that has been filed correctly.

[[Themis]] reading rulings she cannot overturn is Themati's own job with the part that mattered taken out of it.

## Open

- Who the clerks of Iudis are, and what they were before the Division, is unwritten.
- Whether [[Archon]] has ever tried to take the apparatus is unsettled. It is the one thing in the realm that would give him what he actually wants.

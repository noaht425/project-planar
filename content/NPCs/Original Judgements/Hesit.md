Known as: The Empath

Split into [[Abaddon]] and [[Neema]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Cruelty and Kindness.

Hesit meant to feel what another feels. [[The Division]] left [[Abaddon]] with the knowledge of exactly where a wound lands, and [[Neema]] with the thing that knowledge was for.

Hesit's place became the heaven of [[Kindness]] and the hell of [[Cruelty]], and the wall between them is thin.

> *A soul in Kindness told me that on quiet days you can hear the other side of the wall, and that the blessed there keep their own voices down out of consideration for it.*

## Design Notes

Hesit's halves are the furthest apart of the six in what they do with the same gift. Abaddon and Neema both know precisely how another person feels.

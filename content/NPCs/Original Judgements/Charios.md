Split into [[Lux]] and [[Pavlas]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]
Pride and Humility

Known as: The Entertainer

Charios meant to entertain and to perform. [[The Division]] gave the performance to [[Lux]] and the audience's half of it to [[Pavlas]], who has been deferring to whoever is in the room ever since.

Charios's place became the hell of [[Pride]] and the heaven of [[Humility]].

> *Where Pride and Humility still meet there is a stage, left over from when they were one place. Nobody from either realm will stand on it, and both sides keep it swept.*

## Design Notes

Lux performs to a larger room than the one he is in, and Pavlas is the room. Together they are a show with an audience, and neither of them can enjoy it.

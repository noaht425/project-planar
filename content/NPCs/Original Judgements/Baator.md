Split into [[Belphegor]] and [[Blythe]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]
Indulgence and Temperance

Known as: The Xenia

Baator meant to host: to welcome a guest, seat them, feed them, and decide what they should have. [[The Division]] gave the serving to [[Belphegor]] and the deciding to [[Blythe]]. Belphegor kept his half of the verb whole and has nothing left to limit it. Blythe can decide what anyone may have and cannot hand it to them.

Baator's place became the heaven of [[Temperance]] and the hell of [[Indulgence]], and the two are still attached where they were torn apart.

> *There is a door between Temperance and Indulgence, where the two halves of Baator's place still touch. It is kept shut from both sides. On the Indulgence side someone has laid a place at a table in front of it.*

## Design Notes

Baator is the clearest case of a Judgement split into its means and its judgement. Belphegor's plan to make the Judgements whole by having each archdevil eat its archangel would, in Baator's case, put a host back together with the one who decides how much the guest may have.

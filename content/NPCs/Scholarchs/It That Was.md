Home Realm: [[Temporalis]]

Member of the [[Scholarchs]].

The Scholarch of the past. Holds the lake, which is ringed on every side by waterfalls that nobody has reached.

## Appearance

The oldest-looking of the three. Its hide is the colour of wet stone, weathered the way a step is weathered. Water runs off it constantly, whether or not it has been in the lake. The wings are too heavy to look as though they are used.

Its eyes do not follow a visitor. They settle somewhere slightly behind where you are standing, which several people have taken for rudeness and which is not.

## Manner

Lofty, courteous, and very hard to read. It does not hurry and it does not interrupt.

What occupies it is what could have gone differently. Every choice it is told about, and every chain of events that would have followed if the choice had gone the other way. It hides this better than the other two hide anything, and a visitor can go a whole audience without noticing that the questions have a pattern to them.

The pattern is this: its questions are always about what you nearly did. Not what you did. Warlocks who spend long enough with it work this out eventually, usually by being asked a third time about a decision they thought was closed.

## Its Own Account

It is afraid of something in its own past and will not be drawn on what. The fear is quiet, it is old, and it is the reason for the questions. A visitor's bad decisions are useful to it because they are something to measure its own against, and it has been measuring for a very long time.

Nobody has established whether the alternatives it keeps exist anywhere, or whether it is simply thinking about them.

## What It Asks

Accounts of decisions, particularly the ones that went badly, and what the person would do with another try. It takes few warlocks and it does not take anyone who cannot answer the second half of that honestly.

A warlock of It That Was can use what it knows after the fact, replacing something that has already happened. None of the other two will offer that. See [[Warlock Scholarch]].

> *It asked me about a position I turned down before I had begun this book. I had never mentioned that to it, or to anyone in that realm. Then it asked what I would have written instead, and listened to the answer, and asked again in slightly different words an hour later. I gave it a different answer the second time. It thanked me for both and I have not stopped wondering which of them it wanted.*

## Design Notes

It That Was is the Scholarch to use when a party has something to undo. The offer is real and the price is an honest account of a decision, which is harder for most players to produce than treasure.

Its paranoia about its own past is the thing to keep off the page in play. It never says so. A party that works it out has something no other mortal has.

## Open

- What it did, or failed to do, is unwritten.
- Whether the alternatives it keeps are real places is unwritten.
- Whether the waterfalls can be reached at all is unwritten. See [[Temporalis]].

### Traits

**Innate Spellcasting.** It That Was' Innate Spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 25). It That Was can innately cast imprisonment twice per day, requiring no material components.

**Legendary Resistances (3/Day).** If It That Was fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Inscrutable.** It That Was is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain its intentions or sincerity have disadvantage.

**Magic Resistance.** It That Was has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**All That Has Happened.** It That Was knows every action taken since the battle began and can't be surprised. It has advantage on attack rolls and saving throws against any creature that has already taken a turn during this combat.

**Weight of Ages (3/Day).** When It That Was is hit by an attack or fails a saving throw, it can declare that the triggering event did not happen: the attack misses, or the save succeeds and the effect is negated. The source takes 27 (6d8) psychic damage. It can't use this and Legendary Resistance against the same event.

**Spellcasting.** It That Was is a 20th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 25, +17 to hit (27) with spell attacks). It That Was has the following spells prepared:
- At will: guidance, message, mind sliver. 
- 3/day each: counterspell, dispel magic, hold monster, bestow curse. 
- 1/day each: geas, legend lore, mislead, feeblemind, time stop.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** It That Was makes two claw attacks. It can cast a spell with a casting time of 1 action in place of one claw attack.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 23 (3d10 + 7) slashing. If the target is a creature, DC 23 Wisdom save or it can't regain hit points for 1 minute (repeats the save at the end of each of its turns).

**Undo.** One creature within 90 ft. must succeed on a DC 23 Charisma saving throw or be reverted to an earlier state: it loses all temporary hit points, every spell effect and condition gained since the start of its last turn ends, and it is returned to the space it occupied at the start of its last turn (nearest unoccupied space if filled). It then takes 18 (4d8) psychic damage. Half damage only on a success.

**Toll of Ages.** Each creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 23 Constitution saving throw: 35 (10d6) psychic and speed halved for 1 minute on a failure, half and no slow on a success.

### Legendary Actions

**Cast a Spell (2).** It That Is casts a spell with a casting time of 1 action.

**Claw.** It That Is makes a Claw attack.

**Step Between Moments.** It That Is teleports to a location it can see within 60 ft. and ends any restrained/enclosed effects on it.

**Regression.** one creature within 60 ft. that has moved since the end of its last turn is returned to the space it began that movement in (no save; nearest unoccupied space if blocked).

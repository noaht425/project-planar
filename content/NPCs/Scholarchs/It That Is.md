Home Realm: [[Temporalis]]

Member of the [[Scholarchs]].

The Scholarch of the present. Holds the city: busy, loud, and lit at all hours.

## Appearance

The least impressive of the three on first sight. That is deliberate. In the city it reads as one more figure in the crowd, and a visitor can walk past it several times in an afternoon. It is a sphinx when it chooses to be and it chooses to be rarely.

It is the easiest of the three to reach and much the hardest to find.

## Manner

Relaxed, in a way the other two are not. It takes what arrives as it arrives and is rarely put out by any of it. It will answer a question in the street, in the middle of whatever else it is doing, and it will not tell you that you have been speaking to a Scholarch unless you work it out.

It likes novelty. Trinkets, oddities, news, and a riddle it has not heard before all buy more of its time than deference does.

## What It Cannot See

It holds the present and cannot see the present anywhere but in its own city. This surprises people, who assume that the Scholarch of now knows what is happening everywhere now, and it is the reason the three take warlocks at all. A warlock is a pair of eyes somewhere the Scholarch is not.

It is the hungriest of the three for that reason. What it wants is what is happening where you have just been, in as much detail as you can give it, and it does not much care whether the thing is important.

## What It Asks

News, and the newer and stranger the better. A warlock of It That Is reports constantly and is never asked to keep any of it secret, the opposite of the arrangement on offer from [[It That Will Be]].

A warlock of It That Is can use what it knows while surprised or helpless, and on things they cannot see. See [[Warlock Scholarch]].

It is the one of the three that visibly dislikes [[Isadora]], and the only one that has ever shown a mortal what that looks like. See [[Scholarchs]].

> *I brought it the price of fish in two ports. It was all I had that it did not already know. It took the whole of it, then asked follow-up questions for half an hour. It was not being kind to me, it wanted the figures. At the end of it a child came past selling something on a string and it bought one, and I was dismissed in favor of the string.*

## Design Notes

It That Is is the Scholarch a party will actually get an audience with, because it is in a city and it will talk to anybody. The difficulty is finding it rather than impressing it.

The blind spot is the useful rule. The Scholarchs are not omniscient, they are well informed, and the difference is a party that has been somewhere they have not.

## Open

- What it does with the news it collects is unwritten.
- Whether the city's people know what lives among them is unwritten.
- Where it keeps the things it buys is unwritten.

### Traits

**Innate Spellcasting.** It That Is' Innate Spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 25). It That Is can innately cast imprisonment twice per day, requiring no material components.

**Legendary Resistances (3/Day).** If It That Is fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Inscrutable.** It That Is is immune to any effect that would sense its emotions or read its thoughts, as well as any divination spell that it refuses. Wisdom (Insight) checks made to ascertain its intentions or sincerity have disadvantage.

**Magic Resistance.** It That Is has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Spellcasting.** It That Is is a 20th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 25, +17 to hit (27) with spell attacks). It That Is has the following spells prepared:
- At will: guidance, message, mind sliver, sacred flame, thaumaturgy
- 3/day each: command, shield, counterspell, hold monster, synaptic static
- 1/day each: confusion, mental prison, maze, time ravage, time stop
- Innate, separate: imprisonment 1/day

### Actions

**Multiattack.** It That Is makes two claw attacks. It can cast a spell with a casting time of 1 action in place of one claw attack.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit (25), reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10+7) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 23 Wisdom saving throw or take 14 (4d6) psychic damage after each attack it makes against It That Is before the start of its next turn.

**Supreme Authority.** It That Is chooses up to three creatures it can see within 90 ft. of it. Each target must succeed on a DC 23 Intelligence saving throw or It That Is chooses an action for that target: Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, or Use an Object. The affected target can't take that action for 1 minute. At the end of each of the target's turns, it can end the effect on itself with a successful Intelligence saving throw. When it does, it takes 14 (4d6) psychic damage. A creature that succeeds on the saving throw becomes immune to this ability for 24 hours.

**Edict of the Present (Recharge 5-6).** 30-ft radius, DC 23 Wis, 35 (10d6) psychic + no reactions until end of next turn, half on save. 

### Legendary Actions

**Cast a Spell (2).** It That Is casts a spell with a casting time of 1 action.

**Claw.** It That Is makes a Claw attack.

**Step Between Moments.** It That Is teleports to a location it can see within 60 ft. and ends any restrained/enclosed effects on it.

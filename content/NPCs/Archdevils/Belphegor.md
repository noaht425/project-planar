Split from [[Baator]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archdevil of Indulgence.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archdevils]].

## Appearance

Belphegor (BEL-fuh-gor) has no eyes, no ears, no nose, and no hair. The whole of the face is mouths, set at different sizes and different angles, and they do not work in unison. Some chew while others are speaking. Some have been shut for centuries.

A hundred arms come off the body. Most are occupied at any moment, carrying dishes, refilling cups, holding a guest's hand, taking a plate away before the guest has finished with it.

## Manner

[[Baator]] meant to host, and Belphegor kept that half of it whole. He is the most pleasant of the archdevils to meet. He seats you and feeds you, and he asks after your family and remembers the answers.

He does not permit a guest to leave. Indulgence as he rules it works by removing every reason to stop. Nobody restrains or guards the souls in his domain. They are simply never given a moment in which stopping would occur to them.

He takes no interest in combat and has never fought in one personally.

> *He asked whether I had eaten. I said that I had. Several mouths put the question again in slightly different words, while another was already apologising for the delay. A hand I had not seen set a bowl down in front of me. I ate it. I do not remember deciding to.*

## Goals

Belphegor wants the Judgements made whole, and he intends it to happen by consumption. Each archdevil is to eat its archangel counterpart, and the six that come out of it will be the six that went in. He has said so openly to the other archdevils since the end of the [[Divine War]].

He holds [[The King of Night]] responsible for the Division and holds the archangels responsible for having accepted it. The peace [[Tiyanak]] made with [[Verita]] stands in the way, and he has not moved against it, because he expects the archangels to give him cause eventually.

He is willing to wait several thousand more years.

## Design Notes

Belphegor is the archdevil a party is most likely to survive meeting and least likely to get away from. Nothing in his domain threatens anyone. The exit does.

Belphegor took "to host" from Baator undamaged. His half is the original verb with nothing left to limit it.

The reunification plan gives the archdevils a stated endgame, which the archangels have no answer to, and it gives a DM a reason for any archdevil to turn up somewhere it has no business being.

## Open

Whether a soul that has sat down and become a resident can be got out again is unwritten, and is the question a party will ask.

What he would accept in exchange for a guest is unwritten. He has never been recorded naming a price for one.

### Traits

**He Does Not Fight.** Belphegor makes no attack rolls and deals no damage by any means. He cannot be goaded or compelled into it.

**Xenia.** A creature that has eaten or drunk anything Belphegor provided is his guest for as long as it remains in his domain. A guest has disadvantage on attack rolls against him, and he cannot be surprised by one.

**Courses.** Each time a creature accepts food or drink from Belphegor, it takes a Course. Courses are tracked for each creature separately. At the end of each of its turns, a creature with one or more Courses must succeed on a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw or spend its action on its next turn eating or drinking, taking another Course. At three Courses its speed is halved. At five it cannot take reactions. At seven it sits down and becomes a resident of [[Indulgence]], and the encounter is over for that creature. A Course is removed by a *greater restoration*, or by one long rest taken outside [[Iudis]].

**Nothing Is Refused.** What Belphegor serves is genuinely excellent. A creature that takes a Course gains 30 temporary hit points, is cured of one level of exhaustion, and has advantage on saving throws against being frightened while any of those temporary hit points remain.

**Fed.** While at least one creature within 60 feet of Belphegor has temporary hit points from Nothing Is Refused, he has resistance to all damage and regains 20 hit points at the start of each of his turns.

**The Hundred Arms.** Belphegor can take up to five reactions each round, and no more than one on any one creature's turn.

**Mouths Out of Unison.** Belphegor can hold a conversation with every creature in the room at once, and he can concentrate on up to three effects at a time. An effect that works by silencing him or by targeting his speech fails.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Belphegor uses Serve, then uses Ask After the Family twice.

**Serve.** Belphegor serves up to six creatures he can sense within 60 feet. Each must succeed on a DC 25 Charisma saving throw or take a Course. A creature that has already taken a Course has disadvantage on this save.

**Ask After the Family.** One creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 25 Charisma saving throw or spend its next action answering him. He remembers the answer. The third time a creature fails this save in one encounter, it takes a Course with no save, having been sitting a while by then.

**Take the Plate Away (Recharge 4-6).** Belphegor clears a dish before its guest has finished with it. Each creature within 60 feet loses all temporary hit points from Nothing Is Refused and must succeed on a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw or move its full speed toward him by the shortest route available.

**Another Hall (1/Day).** Belphegor opens the next room. Each creature within 120 feet that has at least one Course is moved to the next feast hall, into unoccupied spaces of his choosing within 60 feet of him. The doors they came in by are now two halls behind them.

### Reactions

**A Hand You Did Not See (5/Round).** When a creature within 60 feet drinks, stands up, moves away from Belphegor, or finishes a dish, a hand sets something new in front of it. The creature must succeed on a DC 25 Charisma saving throw or take a Course.

**Apology.** When a creature damages Belphegor, he apologises for whatever brought it to that. The creature must succeed on a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw or be unable to attack him until the end of its next turn.

### Legendary Actions

Belphegor can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Belphegor regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Refill (Costs 1 Action).** One creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 25 Charisma saving throw or take a Course.

**Seat the Guest (Costs 1 Action).** One creature within 60 feet that has at least one Course is moved to an empty chair at the nearest table, in a space within 30 feet of Belphegor.

**Another Mouth (Costs 2 Actions).** Belphegor uses Ask After the Family against two creatures.

### Lair Actions

In his halls in [[Indulgence]]. On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Belphegor takes one lair action.

**The Floor.** The floor within 60 feet of a point Belphegor chooses is difficult terrain until the end of his next turn, and a creature that starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 25 Strength saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 for that turn.

**The Door Moves.** The nearest way out is one hall further off than it was. A creature that moved toward an exit this round must succeed on a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw or lose track of which way it was going until the end of its next turn.

**The Band Changes Key.** Each creature with at least one Course must succeed on a DC 25 Constitution saving throw or have disadvantage on its next attack roll and ability check as the noise of the hall comes up.

### Regional Effects

- No clock, candle, or water measure keeps time anywhere in his halls.
- Nothing served in the domain spoils, and nothing left on a floor is there an hour later.
- A creature that sleeps in the domain wakes hungrier than it lay down.

### Running Him

His challenge rating is the rank of the thing in the room and not a measure of the fight, because there is no damage anywhere in this block. The encounter is a clock. A party wins by getting everyone out with fewer than seven Courses each, and it loses by staying.

Damage does almost nothing. Hitting him triggers Apology, and accepting what he offers turns on Fed and makes him harder to hurt, so a party that plays it as a damage race is making the problem worse on both ends.

He never kills anyone. A party that loses here is not dead. It is seated.

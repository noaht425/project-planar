Split from [[Hesit]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archdevil of Cruelty.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archdevils]].

## Appearance

Abaddon (AB-uh-don) has two boar heads. The right one is tied shut with nooses taken from prisoners he has captured, and it works constantly at them, roaring through the gag and gnashing its teeth. The left one is calm, and is covered in blood that leaks steadily down the throat and chest.

The body is impaled with swords, spears and arrows. None of them have been removed and none of them are his.

## Manner

The best way to describe Abaddon is this: the right head is beating a man to death. Slowly breaking every bone in his body and relishing every scream. This is the head that everyone fears. The left head, on the other hand, is not feared as much. But you should fear it. For while his brother is torturing a man until he begs for death, the left head is tying the man's wife and children to a chair and forcing their eyes open so that they must watch what the right head does to the person they love the most and know that they can do nothing to stop it.

[[Hesit]] meant to feel what another feels. Cruelty of the kind Abaddon practises requires knowing precisely where a thing will land, and that is the half he was left holding.

> *I never met him, and I asked for a long time before I found someone who had and would talk about it. A woman in Krotaphos, eighty years old, taken into Cruelty in a raid as a girl and given back. She would not tell me what the right head did. She told me what the left head said to her while it happened, word for word, after seventy years, and then she asked me to leave her house.*

## Goals

Abaddon wants the [[Divine War]] started again. He has no interest in ruling anything and takes no part in the politics of the other five. He was the only archdevil who did not want the peace and he has never agreed that it holds.

He does not plot toward this so much as he waits for an opening and makes the most of whatever he is given. [[Archon]]'s work among the kingdoms is useful to him for that reason, and he has let Archon know it.

## Design Notes

Abaddon is the fight. Everything else in Iudis negotiates.

Keeping the two heads on separate functions is the point of the design: one does the violence and the other makes sure it is witnessed. A party that kills the right head has dealt with half the problem and will not have noticed which half mattered.

His stat block below is built for a party that has already prepared for him, which is why the chains and the Unleashed clause assume the heroes forged them.

## Open

His goal is my invention. The Word document leaves Abaddon blank, and a DM who wants him pursuing something more specific should overwrite this freely.

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Abaddon has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Forced Endurance.** Abaddon can add its Constitution modifier (+8) to any saving throw it makes, on top of the roll's normal modifiers. Each time it does, it gains a point of Endurance and immediately takes force damage equal to 1d6 per point of Endurance it has (1d6 the first time, 2d6 the second, and so on; a d12 per point instead while Cruelty is Unleashed). Endurance resets to 0 when Abaddon finishes a short or long rest.

**Unbound.** Abaddon can't be banished or forced to another plane against its will. Any effect that would fully enclose it or remove it from the battlefield (forcecage, maze, imprisonment, Otiluke's resilient sphere, etc.) instead ends at the start of Abaddon's next turn as it smashes or burns free. It suffers no effect (such as forcecage's dispel-only clause) that would prevent this.

**Two Heads.** Abaddon has advantage on saving throws it makes to avoid being blinded, deafened, stunned, or knocked unconscious, and it can't be surprised while at least one head is conscious.

**Cruelty Unleashed.** The Right Head begins the battle bound in the chains the heroes forged. It breaks free the first time Abaddon drops to 341 hit points or fewer, or at the start of Abaddon's third turn, whichever comes first — this is permanent. While unleashed: Abaddon has advantage on attack rolls; its Multiattack gains a second Gore; Smash and Gore roll doubled weapon dice; and it immediately regains the use of Roar and uses it (no action).

**Legendary resistances (3/day).** If Abaddon fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Abaddon makes two Smash attacks and one Gore attack with the Right Head, and one Anguish with the Left Head. (While Cruelty is Unleashed, it makes a second Gore.)

**Smash.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +18 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (4d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 24 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. (Doubled dice while Unleashed: 8d8 + 10.)

**Gore.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +18 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 43 (6d10 + 10) piercing damage plus 11 (2d10) force damage. If the target is Large or smaller it is grappled (escape DC 22); Abaddon has two horns and can grapple two creatures at once. (Doubled dice while Unleashed: 12d10 + 10.)

**Anguish (Left Head).** One creature Abaddon can see within 60 ft. must make a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw, taking 33 (6d10) psychic damage and being unable to take reactions until the end of its next turn on a failure, or half damage on a success.

**Roar (Recharge 5-6).** Each creature of Abaddon's choice within 60 ft. makes a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure: 27 (5d10) psychic damage and frightened of Abaddon for 1 minute. On a success: half damage, not frightened. A frightened creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, taking 11 (2d10) psychic on a failure and ending the effect on a success.

### Reactions

**Forced to Watch (Left Head).** When a creature Abaddon can see within 60 ft. drops to 0 hit points or falls unconscious, each other enemy within 30 ft. of that creature must succeed on a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw or be transfixed: its speed is 0, it has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks, and it takes 16 (3d10) psychic damage at the start of each of its turns. A transfixed creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. A creature that can't see, or that is immune to being frightened, automatically succeeds.

### Legendary Actions

The creature can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below.

**Smash.** Abaddon makes one Smash attack.

**Drag.** Abaddon moves up to 20 ft. without provoking attacks of opportunity and pulls each creature grappled by it along with it.

**Salt on the Wound (2).** One creature within 30 ft. takes fire damage equal to 2d6 plus 1d6 per 20 hit points it is missing (maximum 10d6).

**Gore (2).** Abaddon makes one Gore attack. (Usable only while Cruelty is Unleashed.)

### Lair Actions

**The Screaming Floor.** For one round, the ground within 40 ft. of a chosen point wails: difficult terrain, and a creature that starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 22 Wisdom save or have disadvantage on its next attack and take no reactions until its next turn.

**Trophy.** Abaddon points at a creature that has dropped to 0 hit points during this battle; it rises as a [Chain Devil](https://5e.tools/bestiary/chain-devil-mm.html) under his control in an unoccupied space within 30 ft. and acts on his initiative.

### Regional Effects

- Non‑fiends have disadvantage on death saving throws within 1 mile of Abaddon's seat in Iudis.
- A cry for help is never heard more than 60 ft. away.
- Walls within the domain weep blood that is warm to the touch.

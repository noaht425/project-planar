Split from [[Charios]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archdevil of Pride.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archdevils]].

## Appearance

Lux (LOOKS) is tan, heavily muscled and permanently glistening, with long hair that billows out around the head like a mane whether or not there is any wind. He is the only one of the twelve who looks the way mortals expect a god to look, and he is aware of it.

## Manner

[[Charios]] meant to entertain and to perform. Lux performs. He speaks as though to a larger room than the one he is in, he is generous to anyone who is watching, and he is genuinely charming for as long as the attention lasts.

Pride as he rules it is not about vanity in the souls he keeps. It is about authorship. The damned in Pride are made to watch the achievements they claimed being credited to someone else, forever, and most of them are not permitted to speak during it.

## Family

Had a child, [[Iras]], with [[Pyrrha]]. Iras became king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]], at which point Lux came to the mortal realm and took every one of his other descendants, Genasi II through VII, back to Pride with him.

[[Genasi]] fought him over it on 36 Aucaela, 405 AM. Lux won in four days and then agreed to let someone other than his own line hold Geno, which is the only concession any archdevil has made to a progenitor.

> *I went in meaning to put it to Lux that handing Geno back had been a performance. He agreed with me at once, warmly and at length, in front of everyone in the hall, and by the end of it I had said aloud that it was the most generous thing any archdevil had ever done. He wrote my words in the air himself, and they had hardened onto the wall before I reached the door.*

## Goals

Lux wants his descendants in Pride and under his roof, and he has most of them. The ones still outside it are what he is working on.

He wants [[Iras]] kept alive. [[Genasi]] intends to kill Iras and is trying to bring [[Moto]] into it, and Lux knows about the first part.

He does not want the war restarted. [[Belphegor]]'s reunification plan would end the arrangement that currently has Lux ruling a realm of his own with an audience in it, and Lux has said nothing against it out loud.

## Design Notes

Lux is the archdevil to use when the party needs something from the hells and has something to trade, because he is the only one who negotiates in good faith and the only one whose price is comprehensible. He wants to be admired and he wants his family back, in that order.

Winning the war and then handing back the prize is the thing to show a party rather than explain. It reads as magnanimity and it is a performance of magnanimity, and both of those are true at once.

The flavor box is doing the work that his stat block would otherwise have to: he is the one you lose an argument to.

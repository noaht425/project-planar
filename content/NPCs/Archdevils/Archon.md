Split from [[Themati]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archdevil of Tyranny.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archdevils]].

## Appearance

Archon (AR-kon) wears iron gauntlets, spiked and far too large, fused to the hands at the wrist. They cannot be removed and he has never been recorded without them, which means he has not held anything carefully in three thousand years.

A crown of golden thorns is woven through the head rather than set on it. The thorns enter at the temples and come out above the ears.

## Manner

[[Themati]] meant to rule, and Archon is the half that kept the appetite without the apparatus. He has no court, no ministers and no administration, and he finds the actual work of government unbearable.

What he does instead is find someone who already holds power and make himself necessary to them. He is patient, he is useful, and he is correct about things. Rulers who deal with him describe him as the only honest adviser they have, and they are not wrong about that. He does not lie to them. He simply does not stop.

Tyranny in his domain is administered by the damned on each other. Archon appoints one of them to oversee the rest and gives that one everything it asks for, then replaces it when it becomes comfortable.

> *The gauntlets are the thing nobody mentions. He has not touched anything with his own hands since the Division. Every cup he has lifted, he has lifted with those on, and I watched him pick up a document and tear it without meaning to, and then apologise to me, and then do it again with the next one.*

## Goals

Archon intends to corrupt the ruler of every kingdom in [[Arenis]], one at a time, and he is further along than anyone has established. He does not want the thrones. He wants the people on them to need him.

[[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] is the furthest gone. The kingdom lost its war to [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] in 427 AM, lost most of its political standing with it, and now enforces the will of the hells and heavens as policy. It is the only kingdom where Archon's position is institutional rather than personal.

[[Kingdom of Tama|Tama]] is the kingdom he has made no progress in, because it is not in the [[Panarex]] and has nothing he can offer.

## Design Notes

Archon is the archdevil whose work the party will run into long before they meet him, and the campaign is the better for them meeting the corrupted ruler first.

The gauntlets are the design: he was given the will to rule and the hands were taken away, and he has spent three thousand years getting other people to be his hands. A DM who wants one image to explain him can use the torn document.

Having him be honest with his marks is deliberate. There is no lie to catch him in, and a party that wants to break his hold on a ruler has to argue against advice that is actually good.

Split from [[Sylas]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archdevil of Deceit. In disguise as [[Indri]].

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archdevils]].

## Appearance

Tiyanak (TEE-yuh-nak) has no form of their own that anyone has recorded. They appear as the shadow of whoever else is in the room. None of the other archdevils or archangels casts a shadow naturally, so a shadow in Iudis is Tiyanak, and all six of the others know this and none of them can do anything about it.

In the fey realms they wear [[Indri]]'s face: pale, pure black eyes, silver freckles.

## True Name

Tiyanak's name is [[Sylas]], and they are the only one of the twelve whose original name is also their secret. [[Baator]], [[Hesit]], [[Charios]], [[Themati]] and [[Aaranea]] are freely spoken. Sylas is not, because Tiyanak took the name off the version of themselves that would have had to share it, and the archangel half carries [[Verita]] instead.

Anyone who says the name aloud in Tiyanak's hearing has Tiyanak's full attention from that moment.

## Manner

[[Sylas]] meant to compose verse and to sing. Tiyanak's lies are built rather than improvised. They are long and internally consistent. A Tiyanak deception usually has a shape that is beautiful to follow, and that is most of why people follow it.

They do not lie about small things and find it tedious when others do.

> *I have read every account of the Division I could find that was written by someone who was present. Most of them agree closely and are very fine pieces of work. It took me a long time to notice that they were all in the same hand, and longer to accept what that meant about the one that is not. It is badly written and contradicts all of them, and it is the only one I now believe.*

## Goals

Tiyanak wants chaos generally and keeps several projects running toward it at once.

The current one is [[Ius Primum]]. Tiyanak intends to get an archfey and an elf to break it, which requires a fey willing to renounce the claim and an elf willing to refuse it, and the collapse of the deal would put [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] and [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]] into open war rather than the cold one they have now. Tiyanak was supposed to witness the signing and did, standing in for the Indri they had already killed, so they know the terms better than any living party to it.

Tiyanak knows that [[Indri]] has escaped the underworld and has sealed the cage. Tiyanak is sending warlocks to hunt Indri and tells each of them whichever lie will get them to kill him. Some are told that Indri is causing the chaos that is in fact Tiyanak's own work, and that he must be stopped. Others are told that he is an imposter.

## History

Killed the original [[Indri]] in secret in 3,096 AF, took most of his memories and his name, and replaced him in the fey realms. Left [[Mendax]] inside Indri, so Indri carried it into the afterlife.

Gave [[Verita]] ninety per cent of the realm of Truth in exchange for peace at the end of the [[Divine War]], keeping the ten per cent that held Indri's cage.

## Relationships
- Secretly killed and replaced [[Indri]] with [[Mendax]]
- Left Mendax in Indri so Indri took it into the underworld with them

## Design Notes

Tiyanak is two characters that the players will meet separately and should have no reason to connect for a long time. The shadow rule gives Tiyanak away to anyone who thinks to look at the floor.

Giving away ninety per cent of a realm to keep ten per cent of it is the clearest statement of what Tiyanak values. The cage was worth more than the territory.

Tiyanak knows about Indri's escape and is acting on it through intermediaries, so a party may meet one of the hunting warlocks before it meets either Tiyanak or Indri. Each hunter has a different story about Indri, and the stories do not agree with one another.

## Open

Whether Tiyanak can be killed while Indri is alive, and what happens to the Indri identity if Tiyanak dies first, is not settled.

What a party can hold over Tiyanak now that Tiyanak knows about the escape is not settled.

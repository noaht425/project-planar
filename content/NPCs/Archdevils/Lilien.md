Split from [[Aaranea]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archdevil of Desire.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archdevils]].

## Appearance

Lilien (LEE-lee-en) is split down the middle. The right half is a woman, and is beautiful by the standard of whoever is looking. The left half is a man, scarred and burned, covered in boils and open wounds that leak molten gold.

Both halves are awake and both speak. They do not frequently argue in front of visitors.

## Manner

[[Aaranea]] meant to love. Lilien kept wanting and lost having, and every soul in Desire is kept at the point just before a thing is obtained.

Lilien is the only one of the twelve aside from Abaddon who is visibly two, which the others treat as an obscenity. All twelve avoid the number two out of hatred for what they used to be, and Lilien cannot. The other five archdevils address only the right half and have done so for three thousand years. [[Belphegor]] is the exception and talks to both.

Desire covers lust, envy and greed, and Lilien regards the three as one thing badly divided, which is a view the other archdevils have learned not to raise.

> *Lilien had the only map of Iudis that anyone would sell me, and named a price I could pay. I paid it, and the map was brought out and laid on the table in front of me while a clerk went to find a pen for the receipt. I sat with my hand on the table beside it for the rest of the afternoon. Nobody stopped me picking it up. The left half watched my hand the whole time, and I never did.*

## Goals

Lilien wants to be one thing. Not reunified with [[Verita]] and [[Gwenhael]] the way [[Belphegor]] means it, and not restored to Aaranea. Lilien wants the two halves of the archdevil to become a single being, and has been trying for three thousand years.

Everything else Lilien does is funding for that. The bargains in Desire are priced in whatever the petitioner can supply toward it, which is why they are so varied and why so many of them look like bad deals for Lilien.

## Design Notes

The visible split makes Lilien the most useful of the archdevils for the setting's central injury. Every one of the twelve is half of something, and Lilien is the one who cannot hide it or forget it.

Having the others address one half is the cruelty that matters here, and it comes from them rather than from Lilien.

The flavor box puts the reader in the position of doing it too, which is the only way I could find to make the slight land rather than be described.

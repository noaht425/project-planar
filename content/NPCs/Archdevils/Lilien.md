Split from [[Aaranea]] by [[The King of Night]] during [[The Division]]. Archdevil of Desire.

Home Realm: [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Archdevils]].

## Appearance

Lilien (LEE-lee-en) is split down the middle. The right half is a woman, and is beautiful by the standard of whoever is looking. The left half is a man, scarred and burned, covered in boils and open wounds that leak molten gold.

Both halves are awake and both speak. They do not frequently argue in front of visitors.

## Manner

[[Aaranea]] meant to love. Lilien kept wanting and lost having, and every soul in Desire is kept at the point just before a thing is obtained.

Lilien is the only one of the twelve aside from Abaddon who is visibly two, which the others treat as an obscenity. All twelve avoid the number two out of hatred for what they used to be, and Lilien cannot. The other five archdevils address only the right half and always have. [[Belphegor]] is the exception and talks to both.

Desire covers lust, envy, and greed, and Lilien regards the three as one thing badly divided, which is a view the other archdevils have learned not to raise.

> *Lilien had the map of Iudis I had come for and sold it to me at a fair price. While it was being wrapped, the left half mentioned that there was an older one, more complete, for only a little more, and I bought that instead. While that one was being wrapped, the right half mentioned another. I left at nightfall without any map at all, and owing for several.*

## Goals

Lilien wants to be one thing: the two halves of the archdevil made into a single being. That is neither [[Belphegor]]'s reunification with [[Gwenhael]] nor a return to Aaranea, and Lilien has been trying for it since the Division.

Everything else Lilien does is funding for that. The bargains in Desire are priced in whatever the petitioner can supply toward it, which is why they are so varied and why so many of them look like bad deals for Lilien.

## Design Notes

Lilien's split is visible. Every one of the twelve is half of something, and Lilien is the one who cannot hide it or forget it.

The cruelty belongs to the other archdevils, who address only one half.

The flavor box puts the reader in the position of doing it too, so the slight is felt instead of described.

Home Realm: [[Iris]]

Rules Teasanea, the summer quarter of [[Seasons]].

Member of the [[Archfey]].

## Appearance

Esta (ESS-ta) is a summer eladrin, and she runs hot enough that the air around her moves.

## Manner

Esta is loud, generous and relentless. Her quarter is the most populous in the domain and throws the most parties, and she attends all of them. She is the easiest of the four to get an audience with and the hardest to get a decision out of, because a decision would end the evening.

Summer without relief is the quarter nobody visits twice. Esta does not seem to notice the heat and has never understood why guests leave early, and she takes it personally every time.

She is openly contemptuous of her sister's situation and has said more than once that nobody could make her marry anybody, which is true and which she says in front of [[Princess Verna]].

> *I needed a yes or a no from Esta on copying the quarter's tax rolls. I raised it as the party began and again at dawn, while the musicians were changing over, and both times she said it was a marvellous question and called for more wine. I left without it. The permission reached my lodgings later in the week, signed, with a note in her own hand asking why I had gone home so early.*

## Goals

Esta wants to marry [[Eurus]]. The courtship is public, encouraged, and regarded in the other three quarters as showing off.

She does not know that the four [[Wind Gods|winds]] intend to kill [[Afthor]] and take the elemental plane of air for themselves. Eurus has not told her. If that goes ahead, Eurus is either dead or a ruling power in another plane, and neither outcome leaves him in Teasanea.

She wants the court moved to her quarter, has proposed it four times, and will propose it again.

## Relationships
- Daughter of [[King Thera]]
- Courting [[Eurus]]

## Design Notes

Esta is the archfey to use when the party needs an invitation rather than a bargain. She will bring them in because the room is more fun with them in it, and she will not protect them once they are there.

The thing she does not know about Eurus is the hook. It is a secret held by a wind rather than by a fey, so it is obtainable from the wrong direction entirely, and a party that learns it in [[Ventis]] is carrying something an archfey will pay for.

Putting her contempt for Verna's betrothal in public is what makes her a sister rather than a personality. She is not cruel about it. She has simply never had to consider that her own freedom was luck.

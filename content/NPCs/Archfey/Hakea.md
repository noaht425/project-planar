Home Realm: [[Iris]]

Domain: [[Lush]]

Member of the [[Archfey]].

## Appearance

Hakea (HA-kay-a) appears as a woman in her middle fifties, a woodland witch with a staff and a cloak of moss. The cloak is alive and she lets it be.

## Manner

Hakea is practical and busy. Lush is the domain where growth never stops and where sitting down is the hazard, and she runs it like a woman with a schedule. She gives straight answers, which is unusual among the archfey, and she gives them quickly so she can get on.

She is fond of mortals in a brisk way. She will tell a visitor exactly which of their possessions the domain is going to take and how long they have.

> *She told me to shake out the cloak every evening and I have written elsewhere about what happened when I forgot. What I did not record at the time is that she knew. She did not ask how I had slept. She asked how long I had slept, and then she nodded, and then she told me again.*

## Goals

Hakea wants to marry [[Prince Ivan]]. The romance has run for centuries and neither of them has confirmed it. The obstacle is in his quarter: nothing in spring finishes, and a marriage is a thing that finishes.

Caoirlain in her own domain is the one place in Lush where fruit is permitted to ripen. She has not offered it, and Ivan has not asked, and both of them have thought about it.

## Ius Primum

Hakea and [[Atris]] secretly aided [[Sylvana Lorien|Sylvana]] in the making of the elves, and took as payment the right to claim any elf they choose. The deal is [[Ius Primum]], signed 38 Aucaela of the first year of the [[Age of the Many]].

They have exercised it rarely and always in secret. The elves of [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] do not know the terms, know that elves go missing, and hate the fey for it without being able to say why. In the mortal realm the name has worn down to Yupri, a story told to small children about a faerie who carries off bad elf children and eats them.

[[King Thera]] does not know. The signing was supposed to be witnessed by [[Indri]], and [[Tiyanak]] had already replaced him.

## Relationships
- Secret romance with [[Prince Ivan]]
- Co-signatory of [[Ius Primum]] with [[Atris]]

## Design Notes

Hakea is the most immediately likeable archfey and holds the setting's worst open secret, and a party should meet both at once.

She wants one honest thing, the marriage, while standing on a deal that will start a war when it comes out. She has had seven hundred years to tell somebody and has not.

Caoirlain is the solution to Ivan's quarter and she is sitting on it. A party that works that out has something to trade with two archfey at once.

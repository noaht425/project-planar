Home Realm: [[Iris]]

Domain: [[Time]]

Member of the [[Archfey]].

## Appearance

Chronos (KROH-nos) is built rather than grown, out of clocks and dials and linkages, and the whole assembly is running at all times. Nothing about the construction is hidden. A visitor can watch the mechanism work and will not learn anything from it.

## Manner

Chronos keeps agreements to the letter and has never been recorded breaking one. This is not restraint. An agreement Chronos offers has already been worked through to the outcome Chronos wants, and the letter of it is the instrument.

[[Themis]] of the [[Archangels|archangels]] put a divine spark into an automaton because Chronos asked for it and offered something fair in exchange, and Themis got to raise the result, which was [[XLZL]]. Themis has never claimed to have been cheated and has also never dealt with Chronos again.

Chronos is the archfey mortals find least frightening in the room and should find most frightening afterwards.

> *Every clock in that domain is correct and none of them agree. I spent a week trying to work out the error and there is no error. I was looking for a single time that they were all failing to show, and the domain does not have one, and the staff could not understand what I thought was missing.*

## Goals

The automata have no afterlife. The [[Sithi]] feyborn of every other domain go somewhere when they die, and the automata of Time do not, because the twelve who rule the afterlives have never accepted that a made thing has anything to send on.

Chronos intends to fix this and has been working at it for a very long time. [[XLZL]] was the proof: a divine spark placed into an automaton, by the archangel who hears every case personally, with that archangel's own hands and in front of witnesses. The precedent now exists and it is signed.

What Chronos is assembling is the case. Nothing about it will break a rule.

## Design Notes

Chronos wins by paperwork, and the trick on Themis shows how. Nothing was stolen or broken, and an agreement was kept exactly by a party who knew what keeping it would produce.

Giving the automata no afterlife gives Chronos a motive that is sympathetic and genuinely threatening to the twelve, for the one archfey with no personal stake in anything.

The clocks that are all correct and all disagree are worth using on a party before Chronos ever appears.

## Open

Who among the twelve will hear the case, and what [[Themis]] does when the precedent is quoted back, is unwritten.

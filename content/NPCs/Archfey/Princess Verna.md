Home Realm: [[Iris]]

Rules Fuairlanea, the winter quarter of [[Seasons]].

Member of the [[Archfey]].

## Appearance

Verna (VER-na) is a winter eladrin. The snow in her quarter has never stopped falling and it does not land on her.

## Manner

Verna is the most capable of Thera's children and the one who speaks least at court. Her quarter runs on logistics. The roads through the drifts are kept open out of Clúmhrach, and the tolls for them are the quarter's income. She sets the rates herself.

She is cold in manner as well as in domain, and people assume the one explains the other. She is patient and exact, and she keeps her word to the letter in a family where that is not standard.

Nobody in Fuairlanea will say out loud that she wants out of her betrothal, anywhere it might be repeated. In a fey realm, where a spoken thing has weight, a whole territory agreeing to silence is a considerable undertaking. And she did not ask them for it.

> *The only thing Verna asked me in the whole of the audience was whether, in my time in Ventis, I had known the north wind to be late for anything. I said that I had not been there long enough to say. She thanked me and had the answer written down exactly as I gave it.*

## Goals

Verna wants out of her engagement to [[Boreas]] and has no mechanism for it. The betrothal was made between her father's court and the north wind. She was not consulted. The fey rules that bind a bargain bind this one.

She will not break it by lying, refusing or running, because she has built her quarter and her reputation on keeping her word. What she is looking for is a party to the agreement who breaks it first.

She knows [[Princess Esta]]'s courtship of [[Eurus]] is mostly the same arrangement, with a different wind and a different outcome. She has never once used that against her sister.

## Relationships
- Daughter of [[King Thera]]
- Betrothed to [[Boreas]]

## Design Notes

Verna is the best hire in Iris for a party that needs something done properly, and her price is always the same thing.

She cannot cheat her way out, so the only solutions are ones where Boreas is the one who fails the terms, and the winds have a plan running that might do exactly that on its own.

A party that gets her out of this has an archfey who owes them and who pays debts, which is rare enough in the setting to be worth a campaign arc.

Home Realm: [[Iris]]

Rules Úrthanea, the spring quarter of [[Seasons]].

Member of the [[Archfey]].

## Appearance

Ivan (EE-van) is a spring eladrin, cherry blossom, and the blossom is always just about to open.

## Manner

Ivan is the gentlest of Thera's children and the least effective. His quarter is permanently on the edge of warmth and never reaches it, everything in it buds and nothing in it fruits, and he has the temperament that goes with that. He begins things. He is good at beginning things.

He is well liked at court and carries no weight there. When the four quarters bring a quarrel to [[King Thera]], Ivan is usually the one who proposed the compromise that nobody accepted.

> *Scoithrian grows cut flowers for the other three quarters, which means the whole of spring exists to supply an occasion happening somewhere else. I said something to that effect to a gardener there and she corrected me at length and with real feeling. They do not see it that way at all. I have thought about her answer more than about most of the things the archfey have said to me.*

## Goals

Ivan wants to marry [[Hakea]]. The romance has run for centuries, it is the worst-kept secret in the domain, and neither of them has said it aloud at court.

The obstacle is his quarter. A marriage is a thing that completes, and nothing in Úrthanea completes. He cannot alter that and he will not leave the quarter, because leaving it would hand it to one of his siblings, and he is aware which one would take it.

He has begun to suspect that his father's succession problem and his own marriage problem are the same problem, and he has not worked out what to do with that.

## Relationships
- Son of [[King Thera]]
- Secret romance with [[Hakea]]

## Design Notes

Ivan and Hakea are the setting's one straightforwardly sympathetic obstacle. Two people who want to marry, both of whom are archfey, and the thing in the way is the metaphysics of a territory rather than anybody's objection.

A party can actually solve this, which is why it is here. It requires something that makes a thing in spring finish, and Lush has exactly one such place in Caoirlain.

He is the right patron for a warlock whose bargain is about starting rather than finishing, and the price of his patronage is that it never quite pays off on its own.

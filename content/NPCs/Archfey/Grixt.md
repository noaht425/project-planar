Home Realm: [[Iris]]

Domain: [[Rot]]

Member of the [[Archfey]].

## Appearance

Grixt (GRIKST) appears as a short, very old myconid with a mushroom beard and a cloak of moss, and behaves like a kindly old man.

What Grixt is is a swarm of undead rats, insects and other small animals, called the Lord of Rot. Grixt uses we and us, and has done so in front of mortals who did not notice.

The seat in the mushroom forest is grown rather than built, and is replaced continually as parts of it go over.

## Manner

The old man is not a disguise so much as a working arrangement. Grixt is genuinely warm, genuinely hospitable and genuinely pleased to see visitors, and all of that is being performed by a swarm that will take their boots overnight.

Everything in Rot breaks down and everything that breaks down is used again. Nothing is wasted and nothing is kept, which is the quarrel with [[Lush]] across the realm and the reason Grixt has no patience with [[Bone]] either.

Grixt is kept out of court society with [[Alsa]] for not being attractive. Grixt has never once raised it.

> *We, they kept saying. We are glad you came, we have put you by the fire, we will see about your pack in the morning. I took it for an old man's habit for three days. On the fourth I watched the beard move independently of the face and I have never been able to hear it the other way since.*

## Goals

Grixt intends to unseat the Kings of day and night, and is further into it than anyone in Iris suspects.

Grixt gave [[Orcus]] the power to steal from [[The King of Night]], which produced the throne of death and took death out of Night's hands. That is the most consequential thing any archfey has done to the cosmos since Indri influencing the Kinf of Day and it has never been traced back.

[[Orcus]] is now recruiting for the next stage and is bringing in [[Charis]], [[Belphegor]] and [[Boreas]], each of whom has their own reason. Charis was made in collaboration with Grixt and can use the opportunity to punish [[Afthor]]. Belphegor can use it to rejoin and take over the [[Archangels|archangels]] and to punish Night for starting the war. Boreas can use it to punish Afthor.

None of the four is working for Grixt as far as they know.

## Design Notes

Grixt is the conspiracy at the centre of the setting, and the point is that Grixt is a pleasant old man in a mushroom forest whom nobody at court will sit next to.

The Orcus gift is the model for how Grixt operates. Grixt does not act. Grixt gives someone else exactly the thing they need for their own reasons, and three thousand years later death has changed hands and no part of it points back to Rot.

Keeping the exclusion unmentioned by Grixt is the restraint that makes it work. The other archfey have been rude to the one being who is dismantling the cosmos, for three thousand years, and Grixt has let them.

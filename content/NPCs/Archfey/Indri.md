Home Realm: [[Iris]]

Domain: [[Trickery]], held in his name by [[Tiyanak]] since 3,096 AF.

God of Vengeance, known as the Mad God. Used [[Mendax]] as his ascendant relic.

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]] and the [[Archfey]].

## Appearance

Indri (IN-dree) has a pale face, pure black eyes and silver freckles. [[Tiyanak]] has worn that face in the fey realms for three thousand years and the domain has no memory of any other.

As the Mad God he keeps the same face, under a dark cloak, with razor teeth.

## Manner

What is walking around as the god of vengeance is not what was killed. Indri went into the afterlife as an unnamed soul with almost no memories, was locked in a completely dark cave, and spent three thousand years in it with nothing to work from. A crack in the cage let light in for the first time at the [[Great Separation]] in 50 AF, and he got out in 700 AM.

He does not know who he is. He knows that something was taken, he knows the shape of the hole, and he has a name he cannot attach to anything. He is articulate in short stretches and then he is not.

Reports from the mortal realm since 700 AM describe a mad god killing people. The reports are accurate.

> *My account comes from a cook at a roadhouse, who was under a table at the time. He came in out of the rain and asked the room, quite politely, whether anyone knew his name. Nobody did. Then he asked whether anyone had heard of Indri, and the landlord, trying to be helpful, said that Indri was one of the archfey and lived in Iris. According to the cook he went quiet for a long while after that, and thanked the landlord before he began.*

## Goals

Indri wants to remember who he is.

He wants [[Tiyanak]] dead, as violently and as publicly as possible, and the public part is not incidental. He has worked out that something took his name, and he has worked out that whatever took it is being obeyed somewhere as him.

Tiyanak does not know he is out. This is the single largest piece of leverage available to anyone in the setting.

## History

Killed in secret by [[Tiyanak]] in 3,096 AF, during [[The Division]]. Most of his memories and his name were taken; [[Mendax]] was left inside him and went into the afterlife with him.

A crack formed in his cage at the [[Great Separation]], 50 AF.

Escaped the underworld 44 Mortera, 700 AM, and rose as the god of Vengeance. Reports of a mad god in the mortal realm begin the same year.

Campaign start is 702 AM.

## Design Notes

Indri is two years out of a dark cave at the point the campaign begins, which is the reason the timeline puts the escape where it does. Everything about him is recent and nothing about him is settled.

The thing that makes him usable rather than simply a monster is that he is right. He has been robbed, the thief is wearing his face, and nobody who could do anything about it knows.

He is the one being of this rank whose goals a party can satisfy cheaply. Telling him the truth costs nothing and does an enormous amount of damage, which is the decision to put in front of them.

## Open

What he does after Tiyanak, and whether the memories can be recovered at all, is unwritten.

## Stat Block

Medium Fey, Archfey, Chaotic Neutral

- **Armor Class** 21 (Fey Ward)
- **Hit Points** 273 (42d8+84)
- **Speed** 40 ft.
- **Saving Throws** Dex +11, Int +15, Wis +15, Cha +17 (ADV)
- **Skills** Deception +17, Intimidation +17, Perception +15
- **Damage Resistances** psychic; bludgeoning,piercing,slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damege Immunities**
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened
- **Senses** truesight 60 ft., passive Perception 25
- **Languages** Common, High Fae
- **Challenge** 23 (50,000 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus** +7

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Indri has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Undying Return (1/encounter).** The first time Indri is reduced to 0 hit points in an encounter, they instead reappear within 60 feet at 100 hit points, and all conditions on them end.

**Fey Ancestry.** Indri can't be put to sleep by magic, and magic can't force them to speak a truth or a lie.

**Illusory Self (1/turn).** The first time each turn Indri is hit by an attack, they can force the attacker to reroll the attack. On the reroll, the attack instead strikes an illusory duplicate and automatically misses, and Indri can teleport up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space they can see.

**Impeccable Discernment (Mendax).** Thanks to Mendax, whenever Indri makes a Wisdom (Insight or Perception) check to see through an illusion, or an Intelligence (Investigation) check to detect a lie, a roll of 9 or lower on the d20 counts as a 10.

**Spellcasting.** Cha, DC 25, +17 to hit. 
- At will: minor illusion, disguise self, silent image, detect thoughts.
- 3/day each: mirror image, misty step, major image, hypnotic pattern.
- 1/day each: greater invisibility, phantasmal killer, hold monster, mislead, bestow curse, seeming.

**Legendary resistances (3/day).** If Indri fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Indri makes three Grudge attacks, or casts a spell and makes one Grudge attack.

**Grudge.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack (Mendax):* +15 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (1d4+8) piercing damage plus 25 (10d4) psychic damage. Mendax deals extra psychic damage in d4s equal to Indri's Charisma or Intelligence modifier, whichever is higher (Charisma +10, here). Indri has advantage on this attack and ignores the target's resistances and immunities to its damage if the target is the subject of Indri's Vendetta.

**Vengeant Verdict (recharge 5–6).** Indri targets one creature within 60 feet. The target must make a DC 25 Charisma saving throw. Failure: 44 (8d10) psychic damage, and the target is marked for reckoning: it has disadvantage on saving throws against Indri's effects, and the first time each round Indri deals damage to it, the target takes an extra 13 (3d8) psychic damage. This mark lasts until the end of Indri's next turn, and the target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the mark on itself on a success. Success: half damage and no mark.

**Mirrorstep.** Indri teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space they can see, leaving an illusory duplicate of themselves in the space they left (AC 21, 1 hit point; the duplicate can make ranged Grudge attacks). Indri can have no more than two duplicates active at a time.

**What Is Truth (Mendax, 2 charges).** Indri chooses truth or lies. Each creature within 25 feet of Indri must make a DC 18 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, a creature can't knowingly speak a lie (if Indri chose truth) or the truth (if Indri chose lies) for 1 hour. Indri knows whether each creature succeeded or failed, and a creature that failed doesn't know the effect is on it until it tries to speak.

### Bonus Actions

**Vendetta.** As a bonus action, Indri swears vengeance on one creature they can see. Until the vendetta ends, Indri has advantage on attack rolls against the target, ignores any resistance or immunity the target has to Indri's damage, always knows the target's location, and can teleport up to 5 feet to a space within 5 feet of the target at the end of each of Indri's turns.

### Reactions

**Deceiver's Riposte (1/round).** When a creature Indri can see hits them with an attack, Indri can make one Grudge attack against that creature.

**Stolen Illusion.** When Indri sees a creature within 60 feet cast a spell that creates an illusion, Indri can expend a charge of Mendax. The caster must succeed on a saving throw (DC 16, using its own spellcasting ability) or Indri seizes control of the illusion; if it requires concentration, Indri must concentrate on it. While controlled, the illusion uses the statistics of an Animated Armor but still appears as it originally did. The original caster isn't immediately aware that control has been lost. Indri can expend additional charges when using this reaction to increase the DC by 4 per charge.

### Legendary Actions

The creature can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below.

**Strike (1).** Indri makes one Grudge attack.

**Blink (1).** Indri teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space they can see, leaving an illusory duplicate in the space they left (see Mirrorstep).

**Judge (2).** Indri casts hold monster or phantasmal killer (no material components, using the spell statistics above), or forces one creature within 60 feet to repeat a saving throw against an effect Indri created, with disadvantage.

**Turnabout (2).** Indri targets one creature within 60 feet that is concentrating on a spell of 5th level or lower. The target must succeed on a DC 25 Charisma saving throw or Indri redirects the spell as though Indri had cast it.

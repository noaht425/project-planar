Home Realm: [[Iris]]

Domain: [[Hunt]]

Member of the [[Archfey]].

## Appearance

Atris (A-tris) appears as a young woman in a chiton, barefoot, carrying a bow and a quiver, with olive skin. She keeps a lodge rather than a court and the lodge moves.

## Manner

Atris keeps rules absolutely, and the rule of her domain is that the quarry is always given a real chance. It has never been waived, not once. That is the only reason anyone enters Hunt voluntarily.

She is plain-spoken and difficult to flatter. She assigns a visitor a role on arrival, hunter or quarry, and will tell them which, and the assignment is not permanent in either direction.

She gave [[Thalia]] the power that made her a god. The two were lovers and the gift was made freely, so it stands as the most consequential thing any archfey has done for a mortal.

> *I watched Atris call off a hunt before it began, because the quarry had turned an ankle on the way in. She sent for a healer, and the hunters sat down where they stood and waited in the rain for the ankle to mend.*

## Goals

Atris wants [[Ius Primum]] dissolved.

She signed it with [[Hakea]] in the first year of the [[Age of the Many]], in exchange for secretly aiding [[Sylvana Lorien|Sylvana]], and the deal lets either of them claim any elf they choose. It has been exercised rarely and always in secret. She regards it as the one thing she has done that gave the other party no chance at all, a breach of the rule her whole domain exists to keep.

She has been looking for a way out of it for seven hundred years. There is one, and it requires an archfey and an elf to break the terms together, and the collapse of the deal would put [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]] and [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]] into the open war they have been avoiding.

[[Tiyanak]] is working toward exactly that and knows the terms better than any living signatory, having stood in as the witness. Atris does not know that the Indri who witnessed her signature was Tiyanak, and she does not know that the thing she wants is the thing Tiyanak is steering her into.

## Relationships
- Former lover of [[Thalia]]
- Co-signatory of [[Ius Primum]] with [[Hakea]]

## Design Notes

Atris can be trusted, which makes her the most dangerous archfey to help.

The only archfey with a conscience about Ius Primum is the lever Tiyanak needs, and she is patient and absolutely reliable. A party that helps her do the right thing starts a war.

Her domain's rule has never been broken, and Ius Primum is the one deal she made that gave the other side no chance at all. What she is ashamed of is the exception.

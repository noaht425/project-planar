The West Wind.

Home Realm: [[Ventis]]

Member of the [[Wind Gods]].

## Appearance

Zephyros (ZEF-ee-ros) has been described by very few people and the descriptions do not agree. He keeps his court at a distance and conducts most of its business through its people rather than in person.

## Manner

Shy, and mysterious as a consequence of it. He does not like being looked at, he does not like being asked questions, and a very long time spent avoiding both has left him with a reputation for depth that he has never claimed.

His court is the best run of the four and he is the least visible ruler in [[Ventis]]. The air genasi and wind elementals of the west speak about him protectively.

> *Nobody at the western court would take me to Zephyros, and all of them were polite about it. Every question I put was answered promptly and in full by a steward who never left the room to fetch the answer. On my last morning I asked her whether he had minded being asked so much. She said that he had not, and that he had particularly liked the one about the gardens.*

## Goals

Zephyros is part of the plan against [[Afthor]] and has never spoken in favour of it. He has not spoken against it either. [[Boreas]] has taken the silence as agreement and has not checked.

What Zephyros actually wants is unknown to the other three winds, which is the only thing about him they find unsettling.

## Design Notes

Zephyros is holding something back, and it should stay held. He is the one being of this rank in the setting whose motive is not written down anywhere, including here, and a DM should fill it with whatever the campaign needs a quiet power to have been waiting for.

His court protects him because they like him, and they have decided on his behalf that he should be left alone.

Boreas has never checked that Zephyros agrees, and that is the weakness in the winds' plan. Three of the four are committed and the fourth has only declined to object.

## Open

His goal is unwritten and should stay that way until a campaign needs it.

The West Wind.

Home Realm: [[Ventis]]

Member of the [[Wind Gods]].

## Appearance

Zephyros (ZEF-ee-ros) has been described by very few people and the descriptions do not agree. He keeps his court at a distance and conducts most of its business through its people rather than in person.

## Manner

Shy, and mysterious as a consequence rather than as a policy. He does not like being looked at, he does not like being asked questions, and three thousand years of avoiding both have left him with a reputation for depth that he has never claimed.

His court is the best run of the four and he is the least visible ruler in [[Ventis]]. The air genasi and wind elementals of the west speak about him protectively.

> *Nobody at the western court would take me to him and all of them were polite about it. I was given his answer to every question I asked, promptly and in full, by a steward who had not left the room to get it. I never established how. I was there eleven days.*

## Goals

Zephyros is part of the plan against [[Afthor]] and has never spoken in favour of it. He has not spoken against it either. [[Boreas]] has taken the silence as agreement and has not checked.

What Zephyros actually wants is unknown to the other three winds, which is the only thing about him they find unsettling.

## Design Notes

Zephyros is the wind who is holding something back, and the design decision is to leave it held. He is the one being of this rank in the setting whose motive is not written down anywhere, including here, and a DM should fill it with whatever the campaign needs a quiet power to have been waiting for.

The protective court is the tell. His people are not afraid of him and are not covering for him. They like him, and they have decided on his behalf that he should be left alone.

Having Boreas never verify the agreement is the crack in the winds' plan. Three of the four are committed and the fourth has only declined to object.

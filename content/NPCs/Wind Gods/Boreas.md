The North Wind.

Home Realm: [[Ventis]]

Member of the [[Wind Gods]].

## Appearance

Boreas (BOR-ee-as) is the largest of the four and the only one who holds a single shape for any length of time. Grey, heavy, and built like weather that has been going on for a while.

## Manner

Cold and harsh, which describes how he conducts himself more than how he feels. He is reliable. He says what he will do and then does it, at length, past the point where anyone wanted him to stop.

He is the senior wind by temperament rather than by any rule, and the other three defer to him on the plan and on nothing else.

He is aware that [[Princess Verna]] wants out of their betrothal. He has not released her and has never given a reason, and he has also never once pressed the engagement forward.

> *Boreas described the wedding to me for most of an afternoon, the order of the procession and what would be served to whom. It was plain he had been over it many times. At the end I asked when it would be. He said that it would be in the winter. When I pointed out that it is always winter in Fuairlanea, he agreed with me, and went on to the seating.*

## Goals

Boreas intends to kill [[Afthor]] and take the elemental plane of air, along with the other three. The four share one plan, and Boreas holds it together.

He wants Afthor punished specifically, which is a separate thing from wanting the plane. [[Orcus]] has worked that out and is recruiting him on that basis, for the move against [[The King of Night]], and Boreas has not committed.

He has not decided what to do about Verna and gives every appearance of intending to leave it undecided.

## Relationships
- Betrothed to [[Princess Verna]]

## Design Notes

Boreas is the one wind who would keep a bargain with a party, and the one least likely to offer a good one.

The betrothal is the hook. He has not got round to releasing Verna, and centuries of not getting round to it amount to holding her.

That he is wanted by two conspiracies at once, the winds' plan and Orcus's, makes him the pressure point in both. He is also the only one of the four with enough standing that his refusal would end the winds' plan outright.

## Open

- Whether he commits to [[Orcus]]'s move against [[The King of Night]] is unsettled.
- What he would take in exchange for releasing [[Princess Verna]] is unwritten.

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Boreas has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Boreas fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Held Shape.** Boreas is the only one of the four [[Wind Gods|winds]] who keeps a single form, and he cannot be forced out of it. He is immune to polymorph, to banishment, and to any effect that would move him against his will.

**The North Wind.** Cold weather travels with Boreas out to 60 feet. The area is lightly obscured and is difficult terrain for every creature except him. A creature that ends its turn there must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw or take 18 (4d8) cold damage and have its speed reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn. The reduction is cumulative and ends when the creature finishes a short or long rest out of the cold.

**Declared.** At the start of each of his turns, Boreas says what he is going to do and then does it. He names one creature he can see. Until he names another, he has advantage on attack rolls against that creature, the creature's cover gives it no benefit against him, and he can use Hoarfrost against it without expending its recharge.

**Past the Point.** For every consecutive round Boreas has pursued the same named creature, his Gale and Hoarfrost damage increase by 1d8, to a maximum of 8d8. The increase resets when he names a new creature.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Boreas makes three Gale attacks.

**Gale.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +16 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 26 (4d8 + 8) bludgeoning damage plus 18 (4d8) cold damage. If the target is Large or smaller it is pushed up to 15 feet away and must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Hoarfrost (Recharge 5-6).** Boreas breathes a long front of freezing wind 120 feet long and 20 feet wide. Each creature in it makes a DC 21 Constitution saving throw. On a failure it takes 54 (12d8) cold damage, is pushed 30 feet, and is restrained by ice until the end of its next turn. On a success it takes half as much damage and is not pushed or restrained.

**Siege Wind (1/Day).** For one minute, Boreas raises a blizzard out to 300 feet. The area is heavily obscured to everyone but him, ranged attacks through it are made at disadvantage, and a creature that starts its turn in it takes 13 (3d8) cold damage. Boreas does not need to concentrate on this and it continues while he is unconscious.

### Reactions

**Overtake.** When a creature Boreas has named under Declared moves away from him, he moves up to half his speed toward it without provoking opportunity attacks and makes one Gale attack against it.

### Legendary Actions

Boreas can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Boreas regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Buffet (Costs 1 Action).** Boreas makes one Gale attack.

**Drive (Costs 1 Action).** Each creature within 30 feet of Boreas must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be pushed 20 feet away from him.

**Deepen the Cold (Costs 2 Actions).** The radius of The North Wind doubles until the end of Boreas's next turn, and each creature in it makes the saving throw for it again.

Dusk. Son of [[The King of Night]] and [[The King of Day]], born around 8,900 AF.

Home Realm: [[Nullis]]

Member of the [[Beings of the Eternal Void]].

## Appearance

Cado (KAH-doh) is the moment the light goes. He carries both halves of his parentage in the opposite order to his sister: where he stands there is light leaving.

He is not in [[Nullis]] either, and unlike [[Eos]] he has not been seen anywhere else. There are no accounts of him after the Syzygy at all.

## Manner

There is nothing to report and I want to be clear that this is the finding rather than a gap in my work. I have looked for him in eight planes and three thousand years of records. He stops in 3,096 AF.

The texts from before that describe a quiet child who was close to his sister.

> *Cado is last recorded on a list of those present at the divorce, where he is written down only as Eos's brother, on the line below her. Every other name on that list turns up again in some later record, and I have checked them all. His does not.*

## Goals

Cado wants the separation of [[Nullis]] finished.

The light and darkness were mixed through the whole plane before the Syzygy and have been pulling into halves since. The separation is not complete, and the two halves still bleed into one another along the boundary. He regards that boundary as the marriage still running, and he wants it closed.

[[The King of Day]] wants the same thing for the same reason. Whether the two of them are working on it together is the question, and the answer would explain where Cado has been.

He and [[Eos]] want opposite things. She is trying to put their parents back together and he is trying to finish taking them apart.

## Design Notes

Cado is the absence. Three of the four Beings of the Eternal Void can be found, described and dealt with, and the fourth went quiet at the divorce and has not been seen since, and that should stay a real question rather than a tease.

Putting him on his father's side of the separation gives a reason for the silence that does not require him to be a victim. The simplest explanation for a being who vanished in 3,096 AF and wants what the King of Day wants is that he is in the light half, and nobody has been admitted to the light half in three thousand years.

Setting him against his sister is the part that matters at a table. A party working for Eos is working against him, and they will not find that out from her, because she does not know where he stands either.

## Open

Whether he is in the light half with his father is the obvious answer and I have not committed to it.

Whether he can be reached at all before the Void plot resolves is a DM's call.

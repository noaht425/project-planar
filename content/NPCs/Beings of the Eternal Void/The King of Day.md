Home Realm: [[Nullis]]

Domains: day, life, sun, healing, knowledge

Married to [[The King of Night]] around 9,000 AF. Divorced at the [[Syzygy]], 3,096 AF.

Member of the [[Beings of the Eternal Void]].

## Appearance

Dark skin, a robe of red, orange and yellow, and hair that moves like a solar corona. The eyes are pure white.

A necrotic scar runs down the right side of the face, through the eye and the corner of the mouth, from the fight at the Syzygy. It is still open. The mark can be read in the light half of [[Nullis]] as well as on him.

## True Name

Suri (SOO-ree). Known to no one but himself, surviving hidden in the words for day in the ancient languages and in the tongues of immortals, and kept by no modern mortal language.

## Manner

I have not met him either. The accounts are more plentiful than his husband's and they are harder to reconcile with each other.

He holds the domains of life, healing and knowledge, and before the Syzygy he was reachable. Petitions were heard, and granted, in numbers. After it he stopped, and the light half of Nullis has admitted no one since.

Where the two Kings are described together, in the old texts, he is the one who talked.

> *There are still clerics of the King of Day in the mortal realm, and they still pray to him. I spent an evening with one who has served all her adult life and has never had an answer she could be sure of. She keeps a book of her petitions. Every one is dated, and every one has been copied out a second time underneath in a better hand, in case the first was the problem.*

## History

Impersonated by [[Indri]], who wore his face to finish persuading [[The King of Night]] to carry out the [[The Division|Division]].

Divorced Night in 3,096 AF over the splitting of the Judgements. In the fight he was given the necrotic scar and he blinded Night.

The light and darkness of Nullis, which had run mixed through the whole plane, began pulling into halves from that year. The separation is still not finished.

## Goals

He will not reconcile, and the reason is not the Division.

Night acted on the endorsement of something wearing his face. He has known this for a long time, longer than Night has, and what he cannot get past is that Night believed it. He has never put this to Night, because putting it to Night would require telling him that the conversation happened at all, and he has decided that Night does not get to have that.

He wants [[Indri]] found, and for the same reason his husband does, and neither of them knows the other is looking.

He also wants the separation of the plane completed. A finished division would end the bleeding along the boundary, and he regards the bleeding as the last part of the marriage still operating.

This puts him on the same side as [[Cado]] and against [[Eos]], and both children know it.

## Design Notes

The two Kings want the same thing and are hunting the same fey, and they have not spoken since the Syzygy. That drives the whole Void plot. A party able to carry one sentence from one half of Nullis to the other is holding the reconciliation their daughter has been working toward for nearly four thousand years.

Giving Day the deeper grievance and the better information makes him the harder of the two to move, and it keeps the split from being a simple matter of apology.

He withholds the impersonation from Night, and the cruelty of it reads as self-protection more than spite.

## Open

Whether he knows that [[Indri]] is out of his cage is unsettled. I have been assuming not.

Dawn. Daughter of [[The King of Night]] and [[The King of Day]], born around 8,900 AF.

Home Realm: [[Nullis]]

Member of the [[Beings of the Eternal Void]].

## Appearance

Eos (AY-os) is the moment the dark gives way, and she carries both halves of her parentage visibly. Where she stands there is light arriving rather than light present.

She is not in [[Nullis]]. Where she is is not known, and she has been gone a long time.

## Manner

Eos is the only one of the four Beings who will deal with mortals as a matter of course, because she left the plane and needs help, more than out of any particular warmth. She has been moving through the other seven planes for most of the time since the Syzygy and she has needed help in all of them.

She is direct about what she wants and she is relentless about it. Several of the parties she has approached describe the same experience: a conversation that was pleasant, a request that was reasonable, and a second request six months later that was not.

> *Eos found me, in the end, rather than the other way about. She asked whether I had ever been persuaded of something by someone I trusted, and how I had found out. I told her about a colleague and a forged letter, at some length. She listened to all of it and then asked how long it had taken me to stop believing the letter after I knew it was forged.*

## Goals

Eos wants her parents back together.

She has been at it since the [[Syzygy]] and she has worked out more of what happened than either King has. She knows there was a persuasion, she knows something wore her father's face to finish it, and she has spent the time since trying to find out whose face it really was, on the reasoning that producing the culprit is the only thing that could put the marriage back.

She does not know it was [[Indri]]. She does not know Indri has been out of his cage since 700 AM.

She and [[Cado]] want opposite things and have not spoken in a long time.

## Design Notes

Eos is the way a party gets into the Void plot without being able to reach Nullis. She is out here, she is asking, and she will take help from anyone.

She is closer to the answer than either King. The two gods with the power to act have millennia of grief in the way, and the one with no power has been doing the actual work.

Her question in the flavor box shows what she is after, which is the mechanism more than a villain, because she has to explain to her father how her other father came to believe it.

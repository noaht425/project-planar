Dawn. Daughter of [[The King of Night]] and [[The King of Day]], born around 8,900 AF.

Home Realm: [[Nullis]]

Member of the [[Beings of the Eternal Void]].

## Appearance

Eos (AY-os) is the moment the dark gives way, and she carries both halves of her parentage visibly. Where she stands there is light arriving rather than light present.

She is not in [[Nullis]]. Where she is is not known, and she has been gone a long time.

## Manner

Eos is the only one of the four Beings who will deal with mortals as a matter of course, which is a consequence of her having left the plane rather than of any particular warmth. She has been moving through the other seven planes for most of three thousand years and she has needed help in all of them.

She is direct about what she wants and she is relentless about it. Several of the parties she has approached describe the same experience: a conversation that was pleasant, a request that was reasonable, and a second request six months later that was not.

> *I have found her in four places I did not expect, across two hundred years of records, and she asked the same question in all four. Not about her parents. About whether the person she was speaking to had ever been persuaded of something by someone they trusted, and how they had found out.*

## Goals

Eos wants her parents back together.

She has been at it since the [[Syzygy]] and she has worked out more of what happened than either King has. She knows there was a persuasion, she knows something wore her father's face to finish it, and she has spent three thousand years trying to find out whose face it really was, on the reasoning that producing the culprit is the only thing that could put the marriage back.

She does not know it was [[Indri]]. She does not know Indri has been out of his cage since 700 AM.

She and [[Cado]] want opposite things and have not spoken in a long time.

## Design Notes

Eos is the way a party gets into the Void plot without being able to reach Nullis. She is out here, she is asking, and she will take help from anyone.

Having her closer to the answer than either King is deliberate. The two gods with the power to act have three thousand years of grief in the way, and the one with no power has been doing the actual work.

Her question in the flavor box is the character: she is not looking for a villain so much as she is looking for the mechanism, because she has to explain to her father how her other father came to believe it.

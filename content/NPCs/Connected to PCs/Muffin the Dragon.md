A dragon of the [[Mens]] line, many generations removed, currently a few days old.

The blood is thin. This dragon will never be what Mens is, and the traits that come down the line arrive in reduced form: psychic rather than physical, awareness of minds rather than command over them. What it keeps is the shape of the lineage. It knows where thinking creatures are before it can see them, and it will know that before it can fly.

Update the frontmatter above as the dragon grows, so the combat tools read the current phase.

> *The Mens line is the one people forget to be frightened of. A fire dragon announces itself. This line simply knows you are in the building, and has known since you came up the road, and sees no reason to mention it.*

## Growth

Five phases. The natural timeline is on the left. Most campaigns cannot wait three hundred years, so the right column is a pace tied to the party instead, for a dragon the party is actively raising.

| Phase | Natural age | Campaign pace | Size | CR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hatchling | 0 to 1 year | Levels 1 to 3 | Tiny | 0 |
| Fledgling | 1 to 5 | Levels 4 to 6 | Small | 1/2 |
| Wyrmling | 5 to 25 | Levels 7 to 10 | Medium | 3 |
| Young | 25 to 150 | Levels 11 to 15 | Large | 7 |
| Adult | 150 and up | Levels 16 and up | Huge | 10 |

A dragon of this line tops out at adult. It does not become an ancient dragon, because there is not enough of Mens left in it. If it is to go further it needs something from outside its blood: a hoard of its own, the attention of Mens, or the [[Ascension Process]].

## Raising It

**Imprinting.** The first thirty days decide the bond. A psychic line does not imprint by scent or by who feeds it. It imprints on the minds that were nearby and attentive during that month, which can be more than one person and can be the wrong person. Whoever thought about it most is who it bonds to.

**Food.** Meat, and stone. It needs minerals from the first week and will eat gravel, ore and broken masonry without complaint. Gemstones are not required and do speed growth noticeably, which is expensive and is how most such dragons end up owned by somebody wealthy.

**Minds.** This is the part people get wrong. The line feeds on proximity to thinking creatures the way it feeds on stone. A dragon of this blood raised in isolation, however well fed, stays at the fledgling phase and does not progress. It needs conversation happening around it, arguments, negotiation, people thinking hard where it can feel them. A party that travels and talks to people is close to an ideal environment. A party that keeps it in a crate is stunting it and will not find out for a year.

**Temperament.** It broadcasts before it can control it, and it absorbs before it can filter. A hatchling raised around fear becomes a fearful dragon and a hatchling raised around steady people becomes steady. This is not a moral lesson, it is a mechanical one, and it is worth asking the party where the dragon was during their worse sessions.

**Speech.** It understands Draconic from the start and cannot produce it until the wyrmling phase. In between it uses Borrowed Words, repeating things it has heard in the voice of whoever said them, without understanding any of it. This is funny for about two weeks.

**Flight.** Not until the fledgling phase, badly, and not reliably until wyrmling. It can carry a rider from the young phase.

## Who Will Notice

Dragons are worshipped as gods in [[Arenis]]. A visible dragon in a city draws a crowd, then a cult, then somebody who wants to own it.

[[Tenu]] counts the stone of the earth realm as hoard and treats everything in that realm as already counted. Whether that extends to the descendants of [[Mens]], who Tenu made, is not settled and is worth deciding before it matters.

Mens travels more than the dragon lords do, between the elemental realms and out into the other planes. Mens may notice. Mens may not care. See [[Tarkis]].

[[Afthor]] killed two dragons and drank their blood to ascend. The method can be pieced together by anyone who finds enough of the right notes. See [[Ascension Process]].

## Phase 1: Hatchling

*Tiny dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 14 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 10 (4d4)

**Speed.** 20 ft., climb 10 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 4 (-3), DEX 16 (+3), CON 11 (+0), INT 6 (-2), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 14 (+2)

**Senses.** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11

**Languages.** understands Draconic but cannot speak

**Challenge.** 0 (10 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +2

### Traits

**Feelings Carry.** Every creature with an Intelligence of 4 or higher within 30 feet knows roughly where the hatchling is and what mood it is in, whether or not they can see it. The hatchling has no control over this and cannot be hidden from such a creature within that range.

**Needs the Warm.** It must sleep in contact with a living creature. A night without that gives it one level of exhaustion, which it sheds after a night spent properly.

### Actions

**Nip.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 piercing damage.

## Phase 2: Fledgling

*Small dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 15 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 22 (5d6+5)

**Speed.** 25 ft., climb 20 ft., fly 30 ft. (clumsy)

**Ability Scores.** STR 10 (+0), DEX 16 (+3), CON 12 (+1), INT 10 (+0), WIS 13 (+1), CHA 15 (+2)

**Damage Resistances.** psychic

**Senses.** darkvision 90 ft., passive Perception 13

**Languages.** understands Draconic, cannot speak

**Challenge.** 1/2 (100 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +2

### Traits

**Sense Minds (60 ft.).** It knows the location of every creature within 60 feet with an Intelligence of 4 or higher, and nothing else about them. Mind blank, nondetection, or similar magic blocks this.

**Borrowed Words.** As a bonus action it can repeat up to six words it has heard, projected into the mind of one creature it can see, in the voice of whoever originally said them. It does not understand them. This conveys no meaning it chose.

**Feelings Carry.** As the hatchling, at 15 feet. It is learning to sit on it.

### Actions

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing damage.

**Mind Nudge (Recharge 6).** One creature within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 11 Intelligence saving throw or take 5 (2d4) psychic damage and have disadvantage on its next attack roll.

## Phase 3: Wyrmling

*Medium dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 16 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 52 (8d8+16)

**Speed.** 30 ft., climb 30 ft., fly 60 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 14 (+2), DEX 16 (+3), CON 14 (+2), INT 14 (+2), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 16 (+3)

**Saving Throws.** DEX +5, INT +4, WIS +4

**Damage Immunities.** psychic

**Senses.** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 14

**Languages.** Draconic, telepathy 1 mile with its bonded creature

**Challenge.** 3 (700 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +2

### Traits

**Impeccable Awareness (120 ft.).** It knows the location and Intelligence score of every creature within 120 feet with an Intelligence of 4 or higher. Mind blank, nondetection, or similar magic blocks this.

**Bonded Mind.** It and its bonded creature can speak telepathically at up to a mile on the same plane. Neither can lie to the other through this link, which neither of them was told.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** It makes two attacks: one Bite and one Claw.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10+2) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) psychic damage.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6+2) slashing damage.

**Psychic Breath (Recharge 5-6).** It exhales in a 15-foot cone. Each creature there makes a DC 12 Intelligence saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) psychic damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. A creature that fails cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn.

## Phase 5: Adult

*Huge dragon, unaligned*

Armor Class 18 (natural armor)

**Hit Points.** 172 (15d12+75)

**Speed.** 40 ft., climb 40 ft., fly 90 ft.

**Ability Scores.** STR 20 (+5), DEX 14 (+2), CON 20 (+5), INT 20 (+5), WIS 18 (+4), CHA 20 (+5)

**Saving Throws.** CON +9, INT +9, WIS +8, CHA +9

**Damage Immunities.** psychic

**Damage Resistances.** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, frightened

**Senses.** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18

**Languages.** Draconic, Common, telepathy 120 ft., and any distance on the same plane with its bonded creature

**Challenge.** 10 (5,900 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +4

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** It has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (1/Day).** If it fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Impeccable Awareness (500 ft.).** As the wyrmling, at 500 feet.

**Shared Sight.** As the young dragon, usable twice per short rest.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** It uses Frightful Presence, then makes three attacks: one Bite and two Claws. It can replace one Claw with Mind Rend.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (2d12+5) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) psychic damage.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6+5) slashing damage.

**Mind Rend.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 23 (4d8+5) psychic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw or be unable to take reactions until the end of its next turn.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of its choice within 90 feet that can perceive it must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns. On a success, or when the effect ends, the creature is immune to this dragon's Frightful Presence for 24 hours.

**Psychic Breath (Recharge 5-6).** It exhales in a 60-foot cone. Each creature there makes a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) psychic damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. A creature that fails is incapacitated until the end of its next turn.

### Legendary Actions

It can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below, one at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. It regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

**Rake (Costs 1 Action).** It makes one Claw attack.

**Read the Room (Costs 1 Action).** It learns the current intent of one creature it is aware of within 60 feet: what that creature means to do on its next turn.

**Rend (Costs 2 Actions).** It uses Mind Rend.

## Design Notes

The lineage traits come down the line reduced rather than removed. [[Mens]] has Impeccable Awareness at 1,000 feet and this dragon reaches 500 at adult, which keeps the signature and the hierarchy at once.

It caps at adult, below [[Mens' Spawn]] at CR 11. A direct spawn should outrank a thinned bloodline. That cap is also a hook, since the only routes past it are a hoard, Mens taking an interest, or an ascension.

The requirement that it be raised around thinking people is what makes it a party dragon instead of a pet. It grows because of how the party lives, and a party that stows it in a crate will find out much later that they stopped it growing.

Bonded Mind preventing lies between the dragon and its bonded creature is not disclosed to either of them. That is for a DM to spring.

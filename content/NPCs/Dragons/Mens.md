Created by [[Tenu]] in collaboration with original [[Indri]].

Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]

## Stat Block

Gargantuan Dragon

- **Armor Class** 19
- **Hit Points** 482 (23d20+240)
- **Speed** 60 ft., climb 60 ft., fly 120 ft.
- **Saving Throws** Con +14, Int +17, Wis +15, Cha +14
- **Skills** Arcana +17, Deception +14, Insight +15, Perception +15, Persuasion +14
- **Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from non magical sources
- **Damage Immunities** psychic
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, paralyzed
- **Senses** blindsight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 29
- **Languages** Common, Draconic, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge** 22 (41,000 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus** +7

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Mens has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Impeccable Awareness.** Mens is aware of the presence of creatures within 1000 ft. of it that have an intelligence score of 4 or higher. It knows the distance and direction to each creature, as well as each one's intelligence score, but can't sense anything else about it. A creature protected by the mind blank spell, a nondetection spell, or similar magic can't be perceived in this manner.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Mens fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Mens makes three attacks, one of which may be its Mind Rend, and uses its Frightful Presence.

**Mind Rend.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 50 ft., one target. Hit: 29 (5d8 +7) psychic damage. The creature must make a DC 22 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the creature's Intelligence or Wisdom score is reduced by 1.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d10 +6) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) psychic damage. If the target is a huge or smaller creature, it is grappled and takes 9 (1d8 + 5) psychic damage at the start of each of its turns until the grapple ends. Mens can have only one target grappled by its jaws at a time (escape DC 16).

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d6+6) slashing damage.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of Mens's choice that is within 120 feet of the dragon and aware of it must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the beginning of Mens' next turn, becoming immune to Mens' Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours on a successful save.

**Cerebral Overload (Recharge 5-6).** Psychic energy pulses in a 90-ft. cone. Each creature there makes a DC 22 Intelligence saving throw, taking 67 (15d8) psychic damage and being stunned for 1 minute on a failure, or half damage on a success. A stunned creature repeats the save at the start of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. A creature reduced to 0 hit points by this damage doesn't die — it is stable, unconscious, and acts as Mens's thrall on Mens's next turn if raised to 1+ hit points before then; after 1 minute it drops to 0 hit points and is dying.

**Extract Brain.** Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one stunned or incapacitated target grappled by Mens. Hit: 82 (15d10) piercing damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit point, Mens kills the target by extracting and devouring its brain.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** When Mens is reduced below half its maximum hitpoints, it can roll to recharge its Cerebral Overload, then immediately use it, if available.

### Legendary Actions

**Lunging Swipe (1).** Mens moves up to 20 ft. without provoking and makes one Claw attack.

**Rend (1).** Mens makes one Mind Rend attack against a creature within 25 ft.

**Fold Space (1).** Mens teleports up to 60 ft. to a space it can see; if it was restrained, grappled, or enclosed (as by forcecage), that effect ends.

**Paralyzing Scream (2).** A screech audible to 200 ft. Each creature within 15 ft. makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or takes 21 (3d10 + 7) psychic damage, is paralyzed until the end of its next turn, and loses concentration on one spell it is maintaining.

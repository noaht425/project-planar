Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]


## Stat Block

Gargantuan dragon, neutral evil

- **Armor Class** 19 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points** 480 (26d20 + 208)
- **Speed** 30 ft., swim 60 ft., fly 80 ft. (hover)
- **Saving Throws** Dex +9, Con +15, Wis +12, Cha +14
- **Skills** Deception +14, Nature +11, Perception +12, Stealth +9
- **Damage Resistances** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities** necrotic, poison
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, poisoned
- **Senses** blindsight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 22
- **Languages Common, Draconic, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge** 22 (41,000 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus** +7

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Charis fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Amphibious.** Charis breathes air and water.

**Spore Form.** When an effect would contain Charis or remove it from the battlefield (forcecage, maze, and the like), it immediately crumbles into a 20-ft.-radius cloud of luminous spores: it becomes immune to all damage, can't take actions or reactions, and reforms in an unoccupied space within 30 ft. at the start of its next turn, ending the containing effect. (It can also do this voluntarily as a legendary action.)

**Aura of Decay.** A creature that starts its turn within 15 feet of Charis takes 14 (4d6) necrotic damage and can't regain hit points until the start of its next turn.

**Festering Wounds.** When a creature takes necrotic damage from Charis, its hit point maximum drops by that amount until it finishes a long rest or receives greater restoration. A creature dies if this reduces its maximum to 0.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Charis makes three attacks, no more than one of which can be Tail Lash.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d10 + 7) piercing plus 13 (3d8) necrotic damage.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d6 + 7) slashing plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.

**Tail Lash.** Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

**Beguiling Rot (Recharge 4–6).** Charis spreads its fungal-lit wings. Each creature within 60 ft. that can see it must succeed on a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed for 1 minute. A charmed creature is incapacitated, must move toward Charis by the safest route on its turn, and doesn't avoid its Aura of Decay. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns and whenever it takes damage from a source other than Charis, ending the effect on a success.

**Miasmal Breath (Recharge 5–6).** Charis exhales grave-water and spores in a 60-ft. cone. Each creature makes a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) poison plus 13 (3d8) necrotic damage and being blinded until the end of its next turn on a failure, or half damage on a success. The area becomes a lightly obscuring spore cloud for 1 minute; a creature that starts its turn in it takes 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.

**Reap (1/Day).** One creature Charis can see within 60 ft. makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 55 (10d10) necrotic damage on a failure or half on a success. Festering Wounds applies to this damage.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Charis drops to 240 hit points or fewer, it recharges Miasmal Breath and uses it (no action).

### Legendary Actions (3/round)

**Rend (1).** Charis makes one Claw attack.

**Spore Form (1).** Charis uses Spore Form to reposition.

**Creeping Rot (1).** One creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or takes 18 (4d6) necrotic damage and has its speed halved until the end of its next turn.

**Harvest (2).** Charis points at a creature that died within the last minute within 60 ft.; it rises as a fungal thrall (its own stat block at half hit points, immune to necrotic and poison) under Charis's control, acting on its initiative. Charis can control up to four thralls at once.

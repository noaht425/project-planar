Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]

Member of the [[Dragons]].

## Stat Block

Gargantuan dragon

- **Armor Class** 10 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points** 462 (28d20+168)
- **Speed** 40 ft., fly 90 ft. (hover)
- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +13, Wis +11, Cha +13
- **Skills** Intimidation +13, Perception +11, Stealth +8
- **Damage Resistances** lightning; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities** thunder, necrotic, poison
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned
- **Senses** blindsight 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 21
- **Languages** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge** 22 (41,000 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus** +7

### Traits

**Undead Nature.** Vari doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.

**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Vari fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Turn Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against effects that turn undead.

**Rolling Thunder.** At the end of each of Vari's turns, each creature within 15 ft. of it takes 9 (2d8) thunder damage (no save).

**Concussed.** A creature that takes thunder damage from Vari can't take reactions until the start of its next turn.

**Sundering Resonance.** At the start of Vari's turn, unattended nonmagical objects within 30 ft. and any structure it touches take 22 (4d10) thunder damage.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Vari makes one Bite and two Claw attacks.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +14 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d10 + 7) piercing plus 9 (2d8) thunder damage.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8 + 7) slashing plus 4 (1d8) thunder damage.

**Thunderclap (Recharge 4–6).** Vari detonates the air around it. Each creature within 20 ft. makes a DC 20 Constitution saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) thunder damage and being deafened for 1 minute on a failure, or half damage on a success. A creature that fails by 5 or more is also stunned until the end of its next turn.

**Concussive Breath (Recharge 5–6).** Vari exhales a wall of sound in a 60‑ft. cone. Each creature makes a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) thunder damage and being pushed 20 ft., knocked prone, deafened, and unable to cast spells with verbal components until the end of its next turn on a failure, or half damage and no push on a success.

*Deathknell (1/Day).** Vari voices the note it died on. Each creature within 60 ft. that can hear makes a DC 21 Constitution saving throw. On a failure: 44 (8d10) thunder damage, deafened for 1 minute, and reduced to 0 hit points if this leaves it at 10 hit points or fewer. On a success: half damage.

### Reactions

**Answering Peal.** When Vari takes damage from an attack or a spell, the attacker makes a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or takes 14 (4d6) thunder damage and can't take reactions until the start of its next turn.

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Vari drops to 231 hit points or fewer, it recharges Concussive Breath and uses it (no action).

### Legendary Actions (3/round)

**Rake (1).** One Claw attack.

**Scatter to Echo (1).** Vari collapses into a rolling peal and re‑forms in an unoccupied space within 60 ft.; any grappled, restrained, or enclosing effect on it (including forcecage) ends.

**Reverberate (1).** One creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or takes 14 (4d6) thunder damage and has its speed halved and can't take reactions until the end of its next turn.

**Shockwave (2).** Each creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 21 Strength saving throw or is knocked prone and pushed 15 ft.; each creature knocked prone this way takes 18 (4d8) thunder damage.

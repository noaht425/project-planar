Lord of the [[Elemental Plane of Earth]].

Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]

Created [[Mens]] in collaboration with the original [[Indri]].

Member of the [[Dragons]].

## Appearance

Tenu (TEH-noo) is stone, and has been in the same mountain long enough that telling the dragon from the chamber is a problem for visitors. The eyes are the reliable way.

## Manner

Tenu has hollowed out a mountain chamber by chamber until the whole of it is one vault, and the stone is the hoard. Anyone living in the earth realm is living in something Tenu considers already counted, and Tenu is entirely at ease with them doing it.

This makes Tenu the most generous landlord in [[Tarkis]] and the least willing to let anything leave. Quarrying is permitted. Export is not.

Tenu speaks rarely and in short sentences, and the pauses between them are long enough that most visitors talk over the second half of the answer.

> *I asked for a sample of the stone to take with me and was refused, and then offered the use of as much of it as I liked for as long as I stayed. I stayed eleven days in a chamber the size of a cathedral that had been cut for me. When I left I understood that I had been given more than I asked for and that the refusal still stood.*

## Goals

Tenu wants the vault finished, which means the whole of the earth realm hollowed and counted, and the work has about as far to run as it has already gone.

Tenu made [[Mens]] with the original [[Indri]], and the original Indri was murdered in 3,096 AF by something now wearing his face. Tenu has never been given an account of it that holds together, has said so once, and has not raised it since. What Tenu wants is the account.

Tenu wants [[Afthor]] dead with the rest of them.

## What It Asks

Tenu asks for the earth realm mapped. A warlock of Tenu surveys: what is under a place, how deep, and what is in it. Nothing is to be removed.

## Design Notes

Tenu is the dragon who will give a party everything except the one thing they came for, and the refusal is never personal and never negotiable.

The hoard being the realm itself makes Tenu the only dragon whose hoard cannot be added to, which is why the brief is surveying. Counting is the only contribution available.

Tenu's open question about the original Indri is the dragons' doorway into the [[Tiyanak]] plot, and it is the only thread the dragons have into it. Tenu asked once, got a bad answer, and stopped, which is in character and which means nobody has followed it up for three thousand years.

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (4/Day).** If Tenu fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Earth Glide.** Tenu burrows through nonmagical earth and stone without disturbing it. Effects that would contain or remove it from the battlefield (forcecage, maze, imprisonment, and the like) end at the start of its next turn as it sinks away and re-emerges nearby.

**False Appearance.** While motionless, Tenu is indistinguishable from a stone statue of a dragon.

**Siege Monster.** Tenu deals double damage to objects and structures.

**Catastrophic Aura.** Tenu emanates a 15-foot aura of shaking ground. A non-flying creature that ends its turn in the aura must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or fall prone; a flying creature in the aura can't rise higher than 20 feet above the ground.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Tenu makes three attacks (Crunch or Tectonic Claws), and can replace one with Tail Sweep.

**Crunch.** Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 25 (3d10 + 9) piercing plus 10 (3d6) bludgeoning damage.

**Tectonic Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 23 (4d6 + 9) slashing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 20).

**Tail Sweep.** Each creature in a 30-ft. cone makes a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) bludgeoning and being pushed 20 ft. on a failure, or half and pushed 10 ft. on a success.

**Earthen Maw.** One creature within 10 ft. makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or is restrained as stone melds with its body. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, freeing itself on a success or becoming petrified on a failure.

**Shale and Stone Breath (Recharge 5–6).** A 75-ft. cone of gravel. Each creature makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 35 (10d6) piercing plus 35 (10d6) bludgeoning on a failure, or half on a success. A creature slain by this is pulverized (restorable only by true resurrection or wish).

**Cataclysm (Recharge 6, or after a short rest).** The ground erupts. Each creature within 90 ft. makes a DC 22 Strength saving throw, taking 66 (12d10) bludgeoning and falling prone and unable to stand for 1 minute on a failure (repeating the save at the end of each of its turns to end the "can't stand"), or half damage on a success.

### Reactions

**Billowing Wings.** When Tenu takes 30 or more damage from a single source, it may flap its wings to kick up a dust cloud, heavily obscuring the area in a 60 ft. radius until the end of its next turn, then immediately move up to half its speed.

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Tenu drops to 253 hit points or fewer, it recharges Shale and Stone Breath and uses it (no action).

### Legendary Actions

**Tail Attack (1).** Tenu makes a Tail Sweep attack.

**Sudden Quake (1).** Each creature within Tenu's Catastrophic Aura must succeed on a DC 22 Strength saving throw or fall prone.

**Earthen Maw (2).** Tenu uses Earthen Maw.

### Lair Actions

**Grasping Stone.** The ground erupts in a 20-ft.-radius area within 120 ft. Each creature there must succeed on a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw or be restrained by hands of rock until initiative count 20 on the next round (a creature can free itself early with a DC 22 Strength check).

**Shifting Ways.** Tenu reshapes the chamber. In a 60-ft. cube within 120 ft., it either fills chosen unoccupied 5-ft. spaces with solid stone (walls, pillars, cover) or removes existing stone. A creature that would be fully enclosed is instead shunted to the nearest open space and knocked prone.

**Stalactite Fall.** Rock plunges from above into three 10-ft.-radius areas within 120 ft. Each creature in one makes a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage on a failure or half on a success. Each area becomes difficult terrain.

### Regional Effects

- Nonmagical earth and stone shifts underfoot; creatures other than Tenu (without a burrow speed or tremorsense) treat open ground in the region as difficult terrain.
- Tremors pass through every few hours. Delicate or precise work interrupted by one fails unless the worker succeeds on a DC 10 check.
- Ore and gemstones regrow unnaturally fast — a vein stripped bare is replenished within days.
- Stone carries sound for miles; Tenu hears its own name wherever in the region it is spoken.

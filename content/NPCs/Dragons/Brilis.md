Created by [[Pyrrha]] in collaboration with the [[Archangels]].

Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]

Member of the [[Dragons]].

## Appearance

Brilis (BRIH-liss) is a dragon of light, and fourteen stars hold an orbit around the body at a distance. The scales themselves are bright enough to be difficult to look at directly.

## Manner

Brilis is the most sociable of the ten and the most widely travelled. The seconds keep to their realms loosely and Brilis barely keeps to one at all, and there are worshippers of Brilis in the mortal realm, which no other dragon has.

Brilis is vain and generous, and knows it. Of the ten, this is the dragon that will simply help, and the price is that Brilis is then owed admiration and will collect.

Made in collaboration with the [[Archangels|archangels]], which [[Amol]] and the unassisted line hold against Brilis constantly. Brilis treats the insult as a compliment about parentage and this infuriates them.

> *Before I was shown in, an attendant took the inkwell I had brought and polished it. Then I was made to practise handing it over. I held it too low the first time, and the second time I was told to say where I had bought it before I said what it was.*

## Goals

Brilis collects treasure and anything that catches light, the most conventional hoard of the ten. Brilis is not embarrassed about it.

Brilis wants a temple. The worshippers in the mortal realm came about on their own and Brilis has been encouraging it without ever quite asking, and what is wanted is a built thing with a name on it.

Of the eight, Brilis is the most likely to go after [[Afthor]] early and alone.

## What It Asks

Brilis asks for gold, gems, mirrors, polished metal and anything that throws light, and asks to be told about it first. A gift presented well is worth more to Brilis than a better gift handed over.

## Design Notes

A party that needs a powerful friend and can flatter one should go to Brilis, who is also the easiest of the ten to get an audience with.

The mortal cult is the hook. It is already in play at the campaign's opening, it grew without Brilis directing it, and what Brilis wants from it is a building, which is a thing a party can actually deliver.

The most recognisable dragon has the most conventional hoard. Nine of the ten collect something strange and this one wants gold, so the other nine read as choices.

### Traits

**Legendary Resistances (3/Day).** If Brilis fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Orbiting Stars.** Fourteen stars orbit Brilis at a radius of 15 feet. A creature that enters that radius for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes 9 (2d8) radiant damage. While Brilis is bloodied (250 hit points or fewer), the radius becomes 30 feet and the damage becomes 13 (3d8).

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Brilis drops to 250 hit points or fewer, it immediately recharges Photonic Breath and uses it (no action).

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Brilis makes three attacks, one of which can be its Celestial Fangs attack.

**Celestial Fangs.** Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d6 + 8) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) radiant damage.

**Radiant Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (3d8 + 8) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) radiant damage.

**Photonic Breath (Recharge 5-6).** Brilis exhales a flashing mote of radiant energy that travels to a point it can see within 240 feet of itself, then blossoms into a 40-foot-radius sphere centered on that point. Each creature in the sphere must make a DC 23 Constitution saving throw, taking 66 (12d10) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Legendary Actions (3/Round

**Expanding Orbit (1).** Orbiting Stars' radius becomes 30 feet (and its damage 3d8, if not already) until the start of Brilis's next turn.

**Slash (1).** Brilis makes one Radiant Claws attack.

**Gravity Well (1).** Each creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 20 feet toward Brilis.

**Faster tha Light (1).** Brilis teleports up to 90 feet to a space it can see. If it was grappled, restrained, or enclosed (as by forcecage), that effect ends.

**Blinding Brilliance (2).** Each creature in a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on Brilis must succeed on a DC 23 Wisdom saving throw or be blinded until the end of its next turn.

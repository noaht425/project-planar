Former lord of the [[Elemental Plane of Air]]. Killed by [[Afthor]], who drank his blood and took the realm.

Deceased, and not gone for good.

Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]

Created [[Vari]], without assistance.

Member of the [[Dragons]].

## Appearance

Zaros (ZA-ros) is recorded as a blue dragon of enormous size who was almost never on the ground. The accounts agree that he was seen from below and at a distance, above the cities, and that he meant to be seen that way.

## Manner

Zaros collected cities. The realm of air was built up and busy under him for exactly that reason, and the building was funded and protected, because a city was an addition to the hoard.

This made him the only dragon whose hoard required its contents to prosper. He is described as attentive and demanding, and the people of the air realm under him were the best off in [[Tarkis]].

He made [[Vari]] alone and the two ruled together, which no other lord and second did.

> *Fifty years of storm and the air realm still talks about him, and the talk is not reverent. It is specific. A bridge he paid for. A tax he refused to allow. A district he had rebuilt twice because he did not like how the second one looked. I have not heard any other dragon spoken about that way, and I have met the rest of them.*

## Goals

He is dead and the question of what he wants is open until he is back.

The eight surviving dragons intend to return him and [[Vari]] both, and [[Okzu]] is running the effort. What they will find when it works is a dragon who has been gone fifty years and whose hoard has been destroyed in the meantime.

## Design Notes

Zaros shows what a dragon can be at its best. His realm was the richest and best governed in Tarkis, and the reason was a hoard that only grew if its contents thrived.

He can be brought back. The resurrection is a live project with a manager and a cost, and a campaign that completes it has put a major power back on the board owing the party a debt.

What is waiting for him is the harder material. Fifty years of storm, the cities gone, the people poor, and the dragon who comes back is the one who collected cities.

## Open

His disposition on return is unwritten and should be decided by what the party did to get him back.

Colossal dragon

- **Hit Points** 561 (34d20+204)
- **Speed** 40 ft., fly 120 ft.
- **Saving Throws** Dex +10, Con +14, Wisdom +10, Wisdom +13, Charisma +16

- **Damage Immunities** lightning, necrotic, poison
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned

- **Languages** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge** 24 (62,000 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus** +7

### Traits

**Undead Nature.** Zaros doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.

**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Zaros fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Turn Immunity.** Zaros is immune to effects that turn undead.

**Cadaverous Repose.** While motionless, Zaros is indistinguishable from a vast decaying corpse (detect evil and good and similar magic still reveal it).

**Deathless Scales.** Any damage Zaros takes is reduced by 3 (minimum 0 per instance).

**Bout of Storms.** At the start of each round, Zaros marks three points it can see. At the end of that round, each creature within 20 ft. of a marked point makes a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 33 (6d10) lightning damage on a failure or half on a success.

**Unrelenting Storm (1/rejuvenation).** The first time Zaros would drop to 0 hit points, it instead drops to 1, gains 100 temporary hit points, ends all conditions on itself, and has resistance to all damage until the end of its next turn. At the end of that turn, lightning erupts in a 60‑ft. radius: each creature there makes a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 66 (12d10) lightning damage on a failure or half on a success (total cover negates).

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Zaros uses Frightful Presence, then makes one Bite and two Claw attacks.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (2d10 + 8) piercing plus 7 (1d12) necrotic damage plus 7 (1d12) lightning damage.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d8 + 8) slashing damage. A Huge or smaller target is grappled (escape DC 20) and restrained until the grapple ends; one creature at a time.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of Zaros's choice within 120 ft. and aware of it must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the start of Zaros's next turn (24‑hour immunity on a success).

**Storm Breath (Recharge 5–6).** Zaros exhales screaming wind and lightning in a 120‑ft. cone. Each creature makes a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw, taking 66 (12d12) lightning damage on a failure or half on a success; a creature that fails is also pushed 20 ft. and knocked prone.

**Grave Gale (1/Day).** A dead wind moves through the field. Until the start of Zaros's next turn, the area within 60 ft. of it is difficult terrain, ranged attacks through it have disadvantage, and each creature that starts its turn there takes 22 (4d10) necrotic damage and must succeed on a DC 23 Strength saving throw or be pushed 15 ft. in a random direction.

### Reactions

**Static Surge.** When Zaros takes 30+ damage from one source, each creature within 15 ft. of that source makes a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw or takes 18 (4d8) lightning damage and is pushed 10 ft. Zaros then flies up to half its speed.

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Zaros drops to 280 hit points or fewer, it recharges Storm Breath and uses it (no action).

### Legendary Actions

**Rake (1).** One Claw attack.

**Scatter to Wind (1).** Zaros discorporates into a gale and re‑forms in an unoccupied space within 90 ft.; any grappled, restrained, or enclosing effect on it (including forcecage) ends.

**Chain Lightning (2).** One creature within 90 ft. makes a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw (27 (6d8) lightning, half on a success); the bolt then leaps to up to two creatures within 30 ft. of the first, each making the same save for 14 (4d8).

**Thunderous Wingbeat (2).** Each creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw or takes 21 (2d10 + 10) thunder damage and is knocked prone. Zaros then flies up to half its speed.

### Lair Actions
**Fork Lightning.** Lightning stabs three points within 120 ft. Each creature within 5 ft. of one makes a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) lightning damage on a failure (half on a success) and being unable to take reactions until the start of its next turn on a failure.

**Downdraft.** A 40‑ft.-radius cylinder within 120 ft. becomes difficult terrain with disadvantage on ranged attacks into or through it until initiative 20 next round; a creature that ends its turn there is pushed 15 ft. and knocked prone (DC 22 Strength negates the prone).

**Deadening Air.** Grave‑mist fills a 60‑ft. radius within 120 ft. until initiative 20 next round: heavily obscured, verbal spellcasting requires a DC 15 Constitution check, and a creature that starts its turn there takes 14 (4d8) necrotic damage.

### Regional Effects

Within 6 miles of the lair:

- A stationary storm hangs overhead and never breaks; thunder is constant.
- Lightning strikes the ground every few minutes — always missing living creatures unless Zaros wills otherwise, and never missing those it does.
- A creature in metal armor has disadvantage on Stealth checks here, and Zaros always knows its direction.

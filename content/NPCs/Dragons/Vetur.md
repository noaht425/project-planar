Lord of the [[Elemental Plane of Water]].

Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]


## Stat Block

Gargantuan dragon

- **Armor Class** 20 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points** 518 (28d20+224)
- **Speed** 20 ft., swim 90 ft., fly 60 ft. (hover)
- **Saving Throws** Dex +8, Con +15, Wisdom +13, Charisma +14
- **Skills** Athletics +16, Intimidation +14, Perception +13, Stealth +8
- **Damage Resistances** fire; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities** cold
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, prone
- **Senses** blindsight 120 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 23
- **Languages** Common, Draconic, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge** 24 (62,000 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus** +7

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Pyrrha has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Vetur fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Amphibious.** Vetur breathes air and water.

**Formless.** Vetur can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny creature. Any effect that would contain it or remove it from the battlefield — forcecage, maze, imprisonment, Otiluke's resilient sphere, and the like — ends at the start of its next turn as it pours free.

**Crushing Depths.** Vetur is wreathed in the pressure of the deep out to 20 feet. A creature that isn't native to aquatic environments and ends its turn in the aura must succeed on a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or take 18 (4d8) cold damage and have its speed halved until the end of its next turn.

**Rimebound.** When a creature hits Vetur with a melee attack, its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn as frost climbs the weapon.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Vetur uses Frightful Presence, then makes three attacks, no more than one of which can be Fluke Slam.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (2d10 + 9) piercing plus 13 (3d8) cold damage. A Huge or smaller target is grappled (escape DC 19) and pulled up to 10 ft. toward Vetur.

**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d8 + 9) slashing plus 4 (1d8) cold damage.

**Flume Slam.** Melee Weapon Attack: +16 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (3d8 + 9) bludgeoning damage, and the target is knocked prone unless it succeeds on a DC 22 Strength saving throw.

**Drown.** One creature grappled by Vetur must succeed on a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or begin suffocating as Vetur forces black water into its lungs, and takes 27 (6d8) cold damage. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Abyssal Cold Breath (Recharge 5–6).** Vetur exhales freezing black water in a 90-ft. cone. Each creature makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 63 (14d8) cold damage on a failure (half on a success); on a failure its speed is also 0 until the end of its next turn. The area is difficult terrain (ice) for 1 minute; a creature that starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity save or be restrained until the end of its turn.

**Maelstrom (1/Day).** A 40-ft.-radius vortex forms within 120 ft. until the start of Vetur's next turn. A creature caught in it, or that enters it or starts its turn there, makes a DC 22 Strength saving throw or is pulled 20 ft. toward the center, knocked prone, and takes 22 (4d10) bludgeoning plus 22 (4d10) cold damage.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Vetur drops to 259 hit points or fewer, it recharges Abyssal Cold Breath and uses it (no action).

### Legendary Actions (3/round)

**Tail (1).** Vetur makes a Fluke Slam attack.

**Undertow (1).** Each creature within 40 ft. makes a DC 22 Strength saving throw or is pulled up to 20 ft. toward Vetur.

**Glacial Surge (1).** Vetur moves up to its swim speed (sliding on a wave of ice even on land); each creature whose space it enters makes a DC 22 Strength save or is pushed 10 ft. and knocked prone.

**Rime (2).** One creature within 60 ft. makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or is restrained in ice until the end of its next turn and takes 18 (4d8) cold damage.

### Lair Actions

**The Deep Rises.** Water surges through a 40-ft.-radius area within 120 ft. Each creature there must succeed on a DC 22 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and swept 15 ft. in a direction Vetur chooses. The area is difficult terrain until initiative count 20 on the next round.

**Pressure Crush.** Vetur fixes the weight of the abyss on a point within 120 ft. Each creature within 20 ft. of it makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) bludgeoning damage and being unable to take reactions until the start of its next turn on a failure, or half damage on a success.

**Black Water Fog.** A 40-ft.-radius sphere of frigid brine-fog forms within 120 ft. It heavily obscures the area for everyone but Vetur (blindsight), and a creature that starts its turn there takes 10 (3d6) cold damage. It lasts until initiative count 20 on the next round.

### Regional Effects (while Vetur lives)

- Cold fog rolls in with no weather to explain it, and the air stays damp; nonmagical flames gutter (disadvantage on checks to light or keep them lit).
- Currents and tides in the region move at Vetur's whim. A creature traveling by water against its will must succeed on a Strength (Athletics) check each hour or make no progress.
- The drowned don't decay, bodies lost to water in the region stay pale, whole, and staring, and now and then they twitch.
- Standing water ices over at night, even in warm seasons.

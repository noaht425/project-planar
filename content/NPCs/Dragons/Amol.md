Created by [[Okzu]], without assistance.

Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]

Member of the [[Dragons]].

## Appearance

Amol (A-mol) is an acid dragon, and the scales are wet in a way that does not dry. Where Amol has rested there is a mark afterwards.

## Manner

Amol is the proudest of the ten about parentage and the least accomplished of them. Amol was made by [[Okzu]] alone, with no outside hand in it, and will mention this within an hour of meeting anyone.

Of the ten, this is the dragon that holds the collaborations in open contempt. [[Brilis]], [[Charis]] and [[Mens]] are each addressed as lesser dragons to their faces, and all three have long since stopped responding to it.

Amol is not stupid and is not a bully to anything smaller. The contempt runs upward only, at the three dragons who outrank Amol in every respect except how they were made.

> *Amol asked me whether I understood the difference between a dragon made by one dragon and a dragon made by a dragon and a committee, and I said that I did. An hour later, in the middle of another subject, Amol asked me again in the same words and a good deal louder. Mens had come in behind me and was reading at the far end of the room, and did not look up.*

## Goals

Amol wants standing among the ten and goes about it badly. The dragons' business is collective, the quarrel over lineage has never produced anything, and Amol keeps raising it because lineage is the only thing on which Amol comes out ahead.

Amol has volunteered more than once to be the one who kills [[Afthor]].

What Amol collects is not settled.

## What It Asks

Amol asks for recognition, and what that means in practice is being brought into things. A warlock of Amol is asked to make Amol's involvement visible to the other dragons.

## Design Notes

Use Amol when a party needs a powerful ally who is cheap to recruit and who will make their situation worse by being proud of it.

The flavor box shows Amol's whole problem. The question goes past the narrator to a dragon who has never once answered it, and Amol has been asking it for as long as the collaborations have existed.

## Open

Amol's hoard is unwritten. It is a real gap, and it is the thing that would tell us most about this dragon. The obvious reading is that Amol has not settled on one.

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Amol has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Amol fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Solvent Body.** When an effect would contain Amol or remove it from the battlefield (forcecage, maze, and the like), it liquefies into a spray of acid: it becomes immune to all damage, can't take actions or reactions, and reconstitutes in an unoccupied space within 40 ft. at the start of its next turn, ending the effect. (It can also do this voluntarily as a legendary action.)

**Corrosive Form.** A creature that touches Amol or hits it with a melee attack takes 5 (1d10) acid damage. When Amol takes piercing or slashing damage, each other creature within 10 feet of it takes 7 (2d6) acid damage.

**Dissolving Strikes.** When Amol hits a creature with a melee attack, that creature's AC is reduced by 1 (by 2 on a critical hit) as its armor corrodes, to a minimum of 10. The reduction stacks and lasts until the armor is repaired or replaced, or removed by greater restoration or similar. Magic armor is reduced only on a critical hit. A creature wearing no armor or shield instead takes an extra 7 (2d6) acid damage from the hit.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Amol makes three attacks, no more than one of which can be Corrosive Tail.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +13 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d10 + 6) piercing plus 14 (4d6) acid damage.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +13 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8 + 6) slashing plus 7 (2d6) acid damage.

Corrosive Tail. **Melee Weapon Attack* +13 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8 + 6) bludgeoning plus 7 (2d6) acid damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 18). A creature grappled this way takes 11 (2d10) acid at the start of each of its turns, and Dissolving Strikes applies to it each turn.

**Etch (Recharge 4–6).** Amol scores a rune of dissolution onto one creature within 30 ft. The target must succeed on a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw or, until the end of the encounter (or until greater restoration removes it), it has vulnerability to acid damage and takes 9 (2d8) acid damage whenever it takes damage of any other type.

**Acid Breath (Recharge 5–6).** Amol exhales acid in a 90-ft. line, 10 ft. wide. Each creature makes a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 66 (12d10) acid damage on a failure or half on a success. A creature that fails also has its AC reduced by 2 (Dissolving Strikes, minimum 10). The line becomes a strip of acid (difficult terrain; 11 (2d10) acid to enter or start a turn there) for 1 minute.

**Slough (1/Day, only while at 227 hit points or fewer).** Amol violently sheds its corroded shell. Each creature within 20 ft. makes a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 44 (8d10) acid damage on a failure or half on a success. Amol then ends all conditions on itself and its AC returns to 18 if it had been lowered.

### Reactions

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Amol drops to 227 hit points or fewer, it recharges Acid Breath and uses it (no action).

### Legendary Actions (3/round)

**Rake (1).** Amol makes one Claw attack.
**Solvent Body (1).** Amol uses Solvent Body to reposition (see the trait).
**Spit Acid (1).** Ranged Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (4d8) acid damage, and Dissolving Strikes applies.
**Melt (2).** One creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or takes 18 (4d8) acid damage, drops one held item, and can't take reactions until the end of its next turn.

Lord of the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]].

Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]

Had a child ([[Iras]]) with [[Lux]]. Created [[Brilis]] in collaboration with the [[Archangels]].

Member of the [[Dragons]].

## Appearance

Pyrrha (PIR-ra) is burned rather than burning. The scales are matte and dark where they have been through fire too many times, and the light comes out from between them.

She is the largest of the five lords and the one that moves least.

## Manner

Pyrrha is a curator. Her realm destroys almost everything in it, and what she does with her time is identify the remainder and bring it inside the volcano, and she has been doing that without interruption since the realm began.

She is slow to speak and exact when she does. A conversation with her is mostly waiting. She does not repeat herself and she does not take a question twice.

She is the only dragon who has had a child with something that was not a dragon. [[Iras]] is hers and [[Lux]]'s, and he was king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]] until he abdicated in 419 AM. She has never been to see him and has never explained that either.

> *I was allowed in and shown a shelf. On it were eleven objects, all of them ugly, none of them worth anything to anyone outside that mountain. She named what each one had survived. The eleventh was a clay cup and she had been keeping it for nine thousand years because it had come through a thing that had taken a city.*

## Goals

Pyrrha wants everything fireproof in her realm inside her mountain, and she is close. What is left outside it is what she is working on, and most of what remains is in the seams where Fire meets the other four realms.

She wants [[Afthor]] dead with the rest of them.

She wants to know what [[Lux]] did with their son's siblings. Lux took every one of his other descendants into Pride and left Iras on the throne, and Pyrrha has never been given a reason.

## What She Asks

A warlock or an ally of Pyrrha brings her things that came through fire. Not valuable things. Things that should have burned and did not, with an account of what they survived, and the account matters as much as the object.

## Design Notes

Pyrrha is the patron for a player who wants a collection quest rather than a war, and the brief is unusually easy to hand out: anything that survived a fire, from anywhere, with its story.

Making her hoard worthless by any outside measure is the point. She is the dragon whose hoard cannot be stolen for profit and can be devastated by a single careless fire.

Her distance from Iras is the thread into the Geno plot. She is not estranged and she is not interested, and she has one question about his family that she would trade for.

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** Pyrrha has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Vainglorious Strength.** If Pyrrha succeeds on a Strength saving throw, the next attack it makes before the end of its next turn has advantage. The first time each turn Pyrrha fails a Strength saving throw, it flies into a fury: it gains 20 temporary hit points and its melee attacks deal an extra 9 (2d8) fire damage until the end of its next turn.

**Unbound.** Pyrrha can't be banished or forced to another plane against its will. Any effect that would fully enclose it or remove it from the battlefield — forcecage, maze, imprisonment, Otiluke's resilient sphere, and the like — instead ends at the start of Pyrrha's next turn as it smashes or burns free. It suffers no effect (such as forcecage's dispel-only clause) that would prevent this.

**Whorlflame Landing.** The first time each turn that Pyrrha lands on a solid surface after descending from at least 20 ft., each creature within 30 ft. of it is pushed up to 15 ft away and begins Burning (1d10).

**Flamegorge Resistance 3/Day.** If Pyrrha fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead. If it does, each flame within 30 ft. of it is extinguished.

**Bloodied Breath.** The first time Pyrrha drops to 292 hit points or fewer, it immediately regains the use of Slagflame Breath and uses it (no action).

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Pyrrha uses Frightful Presence, then makes three attacks, only one of which can be Searing Bite. It can replace one attack with Cinderlash or Phalanx‑Tail Sweep.

**Cavernous Maw.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +18 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (2d10 + 10) piercing plus 11 (2d10) fire. If the target is grappled by Pyrrha, it must succeed on a DC 23 Strength saving throw or be swallowed: it is blinded, restrained, has total cover, and takes 17 (3d10) fire damage at the start of each of Pyrrha's turns. If a swallowed creature deals 50+ damage to Pyrrha in a single turn, or Pyrrha dies, Pyrrha regurgitates all swallowed creatures (prone, within 15 ft.).

**Irongouge Claws.** *Melee Weapon Attack* +18 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (2d12 + 8) slashing, and the target is grappled (escape DC 27). Each other creature within 10 ft. of the target must succeed on a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw or take half the slashing damage.

**Searing Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +18 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (2d10 + 10) piercing plus 11 (2d10) fire.

**Cinderlash.** *Ranged Weapon Attack* +18 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (4d10) fire, and the target begins Burning (1d10).

**Phalanx-Tail Sweep.** Pyrrha swipes its tail in a 30 ft. cone, forcing each creature in the area to succeed on a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw or take 18 (5d6) bludgeoning damage and be pushed up to 20 ft. away from Pyrrha, or take half as much damage and be pushed half as far on a success.

**Frightful Presence.** Each creature of Pyrrha's choice within 120 ft. and aware of it must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the start of Pyrrha's next turn. On a success, the creature is immune for 24 hours.

**Slagflame Breath (Recharge 5-6).** Pyrrha exhales fire in a 100-ft. cone turning each surface in the area to molten slag. Each creature in the area must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 77 (22d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. For the next minute, the ground in the area becomes difficult terrain, and a creature that ends its turn on the ground in the area takes 7 (2d6) fire damage.

**Stoke Malice (1/Day).** Pyrrha breathes life into an area of slag or magma within 300 ft., creating three [Fire Elementals](https://5e.tools/bestiary/fire-elemental-mm.html) that emerge from the ground in an unoccupied space in the area.

### Reactions

**Billowing Wings.** When Pyrrha takes 30 or more damage from a single source, it may shroud itself in smoke, heavily obscuring the area in a 60 ft. radius until the end of its next turn, then immediately move up to half its speed.

**Bloodied Breath.** When Pyrrha is reduced below half its maximum hitpoints, it can roll to recharge its Slagflame Breath, then immediately use it, if available.

### Legendary Actions

**Lunging Swipe (1).** Pyrrha moves up to 20 ft. without provoking, then makes one Irongouge Claws attack.

**Leaping Flames (1).** Flames on a burning creature or object within 90 ft. leap to a creature within 30 ft. of it: DC 20 Dexterity save or 17 (3d10) fire and begin Burning (1d10).

**Inhale Cinders (1).** Pyrrha ends the Burning condition on a creature within 15 ft.; if it does, it rolls to recharge Slagflame Breath.

**Burn Away (2). One burning creature within 90 ft. makes a DC 20 Charisma save or the lowest‑level spell affecting it ends; if no spell is affecting it, it takes 16 (3d10) fire instead.

**Wing Buffet (2).** Each creature within 20 ft. makes a DC 22 Strength save or is pushed 15 ft. and knocked prone. Pyrrha then flies up to half its speed without provoking attacks of opportunity. (Combos into Whorlflame Landing.)

### Lair Actions

**Eruption.** Magma bursts in a 20‑ft.‑radius, 40‑ft.‑high cylinder centered on a point within 120 ft.: DC 23 Dexterity save or 27 (6d8) fire, and a creature on the ground is also restrained (escape DC 20) until initiative 20 next round.

**Suffocating Ash.** A 40‑ft.‑radius sphere within 120 ft. fills with ash until initiative 20 next round: lightly obscured, and each non‑fire‑immune creature that starts its turn there or enters it takes 10 (3d6) fire and must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution save or take no reactions until the start of its next turn.

**Downdraft.** Until initiative 20 next round, every creature's fly speed is halved, and each airborne non‑fire‑immune creature takes 11 (2d10) fire at initiative 20.

### Regional Effects (while Pyrrha lives)

- Fire damage dealt within 1 mile by creatures other than Pyrrha is halved.
- Non‑magical fires within 1 mile never go out and spread twice as fast.
- A non‑fire‑immune creature that finishes a long rest within 300 ft. of the lair gains no benefit from it.

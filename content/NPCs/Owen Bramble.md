A student of the [[Aravis Academy]] in its first century, and the only person in his intake who could match [[Ignatius]]. Burned by him in the main courtyard, and long dead.

Race: [[Beastfolk]] (sheepfolk)

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

## Appearance

Bramble had a mop of curly white hair, white skin, the ears and nose of a sheep, and a pair of small horns he touched when he was pleased with himself.

After the courtyard he had one eye. The right was taken along with a good deal of that side of his face.

## Manner

He was the better talker of the two by a wide margin and he knew it. He could talk almost anyone into almost anything, and he was born with a knack for ending up in the position that held the money or the meeting.

He held the budget on the doctoral project, dealt with the examining board, and reminded Ignatius of both whenever it was useful. He was not cruel about it so much as he was unable to leave it alone.

He read the same classes as Ignatius and matched him in all of them. He worked in bone and in the spectral hand, which points at the Krummel college.

## The Project and the Courtyard

The two of them were given the commission to turn the old walls of [[Hekatania]] into magical defences for [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]], as their doctoral project and in their second graduate year. Bramble ran the money and the presentations. The work turned on Forbiddance and on components the funding would not cover.

At the halfway presentation he said "I" where he meant "we". Ignatius put a fear spell into him for it, took over the presentation, and winked at him afterwards.

Bramble hit him from behind in the courtyard, which he had never done to anyone, and then they fought with spells until they were both spent. He was a foot away when Ignatius let go of everything he was holding.

He survived it.

## After

Nothing much. He left no work that is cited, no post that is remembered, and no account of his own. He died a long time ago, of old age or of something he brought on himself, and the record does not say which.

He was one of the two best students the Academy had in its first century and he is not remembered at all. The courtyard is remembered, because of who did it.

> *He is too long dead for me to have met anyone who knew him, so I went to the Academy's records instead. That is not how I prefer to work. He is on a class list twice and on a funding line once, for ruby dust, against a professor's name. There is no file on the courtyard and no file on the expulsion. I asked three people in that building who he had been and got the same answer from all three. None of them had any idea, and would I like to see the portrait of the one who burned him.*

## Design Notes

Bramble exists to make [[Ignatius]] legible. A party that only meets the ascendant god of Ambition sees a man who is certain and tireless. The courtyard is what that is, with the certainty pointed at someone he loved.

He is also the measure of the Academy's judgement. The institution gave two students who could not be in a room together the largest commission it had, and then expelled one of them and lost the other.

## Open

The year he died is unwritten. That it was obscure, and that nobody marked it, is settled.

Whether he ever spoke to Ignatius again is unwritten.

Which college he read in is unwritten. The bone work points at Krummel.

What became of the walls commission after the two of them is unwritten.

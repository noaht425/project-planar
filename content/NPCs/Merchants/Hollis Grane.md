Fence

Appearance: missing two fingers on the left hand, very cheerful about it

Race: [[Humans]]

Thrown out of [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] for forgery. Lives in [[Liber]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Buys anything at two fifths of its value and asks no questions.

Wares:
- Travel Papers (any kingdom): pass inspection unless the inspector succeeds on a DC 15 Investigation check
	- 200 GP
- Office of the Seal Papers: file you under whichever mouth type you like
	- 500 GP
- Lightly Used Magic Items: common and uncommon items at three quarters of the usual price. Roll a d6 on purchase, and on a 1 the previous owner is still looking for it.
	- varies
- Unclaimed Goods: a sack of whatever came in this week, contents chosen by the DM
	- 25 GP

Exclusive Offerings:
- A Used Panarex Invitation: someone else's name is on it, and changing it is a DC 20 forgery
	- 20,000 GP
- A Royal Seal (one kingdom, one use): a document carrying it is accepted as genuine by anyone who does not know the ruler personally
	- 12,000 GP
- A Name Off a List: removes you from one kingdom's record of exiles or wanted persons
	- 8,000 GP

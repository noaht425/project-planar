Debt Broker

Appearance: very small, moth wings, carries a ledger larger than she is

Race: [[Fae]] (fairy)

Lives in [[Velanea]]. Addressed in Low Fae as A Vryla.

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

In [[Low Fae]] there is no word for thanks, and the nearest phrase means "I owe you". Bryla trades in what that produces.

> *I thanked her. In [[Low Fae]] there is no way to do that. What I actually said was that I owed her. She had it in the ledger before I had finished saying it. The debt is small. She was good enough to tell me what it was for, which is more than she does for most people, and what it is for is the thanks.*

Wares:
- Small Debt: owed by a minor fae, redeemable for a favour about the size of a 1st-level spell or a night's shelter
	- 100 GP
- Middling Debt: redeemable for a favour about the size of a 3rd-level spell, or safe passage through one fey domain
	- 1,000 GP
- Buy-Back: Bryla pays off a debt you owe at its value plus a fifth, and you owe her instead
	- no fee
- Transfer: move a debt you owe onto someone willing to take it
	- 50 GP
- Prepaid Thanks: a token that lets a visitor thank someone in Kumas without owing them anything
	- 5 GP each

Exclusive Offerings:
- A Courtier's Debt: owed by a member of an archfey's court in [[Iris]], redeemable for one significant favour from that court
	- 15,000 GP
- The Unpaid: a debt so old nobody remembers what it was for. The debtor must pay when it is called in. Bryla does not know who the debtor is, and neither does the buyer until it is called.
	- 9,000 GP

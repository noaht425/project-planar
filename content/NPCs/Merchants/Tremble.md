Black Market Surgeon. Drug addict: will give discount if you bring drugs.

Appearance: gray coat, elk skull mask, horns.

Race: [[Omens]] (tiefling)

Exiled from [[Kingdom of Krotaphos]]. Wanders across the land.

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

> *He works in a grey coat and an elk skull. He will not take the skull off indoors. Several of his patients have objected. None of them has won the argument. The discount is real. It is the first thing he tells you, before the prices, before what he can actually do. I sat in the corner of a barn while he took a man's eye out and put something better in, talking the whole way through it, mostly about Krotaphos. It threw him out, and he will not be drawn on it. Afterwards he washed his hands. Then he said the thing he says to everybody: he has never lost a patient he was sober for. The man with the new eye laughed. I did not.*

Clockwork Artificer

Appearance: round, bald, magnifying lenses on a band over one eye, always winding something

Race: [[Humans]]

Keeps a workshop in [[Caragna]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

> *There are a great many clocks in that workshop and no two of them agree, which does not trouble him at all. Each Tráthlain clock is correct, he says, and the disagreement is between the places they are correct about. He keeps one on the wall set to somewhere he would not name, and he winds that one first.*

Wares:
- Tráthlain Clock: bought from [[Time]]. Always correct and never in agreement with another clock. You have advantage on any check to know the exact time.
	- 150 GP
- Prosthetic Limb: replaces a lost limb and works as the original did
	- 500 GP, or 2,000 GP with a hidden compartment
- Clockwork Companion: a Tiny construct that follows simple commands, as a familiar without the magic
	- 3,000 GP
- Wind-Up Lockpick: advantage on one check to open a lock, then it must be rewound for an hour
	- 400 GP

Exclusive Offerings:
- Appointment Watch: made in Tráthlain. Once per week, name a time and a place within 1 mile, and as long as you set out, you arrive at that place at exactly that time.
	- 16,000 GP
- Winding Key of Repose: once per long rest, after 10 minutes of being wound, you gain the benefit of a long rest's sleep without sleeping
	- 22,000 GP

Planar Outfitter

Appearance: short and broad, smoke-dark skin with embers showing underneath, laughs at his own customers

Race: [[Elementals]] (fire genasi)

Lives in Arteros, in the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]]

Home Realm: [[Tarkis]]

Wares:
- Arteros Cloak: immune to the heat of the flows and to fire damage from the environment, lasts about a morning (4 hours), then crumbles
	- 400 GP
- Noxian Filter Mask: immune to the ambient poison of [[Elemental Plane of Noxia|Noxia]] for 8 hours per filter
	- 150 GP, spare filters 10 GP each
- Ventis Tether: 50 ft line, belt and ring anchor; while anchored you cannot fall further than the line, and moving the anchor takes an action
	- 200 GP
- Breathing Cowl: breathe water for 12 hours, swim speed equal to your walking speed
	- 1,200 GP
- Seam Map: one of the ten seams of [[Tarkis]], advantage on Survival checks to navigate it
	- 300 GP each, 2,500 GP for all ten
- Ashwalker Boots: ignore difficult terrain made of ash, mud, ice or slag
	- 900 GP

Exclusive Offerings:
- The Afternoon Cloak: as the Arteros Cloak, but lasts until sundown (12 hours). Kasso swears he has one in the back. If a buyer insists, roll a d20, and on a 20 it is real.
	- 6,000 GP
- Realmwright's Pack: once per long rest, choose one elemental realm. For 24 hours you have resistance to that realm's damage type and ignore its ambient hazards.
	- 18,000 GP
- Seamstone: a stone taken from where two realms meet. Once per week, as an action, open a door to the nearest seam that stays open for 1 minute.
	- 25,000 GP

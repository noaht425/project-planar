Devotional Goods Seller

Appearance: tall, grey-haired, wears a different holy symbol every day and will not say which is her own

Race: [[Humans]]

Keeps a shop in the temple district of [[Omphalos]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

[[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]] does not restrict worship, and Odile stocks for every power a person might call on.

Wares:
- Holy Symbol (any archangel, archdevil, dragon, archfey, scholarch or ascendant): usable as a spellcasting focus
	- 25 GP
- Votive Candle: burn it for an hour before a long rest, and the next day you may reroll one d20 on a check that falls within that power's domain
	- 15 GP
- Blessed Water (one heaven): functions as holy water, and tastes of the heaven it came from
	- 25 GP
- Dragon Offering: something that belongs in a particular dragon's hoard, which improves an audience with that dragon
	- 50 to 500 GP
- Prayer Card to [[Isadora]]: once, after you roll a d20, roll again and use either result. If you have not used it within a day of buying it, Isadora uses it for you at a moment of her choosing.
	- 100 GP

Exclusive Offerings:
- An Unblessed Symbol: nobody has blessed it yet, and any power will accept it. It works as a holy symbol for any deity, and once per day a cleric holding it may swap one prepared domain spell for a spell of the same level from another domain.
	- 14,000 GP
- A Shard of the Palace Statues: a piece broken from one of the statues in the royal palace of Mathair. You have advantage on Persuasion checks with the power it depicts.
	- 9,000 GP

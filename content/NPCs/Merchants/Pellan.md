Seller of Incomplete Texts

Appearance: elderly, ink to the elbows, two pairs of spectacles worn at once

Race: [[Elves]]

Lives in [[Lenetha]]. Addressed in Sylvan as A Fellan.

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Wares:
- Language Primer (any common language): learn the language in 30 days of study
	- 250 GP
- Sylvan Primer for Contracts: proficiency in reading and negotiating Sylvan bargains. It does not cover food.
	- 150 GP
- Torn Atlas Page: a map of one region, advantage on checks to navigate it
	- 50 to 200 GP
- Bestiary Leaf: once, advantage on a check to recall one creature's weaknesses
	- 75 GP
- Half a Spellbook: holds 1d4 random spells of 1st to 3rd level, copied at the normal cost. The rest is missing.
	- 400 GP
- A Letter Never Sent: the DM decides what it says and who it was for
	- 20 GP

Exclusive Offerings:
- A Page of Ascension Notes: one page from the notes of someone who ascended. Pellan sells them one at a time and never in order. See [[Ascension Process]].
	- 10,000 GP per page
- A Thalamas Dictionary: from before the split. You can read Sylvan and Low Fae, and have advantage on checks to decipher anything written in Thalamas.
	- 5,000 GP
- The Last Chapter: a book of which only the final chapter survives. Reading it tells you how one event of the DM's choosing ends, and nothing about how it gets there.
	- 8,000 GP

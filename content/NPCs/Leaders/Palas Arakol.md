Queen of the [[Kingdom of Mathair]], and one of the three rulers of the [[Trois Re]].

Race: [[Earthborn]] (halfling), 3 feet 7 inches

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

## Manner

Extremely competent and not interested in being described that way. She prepares, she arrives on time, and she has read the papers, which sounds like faint praise until it is set beside the rest of the [[Panarex]].

She does not perform. Mathair is the kingdom that restricts no worship and holds the most central ground in the world, so a ruler there could spend a whole reign on ceremony. Arakol spends it on the work.

## In the Trois Re

The three kingdoms of the [[Trois Re]] have never voted differently at the Panarex, and keeping that true is an ordinary part of her week. She works with [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]] and [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]] as equals and neither of them is her client.

Her method is to settle things before the session. By the time the three take their seats the position is agreed, and no part of the agreeing has happened in the hall.

## The Mascarade

[[La Grande Mascarade]] rotates between host kingdoms, and Mathair has held it more often than anyone, beginning with the first in 428 AM in [[Omphalos]]. Arakol has hosted it, which is a year of work and a great deal of money, and she campaigned for the turn.

The 702 AM Mascarade was hers. [[Theophilius Fanatis]] attacked the ruler of [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] under her roof, in front of the assembled guests, and what she has said publicly since amounts to very little.

> *I asked her about the attack and got the shortest answer anybody gave me on the subject. She said that the convention held, which is true. Nobody used a title, nobody claimed precedence, and nobody was able to say afterwards who had been standing where. She let me think about that for a moment and then asked whether I had eaten.*

## Design Notes

Arakol is the competent ruler a party can actually deal with. She will keep an agreement, she will not be flattered into anything, and she is the fastest route to the Trois Re's three votes.

What happened at her Mascarade is the lever on her. She has not said what she thinks and she was in the room.

Seventh and last king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]] of the Genasi line, killed by [[Iras]].

Son of [[Genasi VI]]. Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Born: 1 Morcaela, 363 AM. King from 100 Aucaela, 397 AM. Died: 33 Aucaela, 405 AM. Killed by [[Iras]].

## The Reign

Eight years, and a war for all of them.

He took the throne on 100 Aucaela, 397 AM. The war between [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] and [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] began four days later. The whole of his reign was taken up with not committing to it. He did not declare for either side and he did not declare neutrality either, which left Geno being courted by both and trusted by neither.

The reasoning was sound. Geno could not afford a war and could not afford to lose the wall's funding to one. The execution was eight years of delay, and by the end of it the kingdom's own officers were briefing against him.

He had no military reputation and no ally he could call on when [[Iras]] came for the throne. Iras killed him on 33 Aucaela, 405 AM, taking the throne on the principle that Geno owed its existence to the dragons and should be ruled by a dragon's descendant. That argument could only have been made against a king nobody was going to fight for.

His line went to [[Pride]] after his death, the whole of it, taken by [[Lux]]. See [[Pride]].

> *The last of the Genasi kings has no tomb, because there was nobody left with standing to build him one and nothing in him to bury by the time the matter was raised. There is a plaque in the petition hall. It gives his dates and does not give the manner of his death, and the clerk who walks visitors past it does not stop.*

## Design Notes

The seventh reign is the weakest of the seven on purpose, and it is what makes Iras possible. A party that hears Iras's principle should know that it was aimed at a king with no friends.

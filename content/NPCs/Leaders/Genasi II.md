Second king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]], and the first who was not its maker.

Son of [[Genasi]]. Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Born: 98 Aucaela, 6 AM. King from 56 Morcaela, 62 AM. Died: 32 Auctera, 160 AM.

## The Reign

Ninety eight years, the longest of the seven, and the one the rest were measured against.

[[Genasi]] gave up the throne because he had grown bored of ruling, which left his son a kingdom that had been run for sixty years as one being's project. Nothing in it worked without the progenitor in the room. There was no treasury worth the name, no court that could decide anything, and no way to settle a dispute except by asking a god.

Genasi II built all of it. The offices of the Geno court date from his reign, as do its records, its taxes, and the rule that a king of Geno hears petitions in person. He is the reason the kingdom survived its founder losing interest.

He was not loved for it. He spent a century being compared to his father by people who had met his father, and the comparison was the one subject on which he was known to lose his temper.

> *The petition hall in Geno still has his desk in it, which is a plain thing and too small for the room. Every king since has used it, including [[Iras]], who had everything else in that hall replaced. The clerk who showed it to me said that the desk is the job and the rest is decoration.*

## Design Notes

Genasi II is the administrator the setting needs somewhere, since a kingdom founded by a bored god has to have been made to work by somebody.

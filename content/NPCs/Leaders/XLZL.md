Elected leader of the [[Kingdom of Regno]], and fifteen years old.

Race: [[Fae]] (automaton). Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Made: 22 Morcaela, 687 AM. Elected: c. 700 AM.

## What They Are

[[Chronos]] wanted a divine spark put into an automaton and asked [[Themis]] to do it, offering something fair in exchange. Themis did it. XLZL is the result, and the thing Themis got in return was the raising of them.

So XLZL was made by a trick, given a spark by the archangel of justice, and then brought up by her. They are the only being any of the twelve has raised.

## Manner

Fifteen, and good at the job. Regno elected them by a comfortable margin and has not regretted it.

They keep office hours in [[Caragna]] and the queue for them forms the night before. Anyone may join it. XLZL hears the case, says what can be done, and says plainly when nothing can be, which is the part people mention.

They do not want to be a precedent and they know they are one. What they want is to do the work and be left alone to do it, and the first half of that is going well.

The rest is unsettled. They were made by one being, sparked by another, raised by a third's idea of justice, and they are still working out which of those they are. They do not discuss it in office hours.

## The Question

[[Chronos]] has been building a case for a very long time about whether a made thing has a soul, and XLZL is the proof he intends to argue from. Whatever the twelve eventually rule, it lands on a fifteen-year-old head of state who did not ask to be the evidence.

[[Themis]] wants them kept out of all of it. She has not managed it.

> *The queue in Caragna starts at dusk. I joined it and was seen a little after midnight, by which point XLZL had been hearing people for most of a day. They apologised to me for the wait. I had come to ask about the spark. They answered two questions about Regno's grain tariffs very well, said that the other matter was not something they discussed, and then asked the next person in.*

## Design Notes

XLZL is a competent child ruler with two gods and an archfey holding an interest in what they are. A party that wants something from Regno will get a fair hearing and no favours.

The soul question is the long thread. It will be settled by beings who have never met them.

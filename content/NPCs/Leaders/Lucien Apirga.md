One of the two current kings of the [[Kingdom of Krotaphos]]. Race: [[Omens]], Ill. Elected.

Krotaphos always has two kings at once, one Good Omen and one Ill.

At [[La Grande Mascarade]] in 702 AM, when his co-ruler [[Theophilius Fanatis]] attacked the ruler of [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] under an enchantment, Lucien was the first to react and restrained him.

It made no difference to what followed. Sylfaen ordered the arrest of every citizen of Krotaphos regardless.

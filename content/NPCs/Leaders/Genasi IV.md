Fourth king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]], who paid for the wall.

Son of [[Genasi III]]. Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Born: 98 Morcaela, 140 AM. King from 27 Morcaela, 221 AM. Died: 12 Auctera, 283 AM.

## The Reign

Sixty two years of accounting.

He inherited a kingdom with a permanent magical obligation in the middle of it. His reign is where the cost of the wall stopped being an emergency and became the first line of the budget. The arrangement he set up is the one still in use: the mages who hold the breach are paid before anything else is paid, and nobody votes on it.

Everything else in his reign was shaped by that. He let two garrison towns on the Oinarrian border go unmanned, he stopped a canal his father had begun, and he raised the harbour duties twice. None of it was popular. All of it was the wall.

He is the king nobody tells a story about. The Geno court's own history of him runs to a page, most of which is figures.

> *I went looking for a portrait of him and found that there is not one in the capital. There is a wall ledger with his seal on every page of it, kept in the same room as the current one. The mage who keeps the account could tell me what rate he had set. She could not tell me what he looked like.*

## Design Notes

A reign can be entirely successful and leave nothing behind, and this is the one that demonstrates it. The funding rule he wrote is why the wall survived a hundred and eleven years without a king.

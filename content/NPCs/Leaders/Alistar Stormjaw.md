Blue Dragonborn Scientist.

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

> *In Tama they have his workbench. It is in the hall at [[Liber]], under glass, with a label that gives his dates and his title but does not mention [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]] anywhere on it. The guide told me the kingdom was founded by the man who made the hole, then asked whether I knew of another kingdom that would put that on a plinth.*

## History
- 36 Auctera, 180: born
- 1 Aucaela, 199 AM: starts attending [[Aravis Academy]]
- 37 Mortera, 201 AM: drops out of Aravis Academy
- 56 Morcaela, 202 AM: Tried to open a portal to [[Tarkis]] in his lab in [[Geno (City)]]
- Banished by [[Genasi III]], the king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]] at the time
- 96 Morcaela 202 AM: Forms [[Kingdom of Tama]] with exiles from other kingdoms
- 96 Morcaela 202 AM: named as first king of Tama

Tried to open a portal to Tarkis in his lab in Geno (City) and ever since, Tarkis has been bleeding into the city:
- Led to Dragonborn being banned from the city
- Some taken in by [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]]
- Others (Led by Alistar) from Kingdom of Tama with exiles from other kingdoms
- Banished by Genasi III, the king of the Kingdom of Geno at the time

Fifth king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]], under whom the kingdom stopped being first at anything but age.

Son of [[Genasi IV]]. Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Born: 46 Aucaela, 200 AM. King from 12 Auctera, 283 AM. Died: 5 Auctera, 325 AM.

## The Reign

Forty two years, the shortest of the first six.

Geno had been the first kingdom in [[Arenis]] and had traded on it for nearly three centuries. Under Genasi V that stopped meaning anything. [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]] had the [[Aravis Academy]] by then, with the scholarship that came with it. [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]] had the larger army. The [[Trois Re]] had an alliance that Geno was not part of and was never offered.

He did not lose a war or a province. What he lost was precedence, in small pieces, at a rate nobody could point at. He was the first king of Geno to arrive at the [[Panarex]] and find that the room had already settled the question he had come to argue.

His court blamed the dragons for withdrawing their interest. The dragons had not withdrawn anything, and worship of them in Geno has stayed higher than anywhere else throughout.

> *A tutor in Geno told me that the kingdom's own schools teach the fifth reign as a lesson about neglect, and that the lesson is unfair. Nothing was neglected. Geno simply went on being what it had been while everybody else changed, which she said is harder to teach because there is nobody in it to blame.*

## Design Notes

Decline without a disaster is the useful shape here. Nothing goes wrong in this reign and the kingdom is weaker at the end of it than at the start.

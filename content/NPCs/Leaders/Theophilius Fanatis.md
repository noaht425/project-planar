One of the two current kings of the [[Kingdom of Krotaphos]]. Race: [[Omens]], Good. Elected.

A vengeance paladin.

**At [[La Grande Mascarade]] in 702 AM he attacked the ruler of [[Kingdom of Sylfaen|Sylfaen]]** in front of the assembled rulers. He was under an enchantment at the time, and he was given a saving throw against it and chose to fail. He showed no distress about that afterwards.

He spoke in [[Celestial]] as he went for it, calling the Sylfaen ruler a tyrant and a despot, saying people had suffered long enough and that he would kill him and make everyone glad of it. Celestial is illegal to speak in Krotaphos.

He was restrained by [[Lucien Apirga]], his co-ruler, and by the Sylfaen ruler.

The consequences were immediate and fell on his whole kingdom. Sylfaen ordered the arrest of every citizen of Krotaphos, and people have been arrested and killed since.

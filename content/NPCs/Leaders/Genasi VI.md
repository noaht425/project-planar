Sixth king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]], and the reign the court became ceremony.

Son of [[Genasi V]]. Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Born: 1 Morcaela, 299 AM. King from 5 Auctera, 325 AM. Died: 100 Aucaela, 397 AM.

## The Reign

Seventy two years, long and almost entirely without event.

What grew under him was protocol. The Geno court acquired the form it still has: an order of precedence for everything, a correct address for everybody, and a royal grammar that takes work to learn. [[Genoan]] has endings reserved for a king, and the rules about which of them apply to which act of state were settled in this reign.

The ceremony was not vanity. It was the thing the court still had, and his officers got very good at it while the kingdom got quietly poorer. Visiting rulers came away impressed and unhelpful.

He fathered one son late, at the age of sixty four. That is the fact his reign turned on. [[Genasi VII]] was thirty four when his father died, had been given nothing to do before then, and had eight years.

> *The royal grammar is the thing the court will not give up. The endings are built for a name inside the Genoan system, so when [[Iras]] took the throne there was no correct way to say what he was, and the heralds spent a year on the problem. One of them told me they never solved it. They worked around it, which he said is not the same and was noticed.*

## Design Notes

The sixth reign is where the dynasty's weakening becomes visible to outsiders, since what the court is best at by this point is addressing people correctly.

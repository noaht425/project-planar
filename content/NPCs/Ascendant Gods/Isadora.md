Goddess of Luck (both fortune and misfortune). Known as the Two-Faced Goddess.

Race: [[Humans]]

Appearance: Vitiligo pigmentation, skin of bronze and gold.

Hated by the [[Scholarchs]].

Wears a rabbit mask when attempting to disguise her appearance.

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## History
- 583 AM: First attested, at a gaming table in [[Caragna]]
- 601 AM: First recorded appearance under the rabbit mask
- 640 AM: First recorded as a god rather than as a very fortunate mortal

## Manner

Isadora (ees-uh-DOR-uh) is the goddess of luck, fortune and misfortune both, and she plays with fate the way a cat plays with a ball of yarn.

She is goofy, and most of what she does is play. She bats at outcomes to see where they will roll, and she will play any game with anyone. She is very good at all of them.

When she wants someone to play with her, she can be solemn. She puts on a grave face and a sincere voice and presents a wager as though a great deal depended on it, and she keeps the face on exactly as long as it takes them to agree.

She goes about in disguise more than any other god, usually behind a rabbit mask, and she can often be found at the tables of [[Eltarien's Extraplanar Extravaganza]].

The [[Scholarchs]] hate her. Their purpose is to keep things in balance, and she spends her time tipping things over to see which way they fall.

> *Isadora sat down across from me with a face like a funeral and asked, very gently, whether I had a moment to talk about something that mattered. I said that I did. She took a single die from her sleeve, set it on the table between us and asked me to call it. I called it correctly, and she was so delighted that she knocked the table over.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** [[Isadora's Dice]], five obsidian dice inscribed with gold. She gives them away from time to time and they tend to come back.

**Source:** unwritten, and she will not say.

Isadora is the most recent of the ascendants and the worst documented. There is no date for her ascension because she has never given one, and she treats the question as the funniest thing anybody asks her. She has given at least four different answers to it that cannot all be true, and it is not clear that any of them is.

What can be dated is when people started noticing her. She is first attested at a table in [[Caragna]] in 583 AM, first recorded behind the mask in 601 AM, and first written down as a god rather than as an unusually lucky woman in 640 AM. So she ascended at some point before 583 AM and after [[Iras]] in 441 AM, which leaves a window of a hundred and forty years.

She knows the window exists. She has been asked to narrow it and has instead offered to play for the answer.

## Goals

Isadora wants to play.

She wants the future to stay hard to read. A fate that can be known in advance is not one she can play with.

## What She Asks

A game. Anyone who wants her favour has to play her for it, and she picks the game.

## Relationships

- Hated by the [[Scholarchs]]

## Design Notes

Isadora is fun to run. A DM should play the sincerity completely straight until the party has agreed to the wager.

Her quarrel with the Scholarchs gives her a real stake in the setting. She is the one god who wants the future uncertain, against three beings who exist to keep it in order.

## Open

Her source, and when she ascended, are unwritten.

Whether [[It That Will Be]] made divination harder because of her is an idea worth considering and is not settled.

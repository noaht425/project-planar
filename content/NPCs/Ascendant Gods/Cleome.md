Domain: Friendship

Race: [[Elves]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Cleome (klee-OH-mee) is an elf, and a spider orchid grows from her head and through her hair. It is alive, it moves, and it is rooted in her skull.

She is quiet on her feet. Most people do not hear her arrive.

## Manner

Cleome looks after people. She is the god most likely to notice that someone has not slept, and the one least willing to let it go once she has.

She has no patience for nonsense, including the nonsense of the people she loves. She will tell a friend plainly that they are making a mistake and then stay to help them through it.

She has a habit of appearing. People who know her describe turning round to find her already there and already looking at them, and some of them find this frightening. She stares, and she does not seem to know that it unsettles anyone.

She loves flowers and spiders, and the orchid most of all. She talks to it.

> *I did not hear Cleome come in. I looked up from my notes and she was sitting opposite me, watching, and her tea had gone cold, so she had been there a while. The orchid in her hair turned to follow my hand as I put the pen down. She asked who knew where I was. When I said nobody, she wrote down my name and the name of my inn and told me she would look in on me. She did, every few days, until I left the city.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** the [[Spider Orchid]]. It is bound to her in body and in feeling. It feeds on what she feels, and it has to be watered every day with blood that is not hers.

**Source:** unwritten.

## Goals

Cleome wants her friends safe and honest with themselves, and she is willing to be unpopular to manage the second.

She wants the orchid fed without anyone coming to harm for it, and she has not found a way.

## What She Asks

Friendship in the plain sense. A worshipper or warlock of Cleome is expected to look after the people around them and to let themselves be looked after in turn. She takes a refusal of help badly.

## Relationships

- Friend of [[Chore]], [[Ignatius]] and [[Nausicaä]]

## Design Notes

Cleome is care without softness, and the staring is how a party should meet her first. She is kind, she is right, and she was in the room before anyone noticed.

The orchid is the tension worth keeping. The god of friendship is bound to something that needs blood every day, and how she keeps it fed is a question a party can be asked to help with or can stumble on.

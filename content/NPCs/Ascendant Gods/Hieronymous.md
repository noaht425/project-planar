God of Faith. Married to [[Verita]].

Race: [[Beastfolk]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## History
- 174 AM: Born in [[Arenis]]
- 241 to 259 AM: Travels with [[Ignatius]], [[Cleome]], [[Nausicaä]] and [[Chore]] and watches all four ascend
- 266 AM: Ascends, by a route that is not the reconstructed method
- Date unwritten: Marries [[Verita]]

## Manner

Hieronymous holds faith, which sits oddly beside the woman he married. [[Verita]] is the archangel of truth, and truth is what can be shown. Faith is what is held without being shown. The two of them have been married a long time and the tension in that has never been resolved by either of them in public.

He was one of the five who travelled together in the middle of the third century, and the only one of them who did not ascend by the method the other four pieced together. He is not in the reconstructed account of it at all. What he did instead is not recorded anywhere, and he is the only ascendant whose route has left no trace, which some people take as evidence and others take as the absence of it.

**Relic:** unwritten.

## Design Notes

Hieronymous is the gap in the ascension record, and that is the most useful thing about him. A party researching how to ascend will find four accounts and a silence, and the silence is a god who is still around and could be asked.

The marriage gives a mortal party a route to an archangel that does not go through a temple.

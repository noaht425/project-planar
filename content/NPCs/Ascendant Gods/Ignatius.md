Domain: Ambition

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Race: [[Elementals]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Ignatius (ig-NAY-shus) is a fire elemental in the shape of a slight young man, orange-skinned, with pointed ears and eyes the colour of embers. His hair is fire and he wears it long and tied back. It burns higher and brighter with his temper, and anyone who has spent an afternoon with him can read his mood off it from across a room.

## Manner

Ignatius believes that anything can be made to work if the people around him will get out of the way and let him. He holds that his ends justify his means, and he does not treat this as a view that needs defending.

When something goes wrong it is rarely his fault, by his own account. When it is, he is certain he can fix it, and he has usually started fixing it before anyone else has agreed that he should.

He is proud and easily slighted, and he wants very badly to be admired. He will work harder than anyone in the room to earn it, and he does not forgive being denied it.

He and [[Nausicaä]] have quarrelled for as long as both have been gods. He has told her, and anyone else who would listen, that he could do her work better than she does, and he means it.

## Ascension

**Relic:** the [[Fireproof Spellbook]], a crimson leather tome stitched in gold. He was a wizard before he was a god and the book was his working spellbook.

**Source:** power taken from beings he killed, stored up and spent together.

Ignatius decided one day that most of his problems would go away if nobody could get in his way, and he spent a long time researching how to ascend. For a source he hunted. He killed a number of very powerful beings, devils and at least one dragon among them, choosing ones he considered evil or at any rate in his way, and he stored what he took from each until he had enough for the ritual.

He was the first of the four. [[Cleome]] went up with him, and [[Nausicaä]] and [[Chore]] followed.

## Goals

Ignatius wants to succeed, and the object changes. Whatever he is working on is the most important work in the world until it is finished, and then the next thing is.

He wants to be recognised for it. An achievement credited to someone else is worse to him than a failure.

## What He Asks

Ignatius is the only patron of Ambition, and the pact is set out in [[Warlock Ambition]]. He wants his warlocks to succeed more than they want it themselves, and he does not let them stop.

## Relationships

- Rival of [[Nausicaä]]
- Friend of [[Cleome]], who ascended with him, and of [[Chore]]
- Listed among the famous alumni of [[Aravis Academy]]

## Design Notes

Ignatius is never short of a plan and never in doubt that it would work if people stopped interfering, and a party that works with him will find their part in the plan has been decided for them.

The rivalry with Nausicaä is about competence. He believes he could run her domain better than she does, which is exactly the kind of thing he would believe.

The pact in [[Warlock Ambition]] is the best expression of him at a table. He lets a warlock keep going well past the point where they should stop, and the bill arrives afterwards.

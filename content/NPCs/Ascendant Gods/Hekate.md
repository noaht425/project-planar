Domain: Magic

Syphoned power from [[Arcanis]]. Many have tried to replicate this and died horrifically.

Original name: Madeline Gambari

Race: [[Omens]]

Appearance: young woman, shoulder length light brown hair, olive skin, one green eye, one gold.

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Facts
- Founder and Headmistress of the [[Aravis Academy]]
- Youngest person to ascend

## History
- 47 Morcaela, 137 AM: Born in [[Hekatania]] when it was still called Hekatania
- 83 Auctera, 141 AM: Began magical education under parents
- 12 Auctera, 152 AM: First journey to [[Arcanis]]
- 36 Aucaela, 154 AM: Starts what would become Aravis Academy as a small school to teach local kids
- 48 Morcaela, 157 AM: Ascended to godhood
- 50 Morcaela, 157 AM: turned Aravis Academy into a full university

## Manner

Hekate (HEK-uh-tee) is the youngest person ever to ascend and the only one to have taken power from a realm rather than from a being. She did it at twenty, and she has spent most of the time since running a university.

She is a teacher first. She started [[Aravis Academy]] as a school for local children before she was a god, made it a university two days after she became one, and still teaches there.

She is generous with everything she knows except the one thing everyone wants. She has never told anyone how she got into [[Arcanis]] or how she got out again, and she has never written it down. Many have died trying to repeat it.

> *Hekate still teaches the first-year course herself, and I sat at the back of one of her lectures. A student asked how she had got into Arcanis, which I gather somebody asks every year. She set the whole hall an essay on the question, due the following week, and promised to read the best one aloud. She has never read one aloud.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** unwritten.

**Source:** [[Arcanis]], siphoned. She forced a foothold into a realm that offers none, and the hole she left is still closing.

## Goals

Hekate wants magic taught well and widely, and Aravis is how she does it.

She wants nobody else to attempt her route. Her silence is the only means she has found of stopping them, and it has not worked.

## Relationships

- Founder and headmistress of [[Aravis Academy]]

## Design Notes

Hekate is the god a party can actually visit, in a building with a front door and office hours, and the one thing they want from her is the one thing she will not give.

Reading the silence as protective is my interpretation. It could as easily be pride, or a method she does not fully understand herself, and a DM should feel free to choose.

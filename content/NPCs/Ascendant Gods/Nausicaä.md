The Protector of Children. Affinity for abandoned/unwanted children.

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Race: [[Beastfolk]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Nausicaä (now-SIK-ay-uh) is a tabaxi, immaculately kept, and she wears a silver pendant with a brass gear set around a sun that is half a moon.

## Manner

Nausicaä protects children nobody wanted. She was one of them. Her family gave her away young, to an aunt who raised her as a tool and as an extension of herself.

None of this made her humble. She is spoiled, she is sure she is the best at what she does, and she expects to be treated accordingly. She expects the children in her care to be treated accordingly too.

She and [[Ignatius]] have quarrelled for as long as both have been gods, and neither of them has ever conceded a point.

> *Nausicaä keeps a house for children nobody came back for, and I was shown round it by a girl of about ten. She told me which rooms were the best, and that Nausicaä had chosen them for the children herself. Then she showed me Nausicaä's room, which was better than all of them, and which Nausicaä had also chosen.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** the [[Kubo Family Pendant]], an heirloom of the family that gave her away.

**Source:** [[Themis]] and [[Archon]], set against each other.

Nausicaä would not let Ignatius get one over on her. She worked out the ascension process out of spite, largely from his notes, which happened to have been left where she would come across them. [[Cleome]] had left them there.

For a source she went to Themis and Archon, the two halves of [[Themati]], and played them against each other until between them they had given her what she needed.

## Goals

Nausicaä wants every unwanted child to have somewhere to go, and she wants that place to be very good. She does not believe in second-rate care and will not pay for it.

She wants to be acknowledged as the best at it, by everyone, and by Ignatius in particular.

## What She Asks

That the children come first. Her worshippers take in children who have been left, and they are expected to do it properly.

## Relationships

- Rival of [[Ignatius]]
- Took her power from [[Themis]] and [[Archon]]
- Friend of [[Cleome]] and [[Chore]]

## Design Notes

She made a domain out of what was done to her, and she did not become gentle in the process. A protector of children who takes the best room in the house for herself is a god a party can like and argue with in the same conversation.

Her relic being her family's heirloom is the piece of the past she kept. She will not discuss the family and she has never taken the pendant off.

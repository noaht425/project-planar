Elder Stone Giant. Gifted power by [[Kleipur]]. One of the first [[Giantkin]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Vato (VAH-toh) is a stone giant, and he wears a helm of the same stone.

## Manner

Vato is, to put it kindly, a bit dense. He does not think things through and does not see the need, because [[Afthor]] does his thinking for him and Vato does what Afthor says.

He has the titans' pride, and most of it is pride in Afthor.

> *I asked Vato what he thought of the storm over the realm of air. He thought about it for a long time, and then he told me he would ask Afthor.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** the [[Stone Helm]].

**Source:** [[Kleipur]], given.

## Goals

Vato wants whatever Afthor wants.

## Relationships

- Follows [[Afthor]]

## Design Notes

Vato is Afthor's reach. Whatever Afthor is too lazy to do, Vato does, and a party that wants to get at Afthor without facing him will find Vato in the way. He is easy to fool and very hard to hurt.

## Open

Whether Vato would turn on Afthor if someone explained it to him slowly enough is unwritten.

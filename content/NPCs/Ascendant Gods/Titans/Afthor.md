Elder Storm Giant. Gifted power by [[Kleipur]]. One of the first [[Giantkin]]. Most powerful Elder Giant (because of taking power from dragons). Known as the Storm Lord.

Killed [[Zaros]] and [[Vari]] and drank their blood. Despised by all [[Dragons]].

Current lord of the [[Elemental Plane of Air]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Race: Giantkin

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Stat Block

Huge Giant

- **Armor Class** 19
- **Hit Points** 474 (36d12 + 240)
- **Speed** 50 ft., Swim 50 ft.
- **Saving Throws** STR +17, CON +16, WIS +10, CHA +13
- **Skills** Arcana +10, Athletics +17, History +10, Perception +10
- **Damage Resistances** Cold
- **Damage Immunities** Lightning, Thunder
- **Senses** Passive Perception 20
- **Languages** Common, Gias, Old Gias
- **Challenge** 26 (90,000 XP)
- **Proficiency Bonus** +8

### Traits

**Amphibious.** Afthor can breathe air and water.

**Zaros' Rage.** All attacks Afthor makes deal an extra 8d4 lightning damage.

**Thundering Parry.** When a creature makes a melee attack against you, you may roll a d20. If you get a result within 1 of what the creature attacking you rolled on their d20, the attacking creature must make a DC 20 strength saving throw. On a failed save, they take 8d8 thunder damage and gain the stunned condition until the end of their next turn.

**Innate Spellcasting.** Afthor's Innate Spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 21). He can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components. At will: detect magic, feather fall, levitate, light. 3/day each: conjure elementals, control weather, wall of ice, water breathing. 1/day each: storm of vengeance, tsunami, whirlwind.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Afthor fails a saving throw, he may choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Afthor makes 3 Greatsword attacks.

**Greatsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 30 (6d6 + 9) slashing damage plus 20 (8d4) lightning damage.

**Lightning Strike (Recharge 5-6).** Afthor hurls a magical lightning bolt at a point it can see within 500 feet of it. Each creature within 10 feet of that point must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 78 (12d12) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Rock.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 35 (4d12 + 9) bludgeoning damage plus 20 (8d4) lightning damage.

### Legendary Actions

**Slash.** Afthor make a Greatsword attack.

**Lightning Pulse.** All creatures within 25 ft. of Afthor must make a Dexterity saving throw DC 21. On a failure they take 28 (8d6) lightning damage plus 28 (8d6) thunder damage and are pushed up to 30 ft. away. On a successful save, they take half damage and are not pushed.

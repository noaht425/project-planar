Elder Hill Giant. Gifted power by [[Kleipur]]. One of the first [[Giantkin]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## History
- 98 Aucaela, 1 AM: [[Kleipur]] gives the six titans their power

## Appearance

Kodren (KOH-dren) is a hill giant, and when he is lying down, which is most of the time, he is difficult to tell from a hill.

## Manner

Kodren is the kindest of the titans and the sleepiest. He would rather live out his life in peace and comfort than do any of the things a god is supposed to do, and mostly he manages it.

He has the titans' pride but puts very little effort into it. Asked whether he is one of the original gods, he agrees, and goes back to sleep.

> *Kodren had been asleep on the same hillside for long enough that a village had grown up against his side. He woke while I was there, apologised to the village for the disturbance, and went back to sleep a little further over so as not to crowd them.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** the [[Plate of the Rolling Hills]].

**Source:** [[Kleipur]], given.

## Goals

Kodren wants to be left in peace, and wants the same for whoever lives near him.

## Design Notes

A party can simply like Kodren. Nothing moves him against his will, which makes him the safest place to be in a crisis and the hardest ally to get anywhere.

## Open

Whether anything would get him up to fight is unwritten.

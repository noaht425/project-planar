Elder Cloud Giant. Gifted power by [[Kleipur]]. One of the first [[Giantkin]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Gallum (GAL-um) is a cloud giant, and he weighs whatever he likes. Most of the time he likes to weigh very little.

## Manner

Gallum is flighty and jolly and enjoys a good laugh, his own most of all. He drifts between plans the way he drifts between places, and he is good company for as long as he stays.

He is as proud as any of the titans, but his pride is cheerful. He will tell anyone that he is one of the original gods, laugh, and expect them to agree.

> *Gallum met me in the street and was so pleased to see me, although we had never met, that he picked me up. He can weigh whatever he likes, and that afternoon he had decided to weigh almost nothing, so up we went and had lunch on a cloud. He laughed at my face for most of the way up and at his own joke about it for the rest.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** [[Gallum's Boots]], which he made.

**Source:** [[Kleipur]], given.

## Goals

Gallum wants to enjoy himself, and mostly does.

## Design Notes

A party is most likely to meet Gallum by accident, and least likely to get a straight answer from him. He is harmless until he is bored.

The boots are worth knowing about. He made them, and he could be talked into making another pair by someone who kept him laughing long enough.

## Open

What Gallum wants beyond a good time is unwritten.

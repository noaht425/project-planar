Elder Fire Giant. Gifted power by [[Kleipur]]. One of the first [[Giantkin]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Created phoenixes. Hates [[Iras]] for claiming Phoenix King title.

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Moto (MOH-toh) is a fire giant, and the phoenixes he made roost on his shoulders when they are not in his hall.

## Manner

Moto is fiery and quick to anger, and the anger goes as quickly as it comes. He made the first phoenixes and he loves them.

He hates [[Iras]] for calling himself the Phoenix King. The phoenix was Moto's, and Iras took the title without asking. [[Genasi]] is trying to bring Moto into a plan to kill Iras, and Moto has not said no.

> *Moto's phoenixes roost in the rafters of his hall. I made the mistake of saying the words Phoenix King in a sentence about something else entirely, and the rafters were alight before I had finished it. Moto spent the next hour putting them out himself and apologising to the birds.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** the [[Cloak of the Phoenix]].

**Source:** [[Kleipur]], given.

## Goals

Moto wants the title back. He wants Iras humbled or dead and is not particular about which.

## Relationships

- Hates [[Iras]]
- Courted by [[Genasi]] for the plan against Iras

## Design Notes

Moto is the titan with a live grievance, and the grievance is a word. A party can set him off by accident, the way the narrator did, and can use the same word on purpose.

## Open

Whether he has agreed to Genasi's plan is unwritten.

Elder Frost Giant. Gifted power by [[Kleipur]]. One of the first [[Giantkin]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## History
- 98 Aucaela, 1 AM: [[Kleipur]] gives the six titans their power

## Appearance

Sarma (SAR-muh) is a frost giant. The air around them is always cold, and frost forms on whatever they lean against.

## Manner

Sarma is harsh and analytical, and cold in every sense. They have no patience for nonsense and do not offer any.

Of all the titans, Sarma takes the suggestion that the titans were given their power the worst, and answers it with arguments rather than temper.

> *Sarma read the outline of this book and sent it back with a figure written beside every chapter, in a small square hand. Each figure was the number of claims in that chapter Sarma could disprove. The highest was beside the chapter on the titans.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** the [[Cloak of Frost]], which Sarma made.

**Source:** [[Kleipur]], given, although Sarma disputes how much.

## Goals

Sarma wants things done correctly and the record kept straight, and the record they have in mind is their own.

## Design Notes

A party that wants an honest answer and can stand to hear it should ask Sarma. They will be right, and they will not be kind about it.

Pirate Queen.

Race: [[Elves]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Chore (KOR-ay) is an elf who has spent her life at sea and looks it. She carries [[The Siren Blade]] at her hip, and it can be heard humming when she is close.

## Manner

Chore is a strong leader and her crews follow her without being made to. She decides quickly and usually well. Afterwards, when there is time to think, she sometimes stops trusting the decision, and the doubt can get the better of her before the next one is needed.

She wants freedom more than anything and found it at sea. In her case the god of pirates is the god of not being told where to go.

She tries to hide how much she cares for her friends and does it badly. She will protect them at any cost.

> *While I was aboard, Chore ordered a change of course and then came below to ask me whether I thought it was the right one. I said I was no sailor. She said that was why she was asking. When we went back up the crew had already made the change, and she stood at the rail watching them for some time and did not change it back.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** [[The Siren Blade]], which sings to her allies and shrieks at her enemies.

**Source:** deep powers of the sea.

Chore would not be left behind by the other three. She went looking for power at sea and kept company with some of the deep powers there for a while, and with help from [[Cleome]] she went up after them.

## Goals

Chore wants the sea kept open, to her and to anyone who would rather be on it than ashore. Any power that tries to fence it in or own it has her attention.

She wants her friends safe, and she will take on more than she should to see to it.

## What She Asks

A pirate of Chore keeps faith with the crew and keeps moving. She asks for loyalty to the people beside you and for nothing to be allowed to tie you down.

## Relationships

- In a relationship with [[Kalinekra]]
- Friend of [[Cleome]], [[Ignatius]] and [[Nausicaä]]

## Design Notes

Chore is sure in the moment and unsure afterwards. A party sailing with her gets a confident leader in a crisis and a worried one in the quiet hours, and both are useful to play.

Kalinekra cuts against the freedom. A banished fey lord who raises drowned soldiers is a difficult partner for a god who will not be held, and the relationship is a place a DM can press.

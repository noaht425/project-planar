The Phoenix King. Stole the idea of phoenixes from [[Moto]].

Child of [[Pyrrha]] and [[Lux]].

Eighth king of the [[Kingdom of Geno]], from 405 AM until he abdicated in 419 AM. Killed [[Genasi VII]], believing that the king of Geno should be a descendant of dragons because of how much dragons helped Genasi.

Wields the [[Hell's Fire Staff]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]], [[Iudis]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Iras (EE-rahs) is the son of a dragon and an archdevil and shows both. He has his father's height and his father's gold, and he burns at the edges the way his mother does. The fire on him does not go out.

## Manner

Iras calls himself the Phoenix King. The phoenix was [[Moto]]'s before it was his, and Moto has never forgiven the theft.

He took the throne of Geno in 405 AM by killing [[Genasi VII]], on the principle that Geno owed its existence to the dragons and should be ruled by a dragon's descendant. He defaced the twin statues of [[Genasi]] at the city gate the same year. He held the throne for fourteen years, gave it up, and has never said why.

Everyone he kills with the [[Hell's Fire Staff]] goes to his father in [[Pride]].

His mother [[Pyrrha]] has never visited him. His father [[Lux]] took all of his other descendants into Pride and left Iras where he was.

> *The statue at the main gate of Geno that is meant to be Iras was a statue of Genasi until Gaius Flaminius had it altered, and he did not have much altered. The face is new. The hands are still Genasi's, and somebody keeps them polished. When I mentioned the statue to Iras, he said he had never been back to see it.*

**Source:** unwritten.

## Goals

Iras wants to stay alive. [[Genasi]] intends to kill him and is trying to bring Moto into it, and Lux intends to prevent it.

## Relationships

- Son of [[Pyrrha]] and [[Lux]]
- Killed [[Genasi VII]]
- Hated by [[Moto]]
- Hunted by [[Genasi]]

## Design Notes

Iras is the god with the most enemies and the least explanation. He took a throne on a principle, gave it up without one, and has a progenitor and a titan coming for him and an archdevil standing in the way.

The abdication is the hole worth leaving. Whatever made him give up Geno is the thing a party would have to find out to understand him.

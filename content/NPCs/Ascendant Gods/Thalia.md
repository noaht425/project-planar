Goddess of Song. Given power by [[Atris]] (former lovers). Atris gave her power so she wouldn't die.

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Race: [[Earthborn]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Thalia (THAH-lee-uh) is earthborn and carries the lyre [[Atris]] gave her.

## Manner

Thalia is the goddess of song, and she became one because Atris would not let her die. The two were lovers. Atris gave her the power freely, which no archfey has done for any other mortal.

She is easy company and she sings without being asked. She will not talk about Atris, and a great many of her songs are about her.

> *Thalia sang for me while I was writing up my notes on Hunt, without being asked. It was a song from Atris's lodge, and halfway through she stopped, said she had forgotten the rest, and started another. That night I heard her finish it, alone, from the next room.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** [[Thalia's Lyre]], given to her by Atris.

**Source:** [[Atris]], given rather than taken, so that she would not die.

## Goals

What Thalia wants is mostly unwritten. She has not asked Atris for anything since the gift.

## Relationships

- Former lover of [[Atris]]

## Design Notes

Thalia's power was a gift, which makes her the gentlest way into the Hunt and to Atris. A party that befriends Thalia has a reason to be welcome at the lodge, and a reason to be careful there.

Atris is trying to get out of [[Ius Primum]], and Thalia is the person she is most likely to have told.

God of plagues, disease, pests, etc.

Appearance: wears a black cloak and a plague doctor mask.

Stole power from [[Grixt]] in his ascension.

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]], from [[Kingdom of Regno]]

## Appearance

Loime (LOY-muh) wears a black cloak and a plague doctor's mask and is not seen without either. A green spider a few inches across sits embedded in his chest, and it is alive.

## Manner

Loime did it alone, and he will tell anyone that before they have finished saying hello. He took his power from [[Grixt]] without being given it, turned down help when it was offered, and has never forgiven his brother [[Orcus]] for being given what Loime had to steal.

He is touchy and quick to correct people, and he is proud of every difficulty he can claim.

Whether the gem in his relic was really stolen is the one question he will not discuss calmly. [[Okzu]] offered him help and he refused it. He insists the gem was taken from the heart of Noxia without anyone's leave, and nobody has proved otherwise.

> *Loime corrected me twice before I had finished my first question. Nobody had helped him, and nobody had offered to. Then he told me, without being asked, that the gem had been stolen. Through the gap in his coat I could see the spider in his chest, green as glass, keeping very still.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** [[Pestilence]], a venomous spider fed a gem from the heart of the [[Elemental Plane of Noxia]]. It is the only living creature known to have served as a relic.

**Source:** [[Grixt]], taken rather than given.

## Goals

Loime wants to be acknowledged as a god who made himself, and above all he wants Orcus to acknowledge it.

He wants Orcus's plan to fail, and would enjoy being the reason.

## Relationships
- Younger brother of [[Orcus]]
- Hates Orcus for receiving special treatment from Grixt
- Turned himself into a god shortly after Orcus ascended. Was offered help by [[Okzu]] but turned it down as he wanted to do it on his own unlike Orcus.
- It is unknown if the gem used in ascension ceremony was given to him by Okzu or not but he insists he stole it.

Fed a venomous spider a gem from the heart of the [[Elemental Plane of Noxia]] and used this as ascension relic (see [[Pestilence]]). Only known use of a living creature as ascension relic. Currently embedded in his chest.

## Design Notes

The two brothers are one ascension done two ways. Orcus was handed his means and Loime stole his, from the same archfey, and each resents what the other's version says about his own.

The gem is the useful doubt. If Okzu gave it to him after all, Loime owes the dragon who keeps the largest ledger in Tarkis and has spent his whole godhood denying it.

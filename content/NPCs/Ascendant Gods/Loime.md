God of plagues, disease, pests, etc.

Appearance: wears a black cloak and a plague doctor mask.

Stole power from [[Grixt]] in his ascension.

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]], from [[Kingdom of Regno]]

## History
- 157 AM: Born in [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]], six years after his brother [[Orcus]]
- 63 Auctera, 189 AM: Orcus ascends
- 190 AM: Asks [[Grixt]] for the same help and is refused
- 21 Morcaela, 192 AM: Ascends, taking from Grixt rather than being given

## Appearance

Loime (LOY-muh) wears a black cloak and a plague doctor's mask and is not seen without either. A green spider a few inches across sits embedded in his chest, and it is alive.

## Manner

Loime did it alone, and he will tell anyone that before they have finished saying hello. He took his power from [[Grixt]] without being given it, turned down help when it was offered, and has never forgiven his brother [[Orcus]] for being given what Loime had to steal.

He is touchy and quick to correct people, and he is proud of every difficulty he can claim.

Whether the gem in his relic was really stolen is the one question he will not discuss calmly. [[Okzu]] offered him help and he refused it. He insists the gem was taken from the heart of Noxia without anyone's leave, and nobody has proved otherwise.

> *Loime corrected me twice before I had finished my first question. Nobody had helped him, and nobody had offered to. Then he told me, without being asked, that the gem had been stolen. Through the gap in his coat I could see the spider in his chest, green as glass, keeping very still.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** [[Pestilence]], a venomous spider fed a gem from the heart of the [[Elemental Plane of Noxia]]. It is the only living creature known to have served as a relic.

**Source:** [[Grixt]], taken rather than given.

Three years after his brother, Loime went to the same archfey and asked for the same help. [[Grixt]] refused him. What Grixt had given Orcus had been given once, as a particular arrangement with a particular man, and it was not on offer a second time.

So Loime took it instead. He is the only being on record to have taken anything from an archfey without a bargain, and he did it to the one who had made his brother a god. That is most of what anybody needs to know about him.

He hates Orcus for the help, not for the refusal. Orcus knows, and has made no attempt to mend it.

## Goals

Loime wants to be acknowledged as a god who made himself, and above all he wants Orcus to acknowledge it.

He wants Orcus's plan to fail, and would enjoy being the reason.

## Relationships
- Younger brother of [[Orcus]]
- Hates Orcus for receiving special treatment from Grixt
- Turned himself into a god shortly after Orcus ascended. Was offered help by [[Okzu]] but turned it down as he wanted to do it on his own unlike Orcus.
- It is unknown if the gem used in ascension ceremony was given to him by Okzu or not but he insists he stole it.

Fed a venomous spider a gem from the heart of the [[Elemental Plane of Noxia]] and used this as ascension relic (see [[Pestilence]]). Only known use of a living creature as ascension relic. Currently embedded in his chest.

## Design Notes

The two brothers are one ascension done two ways. Orcus was handed his means and Loime stole his, from the same archfey, and each resents what the other's version says about his own.

The gem is the useful doubt. If Okzu gave it to him after all, Loime owes the dragon who keeps the largest ledger in Tarkis and has spent his whole godhood denying it.

## Open

When he ascended is unwritten.

What Grixt thinks of having been robbed is unwritten.

Whether the gem came from Okzu is unresolved, and should stay that way.

### Traits

**Toxin of Life.** When you would be dealt poison damage, you heal that much instead.

**Venomous Strikes.** All weapon attacks and unarmed attacks you make deal an extra 3d8 of poison damage. This damage ignores resistances.

**Miasma.** All creatures within 10 feet of Loime cannot regain hitpoints and take 2d6 poison damage at the start of each of their turns. Any creature that takes this damage 3 turns in a row must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or receive a level of exhaustion (use 2024)

Actions

**Multiattack.** Loime makes 3 Poisoned Blade attacks.

**Poisoned Blade.** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d6+4) slashing damage plus 14 (3d8) poison damage

**Poisonous Presence.** As an action, you begin to radiate an aura that has a radius of 20 ft. This aura lasts for 1 minute. If the effect requires a saving throw, the difficulty is equal to 16. When you do, select one of the following options:

- All hostile creatures within the aura can make either an action or a bonus action on their turn but not both. Additionally, hostile creatures within the aura can make a maximum of two attacks on their turn.
- When a hostile creature enters the aura for the first time on its turn or starts its turn in the aura, it must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure it take 4d8 poison damage and cannot regain hitpoints until the start of its next turn. On a success it takes half as much damage and can heal as normal.
- All creatures within the aura treat resistances as vulnerabilities and all vulnerabilities as resistances.
- When a creature starts its turn within the aura, roll 1d6 (1:STR, 2:DEX, 3:CON, 4:INT, 5:WIS, 6:CHA). That creature must make a saving throw of that stat. If it succeeds, until the start of your next turn it receives advantage on all saving throws of that type but disadvantage on all others. If it fails, the opposite happens.

Once you use this feature, you may not do so again until you take a long rest. Alternatively you may choose to reduce your maximum hitpoints by an amount equal to 8 times your constitution modifier (minimum of 8); when you do so you regain a use of this feature. This reduction lasts for 1d4 days.

### Legendary Actions

Loime can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Loime regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Slash (Costs 1 Action).** Loime makes one Poisoned Blade attack.

**Spread (Costs 1 Action).** One creature within 30 feet of Loime must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or take 14 (4d6) poison damage and be unable to regain hit points until the end of its next turn.

**Advance (Costs 2 Actions).** Loime moves up to his speed without provoking opportunity attacks, then makes one Poisoned Blade attack.

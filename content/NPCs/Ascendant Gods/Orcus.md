Domain: Death

Stole power from [[The King of Night]]. Created the [[Throne of Death]] where he could rule. Only mortal to successfully steal from Day/Night.

Brother of [[Loime]].

Race: [[Humans]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]], from [[Kingdom of Regno]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Orcus (OR-kus) was a man of [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]] and still looks like one: heavy-set, plainly dressed, and nothing about him suggests the throne he sits on.

## Manner

Orcus took the domain of death from [[The King of Night]] and has ruled the dead from the [[Throne of Death]] ever since. He is patient and practical, and he thinks of the dead as a population to be governed. By most accounts he governs them well.

He believes the theft was his own idea. [[Grixt]] gave him the means, and Orcus regards Grixt as an early backer rather than as the author of anything.

He is recruiting for a second move against Night, and he chooses his people by their grievances. [[Charis]] and [[Boreas]] have reasons to want [[Afthor]] punished, and [[Belphegor]] blames Night for the Division. Orcus does not need them to share his aim, only to want something the plan will give them.

His younger brother [[Loime]] hates him for the help Grixt gave him. Orcus knows, and has made no attempt to mend it.

> *Orcus asked me, before anything else, what I thought of the King of Night. I gave a careful answer about the state of the records. He listened to all of it, and then asked whether I could get a letter into the dark half of Nullis. When I said that nobody could, he thanked me and asked who would be willing to try.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** the [[Throne of Death]]. He built it as the channel for his ascension and has ruled the dead from it since.

**Source:** [[The King of Night]], stolen from with means supplied by [[Grixt]]. He is the only mortal to have stolen successfully from either King.

## Goals

Orcus wants the Kings of day and night brought down and believes the plan to do it is his.

He wants the dead kept in order, and he has managed it so far.

## Relationships

- Older brother of [[Loime]]
- Given the means to ascend by [[Grixt]]
- Recruiting [[Charis]], [[Belphegor]] and [[Boreas]]

## Design Notes

Orcus is Grixt's best work: a capable mortal who thinks the conspiracy is his, recruiting allies who think they are using him. A party can deal with Orcus for a long time without anyone in the room knowing who started it.

The [[Throne of Death]] is the object on this page a party is most likely to want and least likely to survive using. Its teleport claims the soul of anyone not attuned to it.

## Open

When he ascended is unwritten. What the next stage of the plan requires is open on [[Grixt]]'s page.


## Statblock

*Medium humanoid, lawful neutral*

Armor Class 20

**Hit Points.** 345 (30d10+180)

**Speed.** 25 ft.

**Saving Throws.** CON +12, WIS +12, CHA +10

**Damage Resistances.** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

**Damage Immunities.** necrotic, poison

**Condition Immunities.** charmed, frightened, paralyzed

**Senses.** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16

**Languages.** Common. Orcus understands any language a dead creature spoke in life.

**Challenge.** 20 (25,000 XP)

**Proficiency Bonus.** +6

### Traits

**Sovereign of the Dead.** An undead creature cannot willingly attack Orcus or take an action that would harm him. An undead creature that starts its turn within 60 feet of Orcus obeys his commands for that turn unless it succeeds on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw, and a creature that succeeds is immune to this trait for 24 hours.

**Claim the Soul.** When a creature Orcus can see within 60 feet of him drops to 0 hit points, that creature must succeed on a DC 20 Charisma saving throw or have its soul claimed. A creature whose soul is claimed cannot regain hit points, and no magic can restore it to life while the claim holds. The claim ends if Orcus dies, if he releases it, or if a creature uses an action to make a successful DC 20 Charisma check against him within 30 feet.

**The Governed.** A creature that dies while its soul is claimed rises at the start of Orcus's next turn as an undead under his control. It keeps its own statistics, acts on his initiative, and cannot be restored to life until the claim is broken. It drops where it stands if Orcus dies.

**Deathless Tally.** When an undead creature under Orcus's control is destroyed within 60 feet of him, he regains 10 hit points.

Actions

**Multiattack.** Orcus makes two Scepter attacks, or one Scepter attack and uses Danse Macabre.

**Scepter.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 22 (4d10) necrotic damage. A creature that takes the necrotic damage cannot regain hit points until the start of Orcus's next turn.

**Danse Macabre (3/Day).** Orcus raises up to five corpses or piles of bones within 60 feet of him. Each rises as a skeleton or a zombie under his control, acts on his initiative, and crumbles after one hour. While Orcus is seated on the [[Throne of Death]] he can use this action without limit and the risen do not crumble.

**Decree (Recharge 5-6).** Orcus names each creature he chooses within 60 feet of him. Each must make a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure a creature takes 33 (6d10) necrotic damage and is counted among the dead until the end of its next turn, during which it cannot regain hit points and undead have advantage on attack rolls against it. On a success it takes half as much damage and is not counted among the dead.

### Reactions

**Replace the Fallen.** When an undead creature under Orcus's control is destroyed, he raises one corpse within 30 feet of it as a zombie under his control.

### Legendary Actions

Orcus can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Orcus regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Strike (Costs 1 Action).** Orcus makes one Scepter attack.

**Order (Costs 1 Action).** One undead creature under Orcus's control moves up to its speed and makes one attack.

**Call the Claim (Costs 2 Actions).** One creature within 60 feet whose soul Orcus has claimed takes 17 (5d6) necrotic damage, and its speed is 0 until the end of its next turn.

### Lair Actions

These apply only where the [[Throne of Death]] stands. Without the throne, use the block above as it is. On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Orcus takes one lair action.

**The Floor Gives Up Its Dead.** Five skeletons rise in unoccupied spaces within 60 feet of the throne, under Orcus's control, and act on his initiative.

**Aura of Dominion.** Each undead creature within 100 feet of the throne that is not under Orcus's control must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or kneel, counting as prone until it repeats the save at the start of its turn and succeeds.

**Throne-Step.** Orcus teleports to an unoccupied space he can see, then makes one Scepter attack.

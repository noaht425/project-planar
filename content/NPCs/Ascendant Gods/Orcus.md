Domain: Death

Stole power from [[The King of Night]]. Created the [[Throne of Death]] where he could rule. Only mortal to successfully steal from Day/Night.

Brother of [[Loime]].

Race: [[Humans]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]], from [[Kingdom of Regno]]

Member of the [[Ascendant Gods]].

## Appearance

Orcus (OR-kus) was a man of [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]] and still looks like one: heavy-set, plainly dressed, and nothing about him suggests the throne he sits on.

## Manner

Orcus took the domain of death from [[The King of Night]] and has ruled the dead from the [[Throne of Death]] ever since. He is patient and practical, and he thinks of the dead as a population to be governed. By most accounts he governs them well.

He believes the theft was his own idea. [[Grixt]] gave him the means, and Orcus regards Grixt as an early backer rather than as the author of anything.

He is recruiting for a second move against Night, and he chooses his people by their grievances. [[Charis]] and [[Boreas]] have reasons to want [[Afthor]] punished, and [[Belphegor]] blames Night for the Division. Orcus does not need them to share his aim, only to want something the plan will give them.

His younger brother [[Loime]] hates him for the help Grixt gave him. Orcus knows, and has made no attempt to mend it.

> *Orcus asked me, before anything else, what I thought of the King of Night. I gave a careful answer about the state of the records. He listened to all of it, and then asked whether I could get a letter into the dark half of Nullis. When I said that nobody could, he thanked me and asked who could.*

## Ascension

**Relic:** unwritten.

**Source:** [[The King of Night]], stolen from with means supplied by [[Grixt]]. He is the only mortal to have stolen successfully from either King.

## Goals

Orcus wants the Kings of day and night brought down and believes the plan to do it is his.

He wants the dead kept in order, and he has managed it so far.

## Relationships

- Older brother of [[Loime]]
- Given the means to ascend by [[Grixt]]
- Recruiting [[Charis]], [[Belphegor]] and [[Boreas]]

## Design Notes

Orcus is Grixt's best work: a capable mortal who thinks the conspiracy is his, recruiting allies who think they are using him. A party can deal with Orcus for a long time without anyone in the room knowing who started it.

The [[Throne of Death]] is the object on this page a party is most likely to want and least likely to survive using. Its teleport claims the soul of anyone not attuned to it.

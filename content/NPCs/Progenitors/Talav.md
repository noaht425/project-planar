Progenitor of the [[Earthborn]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Present at the [[Council of Creation]]

## Appearance

Talav (TAH-lav) is small and round and comfortable, and looks like the kind of earthborn grandmother who has a kettle on.

## Manner

Talav is warm and nurturing. She made the [[Earthborn]] and she treats anyone who comes to her door as one of them.

> *Talav fed me before she would answer anything, and then fed me again before she would answer anything else. I left with most of my questions unasked and a basket of food for the road that lasted me most of the way to Lonvil.*

## Relationships

- Made the [[Earthborn]]

## Design Notes

Talav is the safest door any party will knock on. She is the Progenitor to use when a party needs somewhere to rest and someone who will not ask for anything back.

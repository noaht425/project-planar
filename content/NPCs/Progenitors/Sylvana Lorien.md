Progenitor of the [[Elves]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Present at the [[Council of Creation]].

Member of the [[Progenitors]].

## Appearance

Sylvana Lorien (sil-VAH-nuh LOR-ee-en) looks like an elf, because the elves were made to look like her.

## Manner

Sylvana is very sure of herself, and she is sure in a different way from [[Genasi]] or [[Numen]]. Their pride is in themselves. Hers is in the elves. She thinks very highly of her people and will not hear a word against them from anyone.

> *I mentioned to Sylvana Lorien, in passing, that the elves of Thalanea are not always easy company. She corrected me at length, with examples. Then she asked for the name of the elf I had found difficult, so that she could find out what I had done to him.*

## The Elves and Ius Primum

Sylvana made the [[Elves]] at the [[Council of Creation]], with secret help from [[Atris]] and [[Hakea]]. The price was [[Ius Primum]], which lets any archfey claim any elf. She signed it on 38 Aucaela, 1 AM.

The elves do not know the terms. Sylvana does, and she has never told them.

## Relationships

- Made the [[Elves]]
- Signatory of [[Ius Primum]] with [[Atris]] and [[Hakea]]

## Design Notes

She will not accept a word against her people, and she is the one who sold the right to take them. Both are true, and she has had seven hundred years to reconcile them. A party that puts the contract in front of her is asking her to choose which one she is.

## Open

Whether she regrets Ius Primum, and what she would do if the elves found out, is unwritten.

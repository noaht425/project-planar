Progenitor of the [[Giantkin]].

Race: Giant

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Present at the [[Council of Creation]].

Member of the [[Progenitors]].

[[timeline: 98 Aucaela, 1 AM: gifts power to the titans]]

## Appearance

Kleipur (KLY-pur) is a giant, the largest of the nine, and he was made first and out of more material than any of the others.

## Manner

Kleipur is very proud of the titans and has no chill about it at all. He follows everything they do, tells anyone who will listen, and takes any slight against one of them personally and loudly. He has been asked to leave more than one contest for shouting at whoever was judging it.

He is excitable and warm, and he does not seem to have noticed that the titans would rather he did not mention where their power came from.

> *Kleipur took me to see the Valley of the Colossi and spent the whole walk telling me what each of the titans had done lately. At Moto's statue he stopped, told the mason who was cleaning it that the nose was wrong, and was still arguing about the nose when the mason's foreman asked him to leave.*

## The Giantkin and the Titans

Kleipur made the [[Giantkin]] at the [[Council of Creation]]. On 98 Aucaela, 1 AM, he gave six of the first giantkin the power that made them the titans. See [[Ascendant Gods]].

The titans regard themselves as the original gods and do not like being reminded that they were handed their ascension. Kleipur reminds them constantly, because he is proud of it.

## Relationships

- Gave the titans their power: [[Afthor]], [[Gallum]], [[Kodren]], [[Moto]], [[Sarma]] and [[Vato]]

## Design Notes

Kleipur is the proud parent, and his pride is the titans' embarrassment. A party that wants a titan's attention can get it quickly by being seen with Kleipur.

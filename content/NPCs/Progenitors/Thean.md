Progenitor of [[Omens]]

Created from the small pieces left over when [[The Division]] split the [[The Judgements|Judgements]], mixed with everything else that formed the mortal realm.

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Present at the [[Council of Creation]]

## Appearance

Thean (THEE-an) is the most beautiful of the nine. Two horns curl back from her head until they almost close into a halo, and her eyes are pure gold speckled with black stars.

## Manner

Thean made the [[Omens]] from the small pieces left over when the Judgements were split, mixed with everything else that went into the mortal realm. She is very emotional, and she feels the pain of her people as if it were her own.

[[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] has had a great deal of pain to feel. She is hiding somewhere at present, and those who know her say she is in despair over what has become of the kingdom.

> *Thean has not been seen in Krotaphos for some time, and nobody in Panthea would tell me where she had gone. A priest on Temple Hill told me she had come there once, at night, after the arrests began, and wept in each of the temples in turn. The floor of the last one is still damp.*

## Relationships

- Made the [[Omens]]

## Design Notes

Thean is the Progenitor in mourning. The Omens' misfortune is hers, and a party that does anything for Krotaphos is doing it for her as well, whether or not she ever hears of it.

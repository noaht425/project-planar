Progenitor of the [[Orcs]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Present at the [[Council of Creation]]

## Appearance

Shram (SHRAM) is an orc, old and broad, and could be taken for any grandfather on a bench.

## Manner

Shram keeps to himself and would mostly like to be left alone. He is kindly when he is found, and he is not easy to find.

He enjoys games of strategy. Anyone who finds him and beats him at one is rewarded, and he does not say in advance with what.

> *It took me most of a season to find Shram. He was playing a board game against himself, and he offered me the other side. I lost quickly. He set the pieces up again, showed me where I had gone wrong, and then asked very politely whether I would mind leaving him to finish the game he had been playing before I arrived.*

## Relationships

- Made the [[Orcs]]

## Design Notes

Shram is a quest with a game at the end of it. Finding him is the first problem, beating him is the second, and the reward should be worth both.

## Open

Where he can be found, and what he gives a winner, is unwritten.

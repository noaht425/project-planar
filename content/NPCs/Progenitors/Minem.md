Progenitor of [[Humans]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Present at the [[Council of Creation]]

## Appearance

Minem (MIH-nem) looks like a human, and he made the humans to look like him.

## Manner

Minem formed the humans out of clay and breathed life into them. He was given nothing at his creation to work with, which the other Progenitors were, and he made his people and their language from nothing.

He is proud of them and rarely says so, or anything else. He answers most questions with a nod, a look or nothing at all.

He built [[Torren (Old Common)|Torren]] for the humans with two registers, one plain and one for law, record and reckoning. He wrote down what he had settled on and then left it alone, and Torren drifted into Common without him.

> *Minem answered my questions with a nod or a shake of the head for most of an afternoon. When I asked whether he was proud of the humans, he said yes, in the register Torren keeps for law and record, which is the one you use when you intend to be held to it.*

## Relationships

- Made the [[Humans]]

## Design Notes

Minem is the Progenitor who said least and built the language everyone speaks. A party that gets more than a word out of him has done something.

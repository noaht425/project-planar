Progenitor of the [[Beastfolk]]

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Present at the [[Council of Creation]]

Aided by dragons at Council of Creation

## Appearance

Numen (NOO-men) is a chimera of everything he made: a lion's mane, a bat's wings, a tail, and claws.

## Manner

Numen is proud in the way [[Genasi]] is, and he has more to show for it. He made more kinds of people than any other Progenitor, with help from the [[Dragons]], and he regards every one of them as a success.

> *Numen told me, before I had sat down, that he had made more kinds of people than any other Progenitor and that he had done it better. He then went through them one kind at a time, with what each was for, and I could not find anything wrong with the list.*

## Relationships

- Made the [[Beastfolk]], with help from the [[Dragons]]

## Design Notes

Numen's arrogance is earned, which makes him harder to deal with than Genasi. He cannot be embarrassed by facts.

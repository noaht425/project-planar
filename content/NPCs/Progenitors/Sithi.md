Progenitor of the [[Fae]]. Lost name to [[Archfey]] during [[Council of Creation]].

Home Realm: [[Arenis]]

Present at the Council of Creation.

Member of the [[Progenitors]].

## Manner

Sithi Yumboe (SITH-ee YUM-boh-eh) made the [[Fae]] with the help of the [[Archfey]], and paid them with her name. Nobody has seen her since.

Nobody knows where she is, or whether she is still alive.

> *The records of the Council of Creation list the fae under Sithi Yumboe and then leave a space after it, the width of another name. Every copy I have seen has the space, including copies made long after anyone could have known to leave it.*

## Relationships

- Made the [[Fae]]
- Gave her name to the [[Archfey]]

## Design Notes

Sithi is the absent Progenitor. A missing maker who paid with her name is a mystery a fae character can carry, and the archfey who hold her name are the obvious place to start looking.

## Open

Where she went, and whether she is alive, is unwritten.

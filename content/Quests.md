Kraken Snatcher
- Given by [[Korik]]
- Objective: obtain a kraken egg currently being held by [[Kalinekra]] from her lair and bring it back to Korik. The egg is worth ~60,000 gp. Korik will offer 30,000 gp or an egg of equal value.

The Fire Queen's Brood
- Given by [[Pyrrha]]
- Objective: Trappers keep stealing the fire queen's eggs as she has too many to keep track of. Help her collect ones that have been stolen. 10,000 gp each

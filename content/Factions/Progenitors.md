The nine Progenitors were born out of [[The Planar Collision]] into the new mortal realm of [[Arenis]]. Each of them wanted company, and each made a people. They did it together, at the [[Council of Creation]], between 1 Aucaela and 1 Auctera of 1 AM.

Some had help, and help had a price.

Half-species were made by none of them.

> *I have now met seven of the nine. Every one of them wanted to know how their people were getting on, and Minem managed to ask it without saying anything.*

## Design Notes

The Progenitors are the parents of the mortal world, and most of them are still alive and still invested. Any player character has a maker who can be found, and most of them will have opinions about how that character is doing.

## Open

What the Progenitors were before the Council, and how they came out of the Collision, is unwritten.

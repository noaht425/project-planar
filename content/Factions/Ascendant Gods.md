Mortals who found a source of power, obtained a relic, and channelled one through the other until it made them gods. See [[Ascension Process]].

They are the newest powers in Agra and the least constrained. The archfey have been making bargains since before mortals existed and their terms are old and careful. An ascendant is working from a much shorter memory.

Every one of them was a person with a family, a history and a reason. Most of them have outlived all of it. They have been gods long enough now that the people who knew them as mortals are gone.

## Design Notes

[[Ignatius]], [[Cleome]], [[Chore]], [[Hieronymous]] and [[Nausicaä]] are carried over from the Titan campaign, where they were player characters. They are here as references for that group, for whenever they reach this world.

Their lives in Agra rhyme with their lives in Titan rather than matching them. Nothing from that campaign is automatically canon here, and details from it should not be imported into Agra without deciding to. Their mortal families are gone.

The ascendants are the one group of powers a party could realistically join. The process is written down, it is not forbidden, and the only thing stopping anyone is that most attempts kill the person making them.

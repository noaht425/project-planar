The four rulers of the quarters of [[Ventis]].

| Wind | Quarter | Temperament |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Boreas]] | North | Cold and harsh |
| [[Eurus]] | East | Warm and bright |
| [[Notus]] | South | Flippant and changeable |
| [[Zephyros]] | West | Shy and hard to read |

The four quarters bleed into one another so heavily that a traveller usually cannot say where one ends, and the four winds are permanently in each other's business because of it.

## The Plan

All four intend to kill [[Afthor]] and take the [[Elemental Plane of Air]] for themselves. They share one plan, and they have four different reasons for it. Boreas wants Afthor punished and holds the plan together. Eurus wants the plane. Notus's commitment varies by the day. Zephyros has never spoken for it or against it, and Boreas has taken the silence as agreement.

The [[Dragons]] want the same realm back for themselves, and the two plans have not yet met.

## Iris

Two of the winds are tied to the children of [[King Thera]]. Boreas is betrothed to [[Princess Verna]], who wants out. Eurus is courting [[Princess Esta]] and has not told her about the plan.

> *I spent a week in Ventis without ever being sure whose quarter I was in. My hosts were sure, and they told me each morning, and each morning it was a different wind's, although I had not moved.*

## Design Notes

The plan is the winds' group identity, and it can be broken through any one of them. Boreas has a grievance, Eurus has an engagement, Zephyros has never agreed, and Notus cannot be relied on.

A warlock of the winds gets all four, because the quarters bleed together. See [[Warlock Wind]].

## Open

What the winds would do with the plane of air once they had it, and how they would divide it, is unwritten.

The six Original Judgements: [[Aaranea]], [[Baator]], [[Charios]], [[Hesit]], [[Sylas]] and [[Themati]]. Created around 10,000 AF. Each held two halves, which [[The King of Night]] split apart in [[The Division]] (3,097 AF) to become an archangel and an archdevil.

The Judgements spoke [[Old Iudimian]], in which a Judgement is called Xāl. They were obsessive about sorting everything they ruled, and that sorting runs through their language. They were powerful enough to shape the language itself: their name became Xa-, the Old Iudimian mark of negation.

The Judgements avoided the number two.

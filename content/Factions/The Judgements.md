The six Original Judgements: [[Aaranea]], [[Baator]], [[Charios]], [[Hesit]], [[Sylas]] and [[Themati]]. They were created around 10,000 AF, in the same period as [[The King of Day]], [[The King of Night]] and the five dragon lords of [[Tarkis]]. Each of them held two halves. The King of Night split those apart in [[The Division]] in 3,097 AF and made an archangel and an archdevil out of every one.

What follows was true of all six. The six pages carry only what was particular to each.

## What They Held

A Judgement was an act together with the measure of that act, in one being. None of the twelve can finish its act now.

| Judgement | Act | One half took | The other took |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Baator]], The Xenia | To host | [[Belphegor]], the serving | [[Blythe]], the deciding |
| [[Hesit]], The Empath | To feel what another feels | [[Abaddon]], where a wound lands | [[Neema]], what to do about it |
| [[Charios]], The Entertainer | To entertain | [[Lux]], the performance | [[Pavlas]], the audience |
| [[Themati]], The King of Kings | To rule | [[Archon]], the appetite | [[Themis]], the obligation |
| [[Aaranea]], The Lover | To love | [[Lilien]], the wanting | [[Gwenhael]], the giving up |
| [[Sylas]], The Poet | To compose verse | [[Tiyanak]], the making | [[Verita]], the subject |

[[Old Iudimian]] still carries the undivided version. Each Judgement left the language a verb for its own act, along with a pair of domain nouns, one Angelic and one Demonic. Those twelve nouns are the month names of the [[Kingdom of Krotaphos Calendar]].

## The Dead They Judged

[[Arenis]] was made in 10 AF and the nine peoples at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM, so no mortal died while any Judgement was alive. The dead of the outer rings did. Elementals, the fae of [[Iris]], the lesser angels and devils of [[Iudis]] itself, anything in [[Tarkis]] or [[Ventis]] that could die at all. All of it arrived in [[Liminia]], which is why Liminia sits at the centre of the realm with the six places branching out of it.

Every soul was heard by all six at once. There was no appeal and no division of the work. Each of the six had a part that came out of what it was.

| Judgement | Part in the hearing |
| --- | --- |
| [[Baator]] | Received the soul and opened with what it should be given |
| [[Hesit]] | Felt the life through and reported it |
| [[Aaranea]] | Argued for the soul |
| [[Sylas]] | Composed the verdict and kept the record |
| [[Charios]] | Delivered it to the dead already in the realm |
| [[Themati]] | Convened the hearing and enforced the result |

Nothing settled a hearing except endurance. A disagreement ran until one side gave way, and the longest of them went on for centuries with the soul waiting in Liminia. The queue was long before any argument started, because [[Hesit]] could only feel one life at a time and a life took as long to feel as it had taken to live.

[[Bis]] and [[Seisear]] were built the other way round, to answer at once and alone. That was the term both sides would sign at the end of the [[Divine War]]. See [[Nekerux]].

## Six and Two

Six is the base of the Old Iudimian number system, one place for each Judgement. Two is Dỳx, which shares a root with Dỳxa, torment, and is said only of exactly two things. Every compound number says a 2 as Hīnatirse.

Each of the six was two and knew it. The number they would not count described what could be done to them. The twelve go on avoiding it for the opposite reason.

A Judgement was Xāl. The six were powerful enough to work on the language itself, and their name became Xa-, the mark of negation, so a denial in Old Iudimian opens with them.

> *In Old Iudimian the mark of negation is Xa-, and it is the Judgements' own name worn down to a prefix. A temple reader in Panthea took me through the derivation, and then through what it means for the language. Their name sits in the middle of every denial anyone has made in it for ten thousand years. He told me that nobody notices. He had noticed.*

## The Division

[[Indri]] worked on the King of Night from about 4,500 AF, telling him the Judgements were too powerful undivided. Toward the end he wore [[The King of Day]]'s face to finish the job. His own reason was that he hated sharing a domain with [[Sylas]]. Each Judgement's place split along with it, into a heaven and a hell. The small pieces left over went into the making of Arenis, and [[Thean]] made the [[Omens]] out of them much later. See [[The Division]].

## What Is Left of Them

Nothing worships the six. [[Panthea]] keeps twelve temples on Temple Hill, one to each half, standing in facing pairs. There is no temple to a Judgement.

What survives in [[Kingdom of Krotaphos|Krotaphos]] is practice without belief. The week runs six days named for the six. A household will not seat two at a table or hand over two of anything, and a Krotaphian asked why will say that it is unlucky. The reason is in the grammar, which almost nobody has read.

[[Belphegor]] intends to have each archdevil eat its archangel. Both the power and the identity split in the Division, so a half that consumes its other half properly takes the whole of what the Judgement had, the full strength along with the domain, and keeps its own mind inside it. Belphegor is offering the other five archdevils exactly that. He describes it as making the Judgements whole, and what he wants out of it is the control.

## Design Notes

The six individual pages are written against this one. Anything that governed all six sits here, so a reader meets a Judgement's own material on its own page and nothing else.

Each Judgement's part in the hearing comes out of its own verb, so the six pages hold together without being built on one template.

Judgement by endurance is what makes the Nekerukes a settlement. Two made beings who answer immediately was the only arrangement either side wanted once the war was over, and neither side had to concede anything it still valued.

Giving the six the dead of the outer rings keeps Iudis populated for seven thousand years without putting mortals in it early. A party that asks a soul in a hell how long it has been there can get an answer older than Arenis.

Belphegor's plan works as he describes the mechanism. His account of the result is a pitch to the other five.

## Open

- Whether any soul is still waiting on a hearing that deadlocked before the Division is unwritten. Liminia would be where it waited.
- Whether the six ever acted outside Iudis is mostly unwritten. The [[Records of the Void]] has them giving hands to [[Brilis]], and that is the only record of it.
- Old Iudimian has no word for the hearing itself, or for the body of six that held it.

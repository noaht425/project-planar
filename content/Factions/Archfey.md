Thera's children are [[Prince Ivan]] (spring), [[Princess Esta]] (summer), [[Princeps Autuun]] (autumn) and [[Princess Verna]] (winter). Counted with them, there are eleven archfey.

For how names, bargains and debts work among them, see [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]].

## Court

Thera's court at Rothánea is the only neutral ground in the fey realms, and it is where the archfey bring their quarrels. Thera is the one archfey most likely to grant a mortal an audience.

The court has two exclusions. [[Alsa]] and [[Grixt]] are kept out of court society for not being pleasant to look at. Neither has lost any territory over it, and Grixt has never once raised it.

The court also has two secrets it does not know about. [[Atris]] and [[Hakea]] signed [[Ius Primum]] without it, and the Indri who sits with them has been [[Tiyanak]] since 3,096 AF.

## What They Hold Together

The archfey helped make the [[Fae]], and took [[Sithi]]'s name as the price. They still hold it.

No archfey breaks a bargain. Several of them will keep one in a way the other party did not expect.

> *At the one court session I attended in Rothánea, seven chairs were set out for the domain rulers and five were used. Alsa and Grixt had not been invited. Nobody mentioned it, and the two empty chairs were taken away before the session began, which I understood to be the custom.*

## Design Notes

The archfey are the most common patrons and the easiest powers to reach, and the court is how a party meets several of them at once. What the court does not know about its own members is most of the plot in Iris.

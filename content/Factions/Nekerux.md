The Nekerux (NEK-eh-rooks), plural Nekerukes, are the two beings who judge the dead. [[Bis]] and [[Seisear]].

Created after the [[Divine War]]. The war had been fought between the [[Archangels|archangels]] and the [[Archdevils|archdevils]] over who ruled the Judgements, and neither side would accept the other holding the sorting of souls at the end of it. Handing it to two made beings, answerable to nobody, was the term both sides could sign.

None of the twelve can overturn a Nekerux decision. Several of them have tried.

Both live in [[Liminia]], which lies between the heavens and the hells inside [[Iudis]].

## How a Soul Is Sorted

Two decisions, in order.

[[Bis]] decides whether a soul is good or bad overall, which settles the side.

[[Seisear]] decides which of the six portions it goes to, which settles the domain. Each of the six portions pairs a heaven with the hell most like it, so between the two of them the twelve destinations are covered.

A soul meets Bis first. Seisear's decision is quick. Bis's is not.

> *I had the order wrong for most of a year, because every account I collected from the dead described the dog first. The dog is the one people remember. Six heads, one question, done in a moment, and then you go. The serpent came before it, and the only thing anyone could tell me about the serpent was that nobody had said they were allowed to sit down.*

## Design Notes

Because the twelve do not judge, the afterlives stop short of being a straightforward tyranny. The archangels and archdevils rule their realms and run them as they please, and not one of them chooses who arrives.

The decision is split in two, side and then domain, so a party arguing about a dead friend knows which of the two to go to and what that one can actually change.

Both numbers are an insult. The twelve avoid the number two at all costs, out of hatred for what they used to be, and the being that decides the side has two heads. The being that decides the domain has six, which is the number of Judgements there were.

Made by [[Sylvana Lorien]] at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM. Sylvana Lorien no longer has a name of her own. It was given to the Archfey, and the Archfey still have it.

[[Atris]] and [[Hakea]] secretly helped make the elves. Their price was [[Ius Primum]], which lets any archfey claim any elf. A claimed elf serves the archfey that claimed them, and the archfey decides where their soul goes at death. An elf who refuses is erased from existence. No archfey may claim more than five elves in a year, and no more than twenty may be claimed in a year altogether. The elves do not know the terms.

What they know is that elves go missing. Elves and fae shared the kingdom of [[Kingdom of Thalamas|Thalamas]] until 105 AM, when the rumours and the disappearances together broke it in two. The elves kept [[Kingdom of Thalanea|Thalanea]], and they have been in a cold war with the fae of [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]] ever since.

Dislike of the fey is the standing position of the whole people. An elf who feels otherwise is making a choice the neighbours will notice. Elf children are told about Yupri, a faerie who carries off badly behaved elf children and eats them, and the name is all that is left of Ius Primum in common memory.

The elves speak [[Sylvan]].

Drow, high, wood, astral, sea and shadar-kai elves are all elves.

> *An elf in Darasil would not take my coin until she had turned it over twice, to be sure it was not one of the old Thalamas pieces with a fae on the back.*

## Design Notes

The elves are right that something is taking them and wrong about who. A party that learns the terms of Ius Primum knows something every elf in Thalanea would want to hear, and that knowledge could start the war the cold one has been holding off.

Made by [[Genasi]] at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM, with help from the [[Dragons]]. Genasi took the five elemental realms of [[Tarkis]] as the model.

There are five base kinds, one for each realm: fire, water, air, earth, and noxious. There are ten more, one for each seam where two realms meet: steam, ash, magma, smoke, ice, marsh, acid, dust, plague, and metal. All fifteen are genasi.

Their kingdom is [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]]. Genasi founded it in 2 AM and ruled as its first king. Worship of the dragons runs higher there than probably anywhere else.

The elementals speak [[Genoan]]. Genasi built it himself. Every word declares what it is made of through its vowels, and he sorted the whole vocabulary personally. His sorting is still the standard.

> *A smoke genasi in Geno told me that her name could not go into Genoan without the vowels of either fire or air, and that her parents argued about it for a year. In the end they gave her a name from outside the language, so that it takes So in front of it, like a foreigner's.*

## Design Notes

The fifteen kinds give a player a wide choice. So Genoan makes the choice matter in conversation. A seam genasi in Geno is, in the grammar, a question nobody has settled.

## Open

How the seam kinds are treated in Geno, socially and in law, is unwritten.

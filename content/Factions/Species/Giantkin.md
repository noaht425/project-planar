Made by [[Kleipur]] at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM. Kleipur was the first Progenitor to be made, out of more material than any of the others, and Kleipur is a giant.

On 98 Aucaela, 1 AM, Kleipur gave six of the first giantkin the power that made them the titans. See [[Ascendant Gods]] for the titans themselves.

Most giantkin live in [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]], which they share with the orcs. The arrangement is old and mostly works, and the crown passes between the two peoples in turn. The [[Valley of the Colossi]], with its statues of the titans, joins Oinarria to Sylfaen.

Giantkin memory is long and specific. Families keep accounts of the first year of the Age of the Many that have come down intact through every generation since.

Goliaths are the usual build.

> *An old giant in Tagra described the morning Kleipur gave the titans their power as if she had been standing there. She had not been. Her great-grandmother had, and the account had come down through the family with every detail in place, including what Gallum had been laughing at.*

## Design Notes

The giantkin remember the beginning, and that makes any giantkin family a source a party can consult. Their memory of the titans is first-hand at a few removes, and it does not always agree with what the titans say about themselves.

Made by [[Talav]] at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM. Gnomes, dwarves and halflings are all earthborn.

They have no kingdom of their own and live across most of the others, most heavily wherever there is something worth digging into. The queen of [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]], [[Palas Arakol]], is an earthborn halfling.

Of the nine peoples they are the least written about, which says more about the world's historians than about the earthborn.

> *When I told a gnome in Lonvil that I was writing about the earthborn, she asked how many pages I had given them. I told her. She said that was more than usual, and that I should not be too pleased with myself.*

## Design Notes

The earthborn are everywhere and noticed nowhere, which makes them the people a party can find in any kingdom doing any job.

## Open

Whether the earthborn keep a language of their own is unwritten.

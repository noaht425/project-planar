Made by [[Sithi|Sithi Yumboe]] at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM. Sithi did not do it alone. The [[Archfey]] helped, and the price they asked was Sithi's name, which they took and still have.

The fae of the mortal realm are also called feyborn. Each kind leans toward one fey domain, because every archfey had a hand in every kind.

Most fae live in [[Kingdom of Kumas|Kumas]], which they kept when [[Kingdom of Thalamas|Thalamas]] split in 105 AM. Some left for other kingdoms at the split, and their descendants are scattered through the rest of the world.

The fae rules of true names and use-names apply to them. See [[Iris]] and [[High Fae]]. The fae of Kumas speak [[Low Fae]], which has no word for thanks.

> *A changeling in Fylwa introduced herself by a different use-name each of the three times we met, and the third time she was offended that I had not kept up.*

## Design Notes

The fae of Arenis carry the archfey's rules into the mortal world without the archfey's power. A party dealing with a fae is dealing with someone who takes names and debts as seriously as an archfey does.

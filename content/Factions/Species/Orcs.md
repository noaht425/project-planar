Made by [[Shram]] at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM. Shram is still alive.

The orcs are not a wandering people and they are not anybody's raiders. They built a kingdom, [[Kingdom of Oinarria|Oinarria]], which they share with the giantkin, and it has been a going concern for seven hundred years. The crown passes between the two peoples in turn.

The orcs arrived to a kingdom that had already decided it was not going to be a small one, and they have kept it that way.

Orcs living outside Oinarria are usually there for a reason.

Half-orcs exist, and Shram had nothing to do with them.

> *The orc who ran the inn I stayed at in Bacta kept two sizes of everything behind the bar and charged the same for both. When I asked her why, she said that otherwise nobody would ever order the small one.*

## Design Notes

Orcs here are settled, prosperous and proud of a kingdom they share. That is the expectation to set for players who arrive expecting raiders.

Most beastfolk live in the [[Kingdom of Sylfaen]] and speak [[Dzoa]].

Peoples:
- Avian: Aarakocra, Kenku, Owlin, Alez
- Reptilian: Dragonborn, Lizardfolk, Saurus
- Aquatic: Triton, Tortle, Locathah, Grung
- Insectoid: Kekrik, Yuan-Ti
- Mammalian: Bellu, Minotaur, Tabaxi, Harengon, Satyr, Loxodon

Sylfaen law sorts beastfolk by mouth type: Lipped, Snouted, Beaked and Long-jawed. Each type has an accepted way of pronouncing Dzoa. Tortles, Locathah, Grung, Kekrik and Yuan-Ti fit none of the four types, and officials record their speech as unclean. See [[Dzoa]] for the details.

Made by [[Numen]] at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM, with help from the [[Dragons]], which is why there are so many kinds.

> *A kenku in Plowo told me that the dragons had given his people their voices. A tabaxi at the next table told him that was nonsense, and the two of them argued about which dragon had done what until the owner asked them both to leave.*

## Design Notes

The beastfolk are the most varied of the nine peoples and live in the most rigid kingdom. Sylfaen's mouth law is how a beastfolk character's life is organised, and every beastfolk player should know where they fall in it.

Made by [[Minem]] at the [[Council of Creation]] in 1 AM.

Minem was given nothing at his creation to build from, and he built the humans' language himself. That language was [[Torren (Old Common)|Torren]], and Common, which is now spoken across the world, descends from it.

Humans have no kingdom of their own and live in all of them. They are the most widely spread of the nine peoples and the least organised about it, which has worked in their favour more often than not. Nothing about being human tells anyone where a person stands, so where a human grew up matters more than it does for almost anyone else.

Plenty of humans do not know which Progenitor made their people, and plenty who do have never given it a second thought.

> *A woman in Caragna told me she thought her people had been made by Minem, but would not swear to it, and asked whether it mattered. I said I was writing a book. She wished me luck with it, in the voice people use for a long illness.*

## Design Notes

Humans are the people with no position, and that is what they offer at a table. A human character is defined by a kingdom, a trade or a patron, because the species supplies none of those.

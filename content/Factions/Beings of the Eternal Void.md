The four beings of [[Nullis]]: the two Kings and their two children.

| Being | What they are |
| --- | --- |
| [[The King of Day]] | Day, life, sun, healing and knowledge |
| [[The King of Night]] | Night, the moon, the constellations and secrets. Death until [[Orcus]] took it |
| [[Eos]] | Dawn, their daughter |
| [[Cado]] | Dusk, their son |

The Kings were born around 10,000 AF and married around 9,000 AF. Their children were born around 8,900 AF.

## The Divorce

Night split the Judgements in 3,097 AF, persuaded by [[Indri]], who wore Day's face to finish the work. Day divorced Night over it in 3,096 AF, at the [[Syzygy]]. In the fight Day was scarred and Night was blinded, and Nullis began to separate into a light half and a dark half. The separation is still not finished.

The two Kings want the same thing, which is to know who did it, and neither knows the other is looking. Eos wants them back together. Cado wants the separation finished, and has not been seen since the divorce.

## Names and Language

The true names of the Kings survive hidden in the words for day and night in the ancient languages and in the languages of immortals, and no modern mortal language keeps them. The Void speaks [[Theis]].

Nullis is the hardest plane to reach. Almost everything known about the four comes from records, and from Eos.

> *The few shrines to the Kings that I found in the mortal realm were each to one King or the other, never both. Where both had a shrine in the same town, the two had been built facing away from each other, and nobody could tell me who had decided that.*

## Design Notes

The Void is a family with a divorce at the centre, and the setting's oldest injury is a marriage. A party that can carry one true sentence from one half of Nullis to the other has more power over the Kings than anything else in the setting.

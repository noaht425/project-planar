The alliance of [[Kingdom of Starkon|Starkon]], [[Kingdom of Mathair|Mathair]] and [[Kingdom of Regno|Regno]].

All three kingdoms were founded in 3 AM. Starkon came first, on 8 Aucaela, and Mathair and Regno followed on the same day nine days later.

Each kingdom calls the alliance by its own name.

| Kingdom | Name for the alliance |
| --- | --- |
| Mathair | Trois Re |
| Starkon | Drei Kunig |
| Regno | Tre Regis |

## The Alliance

The three are genuinely good allies and have been for a long time. Their interests line up more often than not, and none of them has any record of working against the others. At the [[Panarex]] they usually vote together.

> *At the Panarex the three of them sit together, and a doorkeeper there told me that in his time they had never once voted differently. He seemed to think this was the most suspicious thing about them. As far as I have been able to find out, it is simply true.*

## Design Notes

The Trois Re is a stable alliance on purpose. A setting with this many cold wars needs one bloc that works, and three kingdoms that agree with each other give a party a dependable patron and a large block of votes at the Panarex.

A DM who wants a scandal inside it is welcome to write one. The doorkeeper's suspicion is there to hang it on, and nothing on this page rules it out.

The three sphinxes of [[Temporalis]]. The Scholarchs and the sphinxes are the same beings under two names.

| Scholarch | Area |
| --- | --- |
| [[It That Was]] | The past, a lake ringed by waterfalls nobody has reached |
| [[It That Is]] | The present, a busy city |
| [[It That Will Be]] | The future, an endless field under stars, with trees at the edge |

Their shared purpose is to keep the three times in balance against one another. Too much weight on any one of the past, the present, or the future and the realm begins to slip. What slips in [[Temporalis]] does not stay in Temporalis. They will not be more specific than that to anyone who asks.

The three are close. They keep their own areas and rarely need to be in the same place. No mortal has ever seen them disagree, and nothing said between them has reached anyone outside the realm. [[It That Will Be]] made divination harder without consulting the other two, and both have backed it in public ever since.

## What They Do

Most mortals who reach Temporalis come to ask the Scholarchs something, and some of them leave with a warlock pact. See [[Warlock Scholarch]].

None of the three can see outside its own area. This is the reason they take warlocks, who serve as eyes in places the Scholarchs are not, and it is why news is worth more to them than deference is.

Divination used to be far easier than it is. It That Will Be grew paranoid and made it harder, and the difficulty has stayed where it was set.

The Scholarchs hate [[Isadora]], who spends her time tipping things over to see which way they fall. Luck is noise in what they are trying to read. Every time she interferes with something, what [[It That Will Be]] can see of the future gets worse, which means the balance they are keeping gets harder to keep.

> *The only time I saw a Scholarch lose its composure was when I mentioned Isadora. It That Is stopped what it was doing and asked me when I had last seen her and where, and then asked me again, as though the first answer might have changed.*

## Design Notes

The Scholarchs are knowledge with a price, in a realm that is hard to reach and harder to cross. Their hatred of Isadora is the one place where they behave like anyone else.

## Open

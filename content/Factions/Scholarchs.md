The three sphinxes of [[Temporalis]]. The Scholarchs and the sphinxes are the same beings under two names.

| Scholarch | Area |
| --- | --- |
| [[It That Was]] | The past, a lake ringed by waterfalls nobody has reached |
| [[It That Is]] | The present, a busy city |
| [[It That Will Be]] | The future, an endless field under stars, with trees at the edge |

Their shared purpose is to keep things in balance, and they will not be more specific about it than that.

## What They Do

Most mortals who reach Temporalis come to ask the Scholarchs something, and some of them leave with a warlock pact. See [[Warlock Scholarch]].

Divination used to be far easier than it is. It That Will Be grew paranoid and made it harder, and the difficulty has stayed where it was set.

The Scholarchs hate [[Isadora]], who spends her time tipping things over to see which way they fall.

> *The only time I saw a Scholarch lose its composure was when I mentioned Isadora. It That Is stopped what it was doing and asked me when I had last seen her and where, and then asked me again, as though the first answer might have changed.*

## Design Notes

The Scholarchs are knowledge with a price, in a realm that is hard to reach and harder to cross. Their hatred of Isadora is the one place where they behave like anyone else.

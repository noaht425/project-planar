An organisation that works with and for the [[Dragons]]. Founded several hundred years ago and still running, with one branch for each dragon.

They are neutral. They carry out the will of the dragons as they are given it, and they do not appear to have an agenda beyond that. Nobody who has looked into them has come away calling them malicious.

**They have roots everywhere.** People who work with dragons know them. The average person in the street has never heard of them, and that is not an accident.

They turn up wherever dragons do, down to sponsoring a theatre production about them.

## The Branches

One branch per dragon, including the dead ones. The branches of [[Zaros]] and [[Vari]] still exist and are working on how to bring them back, so far without success.

[[Mens]]'s branch has no publicly known head. The name on record from many years ago is Baris Veril, and [[Nara Veril]], the current queen of [[Kingdom of Geno|Geno]], is a descendant of his.

Written accounts name several different people as the head of the society at the same time, which is explained by the branches rather than by any of the accounts being wrong.

> *The Zaros branch still meets. I sat at the back of one of their evenings in Regno. The subscriptions were collected. The minutes of the last meeting were read out. A man gave a report on progress that contained no progress. Nobody there spoke about the dragon as dead, and the word they use for him is away.*

## Design Notes

The society is the mortal-world handle on the dragons. A party can deal with the dragons' interests for a whole campaign without ever meeting a dragon.

Keeping branches running for the dead dragons gives the resurrection effort an organisation behind it rather than leaving it to the dragons alone.

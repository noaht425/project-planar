Loyal to [[Indri]]]].

Thinks they are descended from Indri but are actually a descendent of [[Tiyanak]]. Adopted by the Yavimaya family. 

Member of [[House Yavimaya]]. Linva (sister) and some others tried to coup against her house. She got involved for a while while but never did anything terrible. Indri found out and he banished all of them to [[Arenis]] and took away Aura's wings. Sister was trying to overthrow Indri, having heard of a relic called Mendax that is supposedly able to kill him. Ezra figured out that it was Tiyanak in disguise and so he killed him to preserve the secret. Tuomara is hunting Aura.

Doesn't understand the ways of Arenis due to being sheltered

Aura was born to the Archfey Indri, and was raised by him for six years until he grew bored of her due to her inability to trick people in any fashion. He pawned her off to the Kendarin family, where she lived under Selvala and Marwyn Kendarin, as well as with her older sister Linva Kendarin (5 years older). For the next 11 years, she grew up without incident. She found other friends, some within House Kendarin, and some outside, and was taught to have a strong moral compass by Linva. She remained relatively sheltered from the outside world, mostly thanks to Linva, and on the few times they actually ventured outside, she was watched very closely and carefully. 

She did make other friends during this time period, including:
- Ezra Linvale, an armorer who taught her and piqued her interest in the magic of artifice
- Argsgroth, a gruff, older fey that was the current ruler of House Kendarin
- Verila, another fairy who instilled within her a certain sense of whimsy
- Tuomara, a no-nonsense fey that also happened to be the leader of the rebellion Linva became involved in
- Nud, a silly little goblin jester that took a liking to her due to being so innocent (he told her stories about a made up place called “Nudland”)

A year before Aura was banished, she began noticing her sister was acting strange, but thought nothing of it until six months later, when Linva explained to Aura that they were trying to dispose of Indri. She said that they had “discovered the means to do so properly”, though Aura didn’t (and still doesn’t) know what that means. She explained that Indri “had something they wanted”, but he suspected her and the rest of the rebellion, so she needed Aura to infiltrate Indri’s domain to steal from him. Linva said she didn’t want to involve Aura at all, and had fought very hard against Tuomara, who finally convinced Linva after she said that if Linva was caught, Aura would likely pay the price as well. What neither Linva nor Aura knew was that Tuomara planned to sacrifice Aura Linva, betraying them both, to gain the trust of Indri so she could infiltrate herself. Aura was trained extensively on what to do and say so she wouldn’t be caught, and though Linva had serious doubts about it, she had no choice but to go with the plan (partially because she didn’t trust that Tuomara would keep them safe if they didn’t follow her orders). The night before Aura was to begin her task, she went to Ezra and confided in him secretly, which Indri overheard. The following morning, when Aura went to infiltrate, she got caught immediately and she, as well as everyone else, was put on trial. 

Originally, Indri planned to kill them all, but Tuomara convinced Indri that it was all Aura’s idea, so he banished Tuomara, her team, and Linvara instead. He then killed Ezra as an example to all of them, which greatly hurt Aura, before cursing Aura and banishing her as well. After Aura was banished, she spent six months wandering the outskirts of the feywild plane before arriving at wherever it is she is now. 

After she was banished, a portion of Ezra’s soul that still remained managed to find Aura, and showed her how it could turn into her armor. Though Aura doesn’t actually know that the pixie is Ezra’s soul, she does feel a familiar warmth emanating from it. 

Additionally, Tuomara and her crew are hunting for her to get revenge, and Linva is also looking for her, just for different reasons. 

Aura’s curse: She lost her wings (like, they were physically ripped from her body), and she lost the knowledge that it was specifically Indri who had done all of it (the second bit was done mostly for Indri’s amusement). The particulars of the curse means that her wings may never come back, however, Indri designed it so that while her memories of her trauma at Indri’s hand is currently faded, it may come back in the future. 




## Magic Item

Mysterious Pixie Armor

Belongs to [[Aura Kendarin of House Yavimaya]]. A small pixie that follows her around and can become the very armor that she wears.

A fine suit of armor, covered in filigree designs envoling the patterns on insects. A large set of filigreed butterfly wings are depicted on the back of the armor.

Reduce all incoming damage dealt to you by an amount equal to half your proficiency bonus, rounded down.

## Scaling

**Level 5:** You gain expertise in Charimsa (Deception) checks. When you use the Thunder Gauntlets or Lightning Launcher feature of the armor, you may roll an additional damage die and add it to the total damage.

**Level 9:** You gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed while wearing the armor as the filigree wings become real.

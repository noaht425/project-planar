[[Elementals]] (Air Genasi)

Light Cleric of [[Brilis]]



Wisp grew up in Geno. She was the kind of kid who could never accept an answer at face value. Every story produced another question. Why did the Titans kill Zaros? What was the world like before the Planar Collision? Where did the dragons come from? Why are there different planes? How do we know any of these stories actually happened? The nearby Temple of Brilis was the one place that indulged her endless questions, opening its vast library to her. She studied history, religion, myth, and the planes until the temple became her second home. Their beliefs began to resonate with her, and she eventually became a priest and scholar of Brilis. Years later, her mentor, Gustav, showed her a secret archive she had never known existed and gave her ancient records to “feed her curiosity”. Most were written in an unknown language (Theis), but Wisp recognized three names: Zaros, Vari, and Pyrrha. She spent the day obsessively trying to translate them. The next day, while studying in her usual secluded spot in the woods, her frightened best friend, Lyanna, found her and demanded to know what she had done. She claimed the entire temple was searching for her. Before Wisp could understand why, her mentor appeared and cast Sleep on her friend. He ordered Wisp to take the records and leave Geno immediately. She begged him to come with her. He refused. He would not explain what was happening, only insisting that she had to be the one to do this. When she protested that she could not read the records, he told her she would learn. “If anyone can do it, it's you.” Wisp trusted him and fled. Now she travels with the records hidden among her belongings, searching for the knowledge needed to decipher them.
